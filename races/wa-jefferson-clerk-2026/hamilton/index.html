The Clerk's Office serves as the hub for Superior Court activity, and I am dedicated to ensuring our services are accessible, efficient, and delivered with professionalism and integrity.

My priorities are to promote access to justice, stay abreast of new legislation and rules affecting the Clerks' statutory duties, and support the people that do this important work.
