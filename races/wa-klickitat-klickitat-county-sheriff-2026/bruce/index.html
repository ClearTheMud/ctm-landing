Statement
John Bruce has built a respected career in law enforcement and is known as a steady, decisive leader with strong integrity. He has handled complex operational and personnel challenges and approaches his work with a clear commitment to public service and to the community he serves. John has experience leading a law enforcement agency, including budgeting, staffing studies, and organizational planning. He works well with other elected officials and community partners. He also prioritizes training and mentoring, helping prepare staff for future roles and strengthening the agency as a whole. John would appreciate your vote.
