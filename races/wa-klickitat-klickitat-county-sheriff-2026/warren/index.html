1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience
None.

Other Professional Experience
Tony brings over 25 years of law enforcement experience, currently serving as Detective Sergeant at the Klickitat County Sheriff’s Office. His career began in 1997 as a reserve Deputy for Skamania County. In 2008, he joined the Bingen-White Salmon Police Department and Klickitat County in 2021. Tony has completed extensive specialized training and holds numerous certifications. Outside of public service, Tony successfully operates a small dump truck and excavating business.

Education
Washington Basic Law Enforcement Academy, Bachelor’s Degree in Criminal Justice, FBI Law Enforcement Executive Development Association (LEEDA) Trilogy.

Community Service
Reserve Deputy for Skamania County.

Statement
Raised in a law enforcement family, Tony Warren leads with honesty, integrity, and proactive public safety. As a hands-on, boots-on-the-ground Sheriff, Tony will stand firmly to protect your safety and freedoms. He will ensure fiscal responsibility, maximizing the budget to increase essential staffing and training. True safety requires collaboration. Tony will reinforce community-oriented policing, placing deputies directly into neighborhoods to listen, build trust, and solve problems together. By partnering with local schools, businesses, and families, Tony will prevent crime, foster deep accountability, and ensure the Sheriff’s Office reflects the true values of Klickitat County.

Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, Klickitat County Sheriff. Accessed 2026-07-16. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
