1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (General Election 2026)

Elected Experience
None

Other Professional Experience
Klickitat County Sheriff’s Office, 2015-2023; Goldendale Police Department, 2008-2015; Bingen-White Salmon Police Department; 2004-2008. Law enforcement duties included patrol, firearms instructor, field training officer, Search and Rescue, and commercial vehicle inspector. Volunteer firefighter, 8 years.


Education
Bachelor of Science, Exercise Science, Central Washington University; Minor in Law Enforcement; Peace Officer Certificate, Washington State Criminal Justice Training Commission.

Community Service
Volunteer Firefighter, Skamania County Fire District 1, 1998-2004; Volunteer Goldendale High School track coach, 2022-2025; Volunteer Firefighter, Rural 7 fire district, 2024-present.

Statement
I want every resident and visitor to Klickitat County to be safe and secure. Nineteen years of full-time law enforcement experience gives me insight into what needs to be changed. I will improve the training and morale of our deputies. I will build relationships with other law enforcement agencies and county departments while managing the budget responsibly.

My allegiance will be to the people of our county, not to a political party. I will serve with integrity, humility, and the highest degree of professionalism, respecting my vow to uphold the Constitutions of the United States and the State of Washington.

Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, Klickitat County Sheriff. Accessed 2026-10-10. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
