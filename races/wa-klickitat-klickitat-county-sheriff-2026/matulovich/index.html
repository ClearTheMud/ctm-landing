Community Service
Volunteer Firefighter, Skamania County Fire District 1, 1998-2004; Volunteer Goldendale High School track coach, 2022-2025; Volunteer Firefighter, Rural 7 fire district, 2024-present.

Statement
I want every resident and visitor to Klickitat County to be safe and secure. Nineteen years of full-time law enforcement experience gives me insight into what needs to be changed. I will improve the training and morale of our deputies. I will build relationships with other law enforcement agencies and county departments while managing the budget responsibly.

My allegiance will be to the people of our county, not to a political party. I will serve with integrity, humility, and the highest degree of professionalism, respecting my vow to uphold the Constitutions of the United States and the State of Washington.
