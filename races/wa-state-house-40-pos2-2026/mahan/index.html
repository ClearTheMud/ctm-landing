1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

This will be your first opportunity to have me represent you, the proud residents of District 40, who call San Juan, Skagit, and Whatcom County your home.

Other Professional Experience

24 Years as the Business Owner and Hearing Aid Specialist at Miracle-Ear, Bellingham.

Education

Completed an intensive undergraduate interdisciplinary program in Pre Medical and Natural Resource Conversation, at the University of Florida.



Community Service

I donate hearing aids and all my services to low-income patients in need. Over the years we have been able to help hundreds of people in this area hear better through the Miracle-Ear Foundation.

Statement

We need an Independent vote in our area who has small business knowledge and community knowledge. I have talked to all kinds of local residents every day at my office for 24 years. I lean socially liberal, but if we keep voting straight down the Democrat ticket, you might be getting career politicians who vote without the normal citizens in mind. Recent decisions keep increasing costs that burden our area and decrease people's disposable income. Taxes, utility increases, government salary increases, and over regulation need to be balanced with the reality of what citizens can afford these days.
