1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience
Third term State Representative. Elected by my peers as Majority Whip.

Other Professional Experience
Clean Energy and Environmental Community Organizer. As Program Director at Sustainable Connections I founded the Community Energy Challenge, creating living-wage jobs, cutting pollution, and reducing energy costs for thousands of people and businesses in Whatcom, Skagit, and San Juan counties.

Education
BA in Environmental Policy and Planning, Western Washington University

Community Service
Past President, Kulshan Community Land Trust: Creating affordable housing for low- and middle-income families. Worker Retraining Advisory Board: during the 2008 recession, helping people build new careers.

Statement
I fight for bold climate action and to lower costs for working families. I helped cap rent increases. We taxed millionaires to provide free lunches for kids, healthcare, and tax relief for small businesses and low-income families. We developed Washington’s energy strategy to reduce pollution and reduce energy costs. I've helped fund new ferries, clean energy development, mental health treatment in Whatcom and Skagit Counties, and a record number of affordable homes. Endorsed by Washington Conservation Action and unions representing nurses and firefighters. I’m 100% pro-choice. 

I’m voting no on all statewide initiatives and ask you to join me.
