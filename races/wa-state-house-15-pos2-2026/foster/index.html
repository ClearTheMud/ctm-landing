Community Service

Current involvement in Scouts, Two completed terms of AmeriCorps Service (3,400 hours), Past involvement with Circle K International (Kiwanis), Yakima Valley Systems of Care, Naches Valley Community Coalition, Youth for Community Growth, and Naches Leo Lions.



Statement

For a decade, Washington legislators have run a reckless cycle of tax increases and deficits. Our schools are not producing results despite massive increases in spending, proving that throwing money at the problem is not the solution.  Our local, rural healthcare is constantly pressured with new regulations that threaten to force even more facility closures across the valley. 

As your representative, I will work to ensure that our family values and rural needs are heard in Olympia, and work to ensure that Washington is a state we can be proud of for generations to come.
