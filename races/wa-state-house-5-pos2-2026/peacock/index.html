1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

Not a career politician.

Other Professional Experience

23 years Army Service, Human Intelligence Warrant Officer, Counter-Intelligence Agent, two Afghanistan Combat tours, Bronze Star, Combat Action Badge. KCGOP Vice Chair, Senior Industrial Security Specialist - Boeing.



Education

Master of Business Administration-Finance Emphasis, Webster University; B.A.-Philosophy with Epistemology focus, University of Washington.



Community Service

My family has lived continuously in Hobart, WA since 1887. Community service has always been part of our tradition, and thanks to my father, Dick Peacock, long-time president of the Maple Valley Historical Society, I’ve developed an in-depth understanding of the histories of the areas around Issaquah, Maple Valley, Ravensdale, Black Diamond, and Enumclaw.



Statement

Washington is becoming increasingly unaffordable, and introducing a state income tax would only worsen the situation. Residents already pay some of the highest fuel taxes, which drives up costs across the board. To address these challenges, we must encourage employers not to leave and prioritize issues like affordable housing, quality education, and reducing crime. Unfortunately, Washington’s one-party rule has failed to deliver effectively on these issues. Continuing to vote the same way and expecting different results is futile—it’s time for a change. There are real solutions, let’s do what works.

Endorsed by the 5th LD and King County Republicans.
