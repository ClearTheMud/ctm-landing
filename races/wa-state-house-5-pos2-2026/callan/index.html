1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

Current State Representative, Vice-Chair, Capital Budget Committee; Member of the House Appropriations Committee and the House K-12 Education Committee; Previously on Human Services Early Learning, Former Issaquah School Board Director.


Other Professional Experience

Former lead engineer and project manager for Boeing. Former consultant managing large-scale e-commerce software development programs for businesses. 

Education

Bachelor of Science in mathematics with an emphasis in computer science from Northern Arizona University.

Community Service

Member of Kiwanis and Issaquah Chamber of Commerce. Former PTA leader, Former loaned executive to United Way of King County, past board member of The Garage Teen Center, and Influence the Choice Drug Prevention Alliance for Youth.

Statement

Good policy comes from listening, not partisanship. I bring an engineer's problem-solving to Olympia. I’ll keep fighting for great public schools, mental health care for kids, workforce housing for the families who serve our communities, and gun safety that saves lives. I'll defend reproductive freedom, fight for affordable childcare, protect forests and clean air, and work for a tax code that doesn't squeeze working people. Endorsed: Educators, State Labor Council, Grocery Workers, Firefighters, Nurses, Sierra Club, Washington Conservation Action, Planned Parenthood, Alliance for Gun Responsibility, Building Trades, Housing Alliance, Congresswoman Kim Schrier, Mayors and Council Members across the 5th LD.
