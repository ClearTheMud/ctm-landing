1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

State Representative; Deputy Majority Floor Leader; previous Everett City Councilmember

Other Professional Experience

Years of public service and business experience, former Everett small business owner, event coordinator, staff at the State House of Representatives, working mom, and co-chair of the Underground Economy Taskforce

Education

BA History – Whitman College; Institute for a Democratic Future Fellow; CERT Training



Community Service

Former Delta Neighborhood Association Chair; Everett Districting Commissioner; Everett Public Schools Fiscal Advisory Council; Council of Neighborhoods; Delta Kids Program Founder; 2020 Snohomish Conservation District Conservation Leader of the Year; Everett Essentials; Food Bank Produce Grower; Downtown Everett Flower Program Volunteer; Denney Juvenile Justice Center Volunteer Teacher



Statement

Too many of us are getting priced out of Snohomish County with rising costs of gas, housing, childcare and healthcare. I experienced childhood poverty and want better for my neighbors. I’m honored to be your voice in Olympia and remain committed to the needs of our families, workers, seniors, small businesses, and most vulnerable.

My priorities the next two years: lowering costs of living, supporting working families, affordable housing, childcare, improving healthcare access, protecting consumers, and continuing to build safe, resilient communities. Endorsements: Senator June Robinson, Sheriff Johnson, SnoCo Democrats, Planned Parenthood Alliance Advocates, Washington State Labor Council and more!
