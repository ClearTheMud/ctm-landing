Community Service

Board Member, Habitat for Humanity of Clallam County; Board Member, Peninsula Dispute Resolution Center;  Board Director, Clallam County Homelessness Task Force; Member, Port Angeles Parks and Recreation Commission;

Statement

You deserve a voice in Olympia who knows firsthand the challenges working families face, and has experience tackling them. My family bounced around between apartments, duplexes, and a trailer until my parents got into a low-income program and bought their first home. I know the importance of stable housing, and it's a key reason why I'm running. My main issues are: housing families can afford, public safety shaped by frontline experience, and economic development that reaches our rural communities and creates good paying jobs.

Endorsed by Senator John Lovick, former Senator Kevin Van De Wege, and former Representative Mike Cooper.
