Statement

Allow me to put my business, technical, and legal skills and experience to work to ensure the Olympic Peninsula’s future for families and retirees. Independent, placing people over party, I will promote affordability by trimming regulations on housing, limiting regressive taxes, and ensuring the state does not tax income. We must prioritize state funding for essentials: education, infrastructure, and public health and safety, while requiring accountability and effectiveness.

I will advocate for a business-friendly regulatory and tax structure; support law enforcement and emphasize treatment to address homelessness; and respect people from all walks of life. Thank you for your vote.
