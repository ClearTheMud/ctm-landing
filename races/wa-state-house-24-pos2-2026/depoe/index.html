1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

Former Vice-Chair, Makah Tribal Council




Other Professional Experience

Commercial fisherman, land manager, and emergency first responder fighting wildfires and cleaning up oil spills. Current Executive Director for the Association of Washington Tribes, where I bring Tribal Nations, business community leaders, labor partners, and elected officials together to solve problems. Former executive team member at the Washington Department of Natural Resources.


Education

A.A., Edmonds College; B.A., University of Washington.

Community Service

Member, Washington Conservation Action Board, Salmon Defense Board, and Low Carbon Energy Siting Study Advisory Board. Governor Inslee appointee to Washington's Environmental Justice Council.

Statement

Our rural schools face devastating cuts, while seniors on fixed incomes are being forced from their homes. Everything from gas to groceries is too expensive. Send me to Olympia to take on corporate special interests that make our lives harder. I’ll cut red tape, regressive taxes, and costs for working families. 

Everyday people need a fighter–especially in rural communities. I’ll fix the system that’s rigged against us and never settle for less than we deserve. Endorsed by Congresswoman Emily Randall, Washington Conservation Action, Washington State Labor Council, American Federation of Teachers, and dozens of legislators. More at depoeforwashington.com.
