Statement

As a democratic socialist my first priority is building a government that works for the working class, rather than for the corporate duopoly. 

Fully divested from private or corporate interests, I can work with other progressive and demsoc legislators to enact legislation made by and for the people – laws that will ensure that our government meets the needs of its citizens, rather than prioritizing the wants of profit-seeking corporations. Pro-worker legislation prioritizes essential services like healthcare, childcare, housing, food, education, and transportation; funded by progressive taxation models that ensure the upper classes pay their fair share.
