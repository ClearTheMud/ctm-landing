1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

While I may be new to elected office. I am not new to serving people, advocating for families, or working to improve lives in our community.

Other Professional Experience

I have also served as an educator, social worker, caregiver, and as a federal civilian employee supporting military families at JBLM.


Education

I hold two Bachelor’s degrees in Commerce and Nutritional Science from Delaware State University, an Associate Degree in Nursing (ADN), and both a Master’s and Doctorate in Divinity.


Community Service

This includes  uplifting  individuals and families in our community,  mentoring youth,  working alongside families in  need, my mission is  to build stronger, healthier communities.

Statement

I have seen the same reality: hardworking people yet facing systems that do not always work for them. I am running to fight for people with disabilities, so they have real access to jobs, transportation, and the support they need to live with dignity. For caregivers and nursing assistants who deserve better pay, respect, and recognition for the critical work they do. Improve transportation in Puyallup so families can get to work, school, and essential services more easily. Strengthening  programs for our youth to help prevent crime and create real opportunities for success. For  working mothers with toddlers.
