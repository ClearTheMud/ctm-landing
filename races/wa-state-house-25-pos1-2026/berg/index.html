1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

David is a Puyallup School District Director, serving since 2021.



Other Professional Experience

Washington State School Directors' Association (WSSDA) Leadership, 2024; Office of the Superintendent of Public Instruction Advisory Committee for Gifted Education, 2011–2021; Washington State Highly Capable Program Technical Working Group, 2010

Education

David is a graduate of the University of Puget Sound, where he earned his BA in Politics and Government.



Community Service

Puyallup School District Volunteer; YMCA Youth Sports Coach; Puyallup PTA Council Legislative Chair; Washington State PTA Region 5 Legislative Chair; Washington State PTA Legislative Committee; President, Washington Coalition for Gifted Education, 2013–2021; Northwest Gifted Child Association Board of Directors

Statement

I am running to deliver practical solutions for the 25th District. With over two decades of experience working for Washington families, I understand that state policy must prioritize the economic stability of working people. My focus is clear: strengthening public education, protecting workers' rights, and expanding economic opportunities. I believe in making government work for everyone. I am a school board director and public education advocate committed to being a responsive representative who listens to your needs. I would be honored to earn your vote for a stronger Washington.
