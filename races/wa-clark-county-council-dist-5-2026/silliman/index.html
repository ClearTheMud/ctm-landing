Statement
Clark County residents deserve leadership that delivers real results, not just talk. That’s why I sponsored Charter Amendments that will prioritize public safety, produce budget transparency, remove ambiguity, and restore checks and balances to our Charter. I will continue working to protect taxpayers, reduce unnecessary government interference, and promote practical, citizen focused solutions.

As a 56-year Clark County resident raised in La Center, who built a home, raised a family, and runs a business here, I understand the values and priorities of our community because I have lived them my entire life. I care deeply about protecting our quality of life and ensuring Clark County remains a place where families can afford to live, work, and succeed.

My four thousand hours of experience working in the Council Office and current service on the Charter Review Commission means I will hit the ground running on day one. I will continue standing with law enforcement, prioritizing public safety, and make reducing crime a priority. I will be promoting housing affordability, and work to deliver parks, trails, and infrastructure our community deserves.

If you want a citizen focused representative who will deliver, I respectfully ask for your support.
