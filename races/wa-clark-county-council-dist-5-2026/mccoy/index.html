1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

Battle Ground City Council since 2021; Mayor of Battle Ground 2023-2025; Battle Ground School Board 2017-2021, served as President for 2 years.

Other Professional Experience

Small business owner in Battle Ground over 20 years; formerly an Account Manager for Buckman Specialty Chemicals.

Education

Chemical Engineering BS, Oregon State University.

Community Service

Battle Ground Education Foundation, board member and volunteer; volunteer for Battle Ground Seniors; Battle Ground Community Education – previous volunteer basketball and softball coach.

Statement

Clark County is home. As a small business owner, husband, and father, I have built my
life in this community. I understand what makes our County a great place to live, work
and raise a family. People choose to live in Clark County and invest in their future. Our
county government should make that easier, not harder. I believe county government
should be open, accountable and responsive to the people it serves. My service on the
Battle Ground School Board, City Council and as Mayor has been guided by listening,
bringing people together and delivering real results.

Clark County taxpayers must not pay for light rail unless approved by voters. Focusing
on responsible growth, infrastructure needs, public safety and overall affordability we
can work together to protect what makes this County special. I will be a strong
proponent of responsible budgets, carefully managed growth and ensuring law
enforcement and emergency services have the tools and facilities needed to protect our
communities.

My record of leadership is being practical, transparent and focused on results. I am
committed to serving with integrity and putting our community first. I respectfully ask for
your support.
