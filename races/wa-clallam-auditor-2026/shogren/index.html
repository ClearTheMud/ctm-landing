Statement

I’m running for Clallam County Auditor to restore trust in our elections and to make sure your tax dollars are spent wisely.

Secure, transparent elections with paper ballots you can watch and count are the foundation of honest government. Clallam County deserves elections where every ballot is verifiable, every process is transparent, and the people—not software or untraceable methods—maintain real oversight.

As Auditor, I will also fight for strong fiscal responsibility. That means watching every county expense closely, cutting waste, and running services efficiently. When government spends less, there’s more money left in your pocket for your family, your bills, and your future.

I believe in simple, honest government that works for the people—not special interests. We deserve accountability, lower costs, and real oversight of our elections and our money.

Together, let’s put voters back in charge. I ask for your support.

Virginia Shogren for Clallam County Auditor
