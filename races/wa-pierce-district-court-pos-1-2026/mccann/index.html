Statement
Judge McCann is proud to represent the citizens of Pierce County and reflects the values of the people he serves.  Judge McCann earned the trust of our community due to his commitment to public safety, victim’s rights, accountability, and justice. Kevin can be counted on to be honest, balanced and fair. 

Judge McCann has over 30 years’ experience serving our community as a deputy prosecutor and judge. Kevin’s reputation for judicial temperament and legal knowledge earned him the respect of his bench who elected him as Presiding Judge in 2022.  Judge McCann worked with justice partners to help launch the Pierce County Mental Health Court for offenders who suffer from thought or mood disorders.  Judge McCann’s philosophy for accountability and rehabilitation is key to keeping our community safe and reducing recidivism.

Kevin is a judge we can depend upon to be fair and decisive.  Please support Kevin with your vote.
