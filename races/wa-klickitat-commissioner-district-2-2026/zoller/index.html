1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (General Election 2026)

Elected Experience
Current Klickitat County Commissioner, District #2

Other Professional Experience
Background in industrial timber, agriculture, and cattle.  Timber Salesclerk, Department of Natural Resources.  Administrator/facilitator for Salmon Recovery, Watershed Planning, for Klickitat County.  Recreational business owner for over 40 years.  Chair for the Southwest Washington Aging and Disability Board, Mid-Columbia Economic Board, Columbia Gorge Housing Authority, Columbia Gorge Regional Airport Board, Columbia River Policy Advisory Group, and Board of Health.  

Education
Real Estate Degree.  One year of a two-year Paralegal degree.

Community Service
10 years as a Klickitat County Planning Commissioner.  Klickitat Community Council Co-Chair.  Wild and Scenic Rivers Council Klickitat and White Salmon Rivers.  Republican PCO appointee.  

Statement
Commissioner Zoller has set civility and respect as a top priority in her first term.  She cultivated a culture where staff feel valued with quality public service as the center of their programs.  She makes data-informed decisions and believes in a strong collaboration with government and community partners.  

Under her direction, accomplishments include bringing the Klickitat County Jail into compliance, reorganizing County departments for cost savings and efficiency, extending line connections for public transportation, focusing work and setting completion goals for the County Comprehensive Plan, Accessory Dwelling, Short Term Rental Units, and developing a balanced and sustainable budget.
