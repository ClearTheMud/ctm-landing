Other Professional Experience
Gene has spent over forty years in the construction trades. As the Klickitat County Building Inspector, he has spent 10 years learning the county infrastructure, the physical layout of Klickitat County, and the needs of its residents. Before that, he spent five years installing and servicing fireplaces and completing construction projects for Kern’s Shop. At Gale Contractor Services, he served as a production manager, handling budget, payroll, inventory, and communication with contractors. He began as a small business co-owner, working with his father for seven years in remodeling and constructing new homes.

Statement
As a building inspector, I’ve gotten to know the people of Klickitat County, both the new faces and the families that have been here for generations. My own kids and grandkids live here too, so I am invested in the economic future of Klickitat County and the wise, thoughtful use of its resources. I believe that leadership starts with listening, honesty, and a strong work ethic. My job as a commissioner will be to work for and represent the residents of Klickitat County in the planning and budgeting of future decisions made within the Klickitat County Commissioner’s office.
