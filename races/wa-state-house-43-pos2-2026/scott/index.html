1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (General Election 2026)

Elected Experience

Washington State Representative, 43rd Legislative District (2025-present). As a first-term lawmaker, I've worked to end corporate tax breaks totaling $645 million in preserved biennial spending on low income housing, wildfire mitigation, public schools, and higher education. In my two campaigns for this seat, I've been endorsed by the Washington State Labor Council, Planned Parenthood Alliance Advocates, Washington Conservation Action, among others.


Other Professional Experience

Executive Director, Got Green (2025-present). Policy Analyst, Statewide Poverty Action Network (2020-2024).

Education

History, BA studies, University of Washington (2003-2008).

Community Service

Member of the Seattle Democratic Socialists of America since January 4, 2017; Precinct Committee Officer, 43rd L.D. Democrats (2020-2023).

Statement

In my first year in office in 2025, the six largest companies in Washington State spent $2.1 million lobbying in Olympia. These oligarchs expect a return on their investment in the form of huge corporate handouts and cuts to vital services the rest of us depend on. All that's stopping them from getting it is working class movements outside our state capitol, and working class champions inside it. I'm eager to go back to work for the people of the 43rd L.D. as the first open socialist in the Washington State Legislature since 1914. 


Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, State Representative Pos. 2. Accessed 2026-10-10. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
