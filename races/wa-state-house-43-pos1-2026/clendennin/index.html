Other Professional Experience

I currently serve as a Resident Director at The University of Washington. I oversee administrative operations, manage a team of Student Leaders, and ensure the safety of nearly 10,000 residents during overnight duty shifts. My primary responsibilities include budget management, collaboration on program and student development, and serving as a Hearing Officer for Student Conduct Cases.

Education

M.A. - Student Affairs Administration, Appalachian State University, B.A. - International Studies with a focus in Security and Diplomacy and a Minor in Political Science, University of West Florida



Community Service

I spend most of my time supporting and working on behalf of students at UW.

Statement

I consider myself a pragmatic progressive Democrat. My priorities include reducing corporate influence in politics, regulating artificial intelligence to prevent unfair pricing, and strengthening public services so every Washingtonian can succeed.

Every Washingtonian deserves a great school, a walkable neighborhood, an affordable home, and a government that prioritizes people over profit. My experiences as a gay man, the son of a teacher, and a resident director have taught me the value of listening, serving others, and building community. As your state representative, I will support bold ideas and believe no problem is too big to solve.
