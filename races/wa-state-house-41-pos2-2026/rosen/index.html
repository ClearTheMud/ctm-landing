Statement

As a fourth-generation Washingtonian born and raised on Mercer Island, I've watched this state lose its way — Olympia keeps spending more and delivering less, treating your tax dollars as a blank check. State spending has more than doubled in two decades while accountability, affordability, public safety, and health outcomes have gotten worse, not better.

I'm an attorney, investor, and Washington-licensed nutritionist. I think independently, follow evidence, and value outcomes over ideology. I will prioritize accountability, affordability, and improving health outcomes for Washington families. District 41 deserves a representative who brings expertise, demands fiscal discipline, and fights for solutions.
