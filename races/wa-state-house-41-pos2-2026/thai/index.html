1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

State Representative: Deputy Majority Leader; Appropriations, Civil Rights & Judiciary, Community Safety, Education, Finance, Healthcare & Wellness, Housing, Rules committees member. Former Bellevue School District Board President. Former Washington State School Board Directors Association Vice-President.

Other Professional Experience

Pharmacist. CEO - Wyoming General & Pediatric Neurology Clinic. Instructor - Vietnam Nursing Master Program.

Education

University of Washington - School of Pharmacy. Council of Government Western Legislative Academy. National Council of Government - Henry Toll Fellowship.

Community Service

Board of Directors - Children's Mental Health Resource Center, Vietnamese Scholarship Foundation. Office of Civil Legal Aids Oversight. Public Health Advisory Board. Family Medicine Residency Network legislator representative.

Statement

I am honored to represent Eastside families and committed to building the safe, supportive communities where everyone can thrive. As a healthcare professional, protecting access to affordable and high-quality care, supporting scientific innovations, and taking meaningful action on climate change are my top priorities. As the first refugee elected, I am committed to advancing a more just, inclusive, and equitable society for all.

With your support, I will prioritize the well-being of our community - focusing on educational opportunities, expanding behavioral health services, improving transportation, and developing strong workforce across our region. I humbly ask for your vote.
