Statement

My first commitment is in guaranteeing safe, reliable and affordable water for our PUD customers. Thurston aquifers in late summers face strain. Continued sustainable supplies demand collaborative efforts. I will work with all water authorities for long-term water security.

My second commitment is to all Thurston residents. During the Great Depression Washington voted for their first initiative, the law creating Public Utility Districts. The supporting statement in the 1930 voter's pamphlet reads, "the mere filing of this measure has caused a reduction of rates practically all over the state, and its enactment will result in still further reductions, and the people will always have in their hands the remedy for extortionate rates."

Thurston families are facing higher utility bills today, especially from the foreign owned private electric monopoly. I believe, as Public Utilities Commissioner, it's my duty to remind everyone about the remedy that was forged by their great grandparents. Farmers and union members warned that if their initiative was defeated "this last remaining natural resource will pass into private hands for all time and be used for the exploitation of our people and industries."

Our forbearers were victorious. Will we now allow ourselves to be exploited for all time?
