1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

Current Thurston PUD Commissioner, District 1 

Other Professional Experience

Lifelong Thurston County resident. Twenty years at Olympia Brewery as foreman, Teamsters Local #378 member. Received Federal retraining under the Trade Adjustment Act working in the water industry since 2003. Most recently as Thurston PUD’s Director of Field Operations, overseeing 270 water systems, emergency preparedness, and system consolidation.

Education

Olympia High School graduate. Certified Water Distribution Manager II, Water Treatment Plant Operator, Cross-Connection Control Specialist, electrician, plumber, electrical administrator.

Community Service

Kiwanis, Recipient of Washington State DOH Commitment to Excellence Award for public service and utility leadership, Labor-Management employee representative for Olympia Brewery, Little Baseball Association.


Statement

I’ve spent my life serving this community, from the production floor of the Olympia Brewery to the pump houses of Thurston PUD. I understand all aspects of our water systems and combine in-the-field problem-solving with administrative management–bridging the gap between technical and executive leadership effectively. I consider the practical, environmental, and financial implications to our customers and personnel with every decision we make. “My real-world experience allows me to ask tough questions, evaluate costs realistically, and ensure taxpayers and customers’ dollars are spent responsibly and efficiently while protecting drinking water.”

As an appointed Commissioner, I am running to bring proven experience and commitment to safe, reliable service for the people of Thurston County.

Utility management is a constant balancing act: infrastructure first, cost second, and safety always. After more than two decades in public utilities, I know how to recognize warning signs before they become costly emergencies. Preventative maintenance and smart planning help stabilize rates for household budgets and maintain public safety. A utility is as strong as the people who operate it, I support a certified, well-trained workforce to ensure safe practices, strong morale, and dependable service for our entire community. Vote Jim Campbell.
