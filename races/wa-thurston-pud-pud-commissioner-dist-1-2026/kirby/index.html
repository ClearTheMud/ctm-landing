1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (General Election 2026)

Elected Experience

Lacey Parks Commissioner for 7 years, oversaw parks, civic infrastructure projects. Ran for Port Commissioner (2023), Lacey City Council (2019).

Other Professional Experience

State agency communications leader, former small business owner, and media/community outreach professional. Recipient of the 2019 Entrepreneur of the Year Award from the Lacey South Sound Chamber.

Education

Timberline High School graduate. Eastern Washington University. Seattle University. Former Saint Martin’s University business instructor.

Community Service

Gateway Rotarian and founding board member of the Gold Star Family Memorial and Purple Heart Monument of Lacey, organizations honoring local veterans, military families, and community sacrifice through public remembrance projects and civic engagement activities across Thurston County.

Statement


As a candidate for Thurston PUD Commissioner, I believe families deserve reliable, quality water service at a fair price. Too often lately, people pay more in taxes and utility costs but feel like they receive less service in return. That is unacceptable. We need practical leadership that respects taxpayers and delivers real results.

Water costs affect housing costs. I will work hard to keep rates affordable by pursuing state grants, infrastructure funding, and polluter settlements to help pay for PFAS cleanup and other major projects. I also support stronger assistance programs so families do not lose basic services during hard times.

We must fix problems before they become emergencies. That means improving aging infrastructure, holding contractors accountable, expanding backup power systems during storms, and making sure our water systems are dependable when families need them most.

I also believe we must protect our aquifers and water rights for future generations through smart regional planning and cooperation. I will listen to residents, support open public meetings, and make sure every dollar spent by the PUD is focused on service, reliability, and community benefit.My focus is simple: affordable rates, reliable service, strong infrastructure, and honest leadership that puts the community first.

Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, Commissioner, District No. 1. Accessed 2026-10-10. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
