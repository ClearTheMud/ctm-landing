Statement

Since being elected to the Court of Appeals in 2020, Judge Coburn not only serves the residents of Snohomish County and the state in her official capacity, she also works to improve the entire legal system. She is a member of the Supreme Court’s Minority and Justice Commission and is a frequent presenter at judicial conferences. She specifically is a resource on how courts can accurately and equitably assess fines and fees.  She was named Judge of the Year by the Asian Bar Association of Washington in 2018.

Judge Coburn has presented to advanced appellate practitioners and at the Washington State Bar Association Solo and Small Firm Conference.  She volunteers as a Snohomish County YMCA Mock Trial judge, mentors attorneys, and teaches new judges at the statewide Judicial College. 

A 34-year resident of Snohomish County where she and her husband raised two sons, Judge Coburn umpires youth baseball, regularly organizes masters’ volleyball teams, and serves as the top ranked high school volleyball official in Snohomish County.  She has had the privilege to officiate at the state volleyball tournament, including having been selected to work championship matches.

Endorsed by: Snohomish County Democrats and National Women’s Political Caucus of Washington
