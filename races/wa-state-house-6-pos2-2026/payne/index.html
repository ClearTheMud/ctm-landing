1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

I am a first-time candidate.

Other Professional Experience

I have worked in several types of offices as an administrator, including law offices and on the re licensing program under Howard Delaney.

Education

I graduated from Medical Lake High School.

Community Service

I have volunteered with Pride for over a decade and served for one year as Vice President of the board. I have also volunteered with Spokane AIDS Network. My time volunteering has given me a wonderful perspective on the affordability issues Eastern Washington faces. Volunteering has also connected me with our community and the most vulnerable members who need our support.

Statement

I moved to Spokane in 1989 as the daughter of Air Force veterans. I have lived throughout the district including Airway Heights and Indian Trail. I am a mom and foster mom officially, but unofficially I’ve been “mom” or “aunt” to many young people.


As a disabled person, I know accessibility and affordability impact the health of our economy, but some of our representatives don't understand. They listen to big voices, but when a regular constituent travels to Olympia they “aren’t available”. How can someone represent you if they won’t listen? I will be “Approachable to everyone, Accountable to you.”
