Other Professional Experience

Girl Scouts of Eastern Washington and Northern Idaho Board Member; International missionary work; Support to local and international non-profits providing food, clothing, medicine, water, and more; Former Spokane Park Board Council Liaison Board Member; Former Spokane Employee Retirement System (SERS) Board Member; Former Junior Achievement Board Member

Statement
The 6th District needs leadership focused on what matters most: safer communities, lower taxes, and a stronger economy for working families. On City Council, I fought for public safety, accountability, and common sense solutions to hold criminals accountable. In Olympia, I’ll do the same and support law enforcement, reduce the tax burden, and protect your paycheck.

Strong families are the foundation of strong communities. I’ll stand for policies that prioritize and protect them. I’m proud to be endorsed by State Senator Jeff Holy and State Reps. Jenny Graham and Mike Volz. Let’s restore balance and put Eastern Washington first.
