1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience
First-time candidate, not a career politician.

Other Professional Experience
22 years in the Air Force, Combat Search and Rescue/Special Operations Helicopter Pilot, Foreign Area Officer; staff experience running 9-figure budgets, coordinating the organization, training, and equipping of thousands of personnel and nearly 200 aircraft. I finished my career as a diplomat at the US Embassy in Bucharest, Romania.

Education
Masters degree in Military Operational Arts and Science; Bachelors in Spanish. Fluent in Spanish, French, and conversational in Romanian.

Community Service
Volunteer firefighter from 2015-2017; served as an advisor in young men's programs. Volunteer with Sleep in Heavenly Peace

Statement
Both parties have had plenty of chances to fix healthcare costs, housing, and the economic rules squeezing working families. They haven't. I'm running as an Independent to change that.

My 22 years as a combat helicopter pilot in the Air Force taught me to lead under pressure, manage complex budgets, and solve real problems. I'll bring that discipline to Olympia. I'll work with anyone willing to expand affordable healthcare, support small businesses, and invest in local jobs. I'll never vote to discriminate against my neighbors. No party boss tells me how to vote. You deserve better. I'll deliver it.
