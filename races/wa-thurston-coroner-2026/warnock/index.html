Finding 2.1: Rising Caseload Under Constrained Resources LOW

What happened
The Coroner’s Office workload has increased significantly: 2,880 reported deaths in Thurston County in 2024 (13% increase since 2018), 364 autopsies (117% increase since 2018). The office has been described as understaffed relative to caseload growth. The state-mandated accreditation deadline (July 2025) adds operational pressure.

Low — Caseload increase reflects county growth and demographics, not office mismanagement. Running unopposed suggests community confidence.
Defense
