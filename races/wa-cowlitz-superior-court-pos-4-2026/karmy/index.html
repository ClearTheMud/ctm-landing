1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (General Election 2026)

Legal/Judicial Experience

I have over 22 years of legal experience and currently serve as a Cowlitz County Superior Court Judge.

Other Professional Experience

I began working as a Court Commissioner in 2022, while running a civil law practice and working as a criminal prosecutor. As an attorney, I litigated cases in both trial and appellate courts.

Education

I graduated from Lewis & Clark Law School in 2003 with a Certificate in Federal Taxation Law. I earned a B.A. in Communications from Pacific Union College.

Community Service

Washington State Bar Association (WSBA) Board of Governors, 2014-2017; WSBA Treasurer, 2016-2017; Created "Justice in Action" mock trial program for local teenagers.



Statement

I have the honor of serving Cowlitz County as a Superior Court Judge, applying the law fairly, impartially, and with integrity. Every day, I make decisions that affect families, businesses, public safety, and individual rights. This tremendous responsibility requires experience and a deep understanding of diverse areas of the law. 

As your incumbent judge, and with experience as a civil litigator and criminal prosecutor, I remain committed to equal justice, respect for every person who enters the courtroom, and decisions grounded in the law, not politics or ideology. 

Judicial experience matters. Proven judicial temperament, balanced decision-making, and experience managing complex cases are essential to maintaining and increasing public confidence in our courts. 

I am endorsed by all current Cowlitz County Superior Court judges, numerous other judicial officers in trial and appellate courts, and lawyers throughout the county and state. I respectfully ask for your vote so I may continue serving our community with fairness, independence, and experience.
