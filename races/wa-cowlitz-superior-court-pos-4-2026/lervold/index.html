1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (General Election 2026)

Legal/Judicial Experience

Jacob Lervold has spent his entire legal career as a Criminal Deputy Prosecuting Attorney.

Other Professional Experience

Previously a business owner and regional manager for a property management company.  

Education

University of Washington, B.A., Double Major in Political Science and Philosophy. Arizona State University Law School, J.D., Cum Laude and selected to Order of the Barristers 



Community Service

Jacob Lervold is the assigned prosecutor to work with the Longview Street Crimes Unit in addressing the use and distribution of illegal drugs. Additionally, Jacob is the assigned prosecutor to work with the Cowlitz County Sheriff Office’s Crime Reduction Team.



Statement

Justice relies on the rule of law. There is no place in our courts for politics, personal opinion, or favoritism. Our courts need to be fair while also holding people accountable.

As a Senior Deputy Prosecuting Attorney, Jacob handles complex and high-profile criminal cases. His caseload consists of homicides, child sex crimes, distribution of illegal drugs, and gun violence. Previously, when at the Thurston County Prosecutor’s Office, he was the Serious Traffic Crimes Prosecutor fighting to keep our roads safe. Jacob is also a member of the Cowlitz County Multi-Disciplinary Team that addresses crimes against children. He has spent his entire career fighting for victim rights and trying to make the community safer. His career has been guided by the principle that the rule of law protects both public safety and individual rights.

Not only does Jacob know his way around the courtroom, but he is also the Cowlitz County Prosecutor Office’s representative for the Washington State Prosecutor Association’s Appellate Committee. Jacob is routinely the attorney of record on cases at the Court of Appeals and Washington State Supreme Court. He brings not only real-life courtroom experience but a deep understanding of the law.
