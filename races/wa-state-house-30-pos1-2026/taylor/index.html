1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience
State Representative: Chair of Civil Rights & Judiciary Committee; Chair of Developmental Disability Advocacy Caucus; Vice Chair of Members of Color Caucus. Member of Transportation Committee; member of Early Learning & Human Services Committee.

Other Professional Experience
Attorney: Former Statewide Advocacy Counsel managing legal aid attorneys assisting domestic violence survivors and crime victims. Youth Advocate: Managed youth violence reduction efforts. Small Business Owner: Private law practice and nonprofit consultant.

Education
JD, University of Oregon Law School; BA, Virginia State University

Community Service
Board member BlackPast.org, Federal Way Human Services Commission, Highline College Foundation, and Girl Scouts; and caregiver for family member living with chronic illnesses.

Statement
I’ve prioritized the health and safety of our families and communities, passing laws that reduce gun violence, improve police training and trust, and address root causes of crime. Committed to addressing affordability, I’ve delivered resources for housing and childcare, reduced small business taxes, and expanded working family tax credits. Committed to a strong economy, I work with local businesses, unions, and schools to protect jobs, expand apprenticeships, and provide career pathways. 

Endorsed: 30th District Democrats, State Labor Council, Planned Parenthood, OneAmerica Votes, Sierra Club, housing advocates, teachers, healthcare workers, and more! Let’s keep working together for the future we deserve.
