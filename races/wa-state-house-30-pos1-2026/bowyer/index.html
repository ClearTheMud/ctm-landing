1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (General Election 2026)

Elected Experience

Other Professional Experience

Small business owner with experience in budgeting, marketing, customer service, and community engagement. This work has provided with me a firsthand understanding of the challenges facing families, workers, and local businesses while strengthening my skills in communication, organization, and problem-solving.

Education

I have a background in health and wellness with training in massage therapy, herbalism, and yoga instruction.

Community Service

Community service includes involvement with my daughter’s co-op, supporting nonprofit fundraising efforts benefiting Seattle Children’s Hospital, and volunteering with local churches. I also support mothers and families in my community through childcare, postpartum support, and hands-on help when needed.

Statement

As a mother and small business owner, I cannot stand by as our state continues down a destructive path. Families are struggling with rising costs, including property taxes, gas prices, and increasing utility bills, while government spending continues to grow. We are not better off than we were four years ago. Our children deserve the opportunity to grow up and succeed in the state we know and love, but that requires a serious change in direction. I am committed to fighting for our future, supporting small businesses, and restoring accountability in leadership that listens to and truly represents the people.
