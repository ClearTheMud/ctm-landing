1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

Appointed to represent the 1st District in 2019. Chair of Local Government Committee. Served on Bothell City Council 2016-2022, Deputy Mayor from 2016-2020.



Other Professional Experience

Only licensed architect in the legislature. Committed to sustainable, affordable design. Served on Eastside Transportation Partnership; WSDOT Executive Advisory Group; Sound Transit Bus Rapid Transit Elected Leadership Group; PSRC Transportation Policy Board and Board of Directors of the Sound Cities Association



Education

Syracuse University, Bachelor of Architecture with a minor in Environmental Geography


Community Service

Ten years on the Bothell Landmarks Preservation Board, eight as chair. Co-founded the Northshore Schools Foundation M.I.L.K. Money campaign supporting students experiencing homelessness.



Statement

Quality of life depends on having affordable homes near jobs, accessible transportation, a healthy environment, and strong public education. Last term, seven of my bills became law, including bills that: make it cheaper to build affordable housing, phase out super-pollutant refrigerants, close a growth management loophole, streamline permits for new housing, and give Ecology enforcement over landfill regulation. Secured $532 million for the 1st District in 2025, including $464.5 million for the I-405 corridor project. Would be honored to have your vote. Endorsed: State Labor Council, Washington Conservation Action, Sen. Stanford, Rep. Kloba, Mayors Thompson, Arndt, Herbig, Curtis and others. 

Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, State Representative Pos. 1. Accessed 2026-07-16. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
