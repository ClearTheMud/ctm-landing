Finding 4.1: Current County Employee Running for Elected Boss’s Position LOW

What happened
Cady is currently employed as a Deputy Clerk in the same office he is seeking to lead. While his insider experience is his primary campaign selling point, opponents may frame this as a conflict of interest. No evidence of ethics complaints or violations has been found.
Source tier
T2 (Campaign website, Nisqually Valley News)
Political impact
Low — His current boss Linda Myhre Enlow is not seeking re-election, reducing any appearance of conflict.
Defense
Insider experience is precisely what makes him the most qualified candidate. County employees have every right to seek elected office.

Finding 4.2: No Degree Completion Documented from Washington State University LOW

What happened
Cady’s campaign website states he “attended” Washington State University but does not claim to have graduated or earned a degree. The distinction between “attended” and “graduated” is notable but not necessarily a vulnerability — many successful public servants lack four-year degrees.
Source tier
T3 (Campaign website)
Political impact
Low — Hands-on operational experience may be more relevant than academic credentials for this role.
Defense
Cady is the only candidate with direct Clerk’s Office work experience. Extensive hands-on experience is more relevant than academic credentials for this operational role.
