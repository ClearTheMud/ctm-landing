Community Service

Statement

I’m honored to currently serve as Deputy Clerk within the Clerk’s Office, diligently working with professionalism while supporting all Court operations. Now, I’m running to be your next County Clerk. I will expand the critical services our office provides while retaining our experienced staff whose institutional knowledge is fundamental to preserving the integrity of the meticulous work performed.

Access to justice is my highest priority and as your Clerk, my obligation shall be to protect your rights indefinitely. With restoration of our domestic violence assistance programs and in-person services successfully progressing, I’ll continue enhancing these capabilities with my firsthand experience to better support victims, children and families. I understand that constant collaboration and communication are the keys to overcoming challenges, and strengthens our partnership with the Superior Court and other law and justice agencies.

I believe the Clerk must be free from any political influence, just as our elected Judges are nonpartisan, to uphold public trust in our justice system.

I’d be truly honored to serve as your next County Clerk and vow to serve with integrity, accountability and exceptional customer service. Thank you Thurston County, with your vote, together we’ll ensure fair and impartial access to justice for all.

Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, Clerk. Accessed 2026-10-10. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
