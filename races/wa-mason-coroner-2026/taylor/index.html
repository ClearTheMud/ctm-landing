Statement
My name is Jaime Taylor, and I am running for re-election as Mason County Coroner. I was born and raised in Mason County and am nationally certified by the American Board of Medicolegal Death Investigators, as well as certified through the Washington State Criminal Justice Training Commission. With over 18 years of experience in death investigation, I am committed to serving families with dignity, compassion, and professionalism during their most difficult times. I will continue to provide thorough, respectful investigations and timely answers for our community. I ask for your vote to continue serving Mason County.
