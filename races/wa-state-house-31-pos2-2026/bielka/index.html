1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

First time public servant.

Other Professional Experience

John is a  Civil engineer and public works director with more than 30 years managing infrastructure, budgets, and operations. John has owned an excavation business and proudly raises cattle at his farm outside of Orting.

Education

John holds a  Bachelors in Civil Engineering from the University of Saskatchewan; graduate coursework in engineering economics and accounting.

Community Service

John is passionate about this community.  He volunteers as a Career and Technical Education advisor at Chief Leschi Schools and supports the Orting Senior Center and Orting Food Bank as a grant writer. 

Statement

I never planned to run for office. As a civil engineer, public works director, and cattle farmer, I have spent my career solving problems, managing budgets, and delivering creative results for our communities. District 31 needs practical leadership focused on affordability, housing, infrastructure, and strong schools.  I will work across the aisle to lower everyday costs, improve roads and utilities, support public safety, and expand career and technical education including trades pathways. Government should be honest, accountable, and able to get things done. I would be honored to earn your vote.
