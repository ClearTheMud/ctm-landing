1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

State Representative (Assistant Ranking Member, Appropriations; Finance; Early Learning & Human Services); Former Mayor (8 years), Councilmember (6 years), and Planning Commissioner (2 years), City of Orting; Former Chair, Pierce County Regional Council; Former Charter Review Commissioner, District 1.



Other Professional Experience

Executive Strategy, Penner & Co; Veteran, United States Marine Corps: Iraq War Veteran & Radio Operator. 



Education

Harvard University, Master's (ALM); St. Martin's University, BA; Green River Community College, AA; Sumner High School. 



Community Service

Co-Founder of PNW Vets, the largest Veteran support network in the Pacific Northwest; Crisis Intervention Trainer for WA-CJTC.



Statement

I grew up in Bonney Lake, graduated from Sumner High School, served as a Marine in Iraq, and led Orting as Mayor. As your Representative, I bring our community’s shared values and that fighting spirit to Olympia.

Together, we saved Buckley’s Rainier School, prevented disastrous cuts for early education, protected care facilities for fentanyl-exposed newborns, and cut red tape to fund the repair of the White River and Fairfax bridges.  With your support, I will keep fighting to lower living costs, demand accountability, protect our most vulnerable, and secure our neighborhoods. Let’s lead with action, not talking points.
