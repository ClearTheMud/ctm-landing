1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

State Representative, 48th LD; former Redmond City Councilmember

Other Professional Experience

Previous experience includes Medical Device Consultant, Fred Hutchinson Cancer Researcher, King County Community Engagement Manager, and Coffee Shop Owner

Education

BS Neurobiology & English Minor, University of Washington (Student Body President); LWSD Graduate

Community Service

Board member for Hopelink, Transportation Choices Coalition, OneRedmond Foundation, Asian Pacific-Islander Americans for Civic Empowerment, Lawyers Helping Hungry Children; Member of LWSD Career & Technical Education Committee; Former member of Eastside Fire & Rescue Fire Corps, Redmond Youth Basketball Coach

Statement

As your State Representative, I’m focused on delivering results. I secured critical childcare and healthcare investments for our district and passed more laws than any legislator this year, championing bills on affordable housing, gun violence prevention, and immigrant protections. As a lifelong Eastsider, I’ll keep fighting for local priorities: public safety, healthcare access, and strong schools. I’m running to continue serving our communities with a commitment to common-sense solutions and economic growth.

Endorsed: Congresswoman DelBene; Governor Ferguson; County Councilmember Balducci; Mayors of Redmond, Bellevue, Kirkland, Medina; Teachers; Nurses; Conservation Action; State Labor Council; 48th Democrats; Planned Parenthood Alliance; and more!
