1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Occupation

Former judge and court commissioner with 10+ years of experience serving in private and public practice.  

Education

UW Tacoma and University of Washington School of Law

Statement

I believe the judiciary must actively reflect our community's core values. This unwavering conviction — backed by a proven track record on the bench — drives my candidacy.

My extensive career across private practice, public defense, and the judiciary gives me a comprehensive, well-rounded courtroom perspective. Having already served as a Municipal Court Judge, a full-time Superior Court Commissioner, and a Judge Pro Tem, I know exactly what it takes to run an efficient, fair, and accessible courtroom. As a proud graduate of UW Tacoma and the UW School of Law, I am deeply rooted in this community.

As a District Court Judge, my mission would be to restore absolute public trust in our legal process, guarantee equal accountability, and protect the rights of every individual. Together, we can secure a safer, fairer, and more accountable future for Pierce County. I respectfully ask for your vote.
