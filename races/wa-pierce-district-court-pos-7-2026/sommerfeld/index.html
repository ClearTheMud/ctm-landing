Statement

Mike Sommerfeld, a deputy prosecutor for 30 years, has prosecuted DUI, domestic violence, kidnapping, child sexual abuse, and murder. As legal advisor to Pierce County District Court, he’s helped judges navigate complex legal and constitutional issues. As a Pro Tem Judge, he’s presided over criminal and civil cases. He has the balanced legal experience needed in a judge.

Mike believes District Court should hold violent offenders accountable while expanding therapeutic and alternative programs that reduce repeat offenses. He serves on the Brady Committee, which holds law enforcement accountable. Outside courtrooms, he’s volunteered with foster youth programs and veterans’ legal clinics.  Mike and his wife have lived in Pierce County for 28 years, where they raised their children.

Mike received the Washington Traffic Safety Commission Judicial Excellence Award. He’s endorsed by a majority of Pierce County District Court and Superior Court judges, Mayor Anders Ibsen, Executive Ryan Mello, and Bruce Dammeier.
