Statement

I have dedicated my career to serving the public with honesty, professionalism, and respect for the rule of law. As a current Assistant Attorney General and Judge Pro Tem, I have extensive courtroom experience litigating workers’ compensation, workplace safety, and administrative cases throughout Washington.

I proudly served our nation for more than twenty years in the United States Army, including eleven years as an infantry officer and eleven years as a Judge Advocate (JAG). I advised commanders, prosecuted criminal cases, and protected constitutional rights while serving both at home and overseas. My service taught me the importance of preparation, accountability, and treating all people with courtesy and respect.

I believe judges must apply the law fairly, remain impartial, and ensure every person in the courtroom is heard. I respectfully ask for your vote to serve Pierce County with integrity, sound judgment, and compassion.
