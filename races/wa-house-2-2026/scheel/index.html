Statement

Is Washington D.C. working for you? Who benefits from rising housing costs, unaffordable healthcare, instability abroad, and the inhumane treatment towards us at home? We see evidence of exactly where the working class stands. We have an opportunity to continue the status quo or choose a representative who will put working Washingtonians first.

The people demand universal healthcare, a clean energy transition, major investments in housing, and an economy centered on working families. We recognize the need for reproductive freedom, access to gender-affirming care, and protection of human rights domestically and abroad. Our immigration system requires reform and ICE must be dismantled, replaced with respect for constitutional rights and human dignity.

I will never accept corporate PAC or foreign lobby money, especially from AIPAC. My decisions will be guided purely by the people I represent.

We do not lack solutions. We lack the follow-through to make them real. I have spent my life in roles where that is unacceptable. As a U.S. Air Force veteran, volunteer firefighter, and small business owner, I have worked in environments where failure has consequences and accountability is not optional.

Your representative must be the most accessible person in the district. It should not be difficult to meet them, speak with them, or hold them accountable. I will remain responsive and available to the people I represent, no matter whether you are a donor or not. You deserve to be able to freely meet your representative.

Public officials must be held to a higher standard, with clear expectations and real consequences. Public service should mean sacrifice, not privilege. As the child and spouse of immigrants, and a fluent Spanish speaker, I believe in building a government that is inclusive, accountable, and works for everyone. I would be honored to earn your vote.
