1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

I am running for this office for the second time and have yet to hold elected office. As AOC and many others have shown, first-time office holders can have major impact. We can't elect the same career politicians and expect a different result.



Other Professional Experience

I've raised hundreds of millions of dollars to fight poverty and created programs that raised over $1 billion to empower girls and women. I've also launched multiple startups, including one I helped take public.

 



Education

Whitman College, BA in Art, Boston University, MBA, certificate in Public and Nonprofit Management.



Community Service

Founded DefundMusk.com and public speaker for gender equity.



Statement

Doormat Democrats have failed us; it's time for a change. Two years ago, I ran for this office and was the only candidate warning about the danger our democracy faced and the need to reform the Supreme Court. Nearly 10,000 of you honored me with your votes. 

Amazingly, Democrats like the incumbent still fail to understand the current crisis. We need representatives who, as Beto says, will "drive it like it's stolen" when we regain power and fight like hell until we do. 

As the founder of DefundMusk.com, I've been working with amazing people to hit billionaires in the wallet, staging ten protests a week, leading the campaign against Sinclair/KOMO when Kimmel was taken off the air, and organizing for a general strike. 

Incremental change won't cut it. After we defeat Trump, we're going to tear our broken system down to the studs and rebuild a new America that fulfills the promise of justice and prosperity for you and your family, not oligarchs and corporations. I won't be afraid to tax billionaires out of existence, end aid to Israel until they stop their decimation of Palestine, and impeach Trump and his whole corrupt crew. Unlike the incumbent in this race, I support the Green New Deal and Medicare for All and will never take a dime of corporate cash.

We can lower costs, tackle our homelessness and fentanyl crises, and deliver the high-paying jobs that our community deserves. But first we need new leadership that will stand up to Trump with more than stern letters. 

My entire life, I’ve been told the things I want to do are impossible—and then I do them anyway. We need vision and courage now more than ever. Send me to Congress to be your voice.
