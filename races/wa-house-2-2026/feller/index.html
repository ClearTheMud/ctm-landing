1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (General Election 2026)

Elected Experience
Edwin Feller is a lifelong 2nd District community member. He has never held public office, remaining entirely uninfluenced by favor-seeking lobbyists, insider trading, or political schemes; bringing only hard work and a commitment to doing the right thing.

Other Professional Experience
Feller worked in the HVAC industry for 60 years, building a reputation for honesty and problem-solving. This sterling character ultimately brought him to the U.S. State Department, where he served sensitive U.S. facilities in 60 countries.

Education
A graduate of Lynden Christian High School, Feller also attended WSU. He offers true independence and decades of real-world experience.

Community Service
The Firs Bible & Missionary Conference

Statement
Edwin Feller grew up on a Whatcom County dairy farm, where he learned the value of hard work, discipline, and fiscal responsibility. His parents taught him to love God; to love America; and to love his neighbors. Politics in our state and our country have become wrapped in partisan ideology. Feller believes true representation is not bound by party, but to the ideals and inspirations of the district we call home.

Washington District 2 deserves more than a life-long ribbon-cutter who shows up like clockwork every two years begging to be re-elected. Feller will bring a common sense approach to Congress focused on responsible economics and accountable public safety. We Washingtonians are in a competition with the rest of the country and the world, and the current leadership in our state and the 2nd Congressional District has us headed in the wrong direction.

He supports robust law enforcement, secure borders, fair elections, and balanced budgets. Feller supports Title IX protections for women and girls. He believes that our first responders deserve respect, appreciation, and support. He adheres to the principles of sovereignty for the individual and the nation, and that the rule of law protects those principles. Feller believes that the affordability crisis is self-inflicted due to taxes being too high, and the government growing too large. 

We are innovators. We are hard workers. Our representation must be our partner, not our handler. We can succeed and prosper together. Visit FellerForCongress.com for more information.

Send some Common Sense to DC – Vote Feller for Congress.
