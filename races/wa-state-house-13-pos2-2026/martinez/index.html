1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

As a former Moses Lake City Council member, I understand the needs of local communities. I'll bring that experience to Olympia helping local government retain control of their decision making.

Other Professional Experience

I'm a Registered Nurse with over 30 years experience caring for and about our rural communities in the 13th district. Former campaign assistant for Representative Alex Ybarra, providing critical support. 

Education

Big Bend Community College/Columbia Basin College- ADN Registered Nurse

Community Service

I've always given back to my community by volunteering with various organizations like Boys and Girls Club, Columbia Basin Cancer foundation, Soroptimists, Grant County Fair, Kiwanis and church projects and events. 

Statement

I'm running for office to overturn the burdensome taxes imposed on citizens of the 13th district. My top priority is abolishing the unconstitutional income tax the Democrat majority sold us as a "millionaire's tax" — a policy that will drive business out of Washington and stunt economic growth, making our state even less affordable. I'll also work to repeal the Climate Commitment Act, which raises gas prices and disproportionately hurts rural Washington. Having grown up in the heart of our agricultural community, I will always support and protect our farmers. I respectfully ask for your vote.



Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, State Representative Pos. 2. Accessed 2026-07-16. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
