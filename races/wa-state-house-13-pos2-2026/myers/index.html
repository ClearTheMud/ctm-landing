1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience
I have proudly served the community of Moses Lake for the past 10 years as a member of the Moses Lake City Council, including service as both Mayor and Deputy Mayor. In addition, I have served on the Grant Transit Authority Board for 10 years and currently serve as Chairman.

 
Other Professional Experience
Currently serves as General Manager of Scotty’s Auto Repair in Moses Lake, overseeing daily operations and customer relations.


Education
Central Washington University. Moses Lake High School.

Community Service
Volunteer with community events throughout Moses Lake and actively serve through my church and local outreach efforts.

Statement
I’m running to serve Central Washington with honest, effective leadership grounded in real world experience. As a small business leader and longtime public servant, I understand the challenges families, workers, and local businesses face every day. My focus is on protecting individual freedom, strengthening families, growing opportunity, and building a future where our communities can thrive. I believe government should be focused on solving problems, not creating them. I will stand for safer communities, responsible spending, improved infrastructure, and policies that respect the values and voice of Central Washington.
