1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (General Election 2026)

Elected Experience

Ellensburg City Councilmember, three term Precinct Committee Officer for the Kittitas County Republican Party. Voted as a delegate to represent the Kittitas County delegation to the Republican State Convention in 2024.

Other Professional Experience

I've spent 20 years working on technical, digital marketing, and cybersecurity projects as an engineer and consultant. During my career I have helped small and large businesses tackle difficult issues. I know how to jump into complex issues and find solutions.

Education

Eastern Oregon University, Bachelor of Science (B.S.), Business/Economics, graduated in 2006.

Community Service

Volunteered to coached 7th grade basketball, youth baseball, as well as umpired games.

Statement

As an Ellensburg City Councilmember, I've built a reputation as a principled conservative leader, supporting families, small businesses, and common-sense government. I've worked hard to bring accountability and transparency while standing up for the values and priorities of Central Washington citizens. I will fight for your Constitutional rights every day in Olympia.

Focused on reducing taxes, supporting law enforcement, and defending ranchers and farmers from government overreach, I will not represent the system but be a true representative of the people. I believe decisions should stay closer to local communities. Unanimously endorsed by the Kittitas County Republican Party. Visit ElectJoshuaThompson.com.



Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, State Representative Pos. 2. Accessed 2026-10-10. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
