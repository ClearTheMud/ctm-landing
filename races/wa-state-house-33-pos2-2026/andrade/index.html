Small business owner with brick-and-mortar business experience; 25+ years in retail management, HR, budgeting, operations, and project management.

AWC Certificate of Municipal Leadership (CML) & WA-CELI Cohort I; Columbia Basin College; South Seattle College; School of Visual Concepts.



Community Service

Current member of four Sound Cities Association (SCA) committees, SOMOS Seattle Board member, WA State Combined Fund Drive (CFD) Advisory Council, North Beach School District CTE Advisory Board, and Burien Elks House Committee; Burien C.A.R.E.S. volunteer; Former Discover Burien Board member and AIGA Seattle Treasurer; longtime volunteer supporting education, music, arts, LGBTQ+ advocacy, animal welfare and community organizations.



Statement

I am a Burien City Councilmember, small business owner, and dedicated volunteer committed to bringing solutions-focused leadership to Olympia. Families across South King County are struggling with rising costs-of-living, safety concerns, affordability, education, behavioral health and drug crisis, and government spending that continues to grow without enough accountability. As a business owner and elected official, I understand balancing budgets and making taxpayer dollars count. I will fight to hold state government accountable, demand transparency in how taxpayer dollars are spent, and support effective solutions for public safety, affordable housing, education, small businesses, pro-choice protections, equality, and stronger futures for Washingtonians.
