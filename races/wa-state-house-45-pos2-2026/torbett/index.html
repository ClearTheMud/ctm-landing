Statement

Proud to be endorsed by Representative Larry Springer. Washington needs practical leadership focused on results, not ideology. I’ll bring urgency, leadership, and experience to lower housing and childcare costs, support small businesses, push back on Trump, and build an economy that works. 

Our district is ground zero for AI. The future is coming fast. Washington should lead by preparing for new risks while encouraging innovation and good jobs. As a father of three and technology attorney, I understand the stakes personally and professionally. I’m the only candidate prepared to meet this moment with the urgency, experience, and judgment it demands.
