1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

In 2024, I was a Delegate and attended the Convention. Then elected PCO, then voted in as State Committeewoman of the County Republican Party. June 2025, I became Chair after the Chair’s resignation.

Other Professional Experience

No information submitted

Education

In 2008 earned a B.S. in Paralegal Studies. I’m trained in Robert’s Rules of Order and the Open Public Meetings Act.

Community Service

Raymond’s National Night Out Events, three years as Secretary ABC. Was Board Member of the Willapa Harbor Chamber of Commerce. I’ve volunteered at Pennywise Thrift Store. I have chaired local festivals. I have donated time to our community all my life.

Statement

I am a proud resident of Pacific County, a tireless advocate for freedom, a Christian and a believer in local government that serves you, not itself. My mission is to bring accountability, transparency, and common sense back to Pacific County Government. I am bilingual in Spanish. I have worked in the legal field for 16 years and worked for attorneys in Grays Harbor County and King County and provide paralegal services locally. Additionally, I am a Notary Public. I believe in individual autonomy and reduced invasion of local government in our lives, which includes less taxation and cutting excessive regulations and red tape holding people back from prosperity. I believe in complete transparency in county government. I want to bring accountability back to the citizens of Pacific County who deserve to see exactly where the tax dollars are going. Stop wasteful the spending. I believe in term limits to prevent career politicians. I would like to bring in a salary commission because Commissioners should not set their own salaries. Public servants should serve the public, not control it. “Do the right thing vote” vote Pattie Gray for County Commissioner #3.
