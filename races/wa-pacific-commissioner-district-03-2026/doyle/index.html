Statement:

I am Jerry Doyle your current County Commissioner for District #3 and I’m running for re-election. Our county deserves leadership that is honest, accountable, and committed to the people that live and work here every day. Although challenges will always be a part of local government, a commissioner’s duty is to always represent and serve the people of our county first.  From fiscal responsibility to county departments, staff, and community interests, it is crucial to have an open-door policy to discuss any issues that arise. Leadership is about bringing practical solutions, transparency, and common sense to county government. My mission has always been to build a stronger future that creates opportunity while preserving the values that make our county a great place to live. Along with my wife Bambi, we have raised our six children and two grandchildren in our wonderful community. We have also built a reputation of honesty and trust within our own business. I will continue to provide leadership as I have for the last four years. I respectively ask for your support and vote for County Commissioner to help keep moving our county in a positive path forward.
