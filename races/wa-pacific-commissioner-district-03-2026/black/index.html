Statement
The opportunity to serve as your Pacific County Commissioner is a privilege and a responsibility I take very seriously. My work will be rooted in dedication, transparency, and a future for our county built on sound governance and fiscal accountability. I'm concerned by any litigation impacting County and the ever-growing cost to provide necessary county services vs. the general fund. My roots here run deep. I grew up in Raymond; my wife Angela and I were married here 43 years ago and raised our family here. My life has been defined by service. I served honorably as a U.S. Marine and Washington National Guard member. Following military service, I began my career with the South Bend Police Department, later serving as the first appointed Superintendent of Corrections for the Pacific County Sheriff’s Department and Supervisor of the Sheriff Department 911 Communications Center. Throughout my career-whether in uniform, as a supervisor, or as a volunteer-my focus has always been our community. As your commissioner, I will ensure that my voice and my actions always reflect the heart and the trust of the people I serve. My commitment is not to a political party; it is to you, your family, and mine.

Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, County Commissioner #03. Accessed 2026-07-16. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
