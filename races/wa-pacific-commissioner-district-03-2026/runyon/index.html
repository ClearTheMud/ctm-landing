1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Other Professional Experience

Education

High School graduate with years of industry experience as well as government operations and management

Community Service

Active supporter of local schools, non-profits, including donation of time, services, including monetary donations for local youth sports, community members, and local families in need

Statement

I am a lifelong resident of Pacific County. My wife and I have been married for 53 years, and all four of our children and nine grandchildren live here. This community is my home, and I care deeply about its future. As your former County Commissioner, I worked to represent taxpayers with honesty, accountability, and common sense. Soon after taking office, our county faced the challenges of a global pandemic. Despite those difficulties, we addressed long-standing issues and focused on responsible leadership during uncertain times. A County Commissioner’s primary responsibility is managing the budget and protecting taxpayer dollars. I believe in fiscal responsibility, transparency, and ensuring public funds are spent wisely and accounted for. I am not afraid to ask tough questions, look deeper into issues, or make decisions that put taxpayers first. I earned my nickname for a reason — I will continue to watch over your tax dollars like a hawk and work hard to keep Pacific County strong, responsible, and moving forward.
