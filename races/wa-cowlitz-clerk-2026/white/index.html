1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement



I am proud to run for Cowlitz County Clerk with a commitment to professionalism, accountability, and public service. As a U.S. Army veteran and lifelong Cowlitz County resident, I understand the importance of integrity, discipline, and serving the community with dedication.

I have several years of experience within the local court system, including five years in the Cowlitz County Clerk’s Office. During my final two years there, I served as a Lead Deputy Clerk, helping oversee daily operations and ensuring cases and records were handled accurately and professionally. I currently work at Cowlitz County Juvenile Court, where I continue serving local families and supporting the important work of our courts.

My experience has shown me how important the Clerk’s Office is in maintaining public trust and ensuring access to justice. I believe the office should be efficient, responsive, transparent, accessible, and committed to modernization that improves public service and keeps pace with the needs of our growing community.

If elected, I will provide dependable leadership, strengthen customer service, support transparency, modernize office operations, and ensure the Clerk’s Office operates with fairness, accuracy, and respect for every person it serves.

Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, Clerk. Accessed 2026-07-16. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
