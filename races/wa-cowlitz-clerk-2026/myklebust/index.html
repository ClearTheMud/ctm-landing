1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

It has been an honor and privilege to serve as the County Clerk for the last 12 years. With a combined 23 years of experience, I understand the importance of protecting the integrity and accuracy of the Courts records. I am passionate and enthusiastic about the opportunity to continue to serve the public and support the Deputy Clerks who carry out the day-to-day operations with compassion and knowledge.

I am a proud wife of 34 years, mom to two wonderful, successful kids, and dog mom to two Frenchies. As a lifelong Cowlitz County resident, I take pride in this county and will continue to do my part to promote access to the Courts and make the legal system easy and accessible. It is my goal to implement an electronic filing system and increase public services, while continuing to be responsible and honest with the taxpayer’s money.

I am running as an Independent because I believe that this position is not about politics and access to the Courts should be fair and equal regardless of your political preference. I work for the People, not a Party.

Please vote to re-elect Staci Myklebust for County Clerk, “Leading with Integrity".
