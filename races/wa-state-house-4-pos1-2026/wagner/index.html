Statement
Across Washington people are working hard, raising families, and trying to build a better future. Olympia is making life harder instead of easier. Higher costs, weaker public safety, and schools drifting from fundamentals. After 21 years in the United States Navy, I learned to stay steady under pressure, solve problems, and take responsibility. That means focusing on affordability, restoring public safety, and getting schools back to basics. District 4 deserves leadership focused on accountability and outcomes. Not politics. Real change starts with you. Your vote will restore common sense leadership and puts government back to work for the people.
