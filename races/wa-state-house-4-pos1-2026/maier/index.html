Statement

I’m a husband, engineer, and small business owner who founded Trailbreaker Cider with my family in Liberty Lake. Over the last 15 years, I’ve built businesses, created jobs, and experienced firsthand the challenges facing Washington families and employers. I’m running to fight for safer communities, lower taxes, less government overreach, and policies that support working families and small businesses. I’m proud to be endorsed by Commissioner Mary (Kuney) Brooks, Commissioner Josh Kerns, and State Rep. Suzanne Schmidt as we work to protect opportunity, strengthen our economy, and keep Eastern Washington values represented in Olympia.
