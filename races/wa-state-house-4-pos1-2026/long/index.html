Community Service

CVHS Craft Fair Chair 1996- Current; Bloomsday Volunteer 2005-2020; Hoopfest Volunteer 2005-2020; Washington State School Board Association Foundation Volunteer since 2003; Have hand-crafted & donated over 100 quilts to our veterans



Statement

As an accountant I apply fiscal conservative principles to a multimillion-dollar operation daily. As school board director, I helped oversee a bond budgeted for 10 schools that built 12 for the same cost. Since 1996 I have chaired CV craft fair raising $1.5 million for band students.  William and I have raised our two boys who graduated from Central Valley High School, and have called this district home our entire lives. As accounting manager at Rockwood Retirement Community for 29 years, I have lived fiscal responsibility every day. I am ready to bring that discipline to Olympia.
