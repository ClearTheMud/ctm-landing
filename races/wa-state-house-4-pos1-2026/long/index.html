Statement

As an accountant I apply fiscal conservative principles to a multimillion-dollar operation daily. As school board director, I helped oversee a bond budgeted for 10 schools that built 12 for the same cost. As accounting manager at Rockwood Retirement Community for 29 years, I have lived fiscal responsibility every day. I am ready to bring that discipline to Olympia.
