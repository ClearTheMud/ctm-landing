Community Service

Founder and Chapter Leader, Indivisible Othello WA and Othello Dog Club. Volunteer with Multicultural Association Services (MAS), Whole Washington, Othello Food Bank, and various other community advocacy organizations.

Statement

Born & raised in this area, I have been employed in the fields, factories, and law enforcement. I have also been a landlord, a local customer, community volunteer and organizer. We have all watched how Olympia treats symptoms instead of causes. I am running to work on cures, not band-aids. How does one gauge integrity? I will accept no corporate PAC money. I intend to prioritize health for Washingtonians, union rights, farmworker protections, water rights, and fix our regressive tax system. Families deserve homes they can not just afford, but also maintain and improve. Thank you for your time.
