Statement
As a lifelong resident of Clark County, I care deeply about our community’s future. I am running for PUD Commissioner because we deserve a utility that prioritizes reliability, fiscal responsibility, and transparent leadership.

With over 34 years of hands-on utility experience, I understand our grid from the field to the front office. I have worked through decades of major regional storms, on the frontline, out in the elements to keep the lights on and our families safe. Additionally, my 10 years specializing in utility protection and control systems give me a sophisticated technical understanding of grid safety, cybersecurity, and operational resilience.

As your Commissioner, my top priorities will be protecting ratepayers by using my utility finance background to provide strict oversight and keep rates stable. I will focus on grid reliability by strengthening local aging infrastructure against extreme weather so it functions under pressure, and ensure safe modernization to prepare for future energy demands without sacrificing system stability.

Clark Public Utilities must focus on dependable service, smart planning, and absolute accountability. I have the technical expertise, financial understanding, and deep local roots to guide our utility district responsibly. I would be honored to earn your vote.
