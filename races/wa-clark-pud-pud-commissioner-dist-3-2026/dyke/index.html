Statement
As your Clark Public Utilities Commissioner, I promote outstanding customer service, energy and water conservation and efficiency, effective management, and community and environmental programs. 

With excellent employee work, we are a winner of the J.D. Power customer satisfaction award and recognized as a Reliable Public Power Provider for reliable and safe electric service including workforce development and system improvement.  Conservation and power and water efficiencies allowed the utility to avoid costly investments.  We exceeded our regional energy conservation goals.  And we supported rooftop and community solar. As needs change, the Integrated Resource and Water System plans will drive future conservation and resource acquisitions.  The utility operates effectively, one electrical rate increase in 15 years and one water rate increase in 13 years.  Operation Warm Heart, Senior Rate Credit and other programs assist income limited customers. Plus tree planting, watershed restoration, and student field trips inform and improve our community.

Utilities are increasingly complex, carbon-free energy requirements and rising electric and water demands.  I'll advocate for power and water at nonprofit rates and use my experience, commitment, and background to meet our power and water supply, reliability, and infrastructure challenges.

I hope to earn your vote for my re-election.
