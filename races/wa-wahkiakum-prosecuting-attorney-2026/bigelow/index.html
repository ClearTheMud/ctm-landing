1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

I have been Prosecuting Attorney of Wahkiakum County for the last twenty years.  I was also county coroner for 18 years.

Other Professional Experience

After serving as deputy prosecutor in Ferry County, I moved to Cathlamet, where my family has deep roots. I worked in private practice in family law, estates, contracts, defense, and appeals.

Education

I earned my bachelor’s degree from the University of Washington and my law degree from the University of Puget Sound School of Law. I am also a certified EMT/IV Technician.

Community Service

For over thirty years, I have served as a volunteer with the ambulance of the Cathlamet Fire Department.

Statement

Thanks for allowing me to serve you! For decades, I have represented Wahkiakum County in criminal courts, appellate courts, and administrative courts. I have spent years building trust as the legal advisor for county departments from the Assessor’s Office to the Sheriff’s Office.   But protecting the public from crime is my most important responsibility. No crime is too big and no crime too small, from homicide to shoplifting.  Your vote means the world to me, and I try to earn it every day!
