1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (General Election 2026)

Elected Experience

Appointed to the Legislature in 2015, served five and a half terms. Chosen by fellow legislators as Ranking Member House Environment and Energy Committee and as a member of the Appropriations and Capital Budget Committees.

Other Professional Experience

Manages a Third Generation Wheat Farm, 1987-present. Peace Corps Volunteer, Ubon, Thailand, 1984-86

Education

Bachelor of Science - Plant Science, Crop Management, University of Idaho, 1983

Community Service

“Legislator of the Year” Washington Farm Bureau 2024. “Distinguished Leader Award” Washington Invasive Species Council 2024. "Honorary Life Member", Columbia Basin Development League 2024.

Statement

Mary Dye leads with bold, innovative policy solutions that cut through outdated assumptions. In the 9th District, energy, water, and environmental challenges collide, demanding leadership that unites rather than divides. Mary brings people together, breaking down siloed systems so communities can fully realize the value of water and hydropower, farming, and fisheries. She champions collaborative stewardship of our watershed to strengthen every river-dependent community. With Mary’s steady, forward-looking leadership, we can replace gridlock with progress and build a resilient, prosperous future for all.

Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, State Representative Pos. 1. Accessed 2026-10-10. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
