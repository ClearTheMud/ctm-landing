Statement

 

Grays Harbor County deserves to have a fully committed auditor who will protect the sanctity of voting, restore licensing through clean audits and honor real fiscal responsibility. I’m on the ballot because I strongly believe that it’s time for everyday citizens to step up and protect our democracy. In a world that conditions us to accepting government as it is, running for office is something I feel called to do and am committed to changing for the better. It’s high time we build a government “of the people” if it is ever going to work “for the people”. It’s time to get money out of politics and work together neighbor with neighbor. Together we can create a thriving Greys Harbor County. At my community of faith, I serve on the Fellowship Council. We are responsible for scheduling events and coordinating efforts amongst the congregation. We collaborate on outreach opportunities throughout all of Grays Harbor County. With this servitude in leadership approach I will put in the work to lift our County up and get the job done.
