Finding 5.1: No Government or Auditing Experience MODERATE

What happened
Paull's entire 19-year career has been in private-sector banking and mortgage lending. She has no experience in government administration, elections management, property tax collection, vehicle/vessel licensing, or any of the county auditor's core functions. The WA county auditor role encompasses elections, financial accounting, recording, and licensing — none of which directly correlate to mortgage origination.
