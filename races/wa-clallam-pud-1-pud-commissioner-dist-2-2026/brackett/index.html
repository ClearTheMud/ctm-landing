Other Professional Experience

American Public Works Association board member and Licensed Professional Engineer. I’ve worked several management positions across rural Northwest Washington and Alaska for 34 years. I prepared Public Works department budgets, was responsible for staff and worked closely with elected officials to identify and achieve goals. Statement

Our PUD must be accountable to the people and businesses it serves. As your District 2 Commissioner, I will work to protect safe, reliable, affordable utility service and thoughtfully make transparent decisions that respect every ratepayer dollar. Customer rates cannot continue to grow 3.5% annually.

For 34 years, I have led public works and infrastructure efforts in Northwest Washington and Alaska, building partnerships, improving service, and managing costs. I know how to succeed by living within a budget, maintain essential systems, communicate clearly, and plan responsibly for the future.

The PUD faces rising costs, changing regulations, and a warming climate on hydroelectric power generation. We must work collaboratively with our power provider, Bonneville Power Administration, pursue grants, expand local capacity, and focus on essential priorities to strengthen resilience and hold down rate increases.

This is the community my family calls home. My volunteer work with Habitat for Humanity, Clallam Mosaic, and Special Olympics reflects my commitment to service, accountability, and neighbors helping each other because we all matter.

I would be honored to earn your vote and serve District 2 as your Clallam PUD Commissioner.
