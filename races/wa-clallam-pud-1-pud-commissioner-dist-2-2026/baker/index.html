Statement

I am a candidate for the Clallam County Public Utility District Board of Commissioner, District 2, because I am a consumer in this district, and I want to serve our community with a willingness to listen, act responsibly, and put the needs of our residents and business owners first.

Our public utility district touches every home and business in our community. The decisions made by the board directly affect our water quality, electric service reliability, and utility bills. My many years teaching and education have provided me with the decision making and negotiating skills necessary to effectively perform in this position. 

I am committed to: ensuring rates remain reasonable while maintaining the quality services you depend on within this economic environment, keeping you informed about district decisions and budget priorities through open communication, and supporting upgrades that protect our water and electric systems and prepare for the future.  Please vote for me to support our community in this position.



Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, Commissioner District No. 2. Accessed 2026-07-16. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
