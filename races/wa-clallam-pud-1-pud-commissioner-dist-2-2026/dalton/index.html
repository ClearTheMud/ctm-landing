Statement

I believe our county is at a pivotal moment, which is why I am running for PUD Commissioner. If elected, my focus will be developing strategies to ensure reliable and affordable energy, expanding broadband access throughout our communities, and supporting infrastructure that promotes economic development and creates housing opportunities. I believe we can strike a responsible balance between environmental stewardship and safe, dependable, and affordable power.

I have more than 20 years of experience in organizational management, overseeing policy development, financial responsibilities, and legislative partnerships. In my current role with Clallam County, I see firsthand the challenges facing communities across the county, particularly regarding housing and utility infrastructure.

Having spent most of my life living in the Eastern Washington, I am very familiar with the Department of Energy, Energy Northwest, and the Bonneville Power Administration. I have a comprehensive understanding of hydroelectric power as well as alternative energy sources, including solar, and wind energy.

I understand the importance of teamwork, dedicated leadership, and an unwavering commitment to solving problems and delivering results. I am asking for your support and the opportunity to represent you while helping shape the future of Clallam PUD.

Thank you.
