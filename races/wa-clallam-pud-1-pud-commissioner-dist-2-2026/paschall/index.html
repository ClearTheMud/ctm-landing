Statement

With over thirty years’ experience in the electric-utility industry, I know the industry, the players, and how things get done.  Almost my entire career was spent working for public-power in power supply, transmission, or regulatory compliance.

Our PUD faces a number of challenges:  increasing regulatory-compliance obligations (potential rate impact: (7%), wildfire risks (?), BPA contractual issues (surcharge & Residential Exchange) (+2%), clean-building standards (?), low-water years (+2%), and the hiring and retention of highly skilled and specialized positions (?).  These are just a few of the challenges that are putting upward pressure on power rates.  (Note:  these potential rate-impact estimates are from my back-of-the-envelope analysis).


Our PUD requires commissioners focused on keeping rates as low as possible.  Because of my knowledge and background, I know the difference between energy and capacity, I know what a flowgate is, and I know how BPA and the other federal agencies work.  Thus, I will ‘hit-the-ground-running’ for ratepayers.  If elected I plan to work directly with the GM and senior staff while formulating positions.  I also pledge to do my best to keep rates as low as possible while ensuring reliable service.
