Statement

For the past 12 years, I have been proud to call Clallam County home. During that time, I have looked for the right opportunity to serve this community in a meaningful way. I believe serving as Clallam County PUD Commissioner, District #2, is that opportunity.

I bring 34 years of utility management experience, including work with Snohomish County PUD, a public electric and water utility; Avista Utilities, a private electric and gas utility; and the City of Seattle’s Office of Housing HomeWise Program, which served low-income customers through energy conservation and housing support.

The majority of my career has been focused on conservation, customer service, utility operations, and helping people use energy and water more efficiently. I have also presented at three national conferences and taught several workshops through the Northwest Public Power Association.

Clallam County deserves reliable service, responsible decision-making, and leadership that understands both the technical side of utilities and the real needs of the people they serve. My experience has prepared me to listen, lead, and make thoughtful decisions for our community.

I would be honored to earn your vote for Clallam County PUD Commissioner, District #2.
