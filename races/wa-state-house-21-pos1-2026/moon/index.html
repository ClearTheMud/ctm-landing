1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

Mukilteo Council President, son of immigrants who owned Prince Cleaners in Lynnwood. Puget Sound Regional Council Economic Development Board, Snohomish County Law and Justice Council, Snohomish County Steering Committee.

Other Professional Experience

Partner at Zeolite Composites innovating solutions to protect the environment. Previous experience: Account management at Microsoft, Amazon, and nonprofits helping vulnerable people in Seattle and Africa.



Education

MBA and Bachelors, University of Washington. King’s and Kamiak High Schools.

Community Service

Mukilteo Food Bank, Mukilteo Kiwanis, Endeavour Elementary PTA, Mukilteo Youth Advisory Council, National League of Cities: Environment and Natural Resources Federal Advocacy Committee and Vice President of Asian Constituency Group, Wildlife Diversity Advisory Committee.

Statement

We need fresh perspectives! Schools are underfunded and social services strained, while Washington continues to struggle with mental health, addiction, housing, homelessness, and public safety. We need common sense leadership and accountable budgeting protecting taxpayers and essential services. Poorly planned state mandated density threatens the character of our communities. Local governments need more control because communities have different needs. 

When elected, I will resign from the Mukilteo City Council because it's impossible to hold two elected offices and fully represent their constituents. I’m endorsed by local firefighters, police, more than 11 local city and county councilmembers, your friends and neighbors.



Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, State Representative Pos. 1. Accessed 2026-07-16. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
