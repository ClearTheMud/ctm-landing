1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

State Representative, Housing Committee Chair,  Appropriations and Civil Rights & Judiciary Committee member, Poverty Reduction Oversight Task Force Committee member; Snohomish County Councilmember; Community Safety and Justice Chair, Snohomish County Board of Health member, Community Transit Board member, former Edmonds City Councilmember and council president.



Other Professional Experience

Small business owner (ASP, Cheesemonger's Table, Olives), former President Downtown Merchants Association, former Board Member Edmonds Chamber of Commerce

 


Education

B.A. University of New Mexico

Community Service

Board Member Progressive Animal Welfare Society (PAWS), Coalition of Children of the Incarcerated, Leadership Alliance Against Coal, Edmonds Community College Veteran Support Center, Edmonds Veterans Plaza

Statement

As your Representative, I’ve fought for affordable housing, safer communities, stronger schools, and protecting our democracy. As Chair of the Housing Committee, I’ve led efforts to improve affordability, reduce homelessness, and keep people in their homes. I’ve delivered investments in transportation, behavioral health, public safety, and workforce training while supporting small businesses, the arts, and protecting our environment. I’ve championed common-sense gun safety laws and defended reproductive freedom. I’ll continue bringing people together, delivering practical results for our communities. 

Endorsed: Planned Parenthood Alliance Advocates, Teachers, Nurses, Firefighters, 21LD/SnoCo Democrats, Conservation Action, State Labor Council, Governor Ferguson, AG Brown, your neighbors.
