1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (General Election 2026)

Elected Experience

State Representative 2023-present. Vice Chair, Maritime Caucus.

Other Professional Experience

Greg co-founded and chairs Moneythink, a nonprofit reducing student debt. President Obama named Moneythink a "Champion of Change" in 2012. He has helped students earn over $27 million in scholarships. Greg grew up in Kitsap and worked as a landscaper, house painter, and firewood splitter.

Education

Kitsap K-12 public schools; UChicago (Truman Scholar); Cambridge University Master’s — Management & Innovation (Gates Scholar).

Community Service

Ran 3,156 miles — from the Atlantic to the Pacific — to mobilize champions for youth mental health. Longtime Kitsap youth mentor and school speaker. Former Board Member, Kitsap Mental Health Services.

Statement

Greg Nance is honored to represent Kitsap, the community that raised him. He's fighting to fix our ferries, protect Puget Sound, and support working families. He worked across the aisle to secure $895 million for ferry construction, maintenance, and workforce. He passed legislation protecting kids from fentanyl, expanding affordable childcare, and bolstering Olympic College healthcare programs. He secured $42 million to modernize West Sound Tech and helped protect 680 acres of Kitsap forest from clearcutting.

Supported by: Suquamish Tribe and Port Gamble S'Klallam Tribe; Washington State Labor Council (representing 600 unions and 500,000 members) — including teachers, nurses, and firefighters.
