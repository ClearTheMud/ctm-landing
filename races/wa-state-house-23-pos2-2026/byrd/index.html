Community Service

Active volunteer at Refuge Church in Bremerton. Coordinates events and assists in strategic planning for the local party.

Statement

Lance is a longtime Kitsap resident with deep family ties and strong community roots. A firm believer in fiscal responsibility, he insists government must live within its means so working families and small businesses can afford to live and prosper here.

He will focus on Kitsap voters’ top priorities: making housing more affordable, reducing crime, supporting law enforcement, lowering the cost of living, balancing the budget, and providing real help and shelter for the mentally ill. Lance will protect education funding and essential services while respecting Washington voters’ clear, repeated decision against a state income tax.
