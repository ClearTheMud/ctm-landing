1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

State Senator (2025-2026); State Representative (2023-2025)



Other Professional Experience

Non-profit leader building quality affordable housing and childcare; former Seattle Office of Housing Director investing $290 million to prevent displacement, support climate resilience, build supportive housing to address homelessness, protect renters, and expand homeownership; award-winning Planned Parenthood organizer; Attorney



Education

BA, Scripps College (summa cum laude); JD, University of Washington School of Law (Gates Public Service Law Scholar)



Community Service

Former Board Member, Washington Low Income Housing Alliance; former member, Seattle Women’s Commission; former Volunteer Attorney, Latino Bar Association of Washington Community Legal Clinic; PTA parent of two Seattle Public School students



Statement

It’s a privilege to represent you in the State Senate. As a lifelong advocate for affordable housing and economic opportunity, I bring deep experience and a proven track record of delivering results. As your Senator, I’ve moved us forward on what matters: high-quality public schools, affordable housing, and access to childcare and healthcare. I’m proud to have championed policies that address the high cost of living by passing historic tax reform to fund schools and services, tax relief for working families and small businesses, and protections for renters, workers, and consumers.

I will continue to push for a fair economy with good-paying jobs and reforms that make homeownership, healthcare, childcare, and groceries more affordable. I’ll fight to improve educational outcomes for kids and strengthen the social safety net for youth and seniors. We all deserve reliable public services, and I’ll keep advocating for the investments needed to make our communities healthy and safe.

You can count on me to stand up for our democracy and civil rights, for reproductive freedom, common sense gun reform, and climate action. I’m committed to making government work for people—not special interests. I’d be honored to earn your continued support.
