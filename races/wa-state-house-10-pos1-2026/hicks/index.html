1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience
Stanwood, WA City Councilman.  Elected 2024

Other Professional Experience
With over four decades of leadership experience, Chili retired as a Master Chief Petty Officer following a 21-year U.S. Navy career. He later served as an HR manager at Boeing, as a telecommunications HR executive and a successful small business owner for 30-years

Education
MBA, Saint Leo University; BA, Columbia College; US Navy Senior Enlisted Leadership Academy

Community Service
Stanwood Planning Commissioner, Stanwood-Camano YMCA Board of Directors, Stanwood Salary Commission, YMCA youth coach, Drug Education for Youth (DEFY) Director, VFW Post 12228, American Legion Post 92 & Riders Director and Boeing Employee Veteran Association (BEVA) Founding President

Statement
As a Navy combat veteran, father, grandfather, and small business owner, I am on a mission to rescue Washington. One-party control in Olympia ignores working families. Their reckless spending, waste, and failed policies directly drive up inflation—skyrocketing your daily costs for gas, groceries, and housing—and no one is accountable.  True leadership demands common-sense fiscal discipline over politics. I will fight to lower taxes, slash government waste, protect your family budget, and restore accountability.   Results matter. I will work tirelessly to protect your paycheck and secure the financial future for our kids and the generations to come.
