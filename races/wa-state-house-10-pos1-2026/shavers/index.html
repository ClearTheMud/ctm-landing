1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience
State Representative (current) – Vice Chair of the House Education Committee (Kindergarten-12th Grade), Member of the WA Artificial Intelligence Task Force

Other Professional Experience
After graduating from the U.S. Naval Academy, Clyde served in the U.S. Navy stationed in the Middle East and Southeast Asia. He graduated from Yale Law School and is a practicing attorney. He lives on Whidbey Island.

Education
U.S. Naval Academy, B.S.; Yale Law School, J.D.

Community Service
Skagit Friendship House, Board Member; Goosefoot, former Board Member; Whidbey Veterans Resource Center, former Board Member

Statement
The economy isn’t working for us, and everything is unaffordable. Gas is too expensive, so I voted no on a tax that would have made it cost even more. Property taxes are too high, so for years I’ve worked on expanding tax exemptions for seniors and veterans. Groceries cost too much, so I co-sponsored legislation to reduce pressure on food prices. Water bills keep rising, so I fought unfair rate hikes. Veterans aren’t receiving benefits they earned, so I passed legislation for more Veteran Service Officers.

 

Every day, I’m fighting to lower costs and make your life more affordable.

Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, State Representative Pos. 1. Accessed 2026-07-16. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
