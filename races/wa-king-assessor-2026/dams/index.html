1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Education: Bachelor of Arts Degree in Communication, University of Washington, Cum Laude

Occupation: Chief Deputy Assessor, King County

Statement: As the current Chief Deputy Assessor, I know how critical the work of the Office is. I lead the efforts to set property values upon which your property taxes are calculated and billed. I lead the review of funding requests from schools, fire departments, and cities to ensure they’re accurate and lawful. If it’s wrong, families are overtaxed, and services are underfunded.

Getting it right is technical and demanding, taking years to learn. I've done it for 11 years as Chief Deputy Assessor, the #2 position in the office. I manage teams of highly trained and educated appraisers, analysts, and technologists who value 750,000 residential and commercial properties annually. 

Because of our office’s high standards, professionalism, and quality, we’ve earned the Certificate of Excellence in Assessment Administration — one of only 65 jurisdictions in the U.S. and Canada to achieve this honor, and one of two in our State.

No candidate in this race has the experience and knowledge I have, nor the track record of reforming the property tax system. As Chief Deputy Assessor, I’ve worked to reform tax exemption programs so thousands of additional Seniors are receiving tax breaks to stay in their homes. I’m committed to expanding tax exemptions so that those of all ages are eligible.

Voters face a real choice: hand this office to someone who must learn it from scratch, and risk costly mistakes. Or elect me, the person who's been doing this work every day for more than a decade. I'm asking for your vote to keep the office in experienced and proven hands.

I’m endorsed by dozens of people of all backgrounds, including County Councilmember Claudia Balducci, former Deputy County Executive Fred Jarrett, State Representative Kristine Reeves, and Bothell City Councilmember Prasad Anguluri. I’d be honored with your vote.

Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, Assessor. Accessed 2026-07-16. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
