1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (General Election 2026)

Education: University of Washington, B.A.; Certified General Real Estate Appraiser; Seattle Public Schools graduate

Occupation: Senior Appraiser, City of Seattle; Former King County Hearing Examiner

Statement: The Assessor's office touches every person's ability to stay in their home. Rising property taxes are pushing homeowners, renters, and small businesses out of their communities, and too many people don't trust that the system is treating them fairly. King County needs an accurate, fair, and transparent Assessor's office that works for everyone.

I'm a fifth-generation Seattleite and the only candidate in this race who has actually done the valuation work this office depends on. As Senior Appraiser for the City of Seattle, I appraise public properties daily, ensuring real estate decisions serve the public interest. My career has included commercial valuation in the private sector. Serving as a Hearing Examiner on the King County Board of Appeals and Equalization, I've sat across the table from homeowners fighting to stay in their communities while corporate landowners brought teams of experts to secure tax reductions. I understand both sides of this system.

I've lived in income-restricted housing. I know what financial pressure looks like from the inside, and it's why I'm a proud PROTEC17 shop steward.

I'll bring real relief for homeowners and renters, and transparency and accountability to a system many people don't fully understand. In Olympia, I'll push to establish homestead protections for primary residences to help homeowners manage rising costs, and create a renter's tax credit to give tenants relief from rising housing costs as well.

I'm proud to be endorsed by the Seattle Times, the Stranger, King County Executive Girmay Zahilay, Chief Deputy Assessor Al Dams, King County Councilmembers Claudia Balducci, Sarah Perry, and Rhonda Lewis, King County Democrats, eleven legislative district Democratic organizations, Seattle Councilmembers Eddie Lin and Dionne Foster, multiple labor unions, and Danny Hagen, President of the Washington State Association of County Assessors, among others. They trust my professional expertise to lead this department.

Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, Assessor. Accessed 2026-10-10. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
