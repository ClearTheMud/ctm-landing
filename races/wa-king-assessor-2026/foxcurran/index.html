1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Education: University of Washington, B.A.; Certified General Real Estate Appraiser; Seattle Public Schools graduate

Occupation: Senior Appraiser, City of Seattle; Former King County Hearing Examiner

Statement: The Assessor's office touches every person's ability to stay in their home. Rising property taxes are pushing out homeowners, renters, and small businesses, while large corporations use high-paid lawyers to minimize their tax bills and shift that burden onto working people. That has to end.

I'm a fifth-generation Seattleite and the only candidate in this race who has actually done the valuation work this office depends on. As Senior Appraiser for the City of Seattle, I appraise public properties daily, ensuring real estate decisions serve the public interest. My career has included high-level commercial valuation in the private sector. Serving as a Hearing Examiner on the King County Board of Appeals and Equalization, I've sat across the table from homeowners fighting to stay in their communities while corporate landowners brought teams of experts to secure tax reductions. I've seen who the current system serves and who it fails. 

I've lived in income-restricted housing. I know what financial pressure looks like from the inside, and it's why I'm a proud PROTEC17 shop steward. Workers deserve to organize and fight for fair treatment.

I’ll ensure large corporate property owners pay their fair share, provide real relief for homeowners and renters, and bring transparency and accountability to a system that too many people distrust. In Olympia, I’ll push to lift the 1% levy cap, establish homestead protections for primary residences, and create a renter's tax credit, so working families stop absorbing a burden the current system was never designed to share fairly. 

I'm proud to be endorsed by King County Democrats, nine legislative district Democratic organizations, Seattle Councilmembers Eddie Lin and Dionne Foster, multiple labor unions, and Danny Hagen, President of the Washington State Association of County Assessors, among others. They've seen me do this work and they trust me to lead it.

Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, Assessor. Accessed 2026-07-16. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
