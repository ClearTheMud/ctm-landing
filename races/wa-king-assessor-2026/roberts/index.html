1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (Primary Election 2026)

Education: Ph.D., UW; B.A., Willamette; National Insurance Credential (AINS); Energy Planning Policy Certificate, U-Idaho

Occupation: Government Affairs Coordinator and Public Policy Instructor, IAAO member

Statement: “Chris Roberts is a proven leader who will deliver for King County.” — Executive Girmay Zahilay.

The King County Assessor’s office is in the midst of a leadership crisis. From the current Assessor’s personal legal issues to delays in processing applications for property tax relief, the office needs a fresh start. This is not a failure of the frontline staff, who do excellent work; it is a leadership failure—the result of understaffing, budgetary mismanagement, and a lack of attention to customer service.

The Assessor must lead with absolute integrity and transparency. With 17 years of experience as a Councilmember and two-term Mayor, I have managed complex budgets and delivered housing affordability. I will rebuild public trust by establishing a citizen oversight committee and creating a tribal liaison position to honor sovereignty and those with the deepest connection to the land.

No candidate can deliver results until the office functions well. I possess deep technical policy knowledge and the proven leadership needed to clear backlogs and cut red tape. I will prioritize modernizing operations to ensure the office supports our $400 billion economy efficiently.

“I have full trust in Chris’s government experience and judgment to serve the taxpayers well.” — Former King County Assessor Lloyd Hara. Endorsed by Congresswoman Suzan DelBene, Lt. Gov. Denny Heck, Councilmembers Rod Dembowski, Teresa Mosqueda, Seattle Councilmember Alexis Mercedes-Rinck, suburban mayors, multiple Democratic organizations, and union labor. Thank you for your vote!

Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, Assessor. Accessed 2026-07-16. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
