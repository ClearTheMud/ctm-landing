Statement
It has truly been a privilege to serve the people of Walla Walla County as your Assessor over the past four years. Every day, I am reminded of the responsibility entrusted to this office and the importance of ensuring that our property tax system remains fair, transparent, and consistent for every resident.

As your Assessor, I will continue to approach this work with integrity, accuracy, and a commitment to public service. I will ensure that our office remains staffed with well-trained, knowledgeable employees who are equipped to provide excellent service and uphold the high standards our community deserves. We will continue to adopt and implement necessary technological improvements so that our process remain efficient, accessible, and aligned with state requirements.

I am grateful for the trust you have placed in me, and I respectfully ask for your continued support. I look forward to the opportunity to serve you for another four years as your Walla Walla County Assessor.
