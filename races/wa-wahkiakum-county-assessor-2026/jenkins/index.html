I attended appraisal school in Chicago and began residential appraisal in 2002, acquiring the necessary certification for the state of Illinois After moving to Washington, I completed Washington state accreditation in 2018 and was commercially accredited in 2020. Community Service
Like everyone, I pitch in as needed to help. For instance, I’ve met a lot of you by volunteering at the county fair, at the Lion’s Club, and other functions over the years.


Statement
Many people know me from working in the County Assessor’s office, or from my visits to their property for State mandated inspections. I worked in the Assessor’s office for seven years, first as residential appraiser, then as residential and commercial appraiser before I was elected as County Assessor. I was directly involved when in 2020 the State asked us to change our appraisal software. Each property required revaluation which we accomplished over two years with very few appeals from taxpayers.  Prior to residing in Cathlamet, I was a residential appraiser in Chicago for 13 years.  In total, I have more than 20 years of experience appraising properties. My history also includes 28 years at United Airlines as a flight attendant and in management, developing and implementing inflight training programs. If I’m known for anything, it’s for being a friendly face behind the counter with a commitment to treating people with kindness and respect. Property values can be a sensitive subject.  I enjoy helping people understand the process for valuation. Public service is an honor I do not take lightly.

Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, County Assessor. Accessed 2026-07-16. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
