1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (Primary Election 2026)

Elected Experience

Candidate for Wahkiakum County Assessor in 2025. Served on the Wahkiakum County Board of Equalization.



Other Professional Experience

Certified Residential Real Property Appraiser with 13+ years of continuous, licensed, verifiable appraisal experience. DOR Accredited Ad Valorem Appraiser. Former County Assessor’s Office employee. Certified Continuing Education Course Provider to real estate brokers.

Education

Associate of Arts. Thousands of hours of professional education, including continuing education, ethics, and anti-discrimination training.

Community Service

Volunteer Firefighter. Grays River Grange member/officer. Pro bono real estate and property tax services.

Statement

My wife and I are proud to call Wahkiakum County home. We are regular, hardworking folks, and like many families, we believe taxes are too high. We care deeply about this community, the people who live here, and keeping Wahkiakum County affordable and thriving for the future.

I believe public service should be dependable, accountable, and people-first. I respect private property rights, and I believe taxpayers deserve to be heard, respected, and helped with patience and understanding. Taxpayers are not numbers on a page. They are our friends, neighbors, families, local businesses, and community members.

My background in property valuation gives me the experience to do the job, but my commitment to this community is why I am running. That commitment is why I serve in the Grays River Grange and became a volunteer firefighter. The future of our community depends on people stepping up to help each other.

I want the Assessor’s Office to be approachable, with an open-door policy where taxpayers can ask questions, get clear answers, and know they can call on me anytime.

A vote for Moriarty is a vote for a neighbor, a friend, and people-first service.
