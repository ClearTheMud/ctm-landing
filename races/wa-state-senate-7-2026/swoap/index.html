Statement

Make Washington for its citizens and not a welfare state for immigrants.  Let’s stop electing the officials who created this state of bankruptcy. Is your life better today than it used to be?  We need positive change.  Not change that destroys what we have.  Our founding fathers risked everything including their lives. We must stop the citizens loyal to other countries from benefiting off Washington citizens.  Our state tax burden inhibits economic growth and takes too much from hard working Washington citizens. 

For sixty years politicians have been promising to fix things and all say the same things in a different way.  If our elected officials wanted them fixed, they would be.  The solutions are easy, yet no one wants to pay the price like our founding fathers did.  When the righteous are in authority, the people rejoice: but when the wicked rule, the people mourn.  Just look at your life today.  If you do choose to elect me, know that I cannot fight for you alone, we need each other, and bring back those things that are good. Will we continue to elect plutocracy politicians who rule and do not govern this state?
