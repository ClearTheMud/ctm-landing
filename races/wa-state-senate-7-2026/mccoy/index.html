Statement

My name is Ronald L McCoy and reside in Chewelah WA. This District was not built by Lobbyist and special interest groups, it was built on the backs of hardworking men and women working in the fields, raising Cattle and all kinds of animals and orchards and other crops for the food supply, forest and mining. Men and women who served our country through dedication and loyalty to our Nation. I will be focused first and upmost on investing in job creation and programs that put our younger generation to work and give them a sense of hope. Strengthen our rural infrastructure, taxes that burden our small Business owners and everyday citizens, I am a retired US Navy Veteran, former Mayor and Chewelah City Council member. I have Spent decades advocating for Rural Communities and serving Northeast Washington and now I'm running to bring strong honest leadership to the 7th District.  I believe in moving forward without losing who we are while supporting and investing in common sense solutions to everyday living in rural Northeast Washinton. Good government is built from bottom up not top down
