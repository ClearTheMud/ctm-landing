Elected Experience

Other Professional Experience

Central Washington University Head Wrestling Coach, City Of Brewster Employee, Lifeguard, MMA Coach, Stand-Up Comedian, Event Organizer, Orchardist (Picking & Sorting), Kids Free Lunch Program Staff, Youth Drug & Alcohol Counselor.

Education

Community Service

Kids Summer Free Lunch Program, Youth Drug and Alcohol Counseling, Brewster City Dog Pound, Volunteer Firefighter (Structure/Rescue & Wildland) Fought Carlton Complex Fires & filled Horse Troughs for surviving livestock. 

Statement

It's time our district has someone in Olympia who will stand up for the common citizens. The Blue Collar workers who wake up every morning to earn an honest dollar to support their families. The Nurses, Caregivers and Educators that nurture our communities. The Native Americans on the three reservations in our district.  The Fireman, EMS and the Police(Especially Elected Sherrifs). The seemingly voiceless will have a voice if I am there.

Our District is largest in the state with immense resources and contributions to the state budget. Unfortunately it's not reflected in our communities. We deserve better for our citizens and I will assure you I will do everything I can to use legislation to bring that money back to our district.

I don’t plan on being the most conventional politician. That’s what I believe is going to make me a better representation of the people, for the people.

The majority of our district isn’t white collar lawyers. The majority of our district is smart, hardworking people with real rural lifestyles. That is who I will represent. My attitude, respect and lexicon will be compatible enough to get the right legislation passed with the white collar representation in Olympia.
