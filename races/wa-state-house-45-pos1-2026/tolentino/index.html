1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

Precinct Committee Officer, five years.

Other Professional Experience

Music teacher for thirty-five years. Retired professional in Governance, Risk, and Compliance software sales.

Education

Attended Wenatchee Valley College and Seattle Pacific University, Music Therapy major.

Community Service

Dedicated hundreds of hours to grassroots activities supporting democracy; worked summers with incarcerated youth.

Statement

The 45th District deserves results, not partisan games. I am not a politician; I am a teacher, mother, and grandmother who believes in putting people first. I will restore accountability to Olympia by defending our constitutional rights, transparency, and equal justice. We must implement secure elections, empower parents, and prioritize academic excellence. I will fight the regulations driving up housing costs and champion affordable energy. We need proven recovery solutions for homelessness and full support for our law enforcement. Endorsed by the King County Republican Party. I am ready for the hard work of restoring trust and defending our freedom.

Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, State Representative Pos. 1. Accessed 2026-07-16. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
