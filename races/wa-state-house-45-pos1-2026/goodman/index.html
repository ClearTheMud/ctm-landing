1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

State Representative, 2007-present; Chair, House Community Safety Committee; Chair, Statute Law Committee; Senior Member, Civil Rights and Judiciary Committee; Human Services and Early Learning Committee.

Other Professional Experience

Attorney in private practice; King County Bar Association, 2000-08; Director, Washington State Sentencing Guidelines Commission 1998-2000; Washington State Law and Justice Advisory Council; Congressional Chief of Staff and Legislative Director, 1988-1995.


Education

Dartmouth College, AB, Senior Fellow; George Washington University, JD; Harvard University, MPA.


Community Service

Hopelink, Board of Directors; President, Kirkland’s Lakeview Neighborhood Association; Rotary Club; King County Behavioral Health Advisory Board; proud father of two.

Statement

I’m honored to represent you in Olympia, leading efforts to improve public safety, reduce gun violence and help people in crisis. I’ve passed laws to protect survivors of crime, expand drug and mental health services, and improve law enforcement accountability and training.

I voted to reduce the tax burden on working people and small businesses and to provide free school meals and more affordable childcare. With your vote, I’ll continue to champion K-12 funding, affordable housing investments, climate action, and reproductive rights. Let’s keep working together to protect our communities and families.

Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, State Representative Pos. 1. Accessed 2026-07-16. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
