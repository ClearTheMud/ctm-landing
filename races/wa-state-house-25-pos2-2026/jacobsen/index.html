1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

Washington State Representative 2021 - present. Washington State Institute for Public Policy (WSIPP) board co-chair, Select Committee on Pension Policy, Puyallup City Councilmember 2018-2021, LEOFF Disability Board, Pierce County Regional Council, Puget Sound Regional Council Transportation Policy Board.

Other Professional Experience

math teacher, Pierce College; para-educator, Puyallup schools; private sector accountant; rental housing provider, entrepreneur.


Education

Master’s in Professional Accountancy, Central Washington University. BA Economics and Accounting, Western Washington University.

Community Service

As your State Representative, Cyndy’s committee assignments include leadership roles on Finance, Housing, and Civil Rights and Judiciary. She has volunteered in the Puyallup Schools, the YMCA, and numerous parent, homeowners, and church organizations.

Statement

Principled, Balanced, Experienced

As your representative, Cyndy fights kindly for sane policy in Washington. Every year, Washington outspends our revenues, resulting in a dangerous game of raiding pension funds, rainy-day funds, trust accounts, and school budgets. This fiscal malfeasance causes an insatiable desire for taxation that most of us cannot afford. Cyndy will continue to push for responsible budgets that preserve our social safety net without taking more of your money. Cyndy advocates for protecting children from fentanyl exposure in homes, supporting our first responders, and holding offenders accountable. Washington needs this balance. I respectfully ask for your vote ~ Cyndy

Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, State Representative Pos. 2. Accessed 2026-07-16. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
