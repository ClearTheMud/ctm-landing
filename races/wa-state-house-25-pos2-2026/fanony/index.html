Statement

Lorenzo "Ren" Fanony is a lifelong resident of Puyallup and Tacoma. While working in architecture, Ren has dealt with issues relating to housing, infrastructure, and energy specifically in Pierce County.

I am running for State Representative to provide simple solutions to the issues I have seen in our community. Specifically in Washington, taxes are rising, and so is the cost of living. This is not sustainable for the state, or for us as individuals, but we still need our public services to run smoothly. I would like to represent you in Olympia to see change and a sustainable Washington.
