1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

Pierce County Charter Review Commissioner elected in 2025, proudly representing County Council District 5.

Other Professional Experience

Jenn Marie is a small-business owner, home renovator, artist, and community leader. Jenn Marie is endorsed by the WA State Labor Council.


Education

An Orting High School grad, Jenn Marie is a proud alumna of Washington State University, where she attended on a rowing scholarship. She also attended Evergreen State College in Olympia.

Community Service

Jenn Marie is a rescue-dog mom who has served as a Family Promise board member and church deaconess, and she is an active member of the local Chamber of Commerce.



Statement

People. Power. Paychecks. Orting raised with small-town values: look out for neighbors, work hard, and never quit. Working people should be at the center of every decision made in Olympia. As your Charter Commissioner, I’m delivering power right now, including housing protections that keep seniors & veterans in their homes. I’ll take the fight to Olympia to protect your paycheck from excessive costs for necessities: gas, groceries, and housing. I’ll champion local jobs that keep you close to home and out of gridlock, and I will work across the aisle to ensure the people’s interests are prioritized.

-Jenn Marie Strickling
