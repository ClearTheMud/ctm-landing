1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

Elected Experience: PCO 11th Legislative District, 2024 Democratic National Committee Delegate, Former 28th Legislative District PCO, Chairwoman.

Other Professional Experience

Over twenty-five years of experience as a registered nurse and Certified Registered Nurse Anesthetist. Small business owner providing anesthesia care, including gender affirming care, abortion, and reproductive care. Navy Veteran, served as a Lieutenant Commander in the Nursing Corps.

Education

Bachelor of Science in Nursing, Case Western Reserve University; Master of Science, York College of Pennsylvania; Doctor of Education in Interdisciplinary Leadership, Creighton University.

Community Service

Pro-Choice Washington PAC member, APRNs of Washington State board member, Past President Washington Association of Nurse Anesthesiology, Medic, Southend Indivisible. 




Statement

For over two decades, I’ve served as a nurse helping local neighbors and in the US Navy. I've worked with teams under pressure while caring for my patients and families. We need that experience fighting for working people squeezed by housing and childcare costs, healthcare bills, and education debt.


No nurses currently serve in the legislature. I’ll fight for accessible healthcare, affordable housing, K-12 investments, career training, and well-paying jobs. Nurses are trained to solve problems- and are needed to bring positive change to Olympia. Endorsed by Nurses, State Employees, Elected Officials, 11th LD Dems, County Dems, Planned Parenthood, more.
