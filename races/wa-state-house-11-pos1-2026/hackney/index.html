Statement

Times are tough, and we cannot count on a gridlocked federal government. While I am incredibly proud of what we have accomplished at the state level, we still have much more work to do. The daily cost of living continues to climb, and our families need immediate relief. My singular focus is finding direct ways to lower costs for everyday people, from housing to healthcare.

I’m honored to be endorsed by Governor Bob Ferguson, Attorney General Nick Brown, local leaders, the 11th District Democrats, the State Labor Council, and many others. I hope to earn your vote.
