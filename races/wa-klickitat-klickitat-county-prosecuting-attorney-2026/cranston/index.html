1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience
Appointed Interim Prosecuting Attorney, January, 2026. 

Other Professional Experience
Deputy Prosecuting Attorney for Klickitat County. Prior to joining the Prosecutor’s Office in 2016, I worked as a civil attorney for a nonprofit law firm in Snohomish County and served as a law clerk for a superior court judge in Snohomish County.

Education
B.A. International Relations, University of California, Davis (2010); Juris Doctorate, cum laude, Seattle University (2014).

Community Service
Founded a 501(c)(3) nonprofit law firm providing reduced-cost legal services to low-income individuals. Classroom volunteer at Trout Lake School and member of the Trout Lake Playground Committee.

Statement
I have spent nearly a decade in the Prosecuting Attorney’s Office representing the county in a broad range of civil matters and criminal appeals. Throughout my service, I have taken pride in building strong relationships with county employees and the citizens of this county.

Since my appointment, I have worked to strengthen partnerships with law enforcement and community organizations. As Prosecuting Attorney, I will continue to prioritize public safety by collaborating with our criminal justice partners to hold offenders accountable while advocating for victims and supporting the community.
