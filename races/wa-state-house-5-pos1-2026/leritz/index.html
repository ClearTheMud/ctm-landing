Statement

I will never say that now is the time for change, because change is long overdue. Now is the time for leadership that focuses on real, relevant, issues, not political buzzwords and reelection.

I am running as a nonpartisan candidate, accepting zero endorsements or special interest donations, because I believe in putting our community above all else. Real change requires focusing on everyone’s issues, whether you are a Democrat, Republican, or Independent. I am committed to representing everyone in this district, not just the people who voted for me. Together, we can build a stronger, more united Washington.
