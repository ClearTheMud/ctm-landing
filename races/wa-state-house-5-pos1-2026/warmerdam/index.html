Volunteer for the Seattle DSA, Whole Washington, and Progressive Victory. Statement

I will fight for universal healthcare, new social housing, and an expansion of our public transit network into our smaller communities. I will fully fund our education system, and reverse cuts to our Department of Natural Resources, paid for by progressive taxation on the ultra rich and largest corporations. I will champion a $26 minimum wage, a 32 hour workweek, and guaranteed paid time off for all workers. Washington needs bold new leaders who fight for the working class, not the corporate duopoly. Because I refuse corporate PAC money, my loyalty lies with the people.

A better Washington is possible.
