1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

Current State Representative; Vice Chair of House Environment & Energy Committee; Member of House Local Government and Transportation Committees; Former Issaquah City Councilmember

Other Professional Experience

Former Legislative Assistant for WA House of Representatives; Previously consulted for cities and state agencies

Education

Master of Public Administration, University of Washington Evans School of Public Policy & Governance; Bachelor of Science, University of Washington

Community Service

Zach previously chaired the Eastside Fire & Rescue Board, ensuring first responders had what they needed to keep us safe. A lifelong Issaquah resident, he’s passionate about salmon recovery and serves on the Friends of the Issaquah Salmon Hatchery Board.

Statement

As your representative, I’m focused on building consensus and strong communities. In my first session, I sponsored and passed bipartisan bills to lower energy costs, prioritized safety and congestion improvements along SR 18, and supported reforms to fix our housing crisis. I’m committed to supporting first responders, fully funding our schools, helping small businesses thrive, and pushing for affordable childcare and healthcare. I’d be honored to earn your vote.

Endorsements: Eastside Firefighters, WA Labor Council, WA Education Association, Planned Parenthood Alliance Advocates, Alliance for Gun Responsibility, Congresswoman Kim Schrier, State Sen. Victoria Hunt, State Rep. Lisa Callan, and local mayors.
