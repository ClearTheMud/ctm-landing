1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (General Election 2026)

Elected Experience

President: King County Police Chiefs/Sheriffs, WA State FBI National Academy Associates, Washington State Police Chief's and Sheriff's Executive Board, Legislative Committee Co-Chair, Northwestern University Alumni Board. 

Other Professional Experience

Instructor: Northwestern University, Bellevue College. Police Chief: Maple Valley, Sammamish, Mercer Island. Planning and Operations Major in King County Sheriff’s Office, KCSO Deputy in North Bend. 

Education

Doctorate in Education, Seattle Pacific University. Master of Science in Organizational Development, Bachelor of Arts in Law and Justice, Central Washington University.

Community Service

Take a Stand Against Bullying curriculum co-creator used in multiple states, created several community programs including Sammamish Healthy Communities Coalitions, and Youth Explorer and Volunteer Programs.

Statement

Families in the 5th Legislative District deserve safer communities, stronger schools, and a state government that respects the people paying the bills. Olympia recently voted for the largest tax increase in state history while many families struggle with rising costs. Police have also been left without clear tools to protect victims and neighborhoods.

With an education doctorate, and as a former police chief in this district, I bring real-world experience in public safety, education, and responsible budgeting. I will ensure state spending delivers measurable results. I will fight for stronger schools, safer communities, and will bring common-sense leadership to Olympia. 



Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, State Representative Pos. 1. Accessed 2026-10-10. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
