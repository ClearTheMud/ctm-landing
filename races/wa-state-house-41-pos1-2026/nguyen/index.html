1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (General Election 2026)

Elected Experience

Candidate for Mercer Island School District School Board 2021; advanced past primary election. Candidate for LD 41, Position 2, in 2022.

Other Professional Experience

Community advocacy, legal research and drafting, youth and family advocacy, and small-business and real-estate investment experience, including service as Chairperson of Connect Care Alliance involving community health, youth programs, and public accountability initiatives.


Education

B.A. in Theology/Religious Studies, Master of Divinity (MDiv) in Practical Ministry, and Juris Doctor (JD).


Community Service

Community advocate involved in homeless outreach, family support, youth well-being, and community care initiatives, including leadership involvement with Connect Care Alliance and outreach involving underserved communities and African nations.

Statement

Parent, community advocate, and leader focused on protecting children, strengthening families, improving public safety, supporting youth well-being, and promoting government transparency and accountability. Committed to practical leadership, traditional values, economic opportunity for working families, support for small businesses, and restoring public trust through common-sense solutions that put communities first.

“Make It Make Sense.”
