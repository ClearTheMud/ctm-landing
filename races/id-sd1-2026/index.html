2 Race Dynamics

BLUF, Bottom Line Up Front

The fourth Woodward-Herndon matchup is resolved: Scott Herndon defeated incumbent Jim Woodward 53.55%–46.45% (8,155–7,075 of 15,230 votes, margin 1,080) in the May 19, 2026 Republican primary, official Idaho SOS results as of 2026-06-09, final certification pending. The race was a proxy war between the Idaho Freedom Foundation/Caucus (backing Herndon) and the traditional Republican establishment/business community (backing Woodward via IACI); both moderate District 1 incumbents lost on May 19 (Rep. Mark Sauter also lost his 1A primary). Spokane Public Radio reported Woodward ($135.2K raised) and Herndon ($104.7K raised) brought in the most contributions of any Idaho Senate candidates (Idaho Sunshine figures as of 2026-06-10). The Republican primary winner is the de facto general election winner in this R+40 district: Herndon enters the November 3 general against Steve Johnson (I), functionally a Democratic candidate with no viable path, as the heavy favorite.
