2 Race Dynamics

BLUF — Bottom Line Up Front

Idaho Senate District 1 (Bonner and Boundary counties, Sandpoint area) is staging its fourth Woodward-Herndon matchup since 2018 — a race that has become a proxy war between the Idaho Freedom Foundation/Caucus (backing Herndon) and the traditional Republican establishment/business community (backing Woodward via IACI). The seat has changed hands between them twice: Woodward won in 2018 (52%), lost in 2022 (44%), and recaptured it in 2024 by just 613 votes (52%). The Republican primary winner is the de facto general election winner in this R+40 district. Both candidates bring significant financial resources (~$96K vs ~$85K) making this one of Idaho’s top-5 funded legislative races. Steve Johnson (I) is functionally a Democratic candidate with no viable path.
