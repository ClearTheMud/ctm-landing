1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

I’m Cora Moser, a proud Skamania County resident with deep roots in our community. Raised in Mill A, I understand the values and priorities that matter most to local families. Our County needs an Assessor who prioritizes our heritage, supports local businesses, and pursues tax exemptions for homeowners. With 20 years of paralegal experience and over 8 years serving Skamania County, I bring knowledge, integrity, and dedication to the Assessor’s office. My goal is fair and accurate property assessments while supporting our community’s growth and stability. I’m running to serve you and would be honored to earn your vote.
