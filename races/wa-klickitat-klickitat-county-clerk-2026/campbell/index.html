1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience
2014, 2018, 2022 elected Klickitat County Clerk

Other Professional Experience
Worked for 24 years in East District Court, 18 years as the Court Administrator, and 20 years as Non-Attorney Judicial Officer presiding over matters at the direction of the Presiding Judge.


Education
Graduated from Glenwood High School. Attended Walla Walla Community College earning an AA in Secretarial Science. Professional Development Academy (Leadership) sponsored by NACO.


Community Service
Superintendent of the Food Preservation Department at the Klickitat County Fair for the last 16 years. Member of the Goldendale Grange and serves on the scholarship committee.


Statement
Renea has proudly served as your County Clerk for the past 13 years. During her tenure, she led modernization efforts, including implementing a new case management system, electronic filing, upgraded document and jury management systems, online public document access through the Washington State Digital Archives, and passport photo services to help supplement office revenue.

Renea has streamlined office processes to improve efficiency while remaining fiscally responsible. Her leadership, knowledge of the law and court rules, commitment to justice, and proven record of innovation and service have prepared her to continue leading the Clerk’s Office effectively for the next four years.

Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, Klickitat County Clerk. Accessed 2026-07-16. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
