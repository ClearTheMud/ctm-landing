Statement

District Court Judges lead a team of dedicated professionals who together handle the majority of this County’s criminal and civil cases. The District Court motto says it all, “Serving Justice through Serving People”.

District Court helps people get their lives back on track. The Veteran’s and Mental Health Courts help people conquer addiction and renew their mental wellness. The Court helps people get relicensed, get engaged in rehabilitative programs, and get back to work as dependable members of the community.

District Court has worked to improve the public’s access to the Court. The use of technology has allowed the public to view and attend court sessions remotely. The District Court is open to the public in person, on the telephone, and online. All users of the District Court are treated fairly, respectfully, and with dignity.

Judge Kalo Wilcox believes that the District Court plays an important and unique role in our community. Often, the people who appear in her court are at a point in their lives when a good judge can help them reform and return to being productive members of our community. Judge Wilcox is committed to being that kind of Judge.
