1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (General Election 2026)

Nicole Knowles is a dedicated public servant, experienced trial attorney, and proven community leader committed to protecting the people of Cowlitz County. Over the course of her legal career, she has tried more than 50 cases in District and Superior Court, prosecuting everything from misdemeanors to the most serious felonies.

For five years, Nicole served as Senior Deputy Prosecuting Attorney for child sexual abuse cases, advocating for vulnerable children and helping ensure justice for victims and their families. She worked closely with law enforcement, service providers, and community partners to support survivors and strengthen public trust in the legal system.

Currently, Nicole serves as Lead Prosecutor for the City of Longview, supervising attorneys in District Court. She helped create and continues to grow the Cowlitz Municipal Therapeutic Court, developing solutions that address addiction and mental health challenges while improving public safety and reducing recidivism. Nicole also serves as Judge Pro Tempore for Cowlitz County District Court, demonstrating integrity, fairness, prudence, and sound judgment.

Nicole and her husband, Brandon, are raising their two young children in Cowlitz County and proudly call this community home. She remains committed to serving and protecting local families with compassion, accountability, professionalism, and dedication to justice.
