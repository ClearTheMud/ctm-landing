Elected Experience

Other Professional Experience

Current member of IATSE Local 887 (Wardrobe Technician, Costume Designer) and Actor's Equity Association (Actor, Musician, Teaching Artist). Previous: Executive Business Administrator, Microsoft; Executive Assistant Amazon; Caterer; Life Guard.

Education

Coastline Community College

Community Service

My time with Girl Scouts USA, for which I received a Silver Award, fostered a lifelong devotion to community service. I volunteer for environmental and educational causes such as Habitat for Humanity and El Corazon, King County Parks, California State Parks, Seattle Public Schools and the Washington State Thespian Society. 

Statement

I am a working parent dedicated to ensuring access to healthcare, education, and a healthy environment for everyone in Washington State. As an elected Union Leader responsible for negotiating and overseeing Collective Bargaining Agreements I have worked to establish fair wages, worker protections, and on the job safety standards. If elected I will work tirelessly for living wages, healthcare access, tax fairness and inflation relief as well as the protection of our parks, natural resources and agricultural lands. I look forward to the opportunity to serve District 12 as your representative. Together we can build a better, brighter Washington.
