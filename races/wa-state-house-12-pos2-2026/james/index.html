1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

None. Endorsed by the Republican Parties of King County, Snohomish County, and Douglas County.




Other Professional Experience

Co-Founder of Grace City Church in Wenatchee. Community leader serving families, outreach ministries, and local initiatives for over 25 years. Founding board member of Garden City Academy, a K-12 private Christian school.

Education

Graduate of Pateros High School. Earned an AAS from Wenatchee Valley College and graduated magna cum laude from Northwest University in Biblical Studies and Church Ministries.

Community Service

Serving families through discipleship, youth mentorship, education, men’s initiatives, and community outreach. During COVID, Adam stood alongside 40 Washingtonians in a lawsuit defending constitutional freedom and local governance.

Statement

Adam James is a Republican running for State House to bring common-sense leadership and accountability to Olympia. Adam is a lifelong North Central Washington resident, husband, father, and community leader. For more than two decades, Adam has served families through ministry and community work in Wenatchee.

Adam is running to lower taxes, protect parental rights, strengthen public safety, and make government transparent. He believes government should stay within its means and answer to the people it serves. Adam is ready to boldly fight for policies that will restore liberty, strengthen families, and secure Washington’s future.
