1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

I am a lifetime resident of Skamania County with 18 years of service with the County, including 14 years in the Clerk’s Office. I have built strong working relationships with many county offices and believe collaboration is essential to providing efficient, professional public service. I am committed to having positive relationships with the community members while ensuring the Clerk’s Office remains accessible, accurate and responsive. My experience, professionalism, and knowledge of county operations have prepared me to serve effectively as County Clerk. I am dedicated to integrity, accountability and continuing dependable service for the citizens of Skamania County.

Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, Clerk. Accessed 2026-07-16. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
