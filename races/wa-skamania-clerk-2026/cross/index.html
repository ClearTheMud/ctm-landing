1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

I am seeking re-election as your Skamania County Clerk and Clerk of the Superior Court. The duties of this office are both detailed and complex. With 20 years of experience in the Clerk’s office—including ten years as your elected Clerk—I bring the institutional knowledge and expertise needed to fulfill these responsibilities with dedication and a deep appreciation for our mission. In my most recent term, I have focused on modernizing our services and improving efficiency by increasing access to public records online, managing record preservation, establishing e-filing & jury summons e-response. I respectfully ask for your vote.

 

Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, Clerk. Accessed 2026-07-16. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
