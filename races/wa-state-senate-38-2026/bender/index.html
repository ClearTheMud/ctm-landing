1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (General Election 2026)

Elected Experience

As a lifelong Washington resident, I have over thirty years of experience in the electrical field, both as a small business owner and a blue-collar worker who knows the value of every dollar earned by working with general contractors and subcontractors. This includes bidding, budgeting, and scheduling jobs, time management, and possessing the ability to work and communicate effectively with others. While I have no previous elected experience, I am looking forward to giving a blue-collared voice to the 38th District in helping to lower taxes and make Washington an affordable place to live.

Statement

I will work for the 38th District with the goal of having Washington become a reasonable, economical, and low-tax state. In this current environment, nearly 1 in 4 small businesses are considering moving out of state per the Association of Washington Businesses. Meanwhile, Washington residents are having to face the difficult decision of making cuts to their budgets to maintain affordable living while the out-of-touch Democrats in Olympia continue to raise taxes to cover their own spending. Washington State voters deserve to have their voices heard, and if they are looking for relief anytime soon, they will have a candidate who will be the voice of the people and stand up for the rights they are entitled to.
