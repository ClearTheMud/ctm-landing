1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (General Election 2026)

Elected Experience

Current State Senator, Chair of Ways & Means Committee. Focused on budget, housing, health, and issues impacting working families. State Representative 2013 - 2020.




Other Professional Experience

Currently employed at Snohomish County Health Department. Previous employment at Public Health Seattle and King County; former Executive Director, Housing Consortium of Everett and Snohomish County; former Director of Planning and Program Management, Community Health Center of Snohomish County. 




Education

Master of Public Health, University of Michigan; BS, University of Delaware

Community Service

Volunteer with Compass Health, Providence General Children’s Association, United Way of Snohomish County, Everett Public Schools, Washington Low Income Housing Alliance.

Statement

In these challenging times for our communities and country, I work hard to make a genuine difference on issues that matter. I’ve fought for record investments in affordable housing and behavioral health services, childcare for working families, and access to health care. I rely on my public health background to confront Trump’s attacks on science and health, and ensure we have the facts and care we deserve.

As the Senate lead on budget and tax reform, I helped push generational change to our regressive tax code, asking millionaires to pay a fair share while winning needed relief for workers and small businesses. This measure will also help protect K-12 education and health care from future economic downturns.  


I’m proud of my record of standing up for everyone in our community. We need principled voices to defend our democracy, immigrant and LGBTQ+ neighbors, and to safeguard reproductive rights. There’s much more work to do to ensure everyone can live with dignity and hope. With your vote, I’ll remain a voice for our shared values of equality, equity, and opportunity.

Endorsed by: Planned Parenthood, State Labor Council, environmental groups, teachers, nurses, Executive Somers, legislative colleagues, local elected leaders and more!




Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, State Senator. Accessed 2026-10-10. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
