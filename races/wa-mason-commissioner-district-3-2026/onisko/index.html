Statement
On the Shelton City Council, and as Mayor I have always placed a strong emphasis on fiscal responsibility, ensuring that we achieve balanced budgets annually while also maintaining a reserve fund that meets the needs of our community. I've overseen significant improvements in our infrastructure and roadways and technology upgrades.

My involvement in city and county operations is hands-on, as I continually work to tackle the pressing housing shortage that we are experiencing. By partnering with state, federal, and local stakeholders, I advocate for sustainable growth that supports both residential and commercial development.

I have taken a strong position against the sale of harmful substances in convenience stores. My efforts to restrict access to products like kratom and nitrous oxide reflect my commitment to protecting the health and safety of our youth. I have made it a priority to testify before lawmakers to voice our community's concerns and push for effective solutions to various issues.

As your District 3 Commissioner, you can count on my dedication, honesty, integrity, and practical approach to governance. Having grown up in District 3 and successfully operated three businesses here, I am excited to embark on my fourth venture.

Thank You for your consideration
