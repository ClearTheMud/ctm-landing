1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

I serve as a council member for the City of Shelton.

Other Professional Experience

I taught Special Education and coached team sports.  I trained and managed volunteers for AmeriCorps.  I am a Community Organizer and directed youth camping programs.

Education

I earned a Bachelor of Arts degree in Political Science at UCLA in American and Comparative Government.  I acquired a Special Education credential and studied Political Philosophy.


Community Service

As a board member of the Rotary Club of Shelton, I believe in service above self. I also serve on the boards of Shelton Memorial Park and Community Lifeline.  I feed the hungry and welcome the stranger.

Statement

Dear Neighbors,


In real life, Mason County is different than the virtual world.  We are blessed with waters of the Puget Sound, evergreens of the Pacific Northwest, and outdoor opportunities to recreate with families, friends, and loved ones.


Every Christmas season, we rejoice under the Big Tree and wander through the maze of Christmas trees.  We enjoy restaurants, sweet shops, curio shops, and Farmers Markets.


It’s a wonderful life here in Mason County.  The future will be brighter if we choose leaders who will enhance our blessings and create a new world through Housing, Healthcare, and Hope.  We can transform our government into a meritocracy with opportunity for all: Youth empowerment, living wages, child care, elder care, affordable housing, and security from medical bankruptcy.


One day we will learn from our history and the rails of our past will become the trails to our future.  Water will be clean and plentiful.  Small businesses will thrive. We will have security in our golden years and youth will have a bright future in our diverse, equitable, and inclusive community. 


The American Dream is still alive in our hearts.  Will you help me manifest this dream into reality?


Peace, Love, & Justice,


Miguel
