1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (Primary Election 2026)

Elected Experience:
I serve on the Pioneer School District and Mason County Transit Boards of Directors. I have formerly served on The Board of Directors for Franklin County Emergency Management and Mosquito Control District.

Other Professional Experience:

I retired from the Cascade Irrigation District as the District Manager. Previously I worked for Badger Mountain irrigation District as Operations Manager and the City of Kahlotus as Public Works Superintendent.

Education:
I graduated from Shelton High School in 1987 and attended SPSCC with studies in math and electronics.

Community Service:
10 Years as a Volunteer Firefighter Little League Baseball coach, Member of Mesa Planning Commission, Bridge Community Church Deacon

Statement:
I come from a long line of Mason County residents; my great, great grandparents settled here in the late 1800’s. My roots in this area and the generations that follow me give me purpose. I am a 1987 high honors graduate from our very own Shelton High School. I moved back home to Mason County in 2017 after retirement.

I feel that I am a good candidate for Count Commissioner because I bring with me a diverse background in public works, public utilities, planning and development, budgetary experience, and people management skills.

I have worked with Federal, State, County, and City agencies, including Bureau of Reclamation, EPA, FDA, WA DOT, Emergency Management, County Fire, Health, and Sheriff's departments.

If elected, I promise to closely collaborate, integrate, and network with anyone and everyone needed for the common good of our Mason County residents. I want to concentrate on public safety including low-income housing and drug control. I will emphasize the importance of family unity and pride in our community. I support managed growth of infrastructure that invites job creating businesses.

Our children are the future of our community, and I strive to leave it to them better than it is now.

Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, County Commissioner District No. 3. Accessed 2026-07-16. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
