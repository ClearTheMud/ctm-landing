Statement:

This community is where I grew up and I’m raising my own family here. I’m dedicated to making this community a safe environment. I’ve witnessed there is a strong need for someone to stand up for what’s best for our community. I’m that person with a strong determination and passion. I’ll lead with integrity and passion for doing what’s right, and not just once in a while, but every single time! I understand the importance of protecting our community. I’ll make those hard decisions with ethical grace while upholding federal and state statutes, and making county functions more efficient.

My goal is to make sure Mason County has ethical representation that cares about the community, and understands that community includes all employees of Mason County as well. I understand elected positions are held to a higher standard. My occupations required a great deal of ethical behavior and professionalism, all of which I excel at.

As your County Commissioner of District 3 of Mason County, I will be the voice of common sense. I’ll reduce Mason County’s risk with training, proper background checks and policies for cost effectiveness. Thank you for your vote.
