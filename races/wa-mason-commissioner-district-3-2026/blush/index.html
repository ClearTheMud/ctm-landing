1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (General Election 2026)

Elected Experience

I am a dedicated Shelton City Council member, President of the Shelton Metropolitan Park Board and Chairman of the Mason County Housing Authority.


Other Professional Experience

For the last 8 years my family has run Nita’s of Shelton — the town’s oldest restaurant, established in 1952 — as our home base.


Education

I studied social work at SCCC Brentwood, NY in 2004 and at Olympic college from 2015-2017



Community Service

I am also the Executive Director of 5XL Shelton pet food bank and I serve as a Crossroads housing board member


Statement

With proven government, business, and nonprofit leadership experience and as the only candidate endorsed by the Mason County Republicans Party. I will deliver results as your County Commissioner in District 3.

I want a cleaner, safer, and more prosperous Mason County. I will unite Code Enforcement with the Sheriff’s Office to address illegal dumping, encampments, and blight — just as we successfully did in Shelton, restoring pride in our neighborhoods. I will also restore a strong Animal Control program to protect animals and our community.

To strengthen public safety, I will improve our roads and expand water access for firefighting, especially as wildfire risks increase. On the fiscal side, I am committed to ending wasteful spending and living within our means — the same common-sense discipline that has kept Shelton financially stable.

I have fought for a cleaner, safer, and more affordable Mason County by opposing garbage rate hikes, cracking down on crime and encampments, protecting free speech, and promoting fiscal responsibility.

Together, we can build a cleaner Safer Mason county for us all to live, work and play. Your support would mean a lot, thank you. Please vote for George Blush.
