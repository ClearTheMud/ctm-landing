1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

Two-term Washington State Senator; previously three-term State Representative.


Other Professional Experience

U.S. Army Veteran, Retired Spokane Police Officer, Detective, and former Police Guild Board of Directors. Jeff is currently an attorney.

Education

J.D. Gonzaga Law School; B.S. Psychology Washington State University; WA State Police Academy

Community Service

Jeff coached youth baseball, is a past board member of Spokane Services for the Deaf and Hard of Hearing, is a past board member of Spokane Neighborhood Action Partners (SNAP), is a Knights of Columbus Member, and is a board member of Law Enforcement & Fire Fighter Retirement system (LEOFF II).

Statement

Jeff Holy is a community leader who has dedicated his professional life to serving our region and will continue to deliver results as our State Senator.

In the State Legislature, Jeff fights for Eastern Washington! He has fought against costly policies that have hurt every family budget. He understands that more regulations on businesses will hurt our economic recovery.

The 22 years Jeff spent protecting our streets as a Spokane police officer and detective taught him the importance of public safety and being tough on crime. As an attorney, he stands strong for the law-abiding citizens of our district and will continue to fight for us as our Senator.

Jeff is committed to the West Plains wildfire recovery, promoting economic and workforce development for future generations.



Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, State Senator. Accessed 2026-07-16. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
