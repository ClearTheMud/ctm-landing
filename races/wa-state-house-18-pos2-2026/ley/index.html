1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

State Representative 2025 to present. Transportation Committee; Environment & Energy Committee; Post Secondary Education Committee; Joint Oregon-Washington Legislative Action Committee. State Committeeman, Clark County Republican Party. Air Line Pilots Association Board of Directors, Chairman Council 124.




Other Professional Experience

Reporter, Clark County Today. Captain Delta Air Lines; Federal Flight Deck Officer; ALPA Major Contingency Fund. United States Air Force Instructor Pilot and Flight Commander; Public Affairs Officer; small business owner.




Education

Portland State University, Bachelor of Science. Distinguished Graduate USAF Squadron Officer School; Air Command and Staff College; ROTC scholarship.



Community Service

Transportation and responsible spending activist; clean up Lacamas Lake water; attends Bethel Community Church.




Statement

It's time for positive change. End state income tax, reverse government employee pension theft. Restore parental control over children's education. Focus on improved learning, not administration.  Fund Brockmann staffing for mental health treatment. Lower cost of living by ending carbon tax. Affordable housing impossible with excessive property taxes; exempt $250,000 from taxation.  Make neighborhoods safer. Fund more police. Stop "catch and release". Support citizen initiatives.

No tolls. No $3.5 billion light rail. We need more Columbia River bridges to reduce traffic congestion and improve freight mobility. $2 billion builds bridge without tolls. Demand value for taxpayer dollars. Re-elect John Ley!
