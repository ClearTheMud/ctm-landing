1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

Working Laborer fighting for better policy for Clark County. 




Other Professional Experience

Assistant Political and Legislative Director, Washington/ Northern Idaho District Council of Laborers. Previous: Construction Laborer, Member of LiUNA Local 335. 




Education

After attending Clark Community College, I completed a Registered Apprenticeship Program and then started working on construction sites, eventually working my way up to a leadership position in my union. I advocate for career training programs because I personally know they are successful.

Community Service

Columbia River Mental Health Board Member 2025 - Present, C.L.U.B. Golf Board Member 2026 - Present, LiUNA 335 Executive Board Member 2020 - Present, Clark Neighbor Food Program 




Statement

As State Representative, I’ll focus on delivering results for Clark County. It’s time to build the bridge, build more housing and build more childcare facilities. I’m Pro-Choice, Pro-Worker and I’ll fight to lower costs of everyday basics. I’m endorsed by teachers, nurses, workers and leaders like Senator Cortes.  

I’ve lived here, worked on construction sites and raised a family in Clark County. I’ve talked to thousands of voters like yourself and I hear your frustration. If you believe, like I do, that hard work should lead to a good life, then I’d be honored to earn your vote.
