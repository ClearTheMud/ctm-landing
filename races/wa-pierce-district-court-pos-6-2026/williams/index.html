Statement
Judge Williams has had the privilege of serving the citizens of Pierce County for almost 30 years as a District Court Judge and District Court Judge Pro Tem.  Judge Williams has earned an exceptional reputation for fairness, integrity and knowledge of the law. 

Judge Williams currently presides over the Civil Department of Pierce County District Court. Many who come to Pierce County District Court are not represented by an attorney.  Judge Williams understands the unique challenges unrepresented litigants have and works hard to ensure every litigant receives fair and impartial justice.

Judge Williams understands the impacts mental health, addiction, and economic disparity have on the court system.   Judge Williams has a proven record of public safety, community service and protecting the rights of victims. Judge Williams is honored to serve the citizens of Pierce County. Thank you for your vote.
