1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience
No information submitted

Other Professional Experience
Appointed to the position of County Commissioner for District 3 this year. Mark retired from a 35-year career as an engineer of the Internet Service Provider/Telecom industry. During his tenure, Mark designed, administered and implemented a state-of-the-art fiber network infrastructure serving a significant portion of Wahkiakum County.


Education
Mark graduated from Granite High and attended SLC Community College.


Community Service

Since 2013, Mark serves Wahkiakum County Fire Department District #3 as a First Responder Fire Fighter, Fire Commissioner and Chair. Mark has also dedicated significant time serving various Councils for the Boy Scouts of America.



Statement
Mark Letham is a Wahkiakum County Commissioner for District #3. He has resided in Wahkiakum County for 19 years, during which time he has developed a genuine care for his community. His career as commissioner has been driven by a belief in transparency, collaboration, integrity, and the importance of listening to every resident.

Mark believes a brighter future can be built by supporting local initiatives, ensuring safety, and fostering growth that benefits everyone.

As a commissioner, he is committed to representing the residents' interests. His objective is to enhance the county's well-being and foster a community that residents are proud to call home.
