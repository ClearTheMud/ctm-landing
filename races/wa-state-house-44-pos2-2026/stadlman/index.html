1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience
PCO, 44th LD and Secretary, 44th Legislative District Republican Organization.

Other Professional Experience
30 years in Washington's housing industry, managing both affordable and conventional residential communities, including operations, multi-million dollar budgets, regulatory compliance, and team leadership. Decades of experience navigating the policies that shape housing affordability across our state.

Education
Institute for Integrative Nutrition, Certified Health and Wellness Coach; Certified Yoga Teacher; studies in holistic wellness supporting my work and personal understanding of addiction recovery and rehabilitation.

Community Service
Snohomish County Community Advisory Board member; Crime Free Housing trained; National Night Out participant; St. Jude Walk/Run committee member; Ronald McDonald House volunteer; Relay for Life organizer.

Statement
Washington families are doing everything right and still falling behind. Our top three issues, cost of living, public safety, and homelessness, share one thing: they're all driven by the laws Olympia passed. After 30 years in Washington's housing industry, I've seen what's gone wrong. Our state ranks 51st in police officers per capita. Families can't afford homes. Employers are leaving. I'll work for you to rebuild safer communities, lower taxes, and a business-friendly climate that keeps jobs local. I'll protect families, restore accountability, and reach across the aisle. I'd be honored to earn your vote.
