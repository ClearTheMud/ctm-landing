Statement

As a lifelong resident, my roots run deep across Pierce County. My grandparents raised their family in East Pierce, my parents met at White River, and both my wife and I grew up here. Today, our children attend the very same local schools and enjoy the same incredible natural settings that our family has cherished for generations. I am running to ensure a strong voice for everyone in District One, from suburban South Hill to the rural communities of Graham, Wilkeson, and Carbonado.

I have dedicated my career to assisting my neighbors, working directly in community settings providing behavioral health crisis response and critical resource coordination. My experience includes working collaboratively to facilitate countywide emergency planning for inclement weather, serving as a governance board member for the Pierce County Continuum of Care to address housing instability, and previously chairing the Puyallup Watershed Initiative. This hands-on experience has shown me that county government works best when we work together to deliver practical, localized solutions.

On the County Council, I will prioritize critical regional infrastructure. We must secure our utility grids, expand transit accessibility, and complete necessary sewer and storm water lines to manage growth responsibly. I am committed to improving public safety through proactive crisis diversion, balancing rapid economic development with strong environmental stewardship, and ensuring strict fiscal accountability for your hard-earned tax dollars.

Our district deserves representation that understands our unique history and is already doing the work on day one.



Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, Council - District No. 1. Accessed 2026-07-16. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
