Statement

Before anything else, I am a father to three children. Family is the center of my life, and it’s why I lead with accountability, respect, service, and a deep belief that government should meet people where they are. 

For more than a decade, I’ve served our community in roles that required leadership, transparency, and results. As a Bethel School Board Director, I have worked to improve community safety, expand access, and keep schools connected to the real needs of families, including safer sidewalks, stronger partnerships in unincorporated Pierce County, and opening healthcare clinics in our schools to bring services directly into a community too often overlooked and underinvested in by the county. In county government, I helped lead public health work that brought education, outreach, and cleaner indoor air strategies to schools, childcare settings, shelters, faith communities, and other shared spaces. My grandfather, a World War II veteran, taught me that a handshake meant something and your word was your bond. That lesson has stayed with me. I believe leadership should be rooted in honesty, follow-through, and a willingness to be present in the community, not just during election season, but every day the work calls for it. 

My priorities are safe communities, strong infrastructure, responsive government, affordable childcare, strong public schools, workforce development, and small business growth. 

I am running because Pierce County deserves leadership that puts people first, leads with integrity, and stays accountable to the public. I would be honored to earn your support and your vote.




Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, Council - District No. 1. Accessed 2026-07-16. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
