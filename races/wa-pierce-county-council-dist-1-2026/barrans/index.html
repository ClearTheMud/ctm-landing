1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (General Election 2026)

Occupation
Social worker for domestic violence advocacy, program management, and supporting student basic needs.

Education
Bachelor and Master of Social Work from the University of Washington.

Statement
I love calling District 1 home - its natural beauty, strong communities, and hard working people are the reasons my family and I live here. Like all of you, we see every day how the decisions made by the County Council impact our community. From access to services, to housing people can actually afford, to reliable roads. As District 1 grows, we need representation that will center our voices. That’s why I’m running for Pierce County Council.

As a social worker, I’ve advocated for survivors of domestic violence, supporting their access to services, safety, and stability. Through my work at a local community college, I’ve established programs that ensure students have access to food, housing, and the resources they need. And as your representative and Chair of the Pierce County Charter Review, I led the commission as we worked together to address the needs of community members from across the county by collaborating, finding common ground, and compromising.

I’m ready to bring the voices of District 1 to the County Council as your next councilmember. I will prioritize Council policies that make it easier for people to stay here, like affordable housing, access to care, and rural infrastructure. I’m committed to listening and learning from you, advocating for what will serve our district, and ensuring the Council is doing what’s best for Pierce County. I’d be honored to earn your vote to represent District 1 on the Pierce County Council. 

It’s time we find common ground for the common good.

Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, Council - District No. 1. Accessed 2026-10-10. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
