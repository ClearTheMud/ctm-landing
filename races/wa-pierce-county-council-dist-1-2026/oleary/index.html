1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (General Election 2026)

Occupation

Education

Statement
Pierce County isn’t just where I live, it’s my home. It’s where I was born, raised, built my businesses, and where my wife and I raise our daughter on our multi-generational family Christmas tree farm. It’s also where I’ve proudly served our community for decades through civic organizations, county advisory commissions, and most recently, as an elected member of the Charter Review Commission from District 1.

With more than 30 years of experience in real estate, construction, and business, I’ve seen firsthand how local decisions affect everyday lives. I’ve worked alongside families, farmers, business owners, and public agencies to find practical solutions to real problems. I know how to listen, bring people together, and deliver results.

Pierce County faces serious challenges: rising housing costs, strained infrastructure, public safety concerns, homelessness, and the need for responsible, long-term stewardship of taxpayer dollars. I’ll bring a practical, business-minded, community-first approach to the Council. Government should be efficient, accountable, and grounded in real-world perspective. I will always put people ahead of partisan politics and keep decision-making focused on the people who live and work here.

I believe leadership requires action, accountability, and meaningful results. I’m not running to become a career politician. I’m running because I care deeply about this community and believe that the skills and experience I’ve spent a lifetime building can be used to serve it well. It would be a true honor to earn your trust, your support, and your vote.

Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, Council - District No. 1. Accessed 2026-10-10. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
