1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (General Election 2026)

Elected Experience

Washington State Representative, 29th Legislative District (four terms); Deputy Majority Floor Leader, Chair of the Black Members Caucus and Members of Color Caucus, and Co-Chair of the Social Equity in Cannabis Task Force. School Board Director, Franklin Pierce Schools.




Other Professional Experience

U.S. Army Veteran, Landlord/Tenant Coordinator, Mortgage Banker, Diversity Trainer, and Domestic Violence; Sexual Assault Advocate.

 


Education

Saint Martin’s University, B.A. Psychology, 1995.




Community Service

Commissioner, Pierce County Housing Authority; Board Member, Community Healthcare, Member, City of Tacoma, MLK Planning Committee; Volunteer, Homeless Feeding Program, Altheimer Memorial Church.



Statement

For 34 years, I have called Pierce County home. I’ve fought for working families, seniors, people with disabilities, healthcare, public safety, and economic opportunity. I have supported legislation and appropriations that benefit the 29th; capped rental increases, reformed zoning laws for affordable housing, supported small business, and made public safety investments. 

I’m unbought and unbossed. I believe leadership requires values, judgment, and independent thinking — not political pressure or ideology. I build coalitions and make thoughtful decisions guided by the people I represent. I will continue fighting for equity, public safety, healthcare, housing, and opportunities for everyone in our district.




Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, State Representative Pos. 1. Accessed 2026-10-10. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
