Statement

As the daughter of immigrants and a mother to two young children,  I see clearly the challenges we are facing. No family should have to choose between rent and putting food on the table. I’m running to bring new leadership to the 29th LD that is focused on our shared values and ensuring everyone can thrive.  In Olympia,  I’ll advocate for workers rights, fair taxation, reproductive freedom, and protecting immigrant communities.
