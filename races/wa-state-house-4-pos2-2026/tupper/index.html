1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

First time running for office. Not a career politician.

Other Professional Experience

For 20 years I solved complex construction and technology problems, uniting experts and stakeholders to find practical solutions to complex issues. Another 10 years as an educator, with summers commercial salmon fishing in Alaska. Experiences which taught me accountability, teamwork and persistence. I will bring the same common-sense leadership to public office, focusing on results, not grandstanding.

Education

Bachelor's in Mathematics, University of Alaska-Fairbanks. Emphasis in Mechanical, Electrical, and Chemical Engineering.  Masters in Secondary School Education, Whitworth University.


Community Service

Advocate and volunteer with World Relief. Episcopal Church Board Member. Community Garden Leader.


Statement

Like you, I am exhausted by finger-pointing in the news. Problem-solving requires practical, rational, bipartisan cooperation to address homelessness, gangs, and drugs facing us statewide. Community-oriented police service, coordinated with social services, is vital to address these issues.  In Olympia, I’ll draw upon my work and life experience to guide legislation towards addressing very complex problems. I will seek solutions grounded in research while remembering Christian compassion. 

I’ll push for improving quality of life in Eastern Washington: good skill-building jobs, quality education and healthcare, reasonable housing, safer communities, never forgetting responsible spending balanced against revenue. Visit RationalRobLD4.org for more information.
