Statement
As Spokane County Treasurer and State Representative, I have worked with members of both parties to pass value-added legislation. As Treasurer, I spearheaded legislation to allow partial payments of any amount at any time and eliminated usurious late fees. I sponsored three bills this last session that passed unanimously. I also passed a bill on the House Floor that would preserve both salmon and dams along the Snake River. I had two wonderful mentors, the late Senator Bob McCaslin and Duane Alton. I believe it is wrong for our generation to leave posterity a world that is worse than ours. 

Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, State Representative Pos. 2. Accessed 2026-10-10. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
