Statement

I’m Jillian England, running for State Senate in LD 36 to restore fiscal sanity and constitutional government in Olympia.

Years of one-party rule have driven residents and employers out of Seattle with excessive taxes and heavy regulations. My opponent claims to be fixing our state’s regressive tax code, yet she ignores the most burdensome taxes—our high sales tax and the cascading Business and Occupation (B&O) tax. Instead, she sponsored the 9.9% “Millionaires Tax,” which opens the door to broader income taxation on working families. Repealing these taxes is my top priority.

I will also work to repeal the costly Climate Commitment Act, which has added 50–65 cents per gallon to gas and increased home energy bills for Seattle households and small businesses. I support an all-of-the-above energy strategy—accelerating nuclear power, expanding natural gas, and preserving reliable baseload sources—to keep electricity affordable and protect jobs.

On homelessness and education, I demand accountability. We must replace failed approaches with practical solutions that clear encampments, restore public safety in our neighborhoods, and ensure our schools educate students rather than indoctrinate them. I will fight to cut taxes and reduce the regulatory burden pushing families and jobs out of Washington.
