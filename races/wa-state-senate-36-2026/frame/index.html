1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

State Senator, 2023-present; State Representative, 2016-2023; Vice Chair of Finance, Ways & Means Committee; Vice Chair, Human Service Committee; Member, Juvenile Justice Partnership Council; Past Co-Chair, Children & Youth Behavioral Health Work Group. 


Other Professional Experience

Managing Partner/Small Business Owner, Uncommon Bridges (2016-present); housing provider (2025-present); non-profit director (2010-2015); management & communications consultant (2005-2010)

Education

Bachelor’s in Political Communication, Master’s in Political Management, George Washington University, Washington, DC

Community Service

Current member of local elementary school Parent Teacher Association (PTA), and past PTA member at Ballard and Garfield High Schools, and Hamilton International Middle School while a foster parent/caregiver to extended family (parts of 2005-2015).

Statement

It has been a privilege to represent the people of the 36th District for the past 10 years. I’ve been honored 15 times for my advocacy for individuals with developmental disabilities and behavioral health challenges, and youth involved in the foster care and juvenile criminal legal systems, as well as for my work on affordable housing, economic development and rebalancing the state’s regressive tax code. I would be honored to earn your vote.

The affordability crisis is hitting our district hard and is being exacerbated by the reckless actions of the Trump Administration. As a tax policy leader, I helped pass the Millionaires Tax to fund our public schools, healthcare, human services and early learning, as well as key affordability policies like expanding the working families tax credit (460,000 new households) and senior property tax exemption program (50,000 new households), establishing a low-income energy assistance program, historic investments in affordable housing, and exempting key household items from sales tax. If given the opportunity to continue to serve, I’ll fight every day for the working people of our community.

I am proud to be endorsed by 36th District Democrats, Working Families Party, Washington State Labor Council AFL-CIO, and Washington Conservation Action.
