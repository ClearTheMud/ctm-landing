BLUF — Bottom Line Up Front

Mike Rogers is the presumptive Republican nominee for the 2026 Michigan US Senate race. A former FBI special agent and House Intelligence Committee chair (MI-08, 2001-2015), Rogers lost the 2024 Senate race to Elissa Slotkin by 0.33%. He has raised $7.6M and holds $4.2M cash on hand, supplemented by a $45M Senate Leadership Fund commitment — the largest single-state investment in SLF history.
