1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

State Senator since 2019. Chaired Joint Legislative Audit Review Committee: closing tax loopholes, evaluating programs to protect your tax dollars. Local Government Committee Chair: cutting red tape, building housing and childcare facilities. Chaired Agriculture and Natural Resources Committee: protecting our environment. Law and Justice Committee member: protecting domestic violence victims. Former Shoreline Deputy Mayor and City Councilmember.

Other Professional Experience

Former child welfare prosecutor protecting abused children; public defense attorney representing low-income community members.

Education

Western Washington University; UW Law School.

Community Service

Hate Crimes Task Force; Marine Resources Advisory Committee; Drought Task Force; River Governance Council; former Shoreline Neighborhood Association member; co-founded homeless youth nonprofit.

Statement

Washington deserves Democratic leaders who deliver on promises. My record shows I deliver.  As your Senator, I banned ICE facemasks, capped rent increases, led the fight to block AI-powered rent-raising schemes, ended sales taxes on nonprescription medicines and hygiene products, expanded low-income tax rebates for 460,000 additional households, and cut taxes on small businesses. I designed and spearheaded legislation that closed corporate tax loopholes, securing hundreds of millions in new funding for healthcare, K–12 schools, and childcare. 

After knocking on thousands of doors, I’ve heard you loud and clear: prices are out of control, and government needs to be more responsible with your tax dollars. I’ll audit spending, expand senior property-tax relief, stand up to ICE, and stop Trump chaos in our state. We must confront the crisis on Aurora with compassion and accountability by connecting people to mental health and addiction treatment services, instead of looking the other way. 

I hope I’ve earned your vote for reelection. 

Endorsed by Democrats: Our 32nd District Representative Lauren Davis, Congressman Adam Smith, Washington Senate Democrats, Lynnwood and Shoreline Mayors; Councilmembers from Lynnwood, Shoreline, Seattle, Edmonds; King and Snohomish County Councilmembers; many unions, childcare workers, Alliance for Gun Responsibility, Snohomish County Sheriff.
