Other Professional Experience

Entrepreneur and international humanitarian worker with experience across twenty countries, including conflict zones. Founder and Executive Director of Frontier Discovery Project and Inspire Arts Project. Owner and Operations Manager of Nova McBee LLC, 2018–present.

Education

Bachelor of Arts, Boise Bible College. Continuing study in public policy, economics, and community development.

Community Service

Led humanitarian outreach, field training, and literacy initiatives locally and internationally. Instructor and youth mentor for a local homeschool cooperative. Active in church ministry and community partnerships for over twenty years.

Statement

If you feel like you are working harder and getting less, you are not imagining it. Families across Washington are strained by rising costs, housing challenges, public safety concerns, and growing distrust in government. Too many neighbors feel ignored by one-party control that lacks legislative balance. I am running because the 32nd District deserves practical leadership focused on real-world results, not political theater.

For over two decades, I have worked in conflict zones and divided communities across twenty countries, building trust under pressure, reconciling enemies, and delivering results without bureaucratic infrastructure. That work built the judgment, integrity, and commitment to people Olympia needs.

I support responsible budgeting, safer neighborhoods, excellent public schools, parental involvement in education, and expanding pathways into skilled trades, vocational training, and small business growth.

I am a nonprofit founder, small business owner, father, and neighbor, not a career politician. As a longtime resident of Lynnwood and Edmonds, I will work to represent every part of the 32nd District with honesty, accessibility, and independent judgment. I believe Washington can become more affordable, accountable, and united again—a place where hard work still pays off. For endorsements see iramcbee.com
