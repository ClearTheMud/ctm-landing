1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience
State Representative 2011-present. Chairs the Technology, Economic Development and Veterans Committee. Shoreline Mayor and City Councilmember 2006-2009.

Other Professional Experience
Allstate Insurance Neighborhood Agent; commercial property manager. Records Management and Systems Analyst, City of Seattle. Medicaid Intake Officer, University Hospital. Admitting Officer, Harborview Medical Center.

Education
UW-Seattle: MBA in Operations Management; Bachelor of Science in Microbiology.

Community Service
Boards - Shoreline Schools Career and Technical Education; Shoreline Parks Bond Advisory; UW Consulting and Business Development Center; Lynnwood Economic Development; Pacific Northwest Economic Region; Richmond Beach Community Association; Shoreline Chamber; Dollars for Scholars; Women in Government; FCC Intergovernmental Advisory. PC-USA Sunday School Teacher, Summer Feeding program.

Statement
It has truly been an honor serving as your State Representative. Now, I’m ready to bring my experience, proven results, and progressive values to the State Senate. I will continue to fight for a fairer tax system that asks the wealthiest to pay their share while providing relief to working families, those on fixed incomes, and helping small businesses succeed.

The contrast between the candidates in this race couldn’t be more stark. My record is one of action and standing up for Democratic values. I’ve championed legislation to protect workers from unfair non-competes, increased affordable housing—particularly for underrepresented communities —and worked to bring broadband access to every corner of our state. I will continue showing up, listening to you, and working tirelessly on our biggest challenges like affordability, job creation, and protecting our communities. That’s the difference in this race.

In the Senate, my priorities remain: Safeguarding the environment, investing in our public schools, and ensuring strong consumer protections against predatory practices. I’d be honored to earn your vote again.

Endorsements:  32nd and Snohomish County Democrats, SPEEA, Labor (WSLC), State Employees (WSFE), Washington Education Association (WEA), WA Housing Alliance, Nurses (WSNA), and former Representative Ruth Kagi, among many others.
