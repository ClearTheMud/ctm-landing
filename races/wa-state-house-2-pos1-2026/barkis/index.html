Statement

WA State Representative Andrew Barkis is seeking re-election after demonstrating steady leadership in the House of Representatives. As Ranking Member on the Transportation Committee, he has helped deliver fiscally responsible budgets and practical transportation solutions. A leading voice on housing, Andrew has supported legislation aimed at increasing housing availability and addressing affordability challenges. He continues to emphasize bipartisan cooperation while advocating for policies that support housing providers, small businesses, and residents. Andrew remains committed to representing the people of the 2nd District and working on solutions to the state’s most pressing issues.

Finding 7.1: Property Manager Voting on Landlord-Tenant and Housing Policy CRITICAL

What happened
Barkis owns Hometown Property Management, which manages approximately 1,200 rental units in Thurston County. He serves as Ranking Minority Member on the House housing committee, is the legislature's most vocal opponent of rent control, and sponsors landlord-tenant legislation. His NARPM certifications (CRMC, MPM, RMP) reflect industry alignment, and he has addressed NARPM members about landlord concerns. Policies he advocates for, opposing rent caps and increasing the supply of units, bear directly on the business he owns.
Source tier
T2
Political impact
Critical
Defense
Barkis has disclosed his business interests and argues his professional experience makes him qualified to shape housing policy. No formal ethics complaint has been filed.
