1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

3 years as Recording Secretary to Amalgamated Transit Union local 758, 1.5 years as Vice President of the Amalgamated Transit Union Legislative Council of Washington, 2 years as Recording Secretary to the Transit Togetherness Pride Caucus.



Other Professional Experience

6 years as an Operator at Pierce Transit, 3.5 years as a Dispatch Supervisor at Pierce Transit.  Communications Technician 5 years, 10 years as a line operator.



Education

Studied Non-Destructive Testing at Spartan School of Aeronautics and Software Engineering at University of Phoenix

Community Service

Union Shop Steward for 5 years, delegate to several labor councils, and worked with various non profits over the years.

Statement

We need representation focused on common people and the issues that we all face. As a Union Officer, I've fought to protect wages, jobs, safety, and access to affordable healthcare. In Olympia, I've lobbied to protect us from Corporate predatory pricing, to expand access to childcare, and protect the right to collectively bargain.  I'll find common sense solutions to lower healthcare costs, protect rural communities like ours, repair our failing infrastructure, and give every individual the chance to live a life of honor and dignity.

Together we can build a Washington that supports and protects it’s Rural and working families.
