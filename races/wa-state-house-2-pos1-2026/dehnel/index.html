1 Campaign Viability

Finding 1.1: Minimal Campaign Infrastructure — $1,345 Raised vs $56,850 Incumbent CRITICAL

What happened
Dehnel has raised only $1,345 against an incumbent with $56,850. His single expenditure of $1,000 went to Taproot Consulting. In a district that runs 60–65% Republican, this level of funding makes a competitive race virtually impossible. No endorsements from Democratic organizations have been identified.
Source tier
T1 (WA Public Disclosure Commission filings)
Political impact
A 42:1 fundraising disadvantage in a heavily Republican district leaves Dehnel with no viable path to a competitive general election campaign. Without organizational endorsements or significant fundraising momentum, this candidacy functions as a placeholder rather than a serious challenge.
Defense
Dehnel’s “Labor Democrat” branding and ATU union credentials provide organizational support beyond raw fundraising numbers.
