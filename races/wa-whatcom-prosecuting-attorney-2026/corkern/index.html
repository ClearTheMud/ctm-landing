Finding 6.1: Washington State Bar admonition in 2018 over failure to respond to a client complaint MODERATE

What happened
Corkern received a Washington State Bar Association admonition in 2018 after failing to reply in a timely manner to a grievance filed by a former client. The former client alleged that Corkern had not communicated about vacating the client's failure-to-register conviction. Corkern returned the client's money and accepted the admonition, characterizing the matter as a communication lapse rather than ethical misconduct. An admonition is the lowest level of formal WSBA discipline. The account was reported by My Bellingham Now / KPUG and Cascadia Daily News in their July 2026 candidate coverage. His current WSBA license status is active.

Defense
Corkern says the issue was a failure to respond to the grievance on time rather than substantive misconduct, that he refunded the client, and that he accepted the admonition. His bar license has remained active and he has practiced for nearly twenty years as a prosecutor, defense attorney, and civil litigator.
