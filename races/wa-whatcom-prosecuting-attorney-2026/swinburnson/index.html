Statement
Whatcom County deserves a Prosecutor focused on public safety, accountability, and restoring trust in the justice system.

Ryan Swinburnson began his legal career as a prosecutor and has spent more than 26 years handling serious criminal cases as both a prosecutor and defense attorney. That experience has given him a broad understanding of the criminal justice system and the leadership needed to improve public safety in Whatcom County.

Ryan has also served as civil counsel for two counties, advising elected officials and county departments on legal and public policy matters. He currently serves as President of the Blaine School Board, helping guide the district through difficult financial and community challenges with transparency and accountability.

Ryan is running for Whatcom County Prosecutor to restore experienced leadership to the Prosecutor’s Office, strengthen coordination with law enforcement, support victims of crime, and improve the efficiency of the justice system. He believes violent offenders must be held more accountable while recognizing that lasting public safety also requires meaningful treatment options for those struggling with addiction and mental illness. Ryan believes the people of Whatcom County deserve safer communities, timely justice, and a Prosecutor who leads with integrity, accountability, and service to the community.
