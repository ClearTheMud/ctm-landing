1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (General Election 2026)

Legal/Judicial Experience
Four years as a District Court Judge for Grays Harbor County, 30 years as an attorney, and 19 years in judicial service, including as a District Court Judge Pro Tem and Hoquiam Municipal Court Judge.

Other Professional Experience
Judicial administration, court leadership, and collaboration with justice system and community partners.

Education
Seattle University School of Law, J.D.; University of California, Davis, B.A.

Community Service
Longtime community involvement, including Judges in the Classroom, supporting education, accountability, and access to justice.

Serving as your District Court Judge has been a privilege and I am grateful for the trust you have placed in me. I am running for re

election to continue providing fair, efficient, and accessible justice for everyone in our community.

District Court is often the public’s first—-and sometimes only—-experience with the court system. I am committed to ensuring that every person who appears in court is treated with dignity and respect, and that each case is handled carefully and efficiently. Working with court staff, law enforcement, and community partners, I will continue focusing on improving public safety, supporting accountability, and connecting people with the services they need.

Even in an unopposed race, I see this election as a renewed commitment to public service. I will continue to uphold the rule of law, promote transparency, and modernize court processes while being a careful steward of public resources.

Thank you for the opportunity to serve as your District Court Judge. 

Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, District Court #1. Accessed 2026-10-10. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
