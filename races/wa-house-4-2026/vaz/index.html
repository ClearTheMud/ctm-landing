Community Service

My Christian faith has helped shape my values, including personal responsibility, compassion, and service to others. I believe strong communities are built when people actively support and invest in one another. I've spent time mentoring others in their professional development and volunteering through my local church and community.



Statement

I’m an engineer and entrepreneur running for Congress because I believe working Americans deserve leadership centered on results, accountability, and restoring economic opportunity.

I graduated from the University of Washington with a degree in Human Centered Design & Engineering and have worked across the technology industry, including roles supporting infrastructure and data systems at companies such as Amazon, Salesforce, and Nordstrom.

I’ve seen rising housing costs, inflation, and growing financial pressure across Washington state make it increasingly difficult for Americans to achieve stability and build a better future. Too many people feel like they are working harder while falling further behind.

As an engineer, I’ve seen firsthand how quickly technology and artificial intelligence are transforming our economy. Washington should continue leading in innovation while protecting workers, supporting domestic investment, and ensuring economic growth benefits American families and communities.

I believe government should prioritize responsible budgeting, public safety, protecting constitutional rights, affordable and reliable energy, and policies that encourage private-sector job growth and long-term economic stability.

Too often, politics has become more focused on division than delivering meaningful results for the people. I want to bring a problem-solving mindset to Congress grounded in practical solutions, transparency, and restoring trust in our institutions. My goal is simple: help strengthen our economy, support American workers and businesses, and ensure future generations have the opportunity to thrive.
