Finding 6.1: $60,000 Open Public Meetings Act Settlement as County Commissioner MODERATE

What happened
In fall 2022, Yakima County settled lawsuits alleging McKinney and fellow Commissioner LaDon Linde violated the state Open Public Meetings Act. The suits, filed in spring 2021 by county commissioner candidate Angie Girard and open-government advocate Arthur West, alleged more than 100 violations, including conducting county business by email and holding improper closed sessions while the commissioners also sat as the county board of health. The dispute stemmed from an ordinance the commissioners adopted that increased their power over the health board during disagreements with state COVID-19 mandates. Yakima County paid $60,000 to resolve the matter ($30,000 to West, $25,000 to Girard, $5,000 to Girard's attorney), and McKinney and Linde were required to complete OPMA training for local officials. A separate commissioner, Ron Anderson, settled for $5,500. The case was dismissed October 13, 2022.

Defense
McKinney did not admit liability. The settlement stated the payments and agreements "are not to be construed as any form of admission of liability." The county, not McKinney personally, paid the settlement and attorney fees.
