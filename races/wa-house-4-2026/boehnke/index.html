Finding 5.1: State Community College Employee Sitting on Higher Education and Budget Committees MODERATE

What happened
Boehnke is a tenured professor in the cybersecurity division at Columbia Basin College, a Washington state community college, while serving as a state senator on the Higher Education and Workforce Development Committee and the Ways and Means Committee. Those committees set policy and funding for the state community and technical college system that employs him. His salary as a legislator and as a public college instructor both derive from the state budget he helps write.
Source tier
T2
Political impact
Moderate
Defense
Boehnke's college employment is publicly disclosed and legislator-educators are common in Washington's part-time legislature. No ethics complaint has been filed, and committee membership does not by itself constitute a violation of state conflict-of-interest rules.
