Statement

From flying helicopters in the Army to answering the call at home, I’m a fourth-generation Tri-Citian who married my middle-school sweetheart and raised two sons here. I retired as a Lieutenant Colonel after 21 years on active duty, then came home to start a small business and founded Columbia Basin College’s cybersecurity program to train the workforce protecting critical infrastructure from growing digital threats.

In the State Senate, I’ve been outnumbered but never outworked. I’ve fought for my constituents every session. As Ranking Member on the Energy, Environment and Technology Committee, I’ve led the fight to protect our dams and hydropower, advance nuclear energy, and keep electricity affordable for the farmers, food processors, manufacturers, and families our economy depends on.

I’ve fought against tax increases and regulations that treat agriculture as an obstacle instead of the backbone of Central Washington. Whether in the minority or majority, I say what I mean and work hard to deliver results.

In Congress, I’ll represent Central Washington, not D.C. insiders. I’ll work to secure our border, stop fentanyl pouring into our communities, support veterans, seniors, and small businesses, and defend the affordable, reliable energy America depends on. 

Hard work, faith, and family built this district, and those values deserve a stronger voice in Congress. I’m backed by leaders who know this district best: former WA-04 Congressman Doc Hastings, 16 state legislators, county commissioners, mayors, city councilmembers, and community leaders across all eight counties.

You deserve a Congressman who has spent a lifetime serving our community, with 21 years in uniform and eight years in the legislature. I’m not done serving Central Washington, and I’d be honored to earn your vote.

Finding 6.1: State Community College Employee Sitting on Higher Education and Budget Committees MODERATE

What happened
Boehnke is a tenured professor in the cybersecurity division at Columbia Basin College, a Washington state community college, while serving as a state senator on the Higher Education and Workforce Development Committee and the Ways and Means Committee. Those committees set policy and funding for the state community and technical college system that employs him. His salary as a legislator and as a public college instructor both derive from the state budget he helps write.
Source tier
T2
Political impact
Moderate
Defense
Boehnke's college employment is publicly disclosed and legislator-educators are common in Washington's part-time legislature. No ethics complaint has been filed, and committee membership does not by itself constitute a violation of state conflict-of-interest rules.
