Statement

I am running for Congress because I believe our Constitution and civil rights are being violated by our President, and I could not stand by and just watch anymore.

I was born in Illinois to loving Mexican, Catholic, migrant farmworker parents. We moved with the agricultural seasons before my family settled in Yakima, Washington. I grew up working alongside my family in the orchards, where I learned the value of hard work, honesty, respect, faith, and perseverance. Those early lessons shaped who I am and motivated me to pursue my education and career in public service.

As a civil rights attorney, I have spent my career standing up for ordinary people against powerful institutions. As an attorney, I swore an oath to uphold and defend our constitution and the civil rights that come from it. More than ever we need to stand up for the rights that our constitution represents. Our constitution and our democracy affirms that we are all equal--that includes our President and other government representatives.

Washington’s 4th District needs common-sense solutions on water, agriculture, housing, healthcare, education, public safety, and economic opportunity. We do not need more partisan fighting. We need leaders who will listen to farmers, workers, families, veterans, business owners, and young people trying to build a future here.

I am an independent candidate because I do not answer to party bosses. I answer to the people of this district. I will work with anyone serious about solving problems in our community. Whether you are a Republican, Democrat, Independent or anything in between, I invite you to stand with me. We are better together.
