1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

My family and my home are in eastern Washington, for many decades, I have seen our friends and neighbors suffer from foolish governance. I have lived and worked in almost every county in the 4th.



Other Professional Experience

In Congress, I will fight for the farmer, rancher, small business owner, tradesperson, and laborer of every walk of life who yearn for a better life for our children.



Education

I know work, the trap of low wages, and a family’s everyday struggle against the shrinking dollar.



Community Service

With everything I have, I will fight to restore our American hope and dream for our children and grandchildren.



Statement

By restoring our American foundational principles of life, liberty, and property and our stewardship of clean air, water, and land, that are practical, we can still grow our economic base to secure our future. Armed with a broad background of experience and education, I possess a high capacity to influence the positive change we all hope for and need. 

First, I offer years of hands-on farming and ranching experience. We lost our family business due to a state that favored the large corporation over the rancher. Second, I spent years studying and practicing law. I am trained in legal writing, analysis, and the practice of law. We must not allow our judges to be politically seduced; they must follow the law and grant equal justice for all. Third, I have spent years studying and applying Chemical Engineering. I worked at Hanford to contain nuclear waste so it could be vitrified. I also combined my legal and technical backgrounds and became an inventor’s attorney. Currently, I serve the Community Health Plan of Washington in helping Medicaid and Medicare patients reduce out-of-pocket expenses.  

I know the struggles of the student working to get through school and then face student loan debt. I understand the difficulty of parents trying to pay their bills and to raise a family on a fixed income, confronted with the high cost of housing and vehicles. I have experienced the difficulty in making a small business survive against all odds. Lastly, I know the limited options seniors face when the insurance company will not pay the bill, and the collectors come knocking.  

I will contend for family, church and community. I will fight for the balance of power, the reduction of national debt, an effective Vo-Tech program, and stimulation of small businesses using innovation and tax breaks. 



Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, U.S. Representative. Accessed 2026-07-16. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
