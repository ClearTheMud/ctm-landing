1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

Current Toppenish Mayor and council member (2018). Candidate for U.S. Congress in Washington’s 4th Congressional District. Former candidate for Yakima County Commissioner District 3.

Other Professional Experience

Dental Hygienist and coordinator, farmworker, legislator and public speaker. Education and parental rights advocate. Bi-vocational minister and church leader.

Education

BAS in Dental Hygiene; Master of Business Administration with a concentration in Public Health Admin at EWU; Ordained Christian Minister. 


Community Service

Dedicated 28 years of public service, committed to supporting working families, lowering taxes, public safety, economic growth, and government accountability. Strong and effective state and federal advocate for community projects and funding for local opportunities.

Statement

Dear Voters, I humbly ask for your vote and your trust. Unlike many in politics today, I am not focused on chasing high-profile endorsements or relying on massive financial backing. Public service was never meant to belong only to the highest bidder. Yet too often, that is exactly what Americans are witnessing, leaving you and I, frustrated and concerned about the direction of our nation.

Across our communities, families are struggling under the weight of rising costs. Food, fuel, healthcare, education, insurance, and taxes continue to increase with little relief in sight. Homeownership is becoming unattainable for many, while small businesses are pushed aside by large corporations. Parents are increasingly concerned about declining academic performance and the erosion of their role in their children’s education. These challenges demand leadership that puts We the People first.

You deserve a representative who will speak boldly, act responsibly, and fight for common-sense solutions in Washington, D.C. I will not be silenced or pressured by political establishments. Washington State’s 4th Congressional District deserves strong and independent leadership.

I am committed to securing funding for local infrastructure, public safety, and community development projects. I support affordable healthcare, housing opportunities, clean water, renewable energy, economic growth, tribal relations, immigration reform, and expanding employment opportunities across all sectors.

My mom Patty, immigrated from Mexico. She taught us to  honor God, family, and our nation. She raised her children to work hard as farmworkers first, and then to serve the public. We have US veterans, law enforcement officers, firefighters, medical and dental professionals, educators, small business owners, and entrepreneurs. Unity and common sense guides my decisions, and with your support, we can send a clear message: government should be Powered by the People, Not the Purse. God Bless You and God Bless America! 

Thank you for your support.
