Statement

We're navigating challenging times nationally. Political polarization has escalated for years. Increasing instability abroad. A ballooning national debt. Impacts of AI. Wealth inequality expanding; everyday costs rising.

Not all these challenges stem from corruption in DC, but our pay-to-play political system has allowed these problems to fester for too long. In my lifetime, I've watched my government waste trillions on pointless wars, bail out Wall Street criminals with taxpayer funds, give lavish tax breaks to the wealthiest Americans, and turn a blind eye to endless slaughter in places like Gaza.

These failures demand accountability and a new generation of leadership.

Beyond anti-corruption reforms - stock trading ban, term limits, campaign finance reform - my focus would remain fixed on our regional needs. Investments to stabilize struggling farms. Range riding programs to build a skilled workforce for livestock protection and border security. Clean, decentralized energy production. Robust wildfire management programs. Sensible and dignified immigration policy. These are investments in long-term prosperity for Central Washington, not simply donor favors.

CD4 has been represented by a Republican for 44 of the last 46 years. The result: the poorest district in Washington, 1400 farms lost in 15 years, and cuts to healthcare programs residents depend on. But voters east of the mountains remain wary of Democrats, too often ignored by Olympia. In our two-party system, voters must choose between a party that has failed them and one that treats them as an afterthought.

I grew up in Ferry County, built a career, and returned to Eastern Washington because this is home. Neither party has truly earned this district's trust; someone has to try something different. That's why I entered this race as an independent before proudly accepting the Cascade Party's nomination. They believe what I believe: CD4 deserves better. We can build it together.
