Statement
I have proudly called the Tri-Cities home for over twenty-eight years. My American journey began in San Francisco, where I witnessed both vibrant opportunities and real urban challenges. Moving to Washington strengthened my appreciation for the ideal balance of innovation, hard work, and tight-knit community that defines our region — values that continue to guide me. The Tri-Cities stands as a remarkable place built on agriculture, energy, science, and the spirit of American ingenuity. From our farms and orchards to the groundbreaking research at Pacific Northwest National Laboratory, this community reflects our nation’s strength and potential. I earned my mechanical engineering degree from Washington State University. Yet I am concerned that America is losing its global edge in science and innovation. The United States is no longer the undisputed leader in cancer research — China has overtaken us in research output and clinical trials. We are falling behind in critical scientific fields, and too many of our schools are not preparing students for world-class competition. Instead of leading the world upward, we risk racing to the bottom — and right now, we appear to be winning that race. I came to the United States seeking liberty, opportunity, and a better future. I have a belief in capitalism, personal responsibility, individual liberty, and the Constitutional principles that make America exceptional. Today, I remain committed to preserving the freedoms and opportunities that define our nation. I believe the government must serve the people, protect our God-given rights, and empower hardworking families. Our nation thrives when citizens stay engaged and work together. My goal is to deliver practical solutions, robust economic growth, real accountability, and respect for the Constitution — so we can reverse this decline, restore American leadership in science and education, and secure the American Dream for our children and grandchildren.
