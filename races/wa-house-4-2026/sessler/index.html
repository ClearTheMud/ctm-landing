Finding 7.1: Said a Devout Muslim Should Not Serve in Congress HIGH

What happened
In a September 2024 interview with the Yakima Herald-Republic, Sessler said 'There is no way that a devout Muslim should be in Congress, because they can't take the oath of office,' and described the Muslim belief system as anti-American, citing Quran passages about killing non-believers. The remarks drew national coverage and criticism from the Washington chapter of the Council on American-Islamic Relations. Sessler doubled down rather than retract. Muslim Americans have taken the oath of office and served in Congress.
Source tier
T2
Political impact
High
Defense
Sessler frames the comments as concern about specific religious texts rather than opposition to Muslims serving generally, and says Muslims are welcome in the United States.

Finding 7.2: Accused of Threatening a Benton County Code Enforcement Officer MODERATE

What happened
In 2022 a Benton County code enforcement officer visited Sessler's property to investigate a report that someone was living in a house still under construction. According to the Tri-City Herald, Sessler told the officer he would get his gun and 'deal with him' if the officer returned. Sessler was not charged and no police report was filed, but the all-Republican Benton County Board of Commissioners sent him a warning letter over the incident.

T2
Political impact

Defense
No charges were filed and no formal police report resulted. Sessler has disputed the characterization of the exchange.

Finding 7.3: Promoted False 2020 Election Claims and a Debunked Ballot-Fraud Video MODERATE

What happened
Sessler has publicly promoted the false claim that Donald Trump won the 2020 presidential election. During the 2024 campaign he shared a video purporting to show how fraudulent ballots could be created; the video omitted that Washington checks voter information against the state voter roll. The Secretary of State's office responded that a ballot returned with fake voter registration information would not be counted and is illegal in Washington. He was in Washington, D.C. on January 6, 2021, and has described the Capitol riot as an FBI-orchestrated setup by paid agitators, while stating he did not enter the Capitol.

Defense
Sessler maintains he never entered the Capitol on January 6 and frames his statements as raising election-integrity concerns.
