Candidate Statement

I am honored to serve as your Pacific County Superior Court Clerk and ask for your continued support. Since joining Pacific County in 2020, I have built my career within the court system—first in Court Administration and now as your elected Clerk. This experience has given me a strong understanding of court operations and the vital role the Clerk’s Office plays in maintaining records, supporting the judiciary, and serving the public. As Clerk, I am committed to transparency, accessibility, and efficiency. I have worked to improve communication, expand access to information, and ensure our office operates with professionalism and integrity. I also serve as the Southwest District Representative for Washington State Association of County Clerks, collaborating with clerks statewide to strengthen our courts. Beyond the courthouse, I serve on the South Bend School Foundation and coach youth basketball. Pacific County is my home, and I would be honored to continue serving our community as your Clerk.

Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, County Clerk. Accessed 2026-07-16. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
