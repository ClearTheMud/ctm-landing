1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (General Election 2026)

Elected Experience
Klickitat County Assessor (2023-current)

Other Professional Experience
10 years’ experience with the Assessor’s office (2016-current), Washington State Department of Revenue Accredited Appraiser, Clean Energy Property Tax Impacts Task Force contributing member in cooperation with the Washington State Association of Counties.

Education
Graduate of Goldendale High School, AA in Business with a certificate in Accounting from Blue Mountain Community College - Pendleton, Oregon.

Community Service
4H leader for over 20 years, youth volleyball, basketball, and equestrian coach, Klickitat County Fair superintendent and committee chair, Klickitat County Cattlewomen’s Association Treasurer, American Legion Auxiliary member, Klickitat County Republican Women’s member, Contributor to The Giving Tree Festival.


Statement
As Assessor, I’ve worked towards fair and accurate assessments, increased transparency, and provided a better understanding of how property values and taxes are determined. Property assessment is complicated, and my experience ensures the office remains fair and trusted by the community. Property taxes are essential for local services, and it’s the Assessor’s role to make sure those taxes are shared fairly and based on reliable methods. The job also requires steady leadership in a role that isn’t always popular but extremely important. I’m proud to serve with experience and dedication to running a fair, consistent office the public can trust.

Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, County Assessor. Accessed 2026-10-10. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
