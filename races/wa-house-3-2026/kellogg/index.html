Statement
My “platform” is first getting real Republicans elected to office; second, nurturing a business friendly environment that brings and keeps good careers for our people; third, to “reset” our democracy back to normalcy - we are losing our way.  We would want the founding fathers to look down on us and say that the great, great, great grandkids got it right. Fourth, and this is a kicker, to bring UFO/UAP disclosure to people the world over.  We can do this.  Because my goal is disclosure, I have no intention at present of signing a Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) and being silenced by the DOD.  No, the truth of the cosmos belongs to all of us.  Instead, I would encourage others to blow the whistle and tell all under congressional protection, and I would be one of the enforcers like the country dentist with a pair of pliers.  

I want to see America thrive.  I do not support schemes about unproven election fraud as an excuse for disenfranchising voters.  I think that if Congress passes a law, then it must be complied with - release the files. .  Do you want a Democratic vote in Congress, or a Republican one?  Because if you wind up with a Democratic vote here and even worse, a Democratic majority in Congress, who knows what will happen, but will it be a Democratic reset to what they were doing?   Open borders?   Normalization of crime?  Free flow of illegal drugs?  Socialism, as in being taxed to oblivion?  Conversely, do you want a foolhardy right wing dictatorship dabbling in theocracy centered around our President?   Were we Republicans railroaded into a District Maga-frontrunner by the National Republican Party? Don’t you want a say in the matter?  I’m a Republican traditionalist, not Maga. Vote for me for the sake America.
