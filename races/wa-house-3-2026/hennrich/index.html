Statement

We all know the reality we are living in. Whether it’s gas, food, rent, or healthcare, prices keep rising. It’s getting harder and harder to scrape by. We need real change that puts working families on a strong path forward.

Insurance companies are gouging families for all they’re worth while denying care. No one should have to choose between buying prescriptions and paying rent. Let’s cap prescription drug prices, work towards universal healthcare, and protect Social Security.

Students are leaving school with tens of thousands of dollars in student debt. We want people to be educated, but we punish them for pursuing it. These predatory loans need to stop. We also need to bolster our K-12 schools. We have the money; we just need to invest it in our future rather than in pointless wars.

Our infrastructure is crumbling before our eyes. We need to replace the I-5 bridge and build up our rural healthcare infrastructure, broadband, and flood mitigation systems. Let’s invest in our communities and provide strong jobs and opportunities. That’s why I have kept my promise to have my printed campaign materials made right here in the district by union labor.

The Trump administration must be held accountable. They have gotten us involved in new, disastrous wars. They have refused to release the entirety of the Epstein files. ICE has been allowed to terrorize communities at will. The rich have robbed working families for decades, and it’s time they paid their fair share. I haven’t taken a dime from Corporate PAC money because I want to change things for you, not those with the deepest pockets.

Endorsed by College Democrats of Washington, Citizens Against AIPAC Corruption (Track AIPAC), Clark County Councilor Wil Fuentes, Centralia City Councilor Ray Chapman Wilson, IAM Local 695, Turn Left, Progressive Victory.
