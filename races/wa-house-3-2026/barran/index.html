1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (Primary Election 2026)

Elected Experience
Beyond once serving as a PCO, I have not held elected public office.

Other Professional Experience
I am an entrepreneur, business builder, and oyster farmer with more than thirty five years in marketing, strategy, hospitality, food, digital commerce, and small business leadership. I founded and grew companies, worked with national brands, created jobs, and help lead Willapa Wild and BCO Consulting.

Education
No formal degree.

Community Service
I serve on the PacMtn Workforce Development Board and volunteer with local high school students through hands on boatbuilding and sailing programs. Through Willapa Wild, I have helped advance eelgrass restoration, Olympia oyster recovery, and responsible aquaculture.

Statement
My campaign is built on a simple belief. Hard work should still mean something in America. For too many families in Southwest Washington, the math no longer works. People are working hard, playing by the rules, raising families, running small businesses, and still falling behind. Housing costs are up. Groceries are up. Energy is up. Wages have not kept pace. Meanwhile, politics has become louder, angrier, and less useful.

I am running because we need a different kind of leadership. Not more political theater. Not more insiders protecting insiders. Not more slogans from people who have never had to make payroll, fix a broken machine, deliver to a customer, or solve a problem with no easy answer.

I am an entrepreneur, oyster farmer, and business builder. I have spent my life creating jobs, building teams, serving customers, and turning ideas into real results. I know what it means to work through uncertainty, manage risk, and keep commitments.

My focus is practical. We need to rebuild the connection between work and opportunity. That means stronger local economies, better workforce training, support for small businesses, responsible natural resource policy, affordable energy, practical education, and leadership that listens before it lectures.

I believe most people are not extreme. They want safe communities, good schools, honest government, a fair shot, and leaders who are accessible whether someone donated or not, whether they agree or not. This campaign is not about left versus right. It is about whether Washington’s 3rd District sends another political insider to Congress, or someone who understands the real economy and the people who keep it running.

I am running to build a bridge to better. Better jobs. Better accountability. Better common sense. Better representation. Hard work built this country, and it should still build a future.

Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, U.S. Representative. Accessed 2026-07-16. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
