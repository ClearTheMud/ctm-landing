Statement

A Chehalis Bearcat (football+; 1971 St. Joes 1st-8th grade), of my dad’s ER trips (a 2022 ER doctor told me, “your dad never refused a patient.”), my 1st mental health job - Lewis County Casemanager/Group Facilitator for Independent Living Skills – naturally dovetailed off his surgical care.

Later encouraged to seek a Ph.D. in a larger arena, I moved to Cali where of an on-the-job car accident, I learned the soliloquy of all less fortunate, homeless, disabled.  The~ ¼ century to full recovery taught me much of gratitude.  I now work at the front lines, immersing in repairing of lives.

My employer~ Sea Mar, also:  ‘turns no one away.‘ Though the majority served are Caucasian, a Latino specialization, but serves all and begun by a migrant farmerworker’s son, UW grad (no endorsement sought~ nor implied). ICE~ remove criminals, leaches, predators. Cherish gems.  

That reveals my base~ in politics.  Instead of someone, ‘moving here~,’ who, ‘talks down,’ or, ‘at,’ people, thinking:  ‘I know what’s best.’  I am of & from here, and I speak, ‘Chehalin,’ (as I answered when asked at UW), my policies quintessentials- of someone homegrown in WA-03.

My 2022 return?  To deter thieves preying on our Centralia family farm:  No DEI in the US Army (Use Merit/Ability).  No extending Portland Light Rail over I-5 to WA.  No XY’s competing with XX’s. Of housing: ‘WPA 2.0.’ Of Iran War: Joe Kent is off- Trump overall, wants to prevent 9-11~ 2.0.

My 2010 lawmakers’ critique (“Civil Beat: Independence… John Roco”) of which my texting, email & sites, are hacked (altered or deleted, etc.), was the inception to my politics of righting hidden wrongs (“Hanabusa, Hirono Co-Sponsor Bill Threatening Closure of Catholic Charities,” etc.). Of aversion to written truths, ‘Defamation?’  ‘Enforced on the Streets~’ allegorical backdrop in:  “Hitman Struck 1.2.”
