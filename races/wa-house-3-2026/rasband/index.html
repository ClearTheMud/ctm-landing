Statement

I’m running for WA-03 to help build a community where everyone gets a fair shake, regardless of their background. I know what it’s like when your card gets declined at the register, when a family has to choose between rent and medicine. That’s not just my story; it’s the reality for too many families in WA-03.


I believe there is a better way forward. One grounded in compassionate, practical policies rather than the fear-based politics that have shaped our culture for too long. That means making healthcare more affordable so our communities can stay healthy, ensuring the wealthiest pay their fair share through measures like a wealth tax, and expanding programs such as SNAP so families can put food on the table.


It is my belief that the power of the elected office is about more than passing policy. It is also about using that position to stand with community members and support the ongoing fight for a more just country. If elected, I will use this office to advocate for you in any way I can. Our strength is in our unity, and the time has come to show it.
