1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

Southwest Washington's Representative in Congress since 2023. I serve on the House Appropriations Committee, including the Agriculture and Rural Development Subcommittee, working for small businesses, growers, and working families across Southwest Washington.

Other Professional Experience

Co-owner of Dean’s Car Care with my husband. We fix cars and create family wage jobs in the trades.

Education

B.A. Economics, Reed College. I was homeschooled by my mom and my dad, a lay pastor, before enrolling in public schools.

Community Service

Hillsboro High School Automotive Advisory Committee, Stevenson Downtown Association Treasurer, Underwood Soil and Water Conservation District Supervisor.

Statement

Most members of Congress are millionaires completely out of touch with life in the real world. I co-own an auto repair and machine shop, and I'm a mom raising a young kid. My husband and I built our own home in rural Skamania County to save money. I know what it costs to fill a tank, a prescription, and meet payroll.

I’m taking on a broken DC that runs on corporate cash and corruption. I don't take corporate PAC money, and I've introduced legislation to ban congressional stock trading and freeze congressional pay. I fight for the people who build, fix, and grow things: good jobs that don't require a four-year degree, more apprenticeships, and stronger support for trades, timber, and manufacturing.

When DC hurts working families, I push back. The President's wars and tariffs have raised costs and hammered small businesses and manufacturers. I'll stand up to any politician, or any party, when their decision isn't right for Southwest Washington. 

I'm cutting red tape to build more homes and lower rents. I've protected funding for schools and roads and tackled the child care shortage. I'm fighting to protect healthcare, stop drug companies from blocking cheaper generics, and rebuild infrastructure. I've fought for your right to repair what you own, taken on corporate consolidation driving up prices, and protected forests and farmland.

I'm protecting your freedoms: a woman's right to make her own medical decisions and your right to vote by mail, which the President wants to take away. And I'll always fight for Medicare, Medicaid, Social Security, affordable prescriptions, and the nurses, caregivers, and rural hospitals our communities depend on.

I hold town halls in every county and answer to no party, no donor, and no agenda. I answer to you. I'd be honored to earn your vote.
