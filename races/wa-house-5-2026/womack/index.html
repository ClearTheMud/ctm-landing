Statement

I’ve lived in three Eastern Washington communities and spent months traveling our region holding Listening Sessions.  I’ve heard people’s hopes, dreams and fears.  Your priorities are now mine.  In Congress, I will bring my extensive experience building coalitions and leading large, complex organizations to work on our toughest problems. 

I will strive for an economy that works for all, not just the rich and powerful.  This starts with an income that supports families.  One full-time job should be enough to provide the basics of modern life - affordable housing, child care and healthcare.  It means access to a secure retirement and solving the looming Social Security funding shortfall.

I will aggressively pursue a national health policy that provides coverage for everyone, ensures rural and urban hospitals are sustainable, protects women’s bodily autonomy, invests in research, grows future healthcare workers, and focuses on prevention and well-being.

I commit to supporting agriculture, protecting farms and ranches, and promoting the next generation of farmers.  I will champion long-term, favorable trade agreements, and investment in crop and climate research.  I will push to advance water stewardship.

I will fight to protect everyone's rights and safety.  This includes ensuring Congress does its duty to provide robust oversight of the Executive Branch, and that our military is fully capable and focused on defense - not offense and forever wars. I will strive for comprehensive immigration reform that humanely secures our borders.  We should provide reasonable access for refugees and asylum seekers, and streamline citizenship pathways for law-abiding immigrants and those who arrived as children.

As your representative, you can trust me to listen to you, and work for you.  I will stay true to my core values of integrity, service and excellence.  Together, we can build a better future for all of us in Eastern Washington.
