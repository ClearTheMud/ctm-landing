1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

This is my first time running for office.

Other Professional Experience

Over past 11 years I have worked for the State of Washington. I am a full dues paying union member. I worked in restaurants prior to employment with the state. In one way or another, my work has always involved serving others.

Education

I spent way too much time taking community college classes. After my daughter was born I knew it was time to wrap it up. I received an Associates degree from North Seattle Community College, and then I graduated from WSU in 2014 with a BA in Business Administration.

No information submitted

Statement

Be better, and demand better!

Our representatives continually fail to protect our interests. They chose party over people by refusing to address the corruption, grifting, market manipulation, and insider trading by the Trump administration.

Elected officials should be limited to investing in index funds. If they insist on trading individual stocks a press release detailing their transactions needs to be released within 24 hours so we can all benefit. 

Universal Healthcare is affordable, the $3.8 trillion estimated cost is substantially less than the $4.9 trillion we currently pay. That $1.1 trillion excess is nearly equivalent to the $1.5 trillion cost of Social Security whose insolvency we're continuously threatened with. Dental care is health care. I will mandate dental coverage is included by every insurer receiving government funds. It's ridiculous that the average family plan costs the employer/worker $25,572.00, yet dental care is not covered, nor does it count towards deductibles.

Market monopolization by corporations we subsidize is a primary cause of the affordability crisis. We subsidize the four corporations controlling 80% of the meat market, and in return the ranchers/farmers receive less for their livestock while the price we pay skyrockets. They say "too much debt" for programs that benefit us, but can find $20 billion for both Argentina and Israel. The same applies to the $200 billion in yearly corporate welfare we provide, which could cover tuition at public universities for 10 million students.

Price fixing and domestic surveillance facilitated by AI must be stopped. We are humans with unalienable rights, not resources from which tech oligarchs can extract as much revenue as possible. Paying for new energy sources to power their data centers is not our burden! They shall no longer be allowed to manipulate, exploit, and divide us via unfettered access to our data and personal information.
