Statement

I’m a progressive Democrat, a renter, and someone who understands firsthand how hard it has become for working people while billionaires and corporations keep getting richer. Washington’s 5th District deserves a representative who answers to the people — not corporate PACs, party insiders, or wealthy donors.

That’s why I refuse to take corporate PAC money.  My accountability is to you. 

I am running a grassroots, people-powered campaign to make life more affordable for working families. I support Medicare for All, universal childcare, stronger labor rights, and preventing private equity firms from buying single-family homes.

As a sustainability consultant and member of Spokane’s Climate Resilience and Sustainability Board, I’ve worked with small businesses, Tribes, and local communities across Eastern Washington to create practical solutions that lower costs and make our communities more resilient and livable. I support investments in local infrastructure that creates good-paying jobs while building a stronger future for Eastern Washington.

As your Congressman, I will hold the powerful accountable. That means banning congressional stock trading, overturning the Supreme Court’s Citizen’s United decision, and bringing a new generation of leadership to Congress that is willing to challenge the status quo. This campaign is about economic fairness, political accountability, and giving ordinary people a stronger voice in government. We can build the future that we deserve, we just have to fight for it. 

I’m Kevin Fagan, and I’m asking for your vote.
