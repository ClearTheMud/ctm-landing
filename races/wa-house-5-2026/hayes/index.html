Statement

Are you tired of politics as usual in the USA?  I am!   As an Independent Candidate, I am ready to serve The People of Eastern Washington with honor and integrity.  You deserve a Representative that puts people before party, service before self-interest, and solutions before political games.  Let's get serious about making life better in Eastern Washington!  I served this country in uniform.  I cared for families as a physician.  I studied law at Gonzaga University because I believe Our Democracy depends the Rule of Law and respect for The Constitution.  My life has been shaped by service: in the Air Force, in the exam room, in the courtroom, and in the community.

Eastern Washington is a fantastic place to call home.  We must work hard to improve our hospitals and clinics as well as the cost of healthcare.  We must support the Whitman County Wheat Farmers.  We need to maintain the Colville National Forest and take action to ensure a clean Spokane River. We should promote the Walla Walla Wineries and recreation in the Selkirk Mountains.  We must always support our wonderful schools, colleges, and universities.  Most importantly, we should all stand up for our beliefs and come together to solve complex problems.  These are just some of the things that I will champion as your Congressman.  

I am not running to serve a political party.  Rather, I am running to serve The People of Eastern Washington. We can disagree without being disagreeable, stand firm in our beliefs without losing our common purpose, and solve real problems with courage.  Thank you for voting!
