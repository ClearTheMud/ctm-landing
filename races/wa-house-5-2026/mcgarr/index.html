Statement

America is being dragged down by partisanship. Our government's goals veer back and forth every few years, or it shuts down completely, because one party believes their razor-thin majority gives them a "mandate," while the other party believes its mission is to thwart them at every turn. Then reverse the process when the "minority" becomes the "majority" by gaining a few seats.

I understand the desire to give up on both of them, but we are a firmly established two-party system, so the only way to fix it is from the inside. That means electing Democrats who are focused not on "fighting" the Republican half of our country -- that will only swing this terrible pendulum in the other direction for a few years -- but who will work with them to find the best path forward to the more perfect union that we can and should be.

I may not be a conventional candidate, but I am a serious one. I recently retired from The Spokesman-Review after a career in fact-based journalism. My top goals are to restore the agencies that have been decimated by the current administration, and to reassert the oversight role of Congress; the president is merely the chief executive of the laws Congress passes. I have no agenda other than a return to bipartisanship (although I have some pretty nifty Big Ideas on my website). I will never vote to shut down the government. All that does is hold government workers -- and millions of regular Americans who depend on their service -- hostage.

I make one promise to everyone who reads this: I will represent you as I would want you to represent me, with honesty, integrity and civility, regardless of which political party either of us is in. And no hypocrisy.
