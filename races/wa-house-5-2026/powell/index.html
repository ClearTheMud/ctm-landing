Statement

Politicians and the billionaires who own them have rigged the economy against us. Eastern Washington deserves an independent voice who will fight to break the system designed to exploit our hard work. I’m a firefighter, Marine Corps veteran, and dad running to put power back into the hands of working people. Gas, groceries, and housing prices are too expensive, while wages are too low. The same corporate political parties that got us here won’t fix these problems. Party bosses and their corporate donors want us pointing fingers left and right so we don’t see who is picking our pockets.

Raised in Eastern Washington with values of hard work and public service, I enlisted after high school to serve this country, came home to fight fires, and I’m running to serve again. As a father raising a daughter with my wife, and as a firefighter, I know firsthand the challenges working families face, and how our government is failing us. The ER used as primary care, rent so high people live in garages, and groceries that cost $50 a bag.

I will fight to make life affordable again for Americans, end billionaire tax dodging, reduce waste to bring down our national debt, fight for every American to have access to healthcare and take on the insurance giants who profit while we suffer, ensure labor protections so the people who build this nation earn a wage that supports a family, end trade policies harming our farms and small businesses, and adopt foreign policy that will not undermine our reputation or economy. 

I’m running as an Independent to take back our government for working families and build a future no corporate donor, Democrat or Republican, can buy. Join us.

Endorsements: WA State Labor Council, Unrig Washington, Working Class Heroes Fund, more at www.NatePowellForCongress.com
