Statement

I have been honored to serve the people of Washington State and the United States of America much of my adult life. I was born in Spokane and have lived in Eastern Washington with my family for 11 years while on full-time active-duty in the Washington Air National Guard.  As an attorney, I know when the law is being followed and when it has been broken.  Upon election, I will immediately start leading progress toward real solutions and to hold those that have violated the law accountable.

This election is about people.  Our families are hurting, our children have not recovered from the long-term impacts of COVID, medical care is difficult to obtain and insurance is unaffordable for too many, suicide rates are high, drug use is a serious problem, the cost of living and housing are unaffordable for so many, and inflation is rising faster than incomes. Our families are working hard and still falling behind. 

Massive spending cuts in the “Big Beautiful Bill” take effect after this election. That means thousands of families in Eastern Washington will lose access to basic health care because of high premiums and potential closures of rural hospitals.  The tax cuts are targeted at millionaires and billionaires leaving scraps for everyone else while dramatically increasing our national debt.

But it’s not too late. This is our time.  Every person is important and needs to be given a real opportunity for success.  In Congress, I will represent you the way I represent my own family: if it’s good for our families, I’m for it; if it’s bad for our families, I’m against it.  I will do the right thing no matter what.  As your representative, I will fight for our families, our neighbors, and our nation.  I would be honored to have your vote.
