Statement

Make America Affordable Again is more than a slogan. It’s the heart and soul of too many struggling Americans just working to cover basic needs. From staples like gas, groceries and rent to healthcare, education and retirement; costs are exceeding the pay for a 40+ hour week. Kitchen table math on 2 incomes, minus baby sitting, and who can make it to soccer practice…? An economy that works for everyone means a Congresswoman who works for you. 

Ann Marie is the only candidate in this race that takes no PAC money whatsoever. Not One Penny Ever. Nope. Not corporate or special interest. No PACs - just People.

In her career, Ann Marie has saved dozens upon dozens of struggling small businesses through calculated budget cuts and inspired investments for expansion and growth. Our country doesn’t need larger invoices for programs that don’t work the way they should. We need restructure and investment in programs that fix problems and pave the way for opportunity and growth for our people. Not more cuts and loopholes for the billionaire class which both parties serve. The parties might not be wings of the same bird, but they are branches of the same bank!

This challenge is not right versus left. It is top versus bottom. Most of us agree on the issues. Supporting small sustainable farming; protecting small businesses; affordable healthcare and education for all; protecting our borders and welcoming hard-working immigrants; safe, legal abortion; building housing; solutions for drug addiction and homelessness; reducing street crime and white collar crimes and fortifying our Constitutional values.

Ann Marie understands what needs to be done and what is standing in the way of your American Dream. A vote 4 Ann Marie is a vote for you!
