1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Education: Bachelor's of Arts, Political Science, University of Notre Dame

Occupation: Former data analytics consultant; recent City Councilmember Policy Lead

Statement: I’m running for King County Council because too many residents feel that our region is becoming less affordable, less accountable, and less functional, all while government continues to frustrate willing taxpayers by asking for more and delivering too little in return.

King County families are being squeezed by rising housing costs, taxes, insurance, and everyday expenses. At the same time, public confidence has declined as we continue to see worsening homelessness, persistent property crime, struggling business districts, and major questions about oversight and spending.

I believe county government must return to its core responsibilities: accountability, affordability, public safety, and effective services.  As your councilmember, I will focus on stronger oversight of county departments and taxpayer dollars. Programs should be measured by outcomes, not political messaging. Residents deserve transparency, independent auditing, and leadership willing to challenge failing policies without clear results, even when they come from their own allies.

I also believe we need a more balanced approach to public safety and homelessness. Compassion matters deeply, but compassion without accountability is not a strategy. We must prioritize treatment, recovery, safer public spaces, and measurable performance standards for the hundreds of millions spent annually across our regional systems.

Finally, I will bring a practical, solutions-oriented perspective shaped by years of leadership experience managing large teams, budgets, and operational change in both the private and public sectors. I’m not running to protect the status quo. I’m running to help restore public trust and ensure King County remains a place where working families, businesses, and future generations can thrive.

We can build a King County that is both compassionate and accountable. That starts with putting measurable results ahead of politics.

Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, Metropolitan King County Council District No. 8. Accessed 2026-07-16. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
