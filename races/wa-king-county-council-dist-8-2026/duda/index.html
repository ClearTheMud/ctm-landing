1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (General Election 2026)

Education: Bachelor's of Arts, Political Science, University of Notre Dame

Occupation: Former data analytics consultant; recent City Councilmember Policy Lead

Statement: Everyone I talk with in our district wants the same things: safe streets, good schools, treatment for those who are suffering, clean air and water, an affordable place to raise a family or start a business, and a government responsive to its community.

As a lifelong Democrat, I share those values, but it’s clear we’re not delivering on them. This position has oversight over the public health crises on our streets and has major influence on fiscal policy.  Not using that authority effectively has resulted in our region now ranking among the nation’s worst for homelessness, overdose deaths, property crime, cost of living, and commercial sex trafficking. These results aren’t for lack of funding; we’ve consistently increased taxes and spent more on homelessness than almost any region in America, all while we've also seen declines in school enrollment, housing construction, office occupancy, and employment.  These are uncomfortable, but honest facts.

Our leaders often measure success by how much they spend, not how effectively those dollars are spent.  Ask yourself honestly: is our region better off than it was nine years ago?

My campaign is about improving our outcomes. That means public safety with real accountability. It means compassionate, treatment-required paths off the street for those struggling with addiction. It means building more housing by fixing permitting and reducing barriers to new construction, and public housing that is safe and stabilizing for our most vulnerable.  

Whether you're a young person priced out of the market, a parent worried about your kid’s future, or a longtime resident being taxed out of your home, our government should work for you, not for special interests and failed programs.

Progress isn’t just about spending more; progress is about delivering better results. I’m asking for your vote to deliver those results that meet our region's values.

Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, Metropolitan King County Council District No. 8. Accessed 2026-10-10. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
