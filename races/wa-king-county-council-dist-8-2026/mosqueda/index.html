1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (General Election 2026)

Education: Masters in Public Administration, focus in health policy, Evergreen State College; BA, University of Washington

Occupation: County Councilmember; labor champion, children’s advocate, public health policy leader

Statement: I’m running for re-election to the County Council to continue building a healthier, safer, more affordable King County where working families can thrive. At a time when costs are rising and many neighbors are struggling to stay housed, maintain access to care, and make ends meet - we need bold leadership that delivers real results for our communities.

I’ve spent my career fighting for workers, kiddos, and healthy communities. At the Children’s Alliance and the Washington State Labor Council, I helped win paid sick leave, minimum wage increases, and stronger protections for workers and immigrants. On the Seattle City Council and now at King County, I’ve built coalitions to expand affordable housing, strengthen worker protections, invest in behavioral health, and support small businesses and community safety.

At King County, I’ve worked to increase investments in affordable and supportive housing, improve behavioral health services, protect immigrant and refugee communities, and strengthen our public health infrastructure. I’ve also championed investments in transit, childcare, and workforce development to help families stay and succeed in our region.

My priorities remain expanding affordable housing and preventing displacement; improving community safety through behavioral health and gun violence prevention; and investing in working families with accessible transit, childcare, healthcare, and union job opportunities.

I believe King County works best when we lead with compassion, accountability, and commitment to opportunity for everyone – regardless of zip code, income, or background. Together, we can continue building a King County where every person has the opportunity to be healthy, housed, safe, and resilient.

Proudly endorsed by Congresswoman Jayapal; Governor Ferguson; AG Nick Brown; all endorsing unions including MLK Labor, SEIU locals, UFCW 3000, and Seattle Building Trades; 34th, 33rd, 11th District Dems; Progreso Latino Progress; Washington Conservation Action; WA Housing Alliance Action Fund; NW Women’s Political Caucus; and 200+ more!
