1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (Primary Election 2026)

Elected Experience

I was appointed in December 2017, giving me the opportunity to begin serving our
community. In 2018, I was elected to my first full term.

Other Professional Experience

After nearly a decade with Bank of the Pacific, I was hired as Accounts Payable with
Wahkiakum County in 2015. I was promoted to Accounting Manager and appointed as
Auditor in 2017.

Education

My education includes a high school diploma from Wahkiakum High School, Class of
1994. I am a certified Department of Licensing agent and a Certified Election
Administrator.

Community Service

Wahkiakum Chamber of Commerce, Dollars for Scholars, WAAG, and Lighted Holiday
Parade Committee.

Statement

As your County Auditor, my duties include administering elections, overseeing
recordings, managing Department of Licensing, and serving as a connection between
departments countywide. My ability to multitask is a valuable asset in ensuring these
responsibilities are handled efficiently and professionally. My office is trusted to perform
many important functions. Not only do we oversee a wide variety of financial and
administrative duties, but we also build strong relationships with community members.
Every interaction provides an opportunity to better serve our county and ensure
transparency, accuracy, and trust in local government.
Wahkiakum County is extremely important to me, and I am committed to continuing to
serve our community with dedication and integrity. I appreciate this opportunity and,
with your support, I would be honored to continue serving as your Wahkiakum County
Auditor.

Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, County Auditor. Accessed 2026-07-16. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
