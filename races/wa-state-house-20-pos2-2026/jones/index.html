1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

Port of Longview Commissioner, elected 2024. Led efforts to strengthen contractor accountability and worker safety in infrastructure projects. Chair, SW Washington Regional Transportation Planning Organization. Board Member, Cowlitz Economic Development Council. Board Member, Cowlitz-Wahkiakum Council of Governments.

Other Professional Experience

Heavy rigging and machinery tradesman, Omega Morgan. U.S. Army combat veteran, served overseas. Longtime resident of the 20th Legislative District.

Education

U.S. Army service and training. Continued education in the trades.

Community Service

Volunteered throughout my career - in uniform, in the trades, and in public service. State Veterans’ Representative, International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers. Active in the communities of this region.




Statement

Every morning I’m on the road before dawn commuting 110 miles because the work that should exist here in the 20th keeps going somewhere else. I’m Evan Jones: combat veteran, tradesman, Port Commissioner, and father. I came home from overseas, found my footing in the trades, and I’m raising my hand again because my community needs someone to deliver. Project after project. Thousands of family-wage jobs in timber, shipping, and construction. Lost. I’m going to Olympia to fix permitting, bring those projects and jobs home, and make sure every worker comes home whole.

I belong to the 20th. Not Olympia.
