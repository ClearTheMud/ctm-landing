1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

Granite Falls High School-Freshman Class President. A public servant, not a politician; offering a servant perspective grounded in frontline service. 



Other Professional Experience

I’ve served Washington—supporting teens in group homes, working in mental health, in schools across Washington, and in law enforcement as a parole officer in both the juvenile and adult systems. I’ve seen these struggles firsthand; and I’m stepping up to deliver the accountability and change our communities deserve.

Community Service

Statement

Please allow me to introduce myself.

When I drive through the 8th District, I don’t just see houses—I see memories. From Darrington to Lake Chelan, the Columbia, The Puyallup, wrestling at CWU, and growing up in Granite Falls, this district isn’t just lines on a map—it’s my heart. Born in Sunnyside, I come from the small towns and suburbs too often overlooked by politics. These aren’t just places to me—they’re who I am.

A public servant and former Parole Officer—I’m no politician. My agenda is built on service. Working in group homes for teenagers, supporting individuals with mental health and developmental disabilities, even serving at the very schools I once attended. My career has been spent on the frontlines of public safety and our community. I go where the problems are biggest, the stakes are highest, and the need is greatest; and stay until the job is done. I’ll usher in a new generation of principled, common sense leadership focused on Justice. Accountability. Affordability. 

That means bringing Federal prosecutions for violent gang and gun crimes. Defending the 2nd Amendment. It means restoring trust in government through federal audits. Permanently securing tax relief for seniors and working Americans. Investments in reliable energy and trades profession apprenticeships—creating real opportunities for generations to come. Securing the future of the 8th District.

I'm the underdog in this race—no establishment backing, no flashy ads, no big money. I bring the unconventional: authenticity, principles over party, and a voice that speaks for you. While others listen to megadonors and lobbyists, I’m listening to you. I represent you. I work for you!

If you want results—not political theater. Vote for a new generation of leadership that’s been tested. Grounded in service. Focused on our District. Valleza for Congress: Rooted. Tested. Leadership.
