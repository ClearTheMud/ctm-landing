Statement

Thirty years ago, my family arrived in America during election season. We had fled communist Vietnam, where my father spent seven years in hard labor camps, simply for standing with America during the Vietnam War.

When we arrived in the United States, we had nothing. But here, we found safety, kindness, and the chance to begin again. America gave me opportunities my parents could only dream of. I went on to earn my Ph.D. from UC Berkeley before working with the Department of Veterans Affairs, where I investigated fraud, expanded veterans’ access to education, and helped recover millions of dollars for those who served our country.

My family learned the hard way that freedom can be lost slowly, then all at once. That’s why I carry a deep gratitude for this nation and the conviction that freedom must never be taken for granted.

Families across Washington’s 8th District carry that same spirit. Veterans, farmers, small business owners, and parents are striving to build stable lives and to pass something better on to their children. Yet, too many families are putting in longer hours only to fall further behind. Those who play by the rules and contribute to their communities shouldn’t feel that gas, groceries, housing, childcare, and healthcare are slipping out of reach.

That’s why I’m running for Congress: to lower costs for families, support small businesses and farmers, strengthen public safety, secure our borders, restore focus on core academics, and bring politics back to the basics—faith, freedom, family, personal responsibility, and service to others.

The freedoms that welcomed my family to America are not guaranteed. Every generation must choose to protect them, strengthen them, and pass them on intact. That’s the responsibility of citizenship—and the calling that guides me. I’d be honored to have your vote.



Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, U.S. Representative. Accessed 2026-07-16. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
