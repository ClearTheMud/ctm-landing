Statement

As your U.S. Representative, I will be a tireless advocate for you and our communities here in Washington, committed to always putting you, your family, and America First.

People here at home are facing unprecedented challenges made worse by the high costs of housing, food, energy, healthcare, and other basic needs while politicians in the other Washington are obsessed with blaming each other and generating clicks and likes on social media. In Congress, I will focus exclusively upon making your life better and improving opportunities for you and our people here at home. Just as I have always done in private life and throughout my career, I will bring people together to solve important problems.

We need a strong voice of the people serving in Congress, someone who has a record of actually getting things done, not just another government career builder, political agitator, or activist trying to build name recognition. My successful career was built outside of The Swamp of government. We need innovative, free-market solutions proven in the real world. I will serve you and our people, take responsibility for solving problems, and deliver what I promise while always being available, accessible, and accountable to you...and never make excuses.

You deserve to keep more of your hard-earned money. You deserve safety and security. You deserve to be able to reach out and take hold of the American Dream. You deserve a better U.S. Representative. I will find common ground with others, build relationships, and take actions to make your life better. That is the job of a Congressman.

Endorsements include multiple Legislative District and County Party committees on both sides of the mountains.

Questions? Please visit my website, watch my videos, join my live events, call, text, or email me and let me answer all of your questions.
