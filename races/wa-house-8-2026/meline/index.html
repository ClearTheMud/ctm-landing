1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

No prior elected office.

Other Professional Experience

Small business owner and restaurateur in Leavenworth, Washington. Built multiple businesses from the ground up, creating jobs and helping drive the local economy. Managed employees, payroll, budgets, permitting, and daily operations while navigating the challenges facing small businesses in Washington. And has spent his career serving customers, supporting employees, and investing in the community.

Education

Completed coursework through Edmonds Community College while pursuing business and career opportunities.


Community Service

Partners with local organizations to help provide meals to the community through his business. Active supporter of local schools, youth activities, churches, and community events throughout the region.

Statement

I’m running for Congress because too many Washington families feel like the American Dream is slipping away. The cost of housing, groceries, fuel, insurance, and everyday life keeps rising. Small businesses are closing at alarming rates, and many people feel ignored by career politicians who have never experienced the challenges facing working families.

I’m honored to have earned the endorsements of both the King County Republican Party and the Snohomish County Republican Party. Their support reflects the growing momentum behind our campaign and our message of restoring opportunity for working families across Washington.

I’m not a politician. I’m a husband, father, and small business owner who built businesses from the ground up. I know what it means to meet payroll, manage rising costs, and work long hours to provide for a family. 

I believe we need leadership focused on lowering costs, strengthening small businesses, supporting family-wage jobs, protecting our communities, keeping our border secure, and responsibly managing our public lands for future generations.

I’m running to bring a real-world perspective to Congress and fight for the people who work hard, play by the rules, and want a fair chance to build a better future.
