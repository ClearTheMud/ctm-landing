1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience
1991 elected Klickitat County PUD District 3, Board of Commissioners. 1994 elected to the Executive Board Energy Northwest. EN owns and Operates Columbia Generating Station, a 1250-megawatt Nuclear Power Plant. Served on the Executive Board 1994-2014. Served as Chair of the Nuclear Oversight Committee 2006-2014.

Other Professional Experience
Grower, Packer, Shipper, of Cherries, Peaches, Apricots. Owner Gunkel Orchards Inc. a third-generation family farm.

Education
Graduate Goldendale High School 1974. Emory University Nuclear training for Nuclear Board members 2012. Institute Of Nuclear Power Operations CEO Conference 2006-2014.

Community Service
Pro Bono capital improvement projects at Maryhill Museum and Stonehenge.

Statement
Commissioner Gunkel’s highest priorities are reasonable electric rates, high reliability, and excellent customer service. If re-elected Dan will continue to serve in the same manner. KPUD rates were third highest when Commissioner Gunkel was elected and today, our rates have fallen to 10th place in our utility group. This took over 30 years of hard work to achieve. During Commissioner Gunkel’s term, KPUD built McNary Hydro generation, HW Hill Renewable Natural Gas, and Transmission facilities, that all generate income to offset your current rates. If re-elected Dan will help facilitate the completion of the pump storage project.

Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, Public Utility District #1 Commissioner Pos. 3. Accessed 2026-07-16. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
