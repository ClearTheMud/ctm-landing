1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (General Election 2026)

Elected Experience
I served three years on the board of directors for the Goldendale Rodeo Bible Camp, gaining experience in organizational governance and community oversight.

Other Professional Experience
I own and operates Siebert Custom Slaughter and Meats LLC, serving Klickitat County families, agricultural producers and dedicating ten years to 4-H youth development.

Education
I am a graduate of Goldendale High School.

Community Service
I serve as a Reserve Officer with the Goldendale Police Department where I had to pass a phycological and lie detector evaluation to meet higher community standards.   

Statement
I'm Logan Siebert, a 26-year Klickitat County resident, small business owner, and Reserve Goldendale Police Officer. As owner of Siebert Custom Slaughter and Meats LLC, I know how to manage budgets and be accountable to the people I serve. I've given back through 4-H, the Goldendale Rodeo Bible Camp, and its board of directors. I'm running because ratepayers deserve a commissioner who listens. Reliable power and fair rates affect every family and business in Klickitat County, including mine. I'm not a career politician. I'm your neighbor who will stand on truth and integrity. I humbly ask for your vote.

Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, Public Utility District #1 Commissioner Pos. 3. Accessed 2026-10-10. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
