Statement
We can all agree that oligarchy needs to go. Far too many oligarchs have been in office for decades making millions in personal wealth, while the people / peasants suffer. In Klickitat County we are surrounded by dams, windmills, and solar. So why do we rate 47 out of 61 for lowest rates in the state. The average bill in Washington is $119.71, ours is 20.31% higher at $148.83. Why? Failed projects, Inappropriate business dealings, lawsuits, and of course oligarchy. No “Kings” desire for personal wealth should come at the cost of the people / peasants. Vote to end oligarchy.
