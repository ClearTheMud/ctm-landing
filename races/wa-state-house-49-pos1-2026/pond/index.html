1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (Primary Election 2026)

Elected Experience

Democratic Precinct Officer; 49th LD Executive Officer.

Other Professional Experience

As an organizer with SEIU775, the home care workers union, I’ve sat at kitchen tables, walked picket lines, and helped workers build power to win improvements. Political organizing at Fuse Washington; communications and marketing at Clark College and ADCO Printing; retail management; UFCW555.

Education

Western Washington University, BA; Clark College, AA; Battle Ground School District; Leadership Clark County; WAIDF.

Community Service

Vancouver Charter Review; Labor Roundtable - Service Award; George Marshall Leadership - Finalist; Eagle Scout; Queer Community Network; Neighborhood Alliance; NAACP; Indivisible. Volunteer: Housing Alliance; Clark County Food Bank; Habitat for Humanity; Friends of Trees.

Statement

Families are being forced out of our community. Costs outpace wages, classrooms are overcrowded, and too many working people are one emergency from crisis. I support taxing the ultra-wealthy and mega corporations to lower taxes for families and small businesses, while investing in education, affordable housing, environmental protections, and pro-choice healthcare. We must replace the I-5 bridge with light-rail.

We deserve a representative who shows up with integrity, listens, collaborates, and delivers results. I will be that representative and humbly ask for your vote. Endorsed by: Washington Education Association, State Labor Council AFL-CIO, Mayor of Vancouver, Clark County Council Chair.

Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, State Representative Pos. 1. Accessed 2026-07-16. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
