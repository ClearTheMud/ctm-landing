1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

No elected experience.

Other Professional Experience

Board-certified psychiatric mental health nurse practitioner with 20+ years in emergency, behavioral health, addiction, homeless outreach care, healthcare leadership, and quality improvement.



Education

Doctor of Nursing Practice, Frontier Nursing University; MSN, Frontier Nursing University; BSN, Oregon Health & Science University; ADN, Clark College.



Community Service

Vice Chair, Oregon Nurses Foundation; Oregon Nurses Association leadership; Leadership Clark County; policy advocacy focused on nursing, workforce development, behavioral health, and practical community solutions.




Statement

Washington needs  practical, transparent, accountable government focused on affordable living, safe communities, and results families can feel.

As a psychiatric mental health nurse practitioner, nurse, mother, and community leader, I have spent my career helping people through crisis. In emergency rooms, behavioral health care, and community service, I have seen the cost of policies that sound good but fail in real life. I will bring a clinician’s discipline, a nurse’s compassion, and a mother’s common sense to Olympia. I will listen, work across differences, and be an independent voice delivering real solutions for Southwest Washington.
