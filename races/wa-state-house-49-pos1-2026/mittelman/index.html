Statement

Washington needs  practical, transparent, accountable government focused on affordable living, safe communities, and results families can feel.

As a psychiatric mental health nurse practitioner, nurse, mother, and community leader, I have spent my career helping people through crisis. In emergency rooms, behavioral health care, and community service, I have seen the cost of policies that sound good but fail in real life. I will bring a clinician’s discipline, a nurse’s compassion, and a mother’s common sense to Olympia. I will listen, work across differences, and be an independent voice delivering real solutions for Southwest Washington.
