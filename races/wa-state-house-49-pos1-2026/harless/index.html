Education

Washington State University Vancouver, Bachelor of Science in Environmental Science; Clark College; Heritage High School

Community Service

Board Member, C-TRAN, Southwest Washington Clean Air Agency; Former Board Member, Fourth Plain Forward; Former Chair, Lodging Tax Advisory Committee; LULAC Councilmember; Former Vice-Chair, Clark County Food System Council; Former President, Urban Abundance & Slow Food SWWA; 2021 Marshall Leadership Award Finalist; Leadership Clark County

Statement

In this critical moment, Vancouver needs a State Representative with proven experience and deep community roots. I have a strong record of addressing affordability, homelessness, climate resilience, and transportation. I'm endorsed by the American Federation of Teachers, numerous state legislators, and organizations ready to work with me in Olympia.

As a mother and multi-racial, Indigenous, and Mexican American raised in Vancouver, I know firsthand the challenges we face. I'll fight for working families, good jobs, unions, immigrant rights, childcare, fully funded schools, universal healthcare, a fair tax system, and our environment. Progress is possible, but only with your vote!

Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, State Representative Pos. 1. Accessed 2026-10-10. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
