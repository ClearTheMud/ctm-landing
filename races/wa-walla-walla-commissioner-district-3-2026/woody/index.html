1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (General Election 2026)

Elected Experience
Troy served on the Columbia Burbank School Board, where he applied fiscal discipline and community-focused governance to benefit local families.

Other Professional Experience
Currently, the owner of Hot Solar Solutions and Mr. Electric of Kennewick has been serving Eastern Washington for over two decades. His background includes management of the TRAC Center and leadership in the Hotel Association.

Education
School of hard knocks.

Community Service
Troy is a 25-year Walla Walla County resident, Rotary member, and nationally certified Judo coach who has served on multiple community boards.

Statement
I am not a politician, as a small business owner, father of seven, grandfather, and a 25-year resident of Walla Walla County who has watched government drift further from the people it is supposed to serve. Elected office was designed as an act of voluntary public service — not a career. Yet here we are, paying over $800,000 annually for eight elected officials while running back-to-back deficits, even as county revenues have grown 152 percent since 2008. Something is broken.

My campaign is built on three commitments. First, I will pursue Charter County Reform and bring it to a vote of the residents — replacing our current structure with a professional County Administrator and reducing commissioner compensation, saving taxpayers an estimated $225,000 per year. Second, reduce government overreach — the permit office closes at 3 pm on weekdays and residents need permission to replace their own windows. Third, I commit to a maximum of two terms. I am running to serve, reform, and step aside — exactly how public service was intended to work.

I am not running to build a career. I am running because someone has to be willing to fix this. Less government. More freedom. Real results.



Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, County Commissioner District 3. Accessed 2026-10-10. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
