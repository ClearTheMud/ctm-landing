1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (General Election 2026)

Making the world a better place has been at the forefront of all my endeavors. Whether working overseas as a Peace Corps Volunteer, advancing community health initiatives, helping young mothers navigate healthcare and education, or teaching S.T.E.M. in a middle school classroom, I have always sought to make a positive impact on the people and places I call home. These diverse experiences have allowed me to gain the skills and perspectives necessary to be an effective and creative leader.

As a classroom teacher, I see how families in our community are struggling every day.  We need our county commissioners focused on creating economic opportunities, bringing investments into the county, ensuring public safety and affordability, being good stewards of our tax dollars, and making government work for all of us. We deserve leaders who listen, bring people together, and focus on real solutions instead of partisan politics.  Too many people have been left behind by a political system that creates division instead of delivering results for working families.

I’m running to help move Cowlitz County forward with common-sense leadership rooted in accountability, transparency, and results that benefit everyone. I humbly ask for your vote.


Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, Commissioner District 3. Accessed 2026-10-10. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
