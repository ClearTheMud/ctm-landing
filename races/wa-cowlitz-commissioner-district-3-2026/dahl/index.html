1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

A 30 year Castle Rock resident, retired from a 39 year banking career as Senior Vise President, Chief Retail Officer.  I have three children and seven grandchildren.

I believe in Citizen led government, not the Government led citizen model currently in use. I have protected your rights by fighting mandates and ordinances that encroach upon them. I opposed a new flood control ordinance that placed further restrictions having little to do with flood control on property owners.  The result was a much smaller document eliminating the harmful aspects to the public.

I have refused to take more from your wallet as has become common practice by our state. In fact, I supported the sunset of a tax returning that money to the citizens of Cowlitz County.  Something that had not been done in many years.

I believe leaders with strong morals are crucial to dealing with the influences of Government.  My daily marching orders can be summed up in three short sentences. Walk in Faith.  Act in Love.  Endure in Hope.  I am called to protect your individual rights as presented in the state and federal constitutions.  I would be honored to serve another four years for you.

Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, Commissioner District 3. Accessed 2026-07-16. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
