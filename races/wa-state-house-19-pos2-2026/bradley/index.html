1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (Primary Election 2026)

Elected Experience

No elected experience.



Other Professional Experience

Taught in Japan for many years, including K-12, business English, language, and culture. Operated an English school and served as an adjunct professor at Tokai University and a technical university in Tokyo. Since returning to Washington State, worked as a paraeducator, substitute teacher, landscaper, and in solid waste, gaining practical, hands-on experience with local needs.

Education

Ilwaco High School, 1999. Eastern Washington University, Bachelor’s degree in Interdisciplinary Studies, minor in Communications, 2005.

Community Service

Engaged in local government meetings, public workshops, and community education; teaches Japanese language and culture.

Statement

Taxpayers deserve more than promises. They deserve proof that public money is spent responsibly, produces measurable results, and serves the communities that paid for it. I am asking government to prove the promises taxpayers already paid for. LD 19 deserves opportunity, respect, and public dollars tied to results residents can see.

I am running to protect taxpayers in LD 19 by making public spending clearer, more traceable, and easier to evaluate. Taxpayer dollars should serve families and communities, not disappear into waste, loopholes, or confusing budget language.
