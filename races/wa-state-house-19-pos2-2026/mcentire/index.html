1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (General Election 2026)

Elected Experience

Elected to the Washington State House of Representatives in 2020 and again in 2022, and 2024. Elected as Chair of the Wahkiakum Republican Party in 2018. Currently serves on K-12 Education Committee, Post-Secondary Education Committee, Labor Committee in the House of Representatives.

Other Professional Experience

Served in the United States Marine Corps for 15 years, deploying overseas 3 times; Worked as a professional educator in the public education system as well as at the University level.

Education

Bachelors degree in Evolutionary Ecology from Central Washington University; Masters degree in Mathematics Education from Western Governors University

Community Service

Boy Scouts Leader ; Young Men's Youth Church Leader


Statement

I am seeking re-election to bring common sense and fiscal responsibility to our beautiful State. We have seen the sharpest tax increases in the last several years with very limited returns. While people struggle to get by affording the basic necessities of life, Olympia politicians continue to demand more and more of the taxpayers. If we are truly serious about fixing our schools and our infrastructure, we first must go back to the timeless principles of living within our means, and allowing businesses to thrive. I will continue to be that voice of common sense for our future.

Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, State Representative Pos. 2. Accessed 2026-10-10. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
