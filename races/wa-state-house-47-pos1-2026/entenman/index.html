1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

State Representative, 47th District, 2018–present; member of House committees on Housing; Civil Rights & Judiciary; Transportation; and Postsecondary Education & Workforce.

Other Professional Experience

12 years of experience serving South King County as a Congressional staffer, including three as the District Director.

Education

Bachelor’s Degree, Political Science, Seattle University.

Community Service

Current member, Washington State Board for Community & Technical Colleges; 11 years on Renton Technical College Board of Trustees; member, Urban League of Metropolitan Seattle; member, National Association for the Advancement of Colored People (NAACP).

Statement

As your State Representative, I have worked to strengthen our social safety nets, improve public safety, support workers, and invest in frontline responders. As a member of the first Seattle/King County Head Start class and a non-traditional college graduate, I know how life-changing early access to opportunity and affordable education can be. Through HB2015, I secured $100 million for public safety. My bill HB1559 created benefits navigators to help college students access wraparound support and complete their education.

I will continue working with my colleagues across the aisle to protect vulnerable households and improve opportunities for families throughout Washington state.



Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, State Representative Pos. 1. Accessed 2026-07-16. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
