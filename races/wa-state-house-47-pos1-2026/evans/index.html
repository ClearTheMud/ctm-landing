1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

First time legislative candidate running as a Cascade Democrat. A Cascade Democrat values actionable rational solutions that don’t waste your tax dollars.

Other Professional Experience

Teacher in both Kent and Auburn School Districts.  I currently teach students who are struggling to stay in school.



Education

Auburn Mountainview High School. Bachelor’s, Western Washington University. Master’s, Western Governors University.

Community Service

Covington Rotary Club, Kent and Covington Chamber of Commerce, Kent Senior Activity Center, and Kent Cornucopia Days volunteer. I volunteer at The Hope Factory building housing for the homeless and with Battlefield Addiction supporting addiction recovery efforts.



Statement

I’m a public school teacher running because the system isn’t working for my students or their families. Costs are too high, housing is too scarce, and addiction is destroying too many lives in our community. As a Cascade Democrat, I’m focused on results and efficient use of your tax dollars. I support high-rise housing near light rail to lower housing costs and a three-strikes policy with six months of mandatory rehab to help people off the streets and into successful treatment. It’s time to unite around rational solutions to build a future for Washington families. 

Endorsed by ATU Local 587
