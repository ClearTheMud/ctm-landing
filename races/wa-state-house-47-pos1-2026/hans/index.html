Statement

Career politicians have become too comfortable doing the minimum while working families struggle with the basics. Housing, groceries, and energy costs keep rising, and we're left with empty words and a list of endorsements. South King County deserves a representative who listens, shows up, and fights tirelessly for real change.

As a legal professional and community advocate, I have expanded access to justice and stood with immigrants, refugees, and those often left behind. In Olympia, I will lower costs, make our tax system fairer, strengthen good-paying jobs, and ensure artificial intelligence creates opportunity, not uncertainty, for our children.
