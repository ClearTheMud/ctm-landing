1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (General Election 2026)

Elected Experience
Clark County Charter Review Commissioner, a nonpartisan role focused on accountability and improving how local government works for the people.

Other Professional Experience
Navy veteran. Registered Nurse with 24 years in healthcare, including emergency and critical care, currently serving as a flight nurse. Former small business owner. Father of three.

Education
Bachelor of Science in Nursing; Graduate study in leadership. My experience in the Navy and healthcare has sharpened my critical thinking and ability to listen, evaluate complex situations, and make practical, common-sense decisions.

Community Service
Youth football coach; Meals on Wheels volunteer; active in civic engagement and community partnerships across Southwest Washington.

Statement
Service is the center of my life. I’m a proud Navy veteran, former small business owner, flight nurse. I’m guided to serve with integrity and a mission to make life better for all people. I believe those who work hard should be able to get ahead, not just get by.  

As your State Representative, I’ll focus on strengthening our public schools, expanding skills training, building more housing, supporting small businesses and expanding access to affordable healthcare. I’ll listen, work across differences, and deliver practical solutions that help families build stable, affordable lives. I would be honored to earn your vote. 

Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, State Representative Pos. 1. Accessed 2026-10-10. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
