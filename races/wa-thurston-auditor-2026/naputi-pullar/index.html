Finding 2.1: Insider Succession — Running to Replace Own Boss LOW

What happened
Naputi-Pullar is the current Elections Manager running to succeed her boss, Auditor Mary Hall, who chose not to seek re-election. While her 24+ years of internal experience is her core qualification, the auditor role encompasses not just elections but also recording, licensing, financial services, and whistleblower investigations — areas where her documented experience is primarily limited to the elections division.
