Finding 4.1: Insider Succession -- Running to Replace Own Boss in the Office She Manages LOW

What happened
Naputi-Pullar is the current Elections Manager in the Thurston County Auditor's Office and is running to succeed her boss, Auditor Mary Hall, who chose not to seek re-election. While her 24+ years of internal experience is her core qualification, this insider succession dynamic may draw scrutiny about whether the office will benefit from fresh perspective or outside oversight. The auditor role encompasses not just elections but also recording, licensing, financial services, and whistleblower investigations -- areas where Naputi-Pullar's documented experience is primarily limited to the elections division.

Defense
Naputi-Pullar's deep institutional knowledge is precisely what voters want in an elections administrator. She has been recognized twice as the state's top elections employee. Internal succession is the norm for county auditor positions, where technical expertise in elections administration, recording, and licensing is critical. The auditor's office has received consecutive clean state audits and national awards under the current leadership team she has been part of.
