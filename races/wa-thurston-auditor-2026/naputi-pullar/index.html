Finding 6.1: Insider Succession -- Running to Replace Own Boss in the Office She Manages LOW

What happened
Naputi-Pullar is the current Elections Manager in the Thurston County Auditor's Office and is running to succeed her boss, Auditor Mary Hall, who chose not to seek re-election. While her 24+ years of internal experience is her core qualification, this insider succession dynamic may draw scrutiny about whether the office will benefit from fresh perspective or outside oversight. The auditor role encompasses not just elections but also recording, licensing, financial services, and whistleblower investigations -- areas where Naputi-Pullar's documented experience is primarily limited to the elections division. As of 2026-06-10 the dynamic has intensified: the retiring incumbent is listed as an endorser on the campaign's website (candidate-stated, T2.5) and contributed $250 to the campaign (PDC, 2026-03-21, T0), and the race is effectively unopposed on the Thurston County ballot, removing electoral scrutiny.

Defense
Naputi-Pullar's deep institutional knowledge is precisely what voters want in an elections administrator. She has been recognized twice as the state's top elections employee. Internal succession is the norm for county auditor positions, where technical expertise in elections administration, recording, and licensing is critical. The auditor's office has received consecutive clean state audits and national awards under the current leadership team she has been part of. A retiring official endorsing and modestly supporting a longtime deputy is common and lawful; the contribution is fully disclosed in PDC records.

Finding 6.2: Campaign Status -- Modest, Insider-Funded Campaign in an Effectively Unopposed Race LOW

What happened
As of 2026-06-10, Naputi-Pullar has raised $10,417.28 (100 itemized PDC contributions) and spent $6,680.72 (32 records through 2026-05-31). Fundraising is essentially flat since mid-May -- only $35 in new receipts after 2026-05-17. The donor base is concentrated among establishment and insider sources: Thurston County Democrats $1,000, the retiring incumbent Auditor Mary Hall $250, family members (Vicky, Andrew, and Tillie Pullar), and her paid campaign consultant Danielle Westbrook $500. Earlier published statements that she had no visible campaign website, no documented endorsements, and no public campaign statements are superseded: a live campaign website (tillieforauditor.com) carries a platform statement and an endorsement wall of 23 current and former elected officials (candidate-stated, T2.5). She is the sole County Auditor candidate on the Thurston County ballot, making the race effectively unopposed.
