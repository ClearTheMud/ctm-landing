1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (General Election 2026)

Elected Experience

No prior elected experience. First-time candidate.

Other Professional Experience

Thurston County Elections Specialist, beginning in 1999; Thurston County Elections Manager since 2013; OSOS VoteWA Advisory Board member since 2019 and current co-chair; Washington State Elections Administrator of the Year, 2014 and 2021.

Education

Proud graduate of Saint Martin’s College, Bachelor of Arts, Political Science and Criminal Justice; Auburn University/The Election Center, Certified Election and Registration Administrator.

Community Service

Capitol Little League Scheduler, Player Agent, and Board Member.

Statement

At a critical time for our democracy, Thurston County deserves proven leadership, experience, and integrity in its Auditor. For 27 years, I have proudly served our community in the Thurston County Auditor’s Office, working in the Elections Division to ensure our elections are secure, accurate, accessible, and transparent. This work has given me a deep understanding of the responsibilities voters entrust to this office.

As a state and nationally certified elections administrator, I have the knowledge and experience to lead the Auditor’s Office effectively and efficiently from day one. I will continue Thurston County’s long tradition of having a qualified Auditor with strong elections expertise.

Most people don’t think about the Auditor’s Office every day — but it’s there when you need it: when you register to vote, license your vehicle, apply for a marriage license, record important documents, or apply for a U.S. passport.

I am committed to transparency, accountability, and protecting public trust in our elections. Now more than ever, Thurston County needs a trusted, experienced election professional as Auditor. Experience matters — because the details matter, the process matters, and the public’s trust matters. I respectfully ask for your vote.

Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, Auditor. Accessed 2026-10-10. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
