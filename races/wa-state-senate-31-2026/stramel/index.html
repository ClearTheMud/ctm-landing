1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (General Election 2026)

Elected Experience

Democratic PCO since 2017. 



Other Professional Experience

Field Organizer for 2024 Sumner-Bonney Lake School Bond (largest in WA state history), and previously employed as a program manager within residential treatment facilities. 



Education

Bachelor of Arts in Psychology with a Minor in Religious Studies from California State University, Bakersfield



Community Service

Currently serving on the Sumner-Bonney Lake School Bond Advisory Committee; as an Executive PTA Board Member and Book Fair Committee Chair; and in my eight year of continuous leadership on the board of the 31st LD Democrats. Past volunteering includes Secretary for the Family Readiness Group of the USS Shoup (DDG-86).



Statement

I’m running for State Senate in the 31st district because I believe our communities deserve a senator who shows up, listens, and works for everyone who calls this district home. Families are being priced out, infrastructure is straining under rapid growth, and our schools are asked to do more with less. At the same time, too many people feel unheard, or afraid to speak up.

I'm a mom, an organizer, and a veteran spouse. I've worked as a youth counselor with kids in crisis, supported military families through deployments, and spent eight years putting down roots near Lake Tapps, raising my two boys with my husband. Our current challenges aren’t abstract policy debates to me; they are my life.

I believe we can govern better. I will lead with honesty, compassion, and determination to solve problems while representing the whole 31st district. I will advocate for our communities to be prioritized by Olympia, particularly in the transportation budget. We deserve leadership that cares about families, champions education, and takes rural infrastructure development seriously. I would be honored to earn your vote. 

Endorsed: Washington State Labor Council, AFL-CIO; Pierce County Democrats; and trusted local leaders. Please see my website for more!
