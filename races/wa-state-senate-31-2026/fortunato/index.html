1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

31st District State Senator since 2017; 31st District State Representative elected 2016; 47th District State Representative 1998-2000

Other Professional Experience

Environmental training and consulting. International trade in Europe and Asia. Owner of small contracting business. Founding Director and first President of the PNW Erosion Control Association.  

Education

Rutgers University, Cook College of Agriculture; Landscape Architecture

Community Service

I’ve been married for 49 years, have 5 children, 3 of whom are decorated U.S. Marines Corps veterans. Knights of Columbus member - 4th Degree, Project Rachel, Fraternal Order of Eagles, Sons & Daughters of Italy, Liberian Association of WA.


Statement

It’s been an honor to serve as your voice in the State Senate and stand up for the values, freedoms, and common sense that make our communities strong.  My approach to decision-making is simple: God first, the district second, and personal preference last. That principle guides every vote I take in Olympia. It’s why I regularly seek your feedback through district surveys and town halls.  Your voice should shape the decisions that affect our communities.

The financial challenges facing our state are not accidental. They stem from excessive spending and the unchecked growth of government programs. Individuals, families, seniors, workers, and small businesses are all being crushed by rising taxes and higher costs and for many of us, we’ve reached the breaking point.

I will continue to stand firmly against efforts to erode our Second Amendment rights, and I will fight for responsible, disciplined use of your tax dollars. I ask for your continued support as I work to protect your rights, defend your prosperity, and fight for a more accountable state government.

Proudly endorsed by law enforcement leaders, local elected officials, community leaders, and neighbors across the district who trust me to represent their values at the state Capitol.

Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, State Senator. Accessed 2026-07-16. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
