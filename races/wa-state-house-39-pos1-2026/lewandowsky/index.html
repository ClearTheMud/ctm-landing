Statement

Forty years as a registered nurse — oncology, ICU, hospice, our community hospital — taught me what’s breaking. Health care and housing are human rights, not yield-generating assets for hedge funds. Public dams shouldn’t shift electricity costs from massive data centers onto families. Rural hospitals shouldn’t close while insurers post record profits. In Olympia I’ll fight for affordable care close to home, rural hospitals, housing working families can afford, and fair electricity rates for ratepayers.  Real healthcare choice for all. Washington’s future should be measured by whether ordinary people can build a decent life here. Endorsed by Snohomish County Democrats.

Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, State Representative Pos. 1. Accessed 2026-07-16. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
