1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (General Election 2026)

Elected Experience
Your current 2 term Washington State Representative LD 39; Snohomish County Council 3 terms; Lake Stevens Council (Council President/ Mayor Pro Tem); Washington State Transportation Improvement Board (2024-2026 chair); Washington State Traffic Safety Commissioner (2025-2026 chair).

Other Professional Experience
Successful small business owner; Elementary school teacher; Snohomish Health 8 years (Chair 2015); North Sound Behavioral Health Skagit 9 years.   

Education
Masters Degree Organizational Leadership- MBU; Bachelor of Arts Theology- PCBBC   

Community Service
Skagit County Boys and Girls Club advocate of the year (2026); Lake Stevens Rotary Club (President 2016-2017); Roadside cleanups; Sky Valley Arts supporter of the year (2022); Basketball coach; Sedro Woolley Food Bank supporter.   

Statement
I have already successfully passed 12 bills into state law. (2025-2026) HB1345 allowing rural ADU's; HB1261 Helping Farms; HB2441 helping first responder widows; and previously HB1962 voter roll cleanup; HB1271 Organ bill saving lives; HB1961 Holding animal abusers accountable; HB1987 Workforce housing

Prioritizing public safety, providing tax relief, reducing traffic congestion, and promoting family-wage jobs are why I am endorsed by over 30 current Republican Members of the State House of Representatives, many local elected officials, Law enforcement, labor, 39th leaders Senator Keith Wagoner; Representative Carolyn Eslick, and former LS Council Member Steve Ewing. I humbly ask for your vote.
