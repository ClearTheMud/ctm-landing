Statement

Most human-made catastrophes start with good intentions and poor foresight. In my profession, I create solutions that will not trigger unintended lethal outcomes. Politicians should be just as cautious when making policies.

The incumbent championed attacks on businesses and high wage earners leading to layoffs and an exodus of tax wealth, jobs, and services from our state, hurting the quality of life in our communities. Our schools and public services lose valuable programs from lost state revenue and our arts and culture communities are made vulnerable due to decline in private philanthropy. We are already seeing shortfalls to local county and city budgets. Washington lost more than $500 million in adjusted gross income to out-migration between 2022 and 2023 after the capital gains tax passed. Current business climate and future uncertainty is leading to further loss of local employment and decline in commercial tax revenues. As the state legislature looks to fix budget shortfalls, they will target Washingtonians already struggling with existing taxes and inflating costs for businesses, goods, groceries, and housing.

I approach challenges through data, discussion, and collaboration. I value community perspectives because they lead to better solutions. I will bring this problem-solving mindset to the State Senate.
