1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

None.

Other Professional Experience

Communications Director, Working Washington, organizing alongside workers and unions to deliver higher wages and stronger labor protections for underpaid workers; Legislative Assistant and Communications Specialist, Washington State House of Representatives and Washington State Senate, supporting state lawmakers with research, strategy, and messaging on transformative policies like the Capital Gains Tax, Postpartum Medicaid expansion, Salary Transparency, and more.

Education

BA, Whitworth University (Political Science & Spanish, with Honors).

Community Service

Member, Pro-Choice Washington PAC Board and The Urbanist Elections Committee, working to advance reproductive justice, reliable transit, affordable housing, and elect candidates up and down the ballot that will fight for working people.

Statement

Paying rent, buying a home, and raising a family should be affordable. The reason they’re not is because of career politicians who protect the status quo and cut deals with corporate special interests that keep costs high. That's why we're the only campaign in this race not taking corporate PAC money.

In the Washington State Legislature, I worked to expand healthcare access, invest in higher education, and tax the ultra-rich. At Working Washington, I fought alongside underpaid workers and took on big corporations to raise wages, strengthen labor standards, and ensure all workers have protections. I organized alongside grassroots workers’ rights, immigrants’ rights, and queer rights organizations to end corporate tax breaks and defend Washington from Trump. 

As your state senator, I’ll fight to ensure the ultra-wealthy pay their fair share for good schools, healthcare, and childcare; reduce rent and put homeownership within reach by building more housing; and build toward universal childcare—ultimately lowering costs and making Washington more affordable for all of us.

We’re endorsed by progressive champions and unions like State Representative Shaun Scott, Seattle City Councilmember Alexis Mercedes Rinck, State Senator Rebecca Saldaña, Seattle Port Commissioner Toshiko Hasegawa, AFT Washington, and Working Families Party. More at hannahforwashington.com.

Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, State Senator. Accessed 2026-07-16. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
