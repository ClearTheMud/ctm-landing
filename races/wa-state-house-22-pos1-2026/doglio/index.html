Community Service

Statement

I'm honored to serve as your State Representative and ask for your vote.

I advocate for issues that matter most to our district and state, passing legislation addressing climate change, protecting domestic violence survivors, strengthening living wage laws and safety protections, providing additional dollars to house our most vulnerable, and expanding collective bargaining rights for state employees. I will continue to advocate for state employees, responsible gun laws, education funding, and a strong social safety net. Endorsements: Planned Parenthood Alliance, Housing Alliance, Washington Conservation Action, Washington State Labor Council, State Employees 443, Washington Nurses Association, Alliance for Gun Responsbility.
