1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (General Election 2026)

Elected Experience
Aberdeen City Councilmember, representing working families and local neighborhood interests.

Other Professional Experience
Working line cook with extensive professional kitchen operations experience; former HVAC tradesman and service industry technician.

Education
Attending The Evergreen State College to obtain Master's in Public Policy and Administration, as well as Geographic Information Systems (GIS) certificate program; vocational training in heating, ventilation, and air conditioning trades.

Community Service
Dedicated local advocate for working-class stability, rural infrastructure investments, rent stabilization initiatives, and a steadfast community ally of the Chinook Indian Nation’s fight for official state and federal recognition.

Statement
I am a working line cook, city councilmember, and tradesman focusing on the material survival of rural families. For too long, career politicians have prioritized online culture wars over the economic realities squeezing our communities. I refuse corporate PAC money, my only loyalty is to you! I will fight to protect rural healthcare infrastructure, secure fair funding for our local public schools, pass statewide rent stabilization, and expand local union apprenticeships to keep family-wage jobs in our communities. It's time to send a worker to Olympia who puts rural families first. I would be honored to earn your vote.

Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, State Representative Pos. 1. Accessed 2026-10-10. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
