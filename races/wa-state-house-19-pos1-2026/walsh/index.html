Statement

Jim Walsh keeps his promise to defend our jobs, kids, schools and neighborhoods. He protects our community. Secures funding for critical infrastructure. Fights back against extremist policies from outside our district that kill local jobs and steal resources from our schools and roads.

Jim knows our corner of Washington. A small business owner, he shares our values: Hard work, honesty, standing up for what's right and true. He advocates for rational policy and against unlawful mandates from Olympia. He raised all five of his children here. He's not leaving. Jim Walsh fights for all of us.
