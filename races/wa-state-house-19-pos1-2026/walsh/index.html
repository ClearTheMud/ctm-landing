1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (General Election 2026)

Elected Experience

Our State Representative.

Other Professional Experience

Owner/Publisher, Silver Lake Publishing. 1998-present. Association of Ameircan Publishers. Publishers Marketing Association. American Association of Business Publications. PMA/Benjamin Franklin Book Award. AABP Feature Award.



Education

Bachelor of Arts, cum laude. Amherst College. Amherst, MA. 1986.

Community Service

Youth basketball coach, Grays Harbor YMCA. 2006-2024. Authorized volunteer/driver, Archdiocese of Seattle, 2006-2025. Charter participant, Aberdeen "Splash Day" Festival, 2008-present.

Statement

Jim Walsh keeps his promise to defend our jobs, kids, schools and neighborhoods. He protects our community. Secures funding for critical infrastructure. Fights back against extremist policies from outside our district that kill local jobs and steal resources from our schools and roads.

Jim knows our corner of Washington. A small business owner, he shares our values: Hard work, honesty, standing up for what's right and true. He advocates for rational policy and against unlawful mandates from Olympia. He raised all five of his children here. He's not leaving. Jim Walsh fights for all of us.
