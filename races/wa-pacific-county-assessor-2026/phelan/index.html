Finding 3.1: Documented history of inflammatory social media posts and a public library trespass notice HIGH

What happened
The Chinook Observer, Pacific County's newspaper of record, reported on April 19, 2026 that Phelan has a history of inflammatory public Facebook posts, quoting several with dates. A September 24, 2025 post called the Chinook Indian Nation "fraudsters" and "a bunch of hobos" and characterized its recognition efforts as "a money grab." A March 2, 2026 post used the phrase "stupid brain-dead liberals" while advocating ICE removal of undocumented immigrants. In September 2025 the Timberland Regional Library system issued Phelan a one-month trespass notice tied to offensive language on social media. Phelan did not respond to the newspaper's request for comment.
