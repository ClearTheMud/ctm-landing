1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Legal/Judicial Experience

I’ve made thousands of judicial decisions as a Snohomish County Superior Court Commissioner pro tem and court-appointed Arbitrator, over the past 18 of my 29 years in family law practice. I have come alongside families in all stages of brokenness, navigating the complexities of litigation with compassion and confidence.

Statement

I am the only judicially experienced candidate for Position 1, having served on the bench more than 20 times as long as all others in this race combined.

Unique among these candidates, I have zero history of partisan activism. You can trust me to measure all laws against the Constitution as written, without any political shadows clouding my objectivity. Impartiality is a judge’s job, and I am your best hope for it here.

More significantly, your vote for me will put a Justice with deep family law expertise onto the Court for the first time since no-fault divorce was legalized in Washington 52 years ago. While the landscape of family law practice has shifted seismically in subsequent decades, our state courts still fall short. Because the Supreme Court—in addition to deciding cases—oversees the procedures and priorities of lower courts, as a Justice I will advocate for “fast-tracking” appeals in family law matters, to bring timely closure to uncertain chapters. Years of legal limbo may be acceptable in other arenas, but for children in crisis you can’t undo damage caused by delay.  

If you value experience, impartiality, or family law expertise on our State Supreme Court, I am your best choice.
