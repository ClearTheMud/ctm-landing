1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience
Ran for hospital commissioner in 2018. 

Other Professional Experience
My career in safety and security has included roles in health care security, psychiatric emergency response, corrections, and currently tribal courts and crisis response. My expertise has been recognized through publication in Security Magazine and Journal of Healthcare Protection as well as interviews for Healthcare Security podcast and Security Magazine de-escalation strategy.

Education
Biblical Studies, Life Pacific University, Chaplaincy, Life Pacific University

Community Service
Ministry in the streets of Port Angeles as well as other areas of our state, collaborating with agencies, churches, shelters, and other ministries to help people get off the streets and into treatment. 

 Statement
I will strive to get state funding back to the drawing board before raising taxes. Our communities in the district are diverse and rural with needs different than other parts of the state. Let's work together with nonprofits, churches, and tax-funded agencies to address our community needs. I want to investigate how state spending compares to real data about effectiveness in order to develop better models for housing, education, harm reduction, homelessness/addiction, etc. The policies we have now are hindering, not helping. Allow me to work for our district with partnerships that help the whole community.
