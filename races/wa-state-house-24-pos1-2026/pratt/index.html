Statement
As a lifelong resident with local family roots, and as your representative, I will carry the longstanding values of our region with me. I champion family-centered policies lowering the cost of housing, energy, healthcare, and groceries. I continue standing against burdensome taxes and over-regulation that impede our housing, businesses, and local jobs. The goal is balanced policies in education, public safety, healthcare, and the environment, framed within rights protections. We will build families’ confidence in themselves, their homes, and their futures on the Olympic Peninsula. Together, you and I will ensure local voices shape the policies that affect our lives.
