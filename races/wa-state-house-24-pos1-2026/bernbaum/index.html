Statement

It has been an honor to serve as your State Representative for the past two years. During my time in the State Legislature, we passed bipartisan legislation to expand access to childcare, build rural housing, and modernize our infrastructure. But families are still struggling, and there's more to do. That's why I'm running for re-election. I want to keep fighting to lower costs, support our hospitals, and create family-wage jobs, because we all deserve to live in a healthy and prosperous community.

Endorsed by Planned Parenthood, the Washington State Labor Council, Washington Conservation Action, Firefighters, Nurses, Educators, and more.
