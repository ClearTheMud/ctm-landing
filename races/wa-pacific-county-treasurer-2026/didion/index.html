1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

I have not previously held elected office, but I am currently working in the Pacific County Treasurer’s Office and learning directly from the current Treasurer. This in-depth experience has given me valuable knowledge of the daily operations, financial responsibilities, and customer service required to serve the people of Pacific County.
Other Professional Experience
My experience in banking, payroll, accounts payable, and county finances has provided strong knowledge of financial responsibility, accuracy, and dependable public service.
Education
I earned my Associate degree and have completed coursework in accounting and business.
Community Service
I have volunteered locally and supported community programs through service and donations.
Statement
As a lifelong resident of Pacific County, I care deeply about the people and community that have shaped my life. My husband and I raised our five children here, and now our grandchildren are growing up in the same community we proudly call home. I enjoy spending time with my family, caring for my animals and hobby farm, gardening, and appreciating the beautiful coastal area we are fortunate to live in. I currently serve as a Senior Accountant in the Pacific County Treasurer’s Office, where I work alongside and learn from the current Treasurer. I previously worked in the Pacific County Auditor’s Office as a Payroll/Benefits Technician and also served the community through the Pacific County Sheriff’s Office as a dispatcher and clerk. These experiences have strengthened my understanding of county operations, financial responsibility, and public service. I am running as an independent candidate for Pacific County Treasurer because I truly care about the people of this county. I want to serve our community with honesty, accountability, compassion, and a positive attitude while helping bring a glimpse of joy and kindness to those around me.
