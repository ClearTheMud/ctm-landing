Statement

Thank you for the honor of serving the 35th District since 2013. Throughout my time in the Senate, I’ve focused on strengthening our communities through economic development, infrastructure improvements, and transportation investments that support long-term growth. I remain committed to advancing these priorities.  I will also continue to speak out about the state’s ongoing spending challenges and advocate for responsible budgeting.  Washington needs a more balanced approach in the legislature, one that reflects the full range of voices and concerns across our state.  I encourage you to reach out to me directly with your questions, ideas, and feedback. It is a privilege to represent you, and I look forward to continuing to serve the people of the 35th District.  I humbly ask for your vote.
