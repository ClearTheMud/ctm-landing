1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (General Election 2026)

Education

Christian Brothers University (BA); University of Washington School of Law. County Leadership Institute Alumni 2025, Public Health Leadership Academy 2026.

Community Service

Kiwanis Club of Olympia, Zonta Club of Olympia, League of Women Voters Member.

Statement

Working families like mine are struggling, and I want to be a voice for lowering costs as your State Senator. Arriving in this country as a young child, I witnessed my parents model a life dedicated to showing up for our neighbors. That’s what still drives me.

As County Commissioner, I helped establish a Home Fund to support our neighbors facing skyrocketing housing costs, helped families afford necessities during a formula shortage, managed a pandemic health crisis, and directed more than $20 million to help struggling families and businesses in our region.

I also worked to develop the first USDA-certified meat processing facility in Western Washington, a public-private partnership that fills a critical gap for our ranchers and strengthens our regional food system. But much more work has to be done.

I see the challenges we face through the eyes of a neighbor and a parent. I want all our kids to grow up in a community with good jobs, safe neighborhoods, quality schools, and access to healthcare. I’ve felt how our families have been left behind while resources and attention go to other communities. I'm ready to listen, work across the aisle, and fight for us.



Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, State Senator. Accessed 2026-10-10. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
