1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

State Senator; Deputy Majority Leader; Chair: Law & Justice Committee; Committee Member: Ways & Means; Environment, Energy & Technology


Other Professional Experience

Senior Deputy Prosecuting Attorney, King County (20 years); Chair: Therapeutic Alternative Unit; Crisis Intervention Training Instructor: WA Criminal Justice Training Commission (10 years). Intern: Sexually Violent Predator Unit, AG’s Office. Clerk: State Supreme Court.

Education

Juris Doctorate UW; B.A. History and Political Science, UC Berkeley

Community Service

Recipient: PTSA “Golden Acorn,” “Champion for Kids’” and NAMI “Behavioral Health Champion” awards. Girl Scout Cookie mom; Chess Club, Math Club and Destination Imagination coach; Hopelink board member; Co-founder of API Chaya, (supporting survivors of gender-based violence).


Statement

We deserve leaders with a proven record of bringing people together to solve tough problems. I have listened to our community to deliver meaningful results. My work is guided by science and data and rooted in collaboration, honesty, and integrity. I have led efforts to strengthen public safety, expand behavioral health care, protect reproductive freedom, and defend civil rights.

We need bold leaders, committed to justice. I’ve worked alongside survivors, law enforcement and community organizations to improve victim services, strengthen accountability, expand crisis response systems, and connect people experiencing mental illness and addiction with treatment.

Each of us has a role to play in the prosperity of our state. We must continue advancing social equity, investing responsibly in transportation, and empowering our children to become the leaders we need to continue our tremendous economic growth. Our state must remain a leader in innovation and in creating opportunities for all.

With your vote, I would be honored to continue working to protect our democracy, improve healthcare access, defend workers, protect seniors, small businesses, and immigrants, and fight for a safe and livable planet. Endorsements: Planned Parenthood, One America Votes, Washington Conservation Action, labor unions, nurses, and community leaders across the Eastside.
