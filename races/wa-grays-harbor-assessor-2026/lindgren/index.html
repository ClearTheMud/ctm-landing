Statement

I have proudly served as your Grays Harbor County Assessor since 2015 after beginning my career in the Assessor’s Office in 2007. Before being elected Assessor, I personally completed more than 14,000 property appraisals throughout Grays Harbor County.

As Assessor, I have focused on fair and accurate property assessments, excellent customer service, transparency, and responsible management of taxpayer resources. I implemented the county’s Taxsifter public records website, upgraded our mass appraisal systems, and built and implement the county’s new MapGeo mapping system.

I believe in open government, strong property rights, and responsible taxation. I do not support unnecessary tax increases and believe government should operate efficiently and responsibly on behalf of taxpayers.

I was unanimously elected by Washington Assessors to serve on the Washington Association of County Officials Executive Board and have served for 11 years as Computer Technology Chair for the Washington State Association of County Assessors. I am honored to have earned the endorsement of the Grays Harbor Republican Party, many local elected officials, and Assessors from across Washington State.  I've spent the last 19 years developing my skills, leadership and knowlege.  I'm confident that I am the best candidate to continue to be your Assessor.

Finding 6.1: Switched from Democrat to Republican months before the primary MODERATE

What happened
Lindgren ran as a Democrat in his 2010, 2014, 2018, and 2022 campaigns and changed his party registration to Republican in April 2026, about four months before the primary. Grays Harbor backed Trump with 51.4 percent in 2024. Lindgren said his "values now align more closely with the Republican Party" when he announced the switch.
Source tier
T2
Political impact
Moderate
Defense
Lindgren says the assessor role is nonpartisan and focused on applying the law equitably, and describes the change as personal integrity rather than disagreement with local Democratic leaders. Many elected officials in rural Washington have changed parties as the parties realigned.

Finding 6.2: PDC complaint over unfiled 2022 campaign finance reports (Case LOW

What happened
A Public Disclosure Commission complaint (Case #6580, filing #151198) was filed against Dan Lindgren by Conner Edwards on February 15, 2024. It alleges a violation of RCW 42.17A.235 and .240 for failing to timely and accurately report contributions and expenditures, specifically a failure to file mandatory C-4 reports for the 2022 election cycle. As of March 2026 the case status is "Investigation of Possible Violation" with no penalties assessed.
Source tier
T1
Political impact
Low
Defense
C-4 filing violations are among the most common PDC complaints and frequently result in warnings or small fines. No penalties have been assessed, and the complaint was filed by a private citizen rather than initiated by PDC staff.
