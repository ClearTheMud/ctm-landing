1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (General Election 2026)

Elected Experience

No elected experience, but a comprehensive knowledge in mass appraisal in the Assessor's office.

Other Professional Experience

Senior Appraiser for Grays Harbor County Assessor’s Office, Current Local 275 Union Shop Steward, Custodian for Grays Harbor County, Wilderness Trail Technician for California Conservation Corps, Wildland Firefighter/First Responder for California Conservation Corps.

Education

IAAO certified Appraiser, Shasta County Community College, California Conservation Corps Wildland Fire training, Emergency Response, Crisis Management.

Community Service

Active with school fundraisers, field trips, and food and clothing drives.

Statement

I believe that the roll of Assessor isn't just a job, it's a duty, one that is funded by your tax dollars. The people deserve to have someone in this role who shows up daily to represent the tax payers. The values we assign to a property aren't just numbers on a data sheet, they are people's livelihoods. I believe that the most important role for the Assessor is to ensure all properties are assessed fairly and equitably. With me, you will get an Assessor who will be the first one in and the last one out of the office each day, ensuring the people of Grays Harbor are given complete transparency and customer service.

For the past four years, I have served as an appraiser in the Assessor’s Office, gaining the vital knowledge required to lead the department. During my tenure, I have developed innovative tools and guides that have streamlined our workflow, significantly increasing both efficiency and accuracy. Exceptional customer service is my top priority; I am dedicated to connecting with property owners and resolving complex issues. My hands-on experience, combined with ongoing professional education, uniquely positions me to guide the Assessor’s Office into a successful future.
