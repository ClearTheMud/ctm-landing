Elected Experience

State Representative; Majority Caucus Chair; Education Committee Member; Children, Youth and Families Committee Member; Labor/Workplace Standards and the Rules Committee Member

Everett School District/School Counselor, Mental Health Counselor, Small Business Owner, Clinical and Executive Director/Mental Health Center, Illinois State Board of Education/Regional Coordinator, Department of Children and Family Services Education Advisor 




Education

Community Service

Legislative Education Opportunity and Accountability Gap Statewide Committee Co-Chair, Wa State Latino Democratic Caucus Co-Chair, Executive Councilmember of the National Hispanic Council of State Legislators, Chair Governors Commission of Hispanic Affairs




Statement

As a school counselor, I’m proud to have secured equitable investments in early learning, education, and technical and career training. As a mother and grandmother, I’ve prioritized community health and safety by expanding behavioral health care, strengthening and diversifying law enforcement, and advancing gun violence prevention. As your representative, I will continue working to strengthen our local economy through support for small businesses, protections for workers, an infrastructure that supports a clean economy and protections from harmful government overreach. 

With your continued support we can achieve a more fair, just and affordable future for all Washingtonians.
