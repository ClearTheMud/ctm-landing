Statement

I’m proud of my bipartisan record delivering for local families and businesses. Committed to safe communities, I worked with law enforcement to secure resources and tools to keep us safe. To address the affordable housing crisis, I supported emergency housing and affordability for seniors, working people, and military families. In education, I’ve invested in affordable childcare, training, and improved K-12 outcomes. 

I’m a military mom and daughter, recognized as a leader in protecting those who serve and sacrifice. A longtime champion for workforce education, I’m focused on local jobs and opportunity. Let’s keep working together for the future we deserve.
