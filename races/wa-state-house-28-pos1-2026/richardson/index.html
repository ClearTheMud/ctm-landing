1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

Board member, ARNP’s United



Other Professional Experience

20-year Army Officer/combat veteran, nurse practitioner, Associate Dean, tenured educator, healthcare leader, and small business owner. 



Education

Doctorate, Marymount University; Masters, Pacific Lutheran University; Bachelors, Eastern Washington University; Associates, Spokane Falls Community College. Board-certified Family and Emergency Nurse Practitioner.  Fellow: American Academy of Nursing, Academy of Emergency Nursing, and Academy of Wilderness Medicine.



Community Service

Provides free sports physicals, mentors nurse practitioner students in community-based care, precepts students from regional universities. Performs military readiness exams. Active in healthcare education, workforce development, patient advocacy, and professional mentorship through teaching, guest lecturing, and leadership service in state and national healthcare organizations.



Statement

I’ve spent my life serving patients, students, military families, and this community. I’ve seen the real-world consequences when government loses focus on the people it serves. Families are struggling with rising costs, housing affordability, public safety concerns, and declining trust in government. Small businesses and working families deserve leadership that listens, understands their challenges, and delivers practical solutions. I’m running to bring accountable, service-driven leadership to Olympia — focused on people, results, and restoring trust between government and the communities it serves, not partisan politics or political theater.
