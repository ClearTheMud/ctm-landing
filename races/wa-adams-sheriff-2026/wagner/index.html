Finding 5.1: Federal civil rights suit naming the sheriff alleged a 2023 handover to Border Patrol custody MODERATE

What happened
A federal civil rights lawsuit, No. 2:25-cv-00003-RLP in the U.S. District Court for the Eastern District of Washington, was filed on January 7, 2025 against Adams County, the Adams County Sheriff's Office, a sheriff's corporal, and Sheriff Dale Wagner in his individual and official capacities. The plaintiff alleged that in March 2023 he was held beyond what was needed to process his release from local charges and was then taken into U.S. Border Patrol custody. The claim against Wagner was failure to train and/or supervise, and a claim under the Washington Law Against Discrimination was pleaded against all defendants. The defendants denied wrongdoing. On January 20, 2026 the Court dismissed the claims against the Sheriff's Office with prejudice because under Washington law it is not an entity that can be sued, and denied summary judgment on the claims against the remaining defendants, finding that issues of fact largely precluded judgment. The Court made no finding of liability. The docket shows the case referred to a settlement conference in 2026. Later docket entries, including a July 2, 2026 order on a motion to dismiss, were not available to read. Wagner said in a statement released August 5, 2026, as reported by the Cheney Free Press on August 13, 2026, that the case had settled under an agreement that is not to be construed as an admission of fault. This is the candidate's own statement; no settlement order or agreement was located.
