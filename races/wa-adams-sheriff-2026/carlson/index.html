Community Service

Statement
I am committed to serving both in and out of uniform, I have spent years working to support our community through public safety, local involvement, and volunteer work.

For me, service doesn’t stop when the shift ends. Whether it’s helping at local events, supporting families during difficult times, standing alongside fellow first responders, or investing in the next generation, I beleive strong communities are built through trust, relationships, and being present.

I believe leadership starts with showing up, listening to people, and staying connected to the community I hope to continue to serve as your sheriff.
