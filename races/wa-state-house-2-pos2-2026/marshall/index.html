1 Militia & Extremism Associations

Finding 1.1: Founded Washington State Three Percenters — ADL/DOD-Classified Militia CRITICAL

What happened
Marshall founded the “Three Percent of Washington,” an organization the Anti-Defamation League classifies as part of the right-wing anti-government militia movement. The U.S. Department of Defense included Three Percenters on its extremist organization training list alongside Atomwaffen Division, Patriot Front, and Proud Boys. In 2021, Canada officially listed the Three Percenters as a terrorist entity. National members were among the most heavily indicted groups in the January 6 Capitol attack. Marshall’s own PDC financial disclosures listed him as “public affairs officer” through 2023, despite claiming he resigned as president in 2020.
Source tier
T2 (multi-source: NPR, Washington Post, WA Democrats press release, Wikipedia)
Political impact
This is the defining vulnerability of Marshall’s political career. The ADL classification, DOD extremist list inclusion, Canadian terrorist designation, and January 6 connection create a compounding credibility problem that no amount of “community service” framing can fully neutralize. PDC disclosures showing continued affiliation through 2023 undermine his departure narrative.
Defense
Marshall claims his Washington chapter is distinct from the national organization, that his members were not present at January 6, and that he departed the organization in May 2023. He characterizes the group as focused on disaster relief and community service.

Finding 1.2: Defended Matt Shea — Found to Have Participated in Domestic Terrorism CRITICAL

What happened
Marshall publicly defended former Rep. Matt Shea (R-Spokane Valley), who was removed from the House Republican Caucus after an independent investigation found he participated in domestic terrorism. Shea authored “The Biblical Basis for War,” applying Old Testament passages about killing to people who practice communism or refuse biblical law. Marshall said he was “convinced that the claims against him are sensationalized” and that the way Shea was “attacked by his own caucus was wrong.” His decision to run for office was reportedly triggered by J.T. Wilcox calling for Shea’s resignation.

Defense
