Finding 7.1: Founded Washington State Three Percenters, ADL/DOD-Classified Militia CRITICAL

What happened
Marshall founded the 'Three Percent of Washington,' an organization the Anti-Defamation League classifies as part of the right-wing anti-government militia movement. The U.S. Department of Defense included Three Percenters on its extremist organization training list alongside Atomwaffen Division, Patriot Front, and Proud Boys. In 2021, Canada officially listed the Three Percenters as a terrorist entity. National members were among the most heavily indicted groups in the January 6 Capitol attack. Marshall's own PDC financial disclosures listed him as 'public affairs officer' through 2023, despite claiming he resigned as president in 2020.
Source tier
T2
Political impact
Critical
Defense
Marshall claims his Washington chapter is distinct from the national organization, that his members were not present at January 6, and that he departed the organization in May 2023. He characterizes the group as focused on disaster relief and community service.

Finding 7.2: Defended Matt Shea, Found to Have Participated in Domestic Terrorism CRITICAL

What happened
Marshall publicly defended former Rep. Matt Shea (R-Spokane Valley), who was removed from the House Republican Caucus after an independent investigation found he participated in domestic terrorism. Finding 7.4: Campaign Website Lists Stewart Rhodes (Oath Keepers) and Washington 3% as Endorsers CRITICAL

What happened
Marshall's own campaign website (marshall4wa.com/endorsements) lists Stewart Rhodes, founder of the Oath Keepers, convicted of seditious conspiracy for his role in the January 6 Capitol attack and sentenced to 18 years in federal prison, as an endorser. The same page lists 'Washington 3%' (the militia Marshall founded) and 'Make Washington Red Again' as endorsers. That a sitting state legislator publicly claims the endorsement of a convicted seditionist is extraordinary. The endorsement page also lists 'Constitutional Grassroots Movement' and 'March For Our Rights.'

Finding 7.5: Healthcare and Pharmaceutical PACs Dominate Donor List MODERATE

What happened
As of July 2026, Marshall's 2026 campaign has raised $21,450 from 26 contributions, the large majority from healthcare, pharmaceutical, and insurance PACs. His single largest donor is the Pharmaceutical Research and Manufacturers of America (PhRMA) at $2,400. Other max or near-max contributors include Altria Client Services (tobacco, $1,200), Johnson & Johnson ($1,200), Delta Dental ($1,200), Premera Blue Cross ($1,200), Regence ($1,200), Proliance Surgeons PAC ($1,200), the Washington Medical PAC ($1,200), Cigna, DaVita, and Elevance Health. Marshall sits on the House Health Care and Wellness Committee as Assistant Ranking Member, the committee with jurisdiction over the industries funding his campaign.
