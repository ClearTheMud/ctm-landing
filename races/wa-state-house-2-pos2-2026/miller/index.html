1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

Five years Eatonville Planning Commission. Advocate of sustainable development and the environment.

Other Professional Experience

Employed 42 years and counting as Power Lineman in district. Keeping the lights on and serving the public was and is my passion.

Education

Graduated Bethel 1975. Couple years college then chose Lineman’s school as my career.

Community Service

I was key player developing Smallwood Park in Eatonville. Involved in conception, planning and construction of Bud Blancher Trail. Volunteered for Mashel River salmon enhancement project. Led many Pack Forest clean ups. Provided housing for recovering addicts. Hosted many scholarship fundraisers. Much more. Too much to list. Lifetime of public service commitment.

Statement

I’ve lived my life in the district. Served the citizens faithfully for 42 years, all weather, all hours. Can anybody name one state program not rife with corruption? The job of government should be to protect rights, not line pockets. Cap gasoline at 3$. Fully fund education, teachers first. Minimum wage $50. Alternate living wage. Subsidize media, outlaw hate speech. Respect all groups. Free car and health insurance. Protect women’s rights. Diversity, equity, inclusion. No property tax for seniors. No sales tax on necessities. Housing a right. End hate. Think of me as your favorite uncle you can always call.
