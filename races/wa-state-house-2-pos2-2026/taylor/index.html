Community Service

Angela has a long history serving this community. Angela has helped with community feeds with first responders for Salvation Army as well as volunteering with: Habitat for Humanity, Scouts, Boosters, School Levies, Food Banks, Yelm Rising, Rotary, Yelm Lions - tirelessly advocating for youth, families, and small businesses for 20 years. Endorsed by Port Commissioner Jerry Toompas, City Councilmembers Carolyn Cox and Griselda Arias, Washington State Labor Council AFL-CIO and more!

Statement

I'm a working mom, small business owner, and caregiver. Like you, I am navigating rising housing costs, unaffordable childcare, and a healthcare system designed to profit from our struggles, not solve them. While families like ours work harder to fall further behind, career politicians beholden to corporate and insurance special interests keep their jobs in Olympia. That ends now. 

I'm running to bring fully-funded schools, accessible rural healthcare, and responsible budgets to Olympia, not empty promises but real action. Rural families deserve representatives who show up and fight for them. A vote for me is a vote for working families!
