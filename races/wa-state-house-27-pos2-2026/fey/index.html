1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (General Election 2026)

Elected Experience

State Representative (2013-present), Transportation Committee Chair; Tacoma City Councilmember (2006-2012)



Other Professional Experience

Director, Washington State University Energy Program; Former Energy Services Manager, Tacoma Power; Board Member, Puget Sound Regional Council Economic Development District and Transportation Policy Board; Board Member, Pierce Transit and Sound Transit; Vice-Chair, Puget Sound Clean Air Agency; Chair, Tacoma Public Utility Board; Board Member, Tacoma-Pierce County Health Department



Education

B.A. Political Science, University of Washington; Master's in Public Administration, University of Puget Sound


Community Service

Board Member, Boys and Girls Club of South Puget Sound; Board President, Tacoma-Pierce County Big Brothers/Big Sisters; Rotary 8

Statement

It’s an honor to serve you and your family, delivering on priorities like affordable and emergency housing, expanding access to behavioral health, and improving public safety. As House Transportation Chair, I help create jobs and build the roads, bridges, ferries, and pedestrian infrastructure we need to keep our communities and commutes safe. 

But there is more to be done. With your vote, I’ll continue to advocate for affordable childcare, K-12 excellence, higher education access, and workforce training. I’m proud of my 100% pro-choice and LGBTQ rights voting record. Endorsed: State Labor Council, Building Trades, WA Conservation Action, Firefighters, Nurses, Teachers.
