Statement

District 14 faces severe crises: skyrocketing utility bills, yearly droughts, contaminated rivers, and rising overdose, domestic violence, and suicide numbers. We must stop throwing public funds at revolving-door jail systems and non-profit grants that fail to deliver results.

My solutions are immediate. I will establish secure, long-term stabilization wards—not police in hospitals—and mandate 72-hour mental health evaluations for domestic violence offenders. I will secure grants for agricultural drip irrigation, cover high-evaporation channels with solar panels, and solarize public buildings. I will advocate to allow EBT for nutritious hot foods, prioritizing public health, infrastructure, and family healing. Constitution law advocate.
