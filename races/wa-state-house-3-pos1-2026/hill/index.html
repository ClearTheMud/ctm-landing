1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (General Election 2026)

Elected Experience

Elected to the State House in November 2024. She serves as Vice Chair of the Housing Committee and is on the Early Learning & Human Services and Capital Budget Committees.




Other Professional Experience

Civil Litigation Attorney, 19 years; Adjunct Law Professor, Gonzaga Law School, 2019-2023; Spokane Realtor, 2023-2025; Interim Editor, The Black Lens, 2024.




Education

Rogers High School; AA, Spokane Community College; BA Sociology, University of Washington; JD, Southwestern Law School.




Community Service

Prior service on Northwest Justice Project, Pre-Employment Preparation Program (PEPP), Vanessa Behan, Inland Northwest Business Alliance (INBA), Spokane NAACP, Spokane County Bar Racial Equity Task Force 2020- 2021 boards.




Statement

I’m running to continue fighting for working families. Born and raised in Hillyard, I’m a mom, small business owner, and advocate. In Olympia, I advanced Spokane-initiated legislation adding tribal representation to local health boards, establishing land banks, and worked with labor to preserve our Waste-to-Energy Facility and save Spokane millions. I helped remove barriers to building condominiums, led on the historic millionaire tax and supported critical protections for immigrants and voting rights. I helped secure investments for affordable housing, economic development and to complete the N-S Freeway.

Endorsed: State Labor Council, Working Families Party, State Nurses, Washington Housing Alliance, FairVoteWA. 




Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, State Representative Pos. 1. Accessed 2026-10-10. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
