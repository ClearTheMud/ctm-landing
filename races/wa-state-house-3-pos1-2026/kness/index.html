Statement

I’m running because I believe our communities deserve leadership that listens, communicates honestly, and stays connected to the people it serves. I’m not a career politician, I’m a veteran, father, and working Washington resident who understands the everyday challenges facing families, workers, and small businesses. Through my “Your Voice = My Vote” approach, I want constituents to have a more direct voice in the decisions that impact their lives. I believe government works best when elected officials listen first and lead with transparency, accountability, and common sense. Together, we can build stronger communities and restore trust between citizens and government.
