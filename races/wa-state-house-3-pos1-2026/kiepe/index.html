1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (General Election 2026)

Elected Experience

None

Other Professional Experience

I moved to Spokane in 2000 after accepting Director of Sales with Hollister-Stier Laboratories.  In 2006 I started my own business and currently sell Medicare



Education

I graduated from University of Memphis in 1985 with a BS in Biology and Chemistry and went back to school for an MBA and graduated in 1998 with an MBA in Business Administration.



Community Service

I joined the Spokane County Conservancy Board in 2019 and am currently the Chair.  I have also volunteered in Boy Scouts of America.  I also volunteered on different committees with Boy Scouts and Family Camp.  



Statement

"Why are politics tearing apart families and communities instead of improving lives?

I believe government should represent all of us, not divide us.  Seniors and young families can't afford housing, and too many are unhoused or struggling to find stability, dignity, and happiness.  We are tired of anger, blame, and constant division that gets in the way of real progress.  I believe we need leaders who bring people together, focus on practical solutions, and restore trust in government.  Housing, public safety, and opportunity must come first.  It's time to build bridges, work together, and move Washington forward today for all"
