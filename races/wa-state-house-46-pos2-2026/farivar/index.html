1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

Darya Farivar is a Washington State Representative for the 46th LD and the first Middle Eastern woman elected to the Washington State Legislature.

Other Professional Experience

Darya serves as an ADA & Disability Equity Specialist with the King County Executive’s Office, where she works to expand accessibility, equity, and inclusion for vulnerable communities.

Education

B.A., University of Redlands.

Community Service

Seattle Women’s Commission Co-Chair; State Special Education Advisory Council Policy Chair; Peyvand Board Member; Washington Coalition for Police Accountability Board Member; recognized as the Disability Community Champion by the Community Employment Alliance and SEIU 775 legislator of the year.

Statement

I believe government works best when leaders listen to people, identify problems, and deliver real solutions. When Lake City lost a major grocery store, we saw how corporate loopholes could leave neighborhoods without access to food and pharmacies. That’s why I passed legislation preventing stores from blocking new grocers and pharmacies. When families struggled to access care, and caregivers faced barriers to employment, I passed legislation expanding the caregiver workforce. That’s the kind of practical, solutions-focused leadership I bring to Olympia every day.

Endorsements: 46th District Dems, Pro-Choice Washington, Washington State Labor Council, Attorney General Brown, and Congresswoman Jayapal.
