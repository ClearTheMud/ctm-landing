Statement

I am your current Grays Harbor County Treasurer and a lifelong resident of Grays Harbor. I was appointed by the County Commissioners to fill the vacancy following the former treasurer’s retirement.  With nearly nine years of experience as Chief Deputy Treasurer, I am arguably the most qualified person to assume the role. During my tenure, I worked closely with the former treasurer gaining extensive knowledge of every aspect of the treasurer’s office. I can assure you that experience matters.

My paramount focus is TRUST.  As custodian of all county funds, acting as the bank for the county and the districts, my role must be executed in a non-partisan manner.  With a commitment to impartiality, I have gained the trust and respect of school district business managers, fire district chiefs and secretaries, and county department personnel.

I also prepare financial reports for the annual state audit.  When the state implemented a new banking and revenue schedule, I was the only chief deputy in the state to complete it correctly. Another year, I received a Leadership and Service Award for exemplary teamwork and outstanding dedication.

Along with my devoted staff, I look forward to continuing a trajectory of growth and success.

Finding 6.1: Appointed treasurer through a party process, never elected MODERATE

What happened
Hill was appointed treasurer in July 2025 through a partisan nomination process after Kenneth Albert retired. The Grays Harbor Democrats submitted three nominees (Hill, Susan Ancich, Mike Cooper), and the BOCC selected Hill by a 2-1 vote, with Commissioner Vickie Raines dissenting. He has not won a competitive election for the office.

Defense
The appointment followed the legally required process for filling partisan vacancies, and Hill's selection cited nearly nine years as Chief Deputy Treasurer. The dissenting vote may have concerned process or other candidates rather than Hill's qualifications.
