Finding 5.1: Appointed Incumbent — Selected by Party Process, Not Elected MODERATE

What happened
Hill was appointed Treasurer in July 2025 through a partisan nomination process after Kenneth Albert retired. The Grays Harbor Democrats submitted three nominees (Hill, Susan Ancich, Mike Cooper), and the BOCC selected Hill by a 2-1 vote, with Commissioner Vickie Raines dissenting. He has never won a competitive election. The non-unanimous vote suggests the appointment was not universally embraced even within the BOCC.

Defense
Hill's appointment followed the legally mandated process for filling partisan vacancies under WA law. His selection was based on nearly 9 years as Chief Deputy Treasurer — he is the most operationally experienced person for the role. The dissenting vote may have been about process or other candidates rather than Hill's qualifications.
