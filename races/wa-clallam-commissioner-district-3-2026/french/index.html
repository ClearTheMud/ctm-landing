1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

4 years, Clallam County Commissioner; 5 years, Port Angeles City Councilmember.

Other Professional Experience

15 years, downtown Port Angeles business owner.

Education

Bachelor of Arts (Music) from Central Washington University; Associate of Arts from Peninsula College through Running Start Program; 2000 graduate of Port Angeles High School.

Community Service

Active Nor’Wester Rotary Club member; Hamilton Elementary PTO Vice President, 6 years; Olympic Peninsula Community Clinic Board Member, 6 years; Port Angeles Chamber of Commerce Board Member, 5 years; Port Angeles School District Capital Advisory Committee, 3 years.

Statement

I was born and raised in Clallam County, and I found opportunities here that allowed me to work hard and get ahead. I’m running for re-election because I want my experience to be possible for every resident of Clallam County. 

While in office, I’ve supported affordable housing development, public safety enhancements, infrastructure projects, and common sense policies that protect our natural resources. I’ve been an advocate of conservative budgeting: maintaining services, avoiding debt, and protecting County reserves.

Recently, I led the Recompete team that created a regional strategic plan and acquired $35.6 million for economic and workforce development. Our goal is to create new good jobs, while also training, supporting, and connecting Clallam residents to career pathways so we can restore the promise that hard work pays off and see our communities thrive.

There’s more work to do to ensure all our residents can be safe and prosperous. I have deep roots in our community, and a wide range of private, non-profit, and public sector experience with a track record of collaboration and problem-solving. I promise to show up, listen, and deliver for the citizens of Clallam County. I humbly ask for your vote.
