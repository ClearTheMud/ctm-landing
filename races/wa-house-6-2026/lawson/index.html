I am not a politician. I am the guy who kept showing up when everyone else wrote off the 6th District. With zero dollars raised or spent, I received a combined 118,178 votes, proving real grassroots energy beats the consultant-donor machine every time. Now Endorsed Six years deep, I have seen the system up close, and I have never backed down. I stand for juvenile rehab that turns lost kids into productive citizens, tribal empowerment, crushing Big Pharma middlemen, secure elections, and policies that put working families, seniors, and veterans first. No more games. No more gaslighting. No more career politicians hiding from debate. Three statewide campaigns in, with 118,178 votes and zero spending, I am the most experienced, most consistent, and most battle-tested America First candidate in the 6th. Together we finish what we started. Leon A.
