1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

Brian has no experience as an elected official.

Other Professional Experience

Brian served in the United States Army and the National Guard as a Field Medic in various types of units for 16 years.  

Education

Brian has a High School Diploma with some lower division college classes.  All medical certifications have expired since his discharge from the military.

Community Service

Since his medical discharge, Brian has volunteered with local community organizations such as Scouting America (BSA) at Camp Hahobas, the Community Emergency Response Team, the American Legion, and the Veterans of Foreign Wars.

Statement

I am running for office because both major political parties have done a disservice to the American People.  Our current political situation at the federal level can be directly traced back to decisions by both parties that placed party over Country.  These decisions not only put us on our current track but went against our most basic Democratic Ideals.  Both the Dems and the Reps have chosen themselves to our detriment.  Whether it be a backroom deal to secure a nomination in exchange for a Congressional seat or choosing a candidate because of money, they are two sides of the same tarnished, fiat-based coin.   In our lifetimes, we have lost many of our Constitutional protections.  These include civil forfeiture, qualified immunity, gerrymandering, and disregarding the will of the people.  Congress's impotence must be remedied.
