1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience
State Representative (2021-present); Precinct Committee Officer (2014-present)

Other Professional Experience
Former Executive Director of the largest nonprofit organization in the region dedicated to protecting the rights of consumers, workers, and patients. I also served as Legislative Director for Congresswoman Gabby Giffords, crafting policies to help military families and promote clean energy technologies.

Education

BA, American University; Executive Certificate in Public Leadership, Harvard University

Community Service
As a past board member of Pro-Choice Washington and lifelong reproductive justice advocate, I’ve fought to protect and expand abortion access. I’m passionate about boosting our creative economy on the ArtsFund board. Proud PTA mom of two Seattle Public School students.

Statement
I’m running for re-election to continue fighting for working families and a more affordable Washington. I supported tax cuts on everyday essentials while making our tax system fairer so we can better fund public schools, childcare, housing, and health care. 

As Labor Committee Chair, I’ve protected workers from wage theft and unfair noncompete agreements while supporting good-paying union jobs. I’m leading on climate action to reduce plastic waste and expand recycling. As a gun safety champion, I passed groundbreaking legislation to require a permit to purchase a firearm. I’ll always stand up for people and push for bold change. 

Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, State Representative Pos. 2. Accessed 2026-07-16. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
