Finding 5.1: State Republican Party lawsuit over OmniBallot ballot curing LOW

What happened
In 2024 the Washington State Republican Party sued King County Elections, led by Director Julie Wise, over its use of DemocracyLive's OmniBallot online tool to help voters resolve signature challenges on their ballots. The suit alleged that roughly 2,100 primary ballots were improperly cured, that the method violated state law, and that it affected the Commissioner of Public Lands primary, in which Dave Upthegrove advanced to the general election by about 49 votes over Sue Kuehl Pederson. Coverage cited Secretary of State Jim Walsh. The disposition of the litigation is not established in the reviewed sources.
Source tier
T2
Political impact
Low
Defense
King County Elections stated that OmniBallot provided an additional lawful option for voters to resolve signature challenges, that the tool had been deployed across thousands of elections, and that its use was authorized by state law.
