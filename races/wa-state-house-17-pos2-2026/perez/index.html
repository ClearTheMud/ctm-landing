1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

Vancouver City Council, 2022-present, where I focus on public safety, affordable housing, fiscal responsibility, listening, and bringing people together.

Other Professional Experience

Small business owner, USDA Forest Service; 30+ years public lands and natural resource management as a biologist, deputy monument manager, and acting district ranger. 

Education

M.S. in Fisheries, Oregon State University; B.S. in Wildlife Management, Texas Tech University; Executive Women in Leadership, Cornell University.

Community Service

Clark County Ending Community Homelessness Organization, Elder Justice Center, National League of Cities, and FISH; Commissioner of Washington State Parks and Recreation, and WA State Housing Finance Commission; Co-Chair of East Old Evergreen Highway Neighborhood Association.
 


Statement

As your legislator I will lead with purpose, integrity, and results. Through service on city council and in the Forest Service, I’ve built a strong record of collaboration, problem-solving, and earning trust across our community. 

I’m running for State House to make life more affordable by lowering housing costs, modernizing infrastructure, investing in public schools, improving access to healthcare and childcare, and supporting first responders who keep our communities safe. I will prioritize fiscal responsibility, practical solutions, and an accountable government that works for you. I’d be honored to represent you full-time and ask for your vote.
