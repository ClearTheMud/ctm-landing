Community Service
Serving on many city, community and county boards.  I truly care and want results. We need leaders to bring people together and deliver real results, not just rhetoric. As a House of Representatives, I voted no on a state income tax because hard working families should keep more of what they earn. 

Statement
I’m committed to strengthening public safety, expanding affordable housing, and holding our state government accountable to the people it serves. With affordability as the top issue facing working families, Southwest Washington needs practical leadership focused on lowering costs, not raising taxes.  I have earned trust and respect from my peers on both sides and working on keeping this a great State. My vision is for a safer, stronger, and more prosperous Southwest Washington where families can afford to live, work, and build their future.

I’m David Stuebe, a servant leader delivering real results. I’d be honored to earn your vote
