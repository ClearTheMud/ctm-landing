1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

Proudly serving as your State Representative since 2025.

Other Professional Experience

Currently serving as Senior Policy Advisor for Seattle’s Human Services Department. Previously served as Chief of Staff for City Council President, Mayor’s Office Labor Liaison, and Campaign Director for multiple progressive initiatives/candidates.

Education

University of Washington, BA in Political Science 2010


Community Service

Seasoned track record of results working for our community. Managed SeaTac's $15 minimum wage campaign in 2013, first in the country to pass on the ballot. Led Seattle's Honest Elections initiative, which established the city's democracy voucher program and served as Field Director for the Washington Low Income Housing Alliance Action Fund.

Statement

My life’s work is about building a better and more just world, and that drives the legislation I have sponsored and passed. This biennium, I contributed to passing the Millionaires Tax, delivering long awaited tax reform to benefit working Washingtonians. I passed bills to guarantee meal and rest breaks for healthcare workers, give domestic workers the protections they’ve long deserved, keep transit moving, and ensure employees’ privacy and bodily autonomy. I’m honored to continue fighting for you and the Fighting 34th!

Endorsements: 34thLD Democrats, WA State Labor Council, AG Nick Brown, County Councilmember Teresa Mosqueda, UFCW, WFSE/AFSCME and many more.
