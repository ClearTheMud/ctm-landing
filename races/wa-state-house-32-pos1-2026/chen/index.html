1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience
Edmonds City Council Member, 2021-Current (2nd Term)

Other Professional Experience
Licensed CPA, retired small business owner, former Fortune 500 auditor, and financial professional with experience in auditing, budgeting, strategic planning, compliance, and organizational accountability.

Education
Bachelor’s Degree in Accounting and Master’s Degree in Accounting from the University of Iowa, and Master’s Degree in Business Administration from Western Washington University.

Community Service
Co-founder of the Asian Service Center supporting underserved communities across South Snohomish County. Edmonds Rotary member, Lynnwood Food Bank board member, former Seattle Bing Kung Family Association president, organizer of the Rally Against Hate, creator of Second Chance Scholarship at Edmonds College.

Statement
I’m running for State Representative because too many working families, seniors, and young people are being priced out of the communities they helped build. Our district needs solutions that address affordability, housing, public safety, transportation, and opportunity while keeping government accountable to the people it serves. As an immigrant who achieved the American Dream through hard work and opportunity, I understand what is at stake for families trying to build a future here. I will fight for affordable communities, strong public services, economic opportunity, and responsible government that works for every part of our district. List of endorsements at ElectWillChen.com.
