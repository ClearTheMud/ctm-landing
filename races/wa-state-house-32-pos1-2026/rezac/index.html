Statement

Washington taxpayers are paying more and getting less. Taxes rise, schools decline, public safety suffers, and accountability is nowhere to be found. As a former voting Democrat of 25 years, Lisa has seen firsthand that one-party control is failing the people it serves. After serving as LD32 Republican Chair and King County Republican Vice Chair, Lisa understands the urgent need for accountable leadership in Olympia.

Her priorities are affordability, restoring excellence in education, safer communities, and exposing waste, fraud, and mismanagement of taxpayer dollars. Lisa will bring transparency, common sense, and results to Olympia. Endorsements: electlisarezac.com
