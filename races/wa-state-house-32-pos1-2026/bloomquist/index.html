Community Service

My Chilean mother escaped authoritarianism to become a US citizen. As a Latino American who champions civil rights, I testified on successful legislation to demask ICE. Former board member, KD Hall Foundation: supported BIPOC girls’ education and leadership. Promoted school funding in Olympia via the PTA and coached youth sports. Statement

We're all economically squeezed. Raised by a single mom, I understand affordability is everything. Quality education, healthcare, and housing should be standards, not struggles. Like all taxpayers, I expect government to deliver results. I've led first responder roundtables envisioning a safer Aurora Avenue. I’ll advance evidence-based solutions to address homelessness. I'll author AI legislation that protects children, benefits society, and centers renewable energy.  I'm raising two daughters; women’s rights are paramount. I’ll scrutinize spending, prioritize your concerns, and work to earn your vote!

Endorsements: Our legislators Senator Jesse Salomon and Representative Lauren Davis, Senator Valdez, Lynnwood Mayor, and more.
