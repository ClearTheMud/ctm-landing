1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

Shoreline City Council (2016–present); Deputy Mayor (2020-22); Mayor (2022–24).

Other Professional Experience

Paramedic (1994-98); King County Deputy Prosecutor (1999-2005); United Nations War Crimes Prosecutor (2005-06); Legal Director, Environmental Nonprofit Futurewise (2006-08); Intellectual Property Attorney (2008-present).

Education

J.D., NYU School of Law; M.A., Middle Eastern History, Tufts University; B.A., History, UCLA 

Community Service

Regional Crisis Response Agency Board; Regional EMS Levy Advanced Life Support Committee Chair; King County Conservation Voters Board; Shoreline Planning Commission Member and Chair; Salmon Recovery Council Board; Continuum of Care Board; Habitat for Humanity; PTA President; involved parent and community volunteer.

Statement

I became a paramedic because I believed every life was worth saving. That same conviction led me to prosecuting war criminals, defending our environment, running for office. In Shoreline, I expanded affordable housing and transit, brought mental health professionals into the field, built sidewalks and parks — all while balancing the budget.

Now I’ll take that record to Olympia. I’ll make housing, energy, and childcare affordable. Fund education and early learning. Demand real climate action. Reform our tax code to be fair, progressive, and sustainable. Endorsed by Snohomish Democrats, Shoreline and South County Firefighters, Rep. Ryu and 40+ elected officials.
