1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (General Election 2026)

Elected Experience

Currently State Representative in my second term.



Other Professional Experience

My parents opened a mechanical contracting company in our garage when I was 5, instilling in me the value of hard work. I’ve taught at a preschool, served as WWU's Assistant Director of Government Relations, and worked in the Governor’s Office to connect citizens, small businesses, tribes, and local governments with state resources. My wife Heather and I live in Bellingham with our son, Malcolm.



Education

Bachelor’s, WWU; Master’s, University of Washington



Community Service

Board Member, Washington State Leadership Board; Former Board Member: Bellingham Food Bank, Children’s Campaign Fund, City of Bellingham’s Community Development Advisory Board.



Statement

My family feels the impact of rising costs just like you do. That's why as your state representative, I’ve worked hard to lower costs for seniors, improve public safety and increase access to education, housing, and healthcare. I’ve supported small businesses so they can create living wage jobs in our community. Each legislative session, I’ve helped secure millions in state funding for local projects including flood protection, community centers, and an emergency response facility. Endorsements include Washington State Labor Council; Washington Education Association; Local Firefighters IAFF 106; Lummi Nation Chairman Anthony Hillaire; Governor Bob Ferguson; Whatcom County Sheriff Donnell Tanksley.
