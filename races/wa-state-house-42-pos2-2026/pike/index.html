1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (General Election 2026)

Elected Experience

First-time candidate for public office. Previously served as elected vice president and president of a law enforcement guild. Served on professional boards.

Other Professional Experience

Small business owner. More than 23 years of military service. Senior leadership roles training and mentoring soldiers. Seventeen years in law enforcement working with families in crisis and seeing firsthand how untreated mental illness and addiction impact public safety.



Education

Graduated from Ferndale High School. Professional development through military leadership and professional education.

Community Service

Youth sports coach in Ferndale. Volunteer board member. Helped rebuild community baseball fields. Supported officers, families and the Whatcom County community through my guild’s benevolent committees.

Statement

Families are being priced out of Whatcom County. Seniors are choosing between prescriptions and groceries, and young families wonder whether they can still afford to build their future here. My family has lived here for generations, so these challenges are deeply personal.

After nearly two decades in law enforcement, often sitting at kitchen tables on people’s hardest days, I have learned that people come before politics. I am running to lower living costs, strengthen public safety by addressing the behavioral health crisis, and bring accountability and transparency to state government. With your vote I’ll tackle these challenges head on.
