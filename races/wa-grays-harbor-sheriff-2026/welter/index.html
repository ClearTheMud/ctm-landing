1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (General Election 2026)

Elected Experience

Current Mayor of Westport.



Other Professional Experience

Worked as a financial analyst before joining the Coast Guard where I returned to Westport. I served as a Fish & Wildlife Officer in the county and am now a Detective for the Gray’s Harbor Sheriff’s Office. I have over 12 years’ experience in law enforcement, serving in roles that include Resident Deputy, Narcotics K9 Handler, Fire Investigator, SWAT member, and Defensive Tactics and OC Instructor.



Education

Associates degree from TCC; BA in Economics from PLU; Certified Peace Officer from WSCJTC.



Community Service

Volunteered over five years with The Mountaineers at Meany Ski Lodge, where families learn backcountry skills.



Statement

I am running for Sheriff because the citizens of Grays Harbor, along with the deputies who serve them, deserve strong leadership, meaningful engagement, and consistent representation. This role is not just about enforcement. It is about accountability, visibility, and building trust within the community while supporting the men and women who serve it every day.

The Sheriff is the face of law enforcement for the county and must be a public-facing and accessible representative of the people. As Sheriff, I will attend public meetings to hear directly from residents, provide updates on the Sheriff’s Office, and ensure transparency in its operations and needs.

I believe the challenges facing the Sheriff’s Office should be openly discussed, including the condition of our facilities and the reduction in staffing despite population growth. I will also work with state legislators to secure available funding and ensure the voice of Grays Harbor is heard when state policies are being considered.

As a mayor, I have gained experience making decisions that reflect the needs of the people.

I bring experience from both public and private sectors, including developing business plans, managing budgets, and working with state legislators to secure funding for the communities I serve.



Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, Sheriff. Accessed 2026-10-10. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.

Finding 5.1: Runs against his current supervisor, the incumbent sheriff LOW

What happened
Welter is a GHCSO detective running against incumbent Sheriff Wallace, who is in his chain of command. There is no public evidence of union endorsement or organized internal support for his candidacy.

Political impact

Defense
Welter also holds elected and appointed roles outside the sheriff's office (Mayor of Westport and Hospital District Board member). A challenger running from within is not unprecedented in Washington sheriff races.

Finding 5.2: No command rank in about eight years at the sheriff's office MODERATE

What happened
Welter joined GHCSO in 2018 as a resident deputy for the South Beach area and currently serves as a detective. He has about eight years of county law enforcement experience and no documented supervisory or command rank within the sheriff's office. His earlier law enforcement work was as a Fish and Wildlife officer.
