Finding 5.1: Appointed Incumbent — Never Elected, Chosen by Party Process MODERATE

What happened
Walker was appointed prosecutor on March 17, 2026, through a partisan nomination process. The Grays Harbor Democrats submitted three nominees (Walker, Brian Lane, Mac Jardine), and the BOCC selected Walker. He has never won a competitive election.
Source tier
T2
Political impact
Moderate
Defense
Washington law requires partisan appointment when a partisan office is vacated mid-term. The process is transparent and legally mandated. Walker's selection was based on demonstrated qualifications — 11 years in the office plus statewide training leadership.

Finding 5.2: Left Grays Harbor for Three Years — Returning Outsider Perception MODERATE

What happened
Walker left the Grays Harbor Prosecutor's Office in December 2022 after 11 years to take a statewide position with WAPA. He returned only upon appointment in March 2026. His opponent Crawford has been actively prosecuting cases in Grays Harbor since August 2024.

Finding 5.3: Inherits a Dysfunctional Office — Tillotson's Legacy HIGH

What happened
Walker inherited an office plagued by two internal investigations, high staff turnover (of 11 positions that received raises in 2023, only 3 employees remained), and what Commissioner Miller called a 'negative reputation' hindering recruitment. Walker must demonstrate he can stabilize the office while simultaneously campaigning.
