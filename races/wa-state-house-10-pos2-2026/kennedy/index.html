Statement

As a Constitutionalist, I will defend the rights and freedoms guaranteed to every citizen while keeping govt accountable, transparent and limited. Parents, not bureaucrats, should guide their children's education, health and values. Women's rights, privacy, safety & equal opportunities while ensuring fairness will be vehemently protected. Ensuring responsible budgeting, fiscal responsibility (no income tax, lower taxes), safe communities, supporting law enforcement, and policies strengthening businesses, families, and individual liberties. With honesty and integrity, principled leadership, independent thinking, and respect for law and order, I'll work diligently to restore trust, protecting citizens' freedoms before politics, for future generations.
