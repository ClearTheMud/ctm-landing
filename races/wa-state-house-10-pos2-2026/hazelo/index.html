1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

International Association of Machinists Union Steward, Congressional Candidate 2020, Precinct Committee Officer 

Other Professional Experience

38 years Navy and government contracting, USS Nimitz, VP-40/69, NAS Whidbey. Deployed from the Middle East and Asia to counter-narcotics operations in Central America. Worked as an Intelligence Analyst and combat simulation technician. Functional test technician for the Boeing 787, taught aviation maintenance and manufacturing. Small business owner, money manager, budget consultant.

Education

Skagit Valley College, Embry Riddle, Central Texas 

Community Service

Volunteer/supporter of VFW, Elks, Toys for Tots, Holiday House, Habitat for Humanity, WAIF, Calvary Chapel, Oak Harbor youth football, and Counselor at Camp Moran for Oak Harbor Schools.

Statement

I am proud to run for LD10! I want Washington State to be affordable for families, seniors, and young adults. My 38 years of service to our country have put me in a position to represent our best interests. I believe in strong communities and healthy economic opportunities. We must stop unsustainable spending that has led to ever-increasing taxation, reduce unnecessary and burdensome regulations that drive up the cost of housing and expenses, fix our ferry systems, roads, and bridges, end cashless bail, and continue to support NAS Whidbey. To ensure prosperity for future generations, vote Hazelo.

Thank you!
