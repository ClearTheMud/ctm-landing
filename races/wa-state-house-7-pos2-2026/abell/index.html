Statement

I love our country. I am a Christian, father, conservative, veteran, constitutionally-conservative attorney, and part owner of Long Rifle Ranch in Inchelium. My family has four generations in this district. I was born in Colville, went K-12 at Inchelium (go Hornets!), and enlisted in the Navy after 9-11.

Today, I am running for reelection to: 1) Protect our constitutional rights; 2) Support our rural way of life; and 3) Make Washington a more prosperous state to raise a family and run a business. I took an oath to defend the Constitution, and will do so in the Legislature.
