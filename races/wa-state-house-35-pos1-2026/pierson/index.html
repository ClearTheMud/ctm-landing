1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (General Election 2026)

Elected Experience

Precinct Committee Officer

Other Professional Experience

30+ years technical and team leadership, Army Special Forces Veteran. Product manager, engineer, architect, inventor, instructor, consultant for large-scale software systems and nine-figure projects worldwide. Team builder, leading complex problem-solving and delivering results.

Education

Graduated high school early. Completed Army Special Forces Qualification Course, Airborne, communications and electronics training, cross-trained in medical and engineering roles. Additional college and technical coursework in engineering, statistics, programming, business management. Performed graduate-level work.

Community Service

Active in efforts to protect residents from eminent domain impacts in Thurston County. HOA architecture committee member.  Regular participant in the Commercial Aviation Workgroup. Member of Rotary. Trail Builder.

Statement

Government should not be an enemy of the people. It should be a steward that makes systems work for people. I will listen to you, protect your right to be who you are, work for fair taxes, housing you can afford, better roads, safe communities, small businesses that can thrive, and climate policy that is practical and lowers costs for families.

Healthcare ties this together. When profit comes first, families pay more and get less. I will push for patient-first healthcare solutions that lower costs and expand access without breaking what works. Endorsements: Kitsap, Mason, Thurston, and 35th LD Democrats.
