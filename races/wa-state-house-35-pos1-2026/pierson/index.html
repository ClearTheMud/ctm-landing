Statement

Government should not be an enemy of the people. It should be a steward that makes systems work for people. I will listen to you, protect your right to be who you are, work for fair taxes, housing you can afford, better roads, safe communities, small businesses that can thrive, and climate policy that is practical and lowers costs for families.

Healthcare ties this together. When profit comes first, families pay more and get less. I will push for patient-first healthcare solutions that lower costs and expand access without breaking what works. Endorsements: Kitsap, Mason, Thurston, and 35th LD Democrats.
