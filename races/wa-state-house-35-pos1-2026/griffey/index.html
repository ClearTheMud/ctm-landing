Community Service

Advocated and supported Turning Pointe for domestic violence survivors (women, men, kids, and pets) Homes for our Troops, Habitat for Humanity, United Way, fed the hungry at St. David’s, chaperoned school events and started as a volunteer firefighter at 16 before making it a 30+ year career. I am dedicated to our community. 



Statement

I enjoy working with you and across the aisle to find real solutions for accountability and affordability. I voted to support first responders (not raid pensions), protect kids from predators, fought for community notifications of sexually violent predators and to stop housing them in our neighborhoods, provide first responder training, support small businesses (voted “No” on the income tax) and worked to end loopholes allowing criminals to walk free. I’ve been able to help countless people in our district work through tough issues and get results. Your input matters to me. I would be honored to have your vote.
