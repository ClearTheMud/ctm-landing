Community Service

My education is grounded in hands-on experience. I learned the legal field through paralegal work mentored by the late Jeanette W. Booth. I organized community programs and am a Certified Peer Recovery Coach. 



Statement

I’m Shaena Garberich, a community advocate and Peer Recovery Coach with experience as a paralegal and outreach specialist. I’ve helped individuals navigate housing and recovery challenges. I served as an elected fundraiser, led church outreach, and helped shape policy with the Washington Recovery Alliance. I’m running to fight for affordable housing, behavioral health, and a government that listens.
