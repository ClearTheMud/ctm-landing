What happened

Finding 3.2: Prior Election Loss -- 2019 Lacey City Council LOW

What happened
Zeman lost the 2019 Lacey City Council Position 3 race with approximately 43% of the vote. While a city council loss does not predict a county assessor race outcome, it demonstrates she has been unsuccessful in a prior competitive election. She was subsequently appointed to the Lacey Planning Commission, maintaining civic engagement.
