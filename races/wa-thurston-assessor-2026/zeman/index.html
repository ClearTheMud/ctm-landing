Zonta Club past president; former Hawks Prairie Rotary board member; PCO, Thurston County Democrats; former secretary, Thurston County Democratic Women; 22nd LD Democrats; League of Women Voters; conservation and land trust organizations volunteer, YWCA, Rebuilding Together.

Statement

The Assessor’s Office is more than a technical office; it is an executive leadership role responsible for people, budgets, systems, and fair, accurate property valuations. Thurston County deserves an Assessor who is present, accessible, and focused on making the office work better for the people it serves.

I bring experience in local government, executive-level administration, and small business leadership. I previously worked in the Assessor’s Office, facilitating budget and fiscal management, office operations, and office-wide administration. I continue to serve in county government, supporting public policy, constituent services, and coordination with local and state partners.

As Assessor, I will focus on the real challenges facing the office: workforce, budget, technology, and transparency. Experienced staff are retiring, workloads are growing, and county budgets are tight. I will strengthen training and mentorship, support a successful transition to modern systems, manage resources responsibly, and ensure staff have the tools they need to provide accurate, dependable service.

Property owners deserve clear answers before frustration turns into appeals. I will improve communication, make valuation information easier to understand, and be visible and responsive in the community. My goal is an Assessor’s Office that is fair, professional, accountable, and worthy of public trust.
