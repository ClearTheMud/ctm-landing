Finding 4.1: Insider Candidate — Decade in the Assessor’s Office Under Retiring Incumbent MODERATE

What happened
Olson has worked in the Thurston County Assessor’s Office for approximately 10 years under retiring incumbent Steven J. Drew. While this institutional knowledge is his primary qualification, it ties him to any office culture issues. Notably, Drew had two PDC enforcement cases: a $300 fine in 2012 for “browbeating employees into making contributions” and a $150 settlement in 2022 for soliciting a campaign contribution from an office employee. Olson’s decade under Drew’s leadership could be used to associate him with these ethics lapses.
