Community Service

Statement

I am running for Assessor because I want to ensure residents of our county receive fair, accurate, and transparent appraisals. After a decade of proven experience, where I started as a field appraiser and now lead in a management role, I acquired the vital knowledge and technical expertise required to execute the complex challenges of this office. I am also the only accredited appraiser in this race.

Throughout my journey I have earned the trust and support of the current Assessor and staff. I am spearheading a technology transformation that will make the office more accessible and transparent.

Every parcel matters, and every assessment has a real impact on a taxpayer. This is why I will prioritize ensuring every property owner is fairly represented and heard. Trust is paramount in this job. Not only does this work depend on flawless execution of technical requirements, but it must be coupled with empathy and integrity.

Thurston County is my home. It’s where my wife and I are raising our four children, and I care deeply about our community. I will lead with trust and impartiality, and will be dedicated to serving the people of our county. I ask for your vote.

Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, Assessor. Accessed 2026-07-16. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
