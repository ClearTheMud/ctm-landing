Finding 6.1: Health Care Committee Chair Funded Heavily by Pharmaceutical and Insurance Industry MODERATE

What happened
Bronoske chairs the House Health Care & Wellness Committee, which writes and vets health policy and drug-pricing legislation. His 2026 contributions include maximum or near-maximum donations from a large number of pharmaceutical manufacturers and health insurers whose business the committee regulates, among them CVS Pharmacy, Genentech, Gilead Sciences, AstraZeneca, Eli Lilly, Cigna, Premera Blue Cross, Elevance Health, UnitedHealth Group, Delta Dental of Washington, and DaVita. Ambulance operators AMR Holdco and Olympic Ambulance also contributed. A voter may weigh whether donations from the industries a chair oversees affect that oversight.
Source tier
T1
Political impact
Moderate
Defense
PAC and corporate contributions to committee chairs are routine in Washington legislative races and are capped by state limits. Bronoske's sponsored health legislation, including a price-transparency directory (HB 1686) and expanded cancer-screening coverage (HB 1626), has drawn support from consumer and labor groups as well.
