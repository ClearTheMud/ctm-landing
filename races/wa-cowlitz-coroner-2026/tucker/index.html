1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (General Election 2026)


Serving the people of Cowlitz County as your Coroner has been one of the greatest honors of my life. This role is more than a job, it is a commitment to our community during some of life’s most difficult and emotional moments.

Every day, my team and I are entrusted with caring for those who can no longer speak for themselves while helping families navigate unimaginable loss. With 13 years of investigative experience, I am proud to lead a dedicated team committed to bringing clarity and answers to every case we handle. We approach this responsibility with compassion, professionalism, and integrity, ensuring every individual is treated with dignity and every family with respect.

During my time in office, I have worked to strengthen the Coroner’s Office through improved investigative practices, strong collaboration with local agencies, and a continued commitment to transparency and accountability. I remain focused on providing the highest standard of service while adapting to the evolving needs of Cowlitz County.

I am proud of what we have accomplished together, and I respectfully ask for your support to continue serving our community. Please vote Dana Tucker for re-election as your County Coroner.

Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, Coroner. Accessed 2026-10-10. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
