1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (General Election 2026)

Elected Experience

Grays Harbor District Court Judge Megan Valentine is running for re-election. Appointed in 2021 and elected in 2022, Judge Valentine is proud to be Grays Harbor's first female District Court judge.



Other Professional Experience

With over 20 years of legal experience as a private attorney, prosecutor and advocate for children and families, she brings a well-rounded and practical approach to the bench.



Education

Judge Valentine has a Law Degree from Cornell, a Master of Business Administration from Eastern Washington University, and a Bachelor of Arts from the University of Washington.  

Community Service

Her service includes friends of the library and teaching youth about law and justice.

Statement

Our state and federal constitutions rely on the courts to safeguard your rights and liberties. As a judge for the past five years, I’ve focused on improving access to justice and strengthening public safety. 

I founded the District Court’s first therapeutic court to address high-risk Driving Under the Influence offenses through accountability and treatment. I also expanded District Court’s probation services to include pre-trial monitoring. Both of those reform are vital steps toward preventing crime and saving taxpayer dollars. 

In our county, every dollar matters. Over the past five years I fought for nearly $1 million in state funding to support innovative local programs, and the District Court’s Blake Day event I led refunded nearly half a million dollars from the state to community members.

That kind of progress draws me to serving as a judge. I can make a real difference, not just in the cases I hear each day, but in the community where we all live. 

 A judge or attorney can easily see dozens of cases in a week. But for the person involved in that case, that might be the only time they’re in a courtroom. And that matters to me.
