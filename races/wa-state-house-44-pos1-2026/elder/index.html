1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

PCO. First time seeking office. 



Other Professional Experience

20 Year US Navy Veteran (Naval Aviation): Recipient of 3 Navy & Marine Corps Commendation Medals, 6 Navy Achievement Medals, Military Outstanding Volunteer Service Medal. Business Owner, Farmer, Realtor, Founder of Mil2Vet Podcast for Veterans transitioning to civilian life.



Education

Navy’s Senior Enlisted Leadership Academy, Naval Aviation Enterprise Green Belt, Command Financial Specialist Certification, Defense Acquisition University — Government Financial Management Training, Command Training Team Instructor Certification, Department of Labor Registered Apprenticeships (USMAP). 



Community Service

Organized Multicultural Committees, Orchestrated American Red Cross blood drives, School volunteer, 4-H volunteer, Teaches transition classes for service members at Naval Station Everett.



Statement

Chris and Sarah enjoy raising their children on their Snohomish farm. They dream of a future where their kids — and every child — can afford to live, work, and build a life here. High taxes and regulations now make it hard to raise families and run businesses. Chris will fight to cut waste, lower taxes, expand skills-based education and family-wage jobs, strengthen public safety, and improve affordability so residents can thrive. Chris asks for your vote.

Endorsements: Mayor Hoffman (Snohomish), Reps. Eslick & Low, Sen. Wagoner, Councilmembers Nate Nehring (SnoCo), Connie Allison, Vincent Cavalari (Mill Creek), SnoCo Republican Party.
