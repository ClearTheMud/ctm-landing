1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

State Representative since 2021. Vice Chair of House Transportation Committee. Member of Technology, Economic Development and Veterans Committee. Consumer Protection and Business Committee. Education Committee. Joint Military and Veteran Affairs Committee. Joint Select Committee on Civic Health. Oral History Committee. Precinct Committee Officer.



Other Professional Experience

US Navy Veteran (Aviation Ordnance). Real Estate Agent. Project Coordinator. 



Education

BA in Business Administration, concentration in Management Information Systems from UW Bothell. Certificate in Energy Policy Planning from the University of Idaho.



Community Service

Community Emergency Response Team Certified. Disaster Preparedness Educator. School District Volunteer. International Disaster Response Organization  Volunteer. Various Advisory Boards.



Statement

I’ve always believed in public service, and have demonstrated that commitment by choosing to serve my nation, community, and the people of Washington. I understand that in order to do great things for our state and district we must listen, engage and work together. I'm dedicated to doing just that.  As your Representative, I've prioritized safety in our communities and on our roadways, affordability, job creation, access to education, consumer protection, and environmental preservation. I would be honored to earn your vote for re-election to continue that work.

Endorsements include: Washington State Labor Council, Snohomish County Democratic Central Committee
