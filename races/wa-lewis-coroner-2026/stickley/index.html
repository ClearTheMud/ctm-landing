Statement
Jessica Stickley has called Lewis County home since 2005 and has dedicated her career to serving its people during some of life’s most difficult moments.

Jessica has been in the medicolegal death investigation field for nearly 15 years. She began her work as an on-call Deputy Coroner in 2009, serving through 2019. Her service continued as a Coroner Investigator from 2023 to 2025, and she currently serves as a Medicolegal Death Investigator. In addition, she has been committed to the human services field since 2008, supporting individuals in times of crisis as a mental health professional.

Jessica has a clear mission: to bring compassion, professionalism, and a focus on prevention to the office. She understands that behind every case is a family, a story, and a community impacted by loss. Her priorities include treating every individual and family with dignity and respect, strengthening collaboration with first responders and community partners, improving transparency and communication with the public, and supporting prevention efforts to reduce avoidable deaths.

Jessica believes the coroner’s office should not only serve the deceased but also protect the living through education, awareness, and community engagement. She is committed to serving Lewis County with integrity, empathy, and dedication.
