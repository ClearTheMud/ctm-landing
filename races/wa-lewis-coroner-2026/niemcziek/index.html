Finding 3.1: Active Insider Challenge — Current Deputy Coroner Running Against Her Boss HIGH

What happened
Niemcziek is a current Lewis County Deputy Coroner who filed to run against her direct supervisor, incumbent Coroner Warren McLeod. This is a significant political dynamic — she is challenging the person who hired her and directly oversees her work. The decision to run suggests possible dissatisfaction with office leadership, a belief she can do the job better, or both. It also creates an awkward employment dynamic for the duration of the campaign.

Defense
Running against a long-term incumbent from within the office demonstrates both courage and deep institutional knowledge. Niemcziek knows the office's strengths, weaknesses, and daily operations better than any outside challenger could. She is not the only insider to challenge McLeod — former Deputy Coroner Jessica Stickley (2008-2019) also filed, reinforcing the pattern.
