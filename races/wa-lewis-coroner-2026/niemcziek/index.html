Statement
I am Chelsie Niemcziek and it would be my honor to be your choice as Coroner. I am an Independent candidate for Coroner in Lewis County and the current Chief Deputy Coroner. I am a fellow voter, taxpayer, and citizen with over a decade of uninterrupted service in this field. The Coroner plays an incredibly important role within the community. I am a forward-thinking, rational, qualified, and caring leader who is serious about their vocation while also making decisions based on facts. As a public servant, I work for you, the citizens! What a blessing and a privilege it is to do what I consider my calling in the place that I call home. It is my pledge that every death will be examined objectively, thoroughly, and without bias. This is a crucial component in protecting the rights of victims and families while ensuring that criminal acts do not go unnoticed or unaddressed. As Coroner, I will remain actively involved in programs designed to educate the public and prevent unnecessary tragedy. My ambition is to build an office grounded in the highest of ethical standards and complete transparency. Please vote for Chelsie Niemcziek, she c.a.r.e.s.!

Compassion. Accuracy. Respect. Experience. Service.
