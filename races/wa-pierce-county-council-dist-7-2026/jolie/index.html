Finding 4.1: On the ballot but not campaigning; filed as a backup for another candidate LOW

What happened
Jolie filed as a Republican candidate for District 7 only as a contingency in case Mike Solan was ruled off the ballot. After a court restored Solan to the ballot on May 18, 2026, Jolie's name remained on the August 4 primary ballot because the candidate withdrawal deadline had already passed. She is not running an active campaign, has reported no fundraising or spending to the PDC, supports Solan, and told Gig Harbor Now, "I certainly hope I get zero votes." Her name appears on the ballot for a candidate who is not seeking the office and asks not to receive votes.

Defense
Jolie has been transparent about the reason for her filing and has publicly directed support to another candidate. Her presence on the ballot is a product of the withdrawal-deadline timing, not an attempt to mislead voters.
