Statement

After 34 years, I retired from the fire department as a Battalion Chief.  I couldn’t just sit still, so I started a small construction business and ran for school board to bring back the shop classes. I have been successful at both, employing eight people in my small business and restoring the trades programs to the school district, but now I see another need. We need common sense in our county government.

I’m running as non-partisan because I feel that both parties are broken. Don’t you agree? I am socially liberal, fiscally conservative, and I believe in property rights. I believe in common sense. I’ll listen. I’ll base decisions on facts, not politics. Being the swing vote on this divided council will give me the power to provided you, in district seven, with the representation you deserve.

The primary role of Pierce County is serving our basic needs of public safety and infrastructure services such as hiring deputies and maintaining our rural roads. Let’s stay focused. For Tacoma we need a loud voice demanding that Sound Transit delivers what we’re paying for. I’ll be your advocate. I’m a proven leader. Vote Chuck West!

I’ve served as a founder of Key Free Clinic, two terms with Land Use Advisory, Flood Control Advisory Board, KP Community Council, PC Prosecutors Advisory Committee and many more. Among my awards are Gig Harbor Chambers Public Safety Officer of the year, KP Fire Officer of the year and KP Lions Club Citizen of the year.
