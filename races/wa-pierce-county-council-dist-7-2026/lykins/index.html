Statement

My professional and civic careers have been committed to improving the lives of the people in our community. I’ve been a resident of Pierce County for over 45 years and have called both Gig Harbor and North Tacoma home. I’ve had the honor of serving as a healthcare provider, Council Member, and Pierce County Charter Review Commissioner.

On Gig Harbor Council, I supported environmental protections, increased funding for public safety, improved infrastructure, and worked to expand economic opportunity for families and small businesses. As Pierce County Charter Commissioner, I have a deep understanding of our county's code, how our county government functions, and the ways in which policy impacts people. 

I’m a respectful, collaborative listener who brings people together to solve the issues most important to you. I’ll prioritize investing in public safety, expanding broadband access, and improving road safety with intentional maintenance. I’ll focus on creating affordable housing while protecting our natural environment, ensuring county regulations are clear and fair for small businesses, and expanding job training and workforce development programs and creating job opportunities. I will ensure that the voice of rural areas is heard, and that every community of District 7 is represented. 

I am a thoughtful, experienced leader who knows how to move ideas into policy and build partnerships in our district. I’m proud to be endorsed by US Representative Emily Randall, County Councilmember Robyn Denson and County Executive Ryan Mello, and the Pierce County Central Labor Council.  I’d be honored to earn your support.

Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, Council - District No. 7. Accessed 2026-07-16. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
