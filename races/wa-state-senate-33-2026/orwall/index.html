1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience
State Senator; Vice Chair of Health & Long-Term Care Committee; on Rules, Human Services, Housing, and the Joint Committee on Veterans’ & Military Affairs. Former State Representative; Speaker Pro-Tempore; Member of the National Council of State Legislators Veterans & Military Affairs.

Other Professional Experience
UW Affiliate Faculty with twenty years’ experience in behavioral health, consulting, and grant writing, working with governments and foundations to better serve our communities.

Education
Master of Social Work & B.S. in Psychology from UW.

Community Service
Kent Chamber of Commerce, Judson Retirement Community Board, United Way Impact Council, Veteran Suicide Prevention Task Force.

Statement
I am honored to serve you in the State Senate, where I’ve built on my record of legislative leadership– advancing measures to address housing and behavioral health needs and of veterans and military families and have worked to create protections for our communities against unregulated AI. I’m proud of this year’s bills that strengthen protections against child sexual exploitation, prohibit insurance agencies from using AI to deny care, invest in flood recovery, reduce youth homelessness, and strengthen the rights of homeowners.

As a career social worker, protecting vulnerable Washingtonians has always been my focus. Protecting survivors of sexual abuse, gun violence, and suicide is absolutely a priority of mine whilst serving in the legislature. With federal cuts threatening programs for struggling families and agencies protecting vulnerable populations, I will continue to work to strengthen and protect these resources in our state. 

With your vote, I’ll continue working on affordable housing, comprehensive healthcare, 988, airport pollution mitigation, childcare access for working families, language access in schools, and finding climate solutions. 

I’m proudly endorsed by local mayors and councilmembers, unions and state leaders, and many more. Let’s keep working together for stronger, safer, and more supportive communities in South King County

Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, State Senator. Accessed 2026-07-16. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
