1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience
Appointed Precinct Committee Officer. I look forward to the privilege of serving our district and delivering real results for the working class.

Other Professional Experience
Lifelong blue-collar worker as an automotive technician, industrial painter, handyman, and laborer. These jobs honed my practical problem-solving skills that I will bring to the Washington Legislature.

Education
Graduated high school 2010. Computer-Based Accounting Certificate 2012. Technical training,  industrial painting. Gained advanced automotive skills through hands-on professional experience.

Community Service
Generous donor to conservative causes that deliver real results locally and nationally. Through consistent tithing, charitable giving, I frequently help those in need with cash, vehicles, tires, clothing, and large gifts.

Statement
I am a blue-collar worker who turned my life around, achieving homeownership through hard work and faith in Jesus Christ. I know what it means to be forgotten by Olympia. Our government ignores your God-given rights and taxes away your hard-earned income. It is time to replace career politicians with leaders who champion limited government, traditional family values, fiscal responsibility, public safety, and constitutional rights. I will fight to abolish energy-killing taxes, support law enforcement, and protect your family from government overreach. Endorsed by the King County Republican Party. Together, we can restore common sense and American values in Washington.
