1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

I served as a precinct committee officer in the 33rd Legislative District. Beyond that, I do not have experience as an elected official. However, I do have experience earned as the result of elected officials voting against the desires of the people they were elected to represent.



Other Professional Experience

I have decades of experience in representing the public.  I worked as a manager for a healthcare company, a trainer for an hmo and customer service roles in housing, technology, hospitality and banking.

Education

California State University Hayward, Computer Science major, Mathematics minor

Community Service

I tutored low-income students after school at a public housing project.



Statement

Policy over party. If your party wins, then what do you win? Democrats won 60% of the seats. You won an income tax. an increase in spending and higher taxes, which my Democrat opponent voted for. He betrayed his constituents by voting for these bills. If I earn your vote I will work towards making life in Washington more affordable and protecting the people from greedy politicians.

It’s time politicians learned that the state needs to live within its’ means, not within our wallets.  Washington has a spending problem.  Vote for Darryl Jones and let’s turn off the spigot.
