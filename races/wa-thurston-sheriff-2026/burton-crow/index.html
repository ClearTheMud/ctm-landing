Statement

Thurston County deserves a Sheriff who is qualified, accountable, and grounded in the community they serve.

I have spent my entire adult life in uniform. Twenty years in the Army, including deployments to Iraq and Afghanistan, retiring as a Sergeant First Class. Eleven years at the Thurston County Sheriff's Office, where I'm a special victims detective serving our most vulnerable neighbors. I brought that same discipline to earning two master's degrees, in criminal justice and public administration. I'm also proud to be President of my union, the Thurston County Deputy Sheriff’s Association.

As Sheriff, I will give this community information it can trust and verify. That starts with seeking outside accreditation: independent reviewers making sure the office follows state law and meets professional standards. It means publishing our policies so people can see exactly how we operate. It means training deputies in victim-centered approaches and harm reduction. And it means clear annual budget disclosures so taxpayers never have to dig to find out how their money is spent.

I will provide strong training, and real transparency & accountability. I will spend your tax dollars wisely, with your input, and create a culture in our Sheriff's office that puts you first.
