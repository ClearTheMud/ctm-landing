1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

I am Patti McLean, currently the Mason County Assessor since 2019, and a lifelong Washington resident.

Other Professional Experience

I started at the front counter of the Assessor's office in 1991. 

Education

Since starting in the office, I took advantage of every training opportunity, which increased my responsibilities and I adapted proficiently to our technology changes to greatly improve accuracy and efficiency within the office.

Community Service

I believe it is important to be a productive community member. Outside of my dedication to this office, I have been a School Board Director, current Nimrod Club member, and regularly volunteer for our local grange and blood drives. 

Statement

My name is Patti McLean, and it has been my pleasure to serve as your County Assessor since 2019. I continue to run as an Independent because I believe local offices should remain nonpartisan.

The Assessor’s Office is required to follow state mandates and regulations established by legislation and to report annually to the Department of Revenue. Throughout my years working in Assessor’s offices, I have served under both parties, while the day-to-day responsibilities of the office have remained the same.

Since taking office, I am proud of the accomplishments we have achieved and remain committed to accountability, fairness, and quality customer service for the residents of this county. My office continues to maintain an open-door policy for both employees and taxpayers to ensure everyone is treated fairly and has the opportunity to express concerns or ask questions.

Moving forward, I will continue providing taxpayers with the tools and information they need to better understand the responsibilities and requirements of the Assessor’s Office.

With 34 years of experience, I have the knowledge and leadership necessary to continue overseeing the many duties of the Assessor’s Office with INTEGRITY, ACCOUNTABILITY, AND FAIRNESS.

I would appreciate your vote on November 3, 2026.
