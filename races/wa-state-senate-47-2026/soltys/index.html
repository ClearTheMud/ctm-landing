1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (General Election 2026)

Elected Experience

Covington City Council, 2019 to present; Sound Cities Association Public Issues Committee; South King Housing and Homelessness Partners Executive Board 




Other Professional Experience

Diagnostic Medical Sonographer, (ARDMS, OB, RVT); Loan Originator; Choir Teacher; Piano Teacher; Mother of three

Education

AA, Bellevue College; Registered Diagnostic Medical Sonographer (RDMS) certification

Community Service

Covington Chamber of Commerce, Covington Storehouse Food Bank, Covington City litter cleanup, Ukraine medical supply relief effort organizer, school volunteer during COVID-19 school closures, PTA school board member, and more

Statement

For too many families in Kent, Auburn, and Covington, life is harder than it should be. Our leaders in Olympia are focused on political power instead of what our families, workers, and businesses need. That has to change. It's why I'm running for State Senate, and it’s the reason I first ran for office seven years ago.

I came to this country from Ukraine when I was five years old without knowing the language, but I was blessed with opportunities made possible by strong schools, safe neighborhoods, thriving businesses, and a community focused on the future. When those values felt threatened, I stepped up to serve.

In 2019, I defeated a fourteen-year incumbent to serve on the Covington City Council because our families deserve better. Since then, I've focused on real results: launching the Clean Team to improve public spaces; fully funding our police, including funding for an additional officer; pioneering a community resource officer program for neighbors facing homelessness or mental health crises; and fighting to reduce traffic congestion. 

The 47th deserves a senator focused on results, accountability, and common-sense solutions - not partisan games. I'm ready to bring that to Olympia and I’d be honored by your vote.




Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, State Senator. Accessed 2026-10-10. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
