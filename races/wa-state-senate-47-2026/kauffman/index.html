Statement

Our community is at a crossroads where the rising cost of living threatens the stability of every family. As your State Senator, my priority remains clear: making Washington affordable for everyone. We must tackle the housing crisis by increasing supply and protecting renters from predatory price gouging. I’ve championed legislation to build more housing and will continue fighting for middle-income housing so people can live where they work.

I know what it’s like to balance rent and healthcare costs, groceries and gas, and try to make it all work. I’ve worked for property tax relief for seniors and expanded childcare access for working parents. We must ensure our social safety net is robust enough to catch those falling behind while building an economy where living-wage jobs are the standard, not the exception.

I’m running to ensure that South King County has a proven leader who understands the challenges of our diverse district. I’m proud to be endorsed by the Mayors of Auburn, Covington, and Kent, King County Executive Girmay Zahilay, the 47th District Democrats, the State Labor Council, and many others. I would be honored to earn your vote to keep working for a fair, inclusive, and affordable Washington.
