1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (General Election 2026)

Legal/Judicial Experience
Klickitat County East District Court Judge.  

Other Professional Experience
Rick has practiced law in Goldendale since 1984, handling a variety of civil and criminal cases in both Klickitat County East District and Superior Courts. Rick has also appeared before the Washington State Court of Appeals and Washington State Supreme Court.  Rick has been serving the citizens of Klickitat County as East District Court Judge since 2010. 

Education
Graduate of Centerville Grade School & Goldendale High School; BA in Business, Eastern Washington University; JD, Gonzaga University.

Statement
I am looking forward to serving the citizens of Klickitat County as East District Court Judge for another 4 years.
