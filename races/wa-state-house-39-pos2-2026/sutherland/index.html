1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

Washington State Representative (2019-2023). Snohomish County Republican Party LD Vice Chairman. Precinct Committee Officer.

Other Professional Experience

Biochemist. Lung and breast cancer drug development; DNA Research, Seattle. Developed new DNA extraction and purification method. My research disproved claims of a new form of DNA, allowing valuable research funds to be redirected to more productive cancer research laboratories.



Education

Biochemistry degree, Gonzaga University, Spokane.

Community Service

US Air Force Veteran. Raised critical funds for Children's Hospital in Seattle. Gospel Mission Donor. Officer/Treasurer of a Scottish-American 501(c)3 non-profit. Boys and Girls Club youth basketball coach. Snohomish County 4H equine club volunteer. 'Cigars for Veterans' charitable volunteer.



Statement

Endorsements: Snohomish County Republican Party Executive Committee; 39th Legislative District Republican Party. NRA rated “A”

Priorities: Election Integrity reforms. Eliminating state level property taxes to increase housing affordability. Reduce traffic congestion by building additional lane capacity, creating family wage jobs. Improving mass transit. True $30.00 car tabs. Greater educational choices for parents, let education dollars follow the child to the school of their choice. First, Second Amendment Rights. Limiting Emergency Powers of the Governor. Solving homelessness. Adequately fund police to decrease crime rates. Eliminate the Income and Capital Gains taxes; Reduce gas tax. Let’s build a better community.
