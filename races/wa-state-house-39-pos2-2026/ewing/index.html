Statement
Steve Ewing is our best choice for Representative, endorsed by the entire 39th District delegation: Sen. Wagoner, Rep. Low, and retiring Rep. Eslick. He's an expert in public safety, a father, and a lifelong Washingtonian. As a leader in local government, Steve passed balanced budgets, opposed higher taxes, prioritized public safety, and worked for better roads.

 

We need to be honest; people are leaving Washington because state government is getting weird. Steve Ewing will stand up for "normal" government--normal good schools, normal better roads, normal balanced budgets, normal affordable taxes. Good news, Steve Ewing is our "normal" choice for Representative.
