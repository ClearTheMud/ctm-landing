1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

First-time candidate. Lifelong advocate, leading efforts to protect vulnerable children and youth, support families, and strengthen communities.



Other Professional Experience

Consultant; Former Juvenile Court Administrator, Superior Court Program Manager, DCYF State Area Administrator, Dawson Place Founding Member, Crisis Residential Center Program Manager




Education

BA, Sociology and Criminal Justice, WSU



Community Service

Past Chair Children’s Campaign Fund; YMCA of Snohomish County, Marysville; Citrine Women’s Health and Wellness; WA Assn of Juvenile Court Administrators; WA Assn of Court Appointed Advocates. Married to a high school teacher, and proud parent of two children and two rescue dogs, Ida volunteers with a local animal shelter, and her daughter’s school. 




Statement

After a childhood marked by instability, I chose a path of service, working alongside communities and the justice system. Balancing compassion with accountability, I’ve dedicated my career to improving broken systems from within. 

With your vote, I’ll continue advocating for neighbors struggling to afford housing, insurance, and childcare. I’ll work across the aisle to fund our schools, address special needs, and expand career and college pathways. I’ll fight for fair jobs and local businesses that strengthen our economy. Let’s challenge broken systems, unite around shared values, and listen to people, not the powerful. Endorsed: Washington State Labor Council|IAM 751|Congresswoman DelBene
