1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (General Election 2026)

Elected Experience

I have served as the elected County Clerk since 2019, focused on accuracy, accountability, and reliable service. I lead a team that keeps cases moving, supports judges, and serves the public with respect. I believe in practical, common-sense solutions, fiscal responsibility, and doing the job right.  

Other Professional Experience

No information submitted

Education

No information submitted

Community Service

Longtime volunteer in youth sports, including football, Little League, and wrestling. Active with Miss Grays Harbor and community events like the Elma Wine and Seafood Festival. Member of P.E.O., supporting women’s education. I believe in giving back and staying connected to the community I serve.

Statement

Being your Clerk has shown me one thing very clearly. This office matters more than most people realize.

At some point, everyone walks through our doors. When they do, the experience they have matters. We’ve made a conscious decision to be different. More responsive. More efficient. More accountable.

We don’t just process paperwork. We help people navigate a system that can feel overwhelming. That takes a team that cares, pays attention, and takes pride in getting it right.

We’ve built real momentum, and we’re not slowing down. What we’ve done so far is just the beginning.

I take this job seriously. I expect a lot from my office because the public deserves it.

I’d appreciate your support to continue the work we’ve started.
