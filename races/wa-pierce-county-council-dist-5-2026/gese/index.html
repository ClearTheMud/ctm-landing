1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (General Election 2026)

Occupation
Commercial geoduck diver, real estate agent, and operator of family-owned Thunderbird Trading Post. Mom and Grandma.

Education
Puyallup High School graduate. Attended Pacific Lutheran University. 

Statement
As a Native American and granddaughter of a former Tribal Chief, representation is deeply personal to me. This isn’t just a district on a map—this is our ancestral land. My people have called this home for centuries, yet our voices have often been absent from rooms where decisions about our communities are made. If elected, I would be the first Indigenous woman to represent our land in this office. I’m honored to be endorsed by the Puyallup Tribe of Indians.

Before deciding based on a party label, I ask you to hear my message. The challenges facing Pierce County aren’t Republican or Democratic—they affect all of us. I’m not a career politician. I’m a hands-on community leader, small business owner, and real estate agent. I’ve served as PTA President, football booster President, volunteered with youth leagues, operated the Thunderbird Trading Post, and represented the Tribe and business owners in negotiations with Washington State.

On the County Council, I’ll focus on infrastructure, public safety, and protecting small businesses. For Infrastructure, I’ll prioritize neglected roads and bridges, reduce traffic, and improve quality of life. On Public Safety, I’ll expand pathways to long-term recovery and help end the revolving door. For Small Businesses, I’ll remove unnecessary red tape so local entrepreneurs have the same opportunity to succeed as larger, well-resourced companies. I'll ensure our tax dollars are spent transparently.

Our district should be both recognized and represented. I’m Bettina Gese, and I humbly ask for your vote.

Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, Council - District No. 5. Accessed 2026-10-10. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
