1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Occupation

Your Pierce County Councilmember; previously Fife City Councilmember

Education

Decatur High School, and attended Highline College. Advanced Certificate of Municipal Leadership, Association of Washington Cities

Statement

My father immigrated to this country in search of the American dream. After years working service jobs, he found stability through a union career that helped provide opportunity for our family. That experience shaped my core values — hard work, community, and connection — and inspired my commitment to fighting for working families. It's a commitment I carry into public service every day, because I believe government should work for everyone.

For 11 years on the Fife City Council and now on the Pierce County Council, I've focused on practical solutions that make a real difference. In my first year as your Councilmember, I helped secure $12.2 million to create or preserve 646 affordable homes, passed the Justice Fund to advance safer communities and a fairer justice system, strengthened behavioral health investments, and secured emergency food assistance for local families after federal funding cuts. I've worked to elevate food security as a county priority and expand support for seniors, working families, and vulnerable residents.

Whether you live in Tacoma, Fife, or unincorporated Pierce County, I'll keep fighting for you. I’ll keep listening, building coalitions, and bringing your voices to the table — because real progress happens when leaders show up, work collaboratively, and stay focused on solutions. I've delivered for District 5, and I'm just getting started. I would be honored to earn your vote.

Endorsed by Tacoma Firefighters, Planned Parenthood, labor unions, State Treasurer Pellicciotti, Senators Conway and Trudeau, County Executive Mello, and many more!
