1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

Chair, Education Committee; Member, Finance Committee and Consumer Protection and Business Committee.

Other Professional Experience

Non-profit governance and management, including creating three non-profit organizations (two national, one local); Local government executive branch policy and community relations

Education

Franklin High School; B. A., The Evergreen State College; M.A., Northeastern University.

Community Service

Board Member, Neighborhood House; Member, Washington Civil Rights Coalition; Founding Board Member, Hate Free Zone of Washington (OneAmerica); Founding Board Member, Puget Sound Public Radio, KUOW; Founding Member, Washington Tax Fairness Coalition; Founding Member, Task Force on Immigration and the States, National Conference of State Legislatures (NCSL)

Statement

In these tough times, Washington families face rising costs and complex challenges. As your State Representative and the House Education Chair, I am focused on a future where economic security and quality education are accessible to everyone. I am working to lower the cost of living while protecting the essential services upon which we all rely.

Our path forward requires equity expertise and bold, practical solutions. My deep community roots shape my commitment to modernizing our workforce, increasing our housing supply, and ensuring tax fairness for working families. I respectfully ask for your vote to build a resilient, inclusive Washington.
