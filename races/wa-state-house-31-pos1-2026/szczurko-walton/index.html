1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (General Election 2026)

Elected Experience

None. First-time candidate.

Other Professional Experience

I know what it's like to work the register at Office Depot or make commission on the floor at Nordstrom. As a campus security guard, I walked people home at night when they didn't feel safe on campus. U.S. Navy nuclear reactor operator, 2015–2024. Three deployments aboard USS Theodore Roosevelt: operations supporting Kurdish allies against ISIS in the Middle East and freedom of navigation in the South China Sea. Currently a commercial HVAC technician serving schools, hospitals, and small businesses.

Education

B.A., Political Science, UC Santa Barbara (2014). Master of Public Administration (2025).

Community Service

None at this time.

Statement

I’m running because my service did not end when I hung up the uniform. My son deserves a community of neighbors who know each other, not one torn apart by outrage media. And you deserve to be treated with dignity and respect by the people you elect.

I’ll fight to pass SB 6358 and end corporate election spending in Washington, even if it means going to every senator’s district. I’ll work to bring down housing, healthcare, and tax costs crushing working families. And I’ll defend the Climate Commitment Act and modernize our grid for the century my son will inherit.

Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, State Representative Pos. 1. Accessed 2026-10-10. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
