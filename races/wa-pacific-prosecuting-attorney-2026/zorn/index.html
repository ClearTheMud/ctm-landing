Finding 6.1: Named in a pending employment lawsuit alleging witness intimidation MODERATE

What happened
Zorn, in her role as Chief Criminal Deputy Prosecutor, is named in a pending lawsuit brought by two Pacific County employees (senior court administrator Rikki Thompson and former prosecutor's office staff member Brandi Huber) in Lewis County Superior Court. A tort claim was filed July 1, 2025, converted to a lawsuit November 5, 2025, and amended December 19, 2025. The amended complaint alleges that Prosecutor Michael Rothman and Zorn directed employees, who are also potential witnesses, not to speak with anyone outside the office while the office was under scrutiny, conduct the plaintiffs characterize as intimidation and silencing. The suit lists seven causes of action, including Washington Law Against Discrimination violations, civil assault, civil false imprisonment, and Silence No More Act violations. Most allegations center on Rothman; the allegations naming Zorn concern the directive not to speak outside the office.

Defense
The allegations are unproven and the case is pending, with no court finding against Zorn. The bulk of the complaint concerns the conduct of Prosecutor Rothman, not Zorn. Neither Zorn nor the county offered a specific public response to the Zorn allegations in the coverage reviewed.
