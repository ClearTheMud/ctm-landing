Statement

I was born and raised and have spent most of my life in Pacific County. I lived elsewhere for a few years and returned to Pacific County permanently in 1996. In the 30 years since I returned, I have been privileged to serve the people of Pacific County in several roles, including prosecuting for the four cities (Raymond, South Bend, Long Beach, and Ilwaco), as well as providing services as City Attorney for Raymond and South Bend.  I have also served as a Superior Court Commissioner and Pro-Tem District Court Judge for much of that time. I have been very active in the community, belonging to several service organizations and several fraternal organizations and have been to the chief officer position on multiple occasions. I hope this demonstrates my commitment to the people of Pacific County. I believe that my extensive legal experience and demonstrated commitment to the people of Pacific County and show that I am the person for job of Prosecutor. I hope the voters of Pacific County agree with me and give me the opportunity to serve them as Prosecutor.

Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, Prosecuting Attorney. Accessed 2026-07-16. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
