Statement

Hi everyone – 
Having retired, I can now devote full attention to upcoming Mason County financial issues, both current, and in the future under several financial and economic scenarios I am a member of the Mason County Planning Advisory Commission, basically defining what we want Mason County to be. Now it is time to quantify these aspirations under several potential scenarios. Characteristics of an actuary include keen analytical, and problem-solving skills. Good business sense. Finance, economics, most of all solid communication skills

With upcoming challenges facing us all, my experience in financial forecasting and risk management will be helpful in helping guide Mason County. Examples include efforts to optimize assets vs liabilities and address other future challenges such as inflation.  
Financial analysis is central to the many challenges faced by Mason County in upcoming years, such as what will be the relationship of revenue vs expenditures going forward as the community grows, and increasing demand for County services.    
This is not to say I am simply a backroom nerd. On the contrary, I love to collaborate with diverse sets of stakeholders. You are now my most important stakeholder. Send to me any questions/comments on Mason County financial issues!
