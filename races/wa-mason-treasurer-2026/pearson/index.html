1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

I currently hold the appointed position of Mason County Treasurer.

Other Professional Experience

25 years of government experience within the Mason County Treasurer’s Office. I have served the public in multiple positions, with extensive experience in property tax collection, county finance, banking procedures, investments, customer service, management and daily office operations.

Education

Shelton High School graduate with ongoing professional training in county finance, treasury management and public service administration.

Community Service

Committed to serving the citizens of Mason County with transparency, integrity, accountability and dependable public service.

Statement

I am honored to serve as your appointed Mason County Treasurer and am asking for your vote to continue serving our community with transparency, integrity, and accountability.

For 25 years, I have worked in the Treasurer’s Office and have served in multiple roles that provided me with extensive knowledge of county finances, policies and procedures. That experience has prepared me to successfully lead the office while maintaining the high level of service Mason County residents deserve.

As Treasurer, my priorities are fiscal responsibility, accuracy, accessibility and excellent customer service. I understand the importance of safeguarding taxpayer dollars while ensuring the office operates efficiently and professionally. I am committed to open communication, strong financial stewardship and treating every citizen with respect and fairness.

Mason County deserves experienced leadership and a Treasurer who understands the office from the ground up. I am proud to continue serving the community where I was raised and would be honored to earn your vote.
