Statement
I was born and raised in Eastern Washington and have spent my entire adult life in Walla Walla County. My wife and I chose to build our life here because the values of hard work, family, and service are deeply rooted in this county.

It has been my privilege to serve with the Walla Walla County Sheriff’s Office for over 23 years. For the past nine years, I have served as the Chief Deputy supporting and overseeing the daily operations of our deputies. Every day, I strive to further our mission and empower my team to succeed.

I believe the people of Walla Walla County deserve the highest level of public safety. My priorities have always been, and will continue to be, protecting what matters most:

     The safety of our loved ones and neighbors.

     The prosperity and security of our businesses.

     The protection of our individual freedoms.

As Sheriff, I will uphold our laws with integrity, strengthen community partnerships, and prioritize recruiting and retaining the quality personnel our county deserves.My training, education, and more than two decades of service have prepared me to serve as your next Walla Walla County Sheriff. I respectfully ask for your vote.
