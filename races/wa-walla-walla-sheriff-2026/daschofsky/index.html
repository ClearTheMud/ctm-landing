Other Professional Experience

Education
Walla Walla High School Graduate, Washington State Criminal Justice Training Commission Basic Law Enforcement Academy, FBI LEEDA Trilogy (Supervisor, Command, and Executive Leadership Institutes), numerous advanced certificates in DUI enforcement, K9 handling, narcotics interdiction, public safety dispatching and more.

Community Service
United States Marine Corps Veteran (funeral honor team for 25 military funerals while active duty), K9 Handler with seven years of community demonstrations and events, 2026 lifesaving award (off duty rescue of trapped person in vehicle fire).

Statement
Born and raised in Walla Walla. Family roots here since the 1860s. I’m a United States Marine Corps veteran with 19 years of law enforcement experience—and I’m running for Walla Walla County Sheriff to keep our community safe.

My commitment to your family's safety are not political talking points which are exemplified by the six times, (2016, 2021-2025), I’ve been named the Washington State Traffic Safety Commission’s DUI Enforcement Officer of the Year for taking impaired drivers off our roads. From 2017-2025, I served as a K9 Handler while handling regular patrol, proving I can take on a large workload and still get things done. 

As your Sheriff, I won’t hide in an office. I’ll lead from the front. I will slash bloated administrative positions and redirect every dollar to real priorities: victim advocates who help those who’ve been hurt and need avenues of escape, and code enforcement to protect our neighborhoods, and clean them up. 

I’m 100% committed to leading a transparent, proactive Sheriff’s Office that puts Walla Walla County first—keeping families safe and holding criminals accountable. This is personal. This is my home. Let’s make it safer together.



Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, Sheriff. Accessed 2026-07-16. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
