1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

Your State Representative, 2017-2019 & 2023-present.

Other Professional Experience

Federal Way small business owner, specializing in organizational development consulting. Previously served as Director of Economic Development for Washington's Military and Defense Sector at the Washington State Department of Commerce, and Executive Director of the Washington Military Alliance. Served as U.S. Senator Patty Murray's Statewide Veterans Constituency Coordinator and regional outreach director.

Education

Graduate, Moses Lake High School; BA, Washington State University; MA, Gonzaga University; ABD, Seattle Pacific University.

Community Service

Member, Zeta Phi Beta Sorority; Volunteer, Brigadoon Elementary; Volunteer, Chamber of Commerce; Volunteer, Children’s Dance Theater.

Statement

An open door, open mind, and commitment to results. That's how I've served the 30th District, and I'm ready to keep going. I've passed laws to protect homeless students, expand access to nutritious food for seniors, mitigate wildfire risk, and invest $100 million in local public safety. I founded the bipartisan Veterans & Military Families Caucus, passed the Wounded Warrior Shared Leave Act, and now chair the House Agriculture and Natural Resources Committee, focused on food, farms, and the people who put dinner on our table. Endorsed: Congressman Adam Smith, Mayors & City Leaders, Labor, Nurses, Teachers, Police, & Environmentalists
