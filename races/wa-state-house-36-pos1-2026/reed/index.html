1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

Current State Representative, 36th LD

Other Professional Experience

Consultant at Kinetic West, providing strategic planning and organizational management services to workforce and youth apprenticeship programs. Former Senior Policy Advisor & Regional Affairs Director for City of Seattle, leading youth employment, workforce, transit, arts policy. Former Obama administration staff, US State Department and Office of Management and Budget.

Education

MPA, Princeton University; BA, Smith College

Community Service

I grew up in Seattle, the daughter of public school educators. I'm an active parishioner at St. Anne Catholic Church and member of Holy Names Academy Alumnae Board. Former board member: YMCA Social Impact Center, Global Leadership Forum, Fuse WA.

Statement

As State Representative, I remain committed to safe, affordable, thriving communities. With your support, I’ll build on my expertise in supporting youth and workforce development, helping the next generation reach their full potential. I’ll advance pro-housing policies to meet Seattle's urgent and long-term needs, invest in pathways to apprenticeship and higher ed, expand Washington’s creative economy, and be a strong voice for climate-friendly transportation solutions and the maritime economy.


Endorsed: State Labor Council; Planned Parenthood Alliance Advocates; Alliance for Gun Responsibility; WA Nurses Association PAC; WA Federation of State Employees; 36th LD Democrats,  legislators, labor & community leaders, more!
