Statement

As Thurston County grows, we’re facing real challenges — rising housing costs, pressure on essential services, and concerns about safety and belonging. Meeting this moment requires steady, thoughtful leadership focused on practical solutions. In difficult times, it matters to have leaders who listen, collaborate, and follow through.

Grounded in years of volunteer service, nonprofit operations management, public service, and elected office, I have worked with residents, organizations, and local leaders to strengthen programs supporting health and wellbeing, youth and families, and meaningful community connection.

I am committed to expanding access to affordable housing, strengthening community safety and wellbeing, supporting youth and families, and protecting our environment while planning for responsible growth that preserves what we love about Thurston County. I believe in working alongside the community to build a county where everyone can thrive.

I am proud to be endorsed by State Representative Beth Doglio, a Lacey City Council majority, Tumwater Mayor Leatta Dahlhoff, Olympia Mayor Dontae Payne, and many other leaders who share my commitment to steady, collaborative leadership. I ask for your vote and would be honored to serve as your Thurston County Commissioner.
