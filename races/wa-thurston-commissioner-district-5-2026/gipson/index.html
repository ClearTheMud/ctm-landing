Finding 5.1: Limited Public Profile Beyond School Board Service MODERATE

What happened
Gipson's public record is primarily limited to her role as Vice President of the North Thurston Public Schools School Board. No extensive news coverage, policy statements beyond education, or county-level government experience was found in public searches. For a candidate seeking to serve on the Board of County Commissioners -- which oversees a $23.8M budget deficit, county departments, and land use policy -- the lack of documented county-level experience or detailed policy positions is notable.

What happened
