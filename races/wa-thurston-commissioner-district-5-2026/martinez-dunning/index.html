Finding 3.2: Name Recognition Challenge — Martinez-Dunning vs. Nic Dunning LOW

What happened
Martinez-Dunning filed with the WA Secretary of State as “Nicolas Martinez-Dunning” but is known on the Lacey City Council and in public life as “Nic Dunning.” This dual-name situation could cause voter confusion on the ballot.
Source tier
T1 (WA SOS, City of Lacey)
Political impact
Low — Minor but could affect ballot recognition for voters who know him from city council under a different name.
