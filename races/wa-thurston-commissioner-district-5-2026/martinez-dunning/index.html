Statement

Like so many of you, I’m deeply concerned about issues facing Thurston County. Commissioner scandals broke public trust. $20–$30 million deficits are forcing painful cuts to essential services. A decade of candidates that have promised solutions to homelessness, rising costs, and public safety and yet we’ve seen almost no real progress.

As a member of the Lacey City Council, Regional Housing Council, and non-profit founder of We Heart Olympia I’ve worked across party divides to deliver real solutions to real problems. It’s time to move beyond political fighting and unite around practical solutions that improve safety, affordability, opportunity, and transparency for everyone in Thurston County.

As a small business owner, I received the South Sound Best Place to Work and Business of the Year awards in 2025. I hear the same priorities and frustrations from state employees, police officers, firefighters, military, veterans, and neighbors from every background in our community. I will bring the same leadership to the county that builds trust between the county staff, elected officials, and the public.

Campaign Finance Note: We have rejected all funding from special interest groups and political action committees and I'm endorsed by over 600 individuals & businesses representing every party.
