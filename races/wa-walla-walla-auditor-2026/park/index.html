Finding 5.1: The county's elected elections administrator concurrently serves as treasurer of the Walla Walla County Republican Central Committee MODERATE

What happened
The Walla Walla County Auditor administers the county's elections. Sheryl Park holds that office by appointment and is running to keep it. Washington PDC records list Park as the treasurer of the Walla Walla County Republican Central Committee (WWGOP, committee id 20458) for the 2026 cycle, a role she has held continuously since at least 2015 and retained after her appointment as auditor in February 2026. She therefore serves as the financial officer of one party's county central committee while simultaneously running the office that administers county elections.
Source tier
T1
Political impact
Moderate
Defense
Washington county auditor is a partisan elected office, and holding a party role is legal. Park was already the WWGOP treasurer for roughly a decade before her auditor appointment, and no law or PDC action bars an auditor from also serving as a party committee treasurer. Day-to-day election operations in Washington are also governed by uniform statewide procedures and canvassing boards rather than by the auditor alone.
