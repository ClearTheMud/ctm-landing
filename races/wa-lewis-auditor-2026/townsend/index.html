Finding 5.1: Resigned as Lewis County GOP Chair After Seven Months HIGH

What happened
Townsend was elected chair of the Lewis County Republican Party in December 2024 but resigned effective immediately on June 16, 2025, after only seven months of his two-year term. In his resignation email, he stated that his goal of uniting the party was "not possible." He cited an inability to unite the party as his reason for stepping down.
Source tier
T2
Political impact
High
Defense
The Lewis County GOP had well-documented internal divisions predating Townsend's tenure, including a factional split that created the Conservative Coalition of Lewis County. Townsend inherited a fractured organization. His willingness to step aside when unable to achieve goals could be seen as responsible leadership.

Finding 5.2: Bigfoot Research May Undermine Professional Credibility HIGH

What happened
Townsend is publicly known as a Bigfoot researcher who teaches Bigfoot classes at Centralia College and Lower Columbia College, has published a textbook ("Bigfoot Solved, Hybrid Hominin"), released scientific papers claiming to have found giant hominin teeth marks on deer and elk bones near Mt. St. Helens, and proposed opening a Bigfoot learning center in Winlock. He theorizes Bigfoot is a hybrid that has been interbreeding with Native Americans for 80,000 years. While this reflects personal intellectual curiosity, opponents and media may use it to question his judgment and seriousness as a candidate for a position requiring strict adherence to evidence-based financial auditing and election administration.

T2
Political impact
High
Defense
Townsend's Bigfoot research is separate from his professional qualifications. His academic credentials (three graduate degrees) and military service demonstrate intellectual rigor in other domains. The research is conducted through a college continuing education program, not pseudoscience outlets.

Finding 5.3: No Financial, Accounting, or Auditing Background HIGH

What happened
Townsend's professional background is in education, military service, and party leadership. He holds degrees in European history, education/computers, and organizational leadership — none in finance, accounting, or public administration. The county auditor role requires oversight of county financial transactions, elections, recording, and licensing. Both the current auditor (Larry Grove, CPA) and predecessor (Gary Zandell) were financial professionals. Competitor Merrill holds CPA and EA credentials.
Source tier
T2
Political impact
High
Defense
The county auditor is an elected executive who manages professional staff. Leadership and organizational skills may be as important as technical accounting knowledge. Townsend's military leadership and advanced degrees in organizational leadership could translate to the role.

Finding 5.5: Party Infighting and Divisive Political Environment MODERATE

What happened
Townsend entered the Lewis County GOP leadership during a period of significant internal party conflict, including the formation of the breakaway Conservative Coalition of Lewis County. He described the previous party leadership as "dysfunctional" and the party infrastructure as "antiquated." His resignation cited the impossibility of uniting local Republicans. This divisive environment could follow him into the auditor race, where he may face opposition from factions within his own party.
