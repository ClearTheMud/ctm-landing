Finding 5.1: Resigned as county GOP chair after seven months HIGH

What happened
Townsend was elected chair of the Lewis County Republican Party in December 2024 but resigned effective immediately on June 16, 2025, after only seven months of his two-year term. In his resignation email, he stated that his goal of uniting the party was "not possible." He cited an inability to unite the party as his reason for stepping down.
Source tier
T2
Political impact
High
Defense
The Lewis County GOP had well-documented internal divisions predating Townsend's tenure, including a factional split that created the Conservative Coalition of Lewis County. Townsend inherited a fractured organization. His willingness to step aside when unable to achieve goals could be seen as responsible leadership.
  • https://www.chronline.com/stories/lewis-county-republicans-seek-new-leader-as-chairman-resigns,382928

Finding 5.2: No Financial, Accounting, or Auditing Background HIGH

What happened
Townsend's professional background is in education, military service, and party leadership. He holds degrees in European history, education/computers, and organizational leadership, none in finance, accounting, or public administration. The county auditor role requires oversight of county financial transactions, elections, recording, and licensing. Both the current auditor (Larry Grove, CPA) and predecessor (Gary Zandell) were financial professionals. Competitor Merrill holds CPA and EA credentials.
Source tier
T2
Political impact
High
Defense
The county auditor is an elected executive who manages professional staff. Leadership and organizational skills may be as important as technical accounting knowledge. Townsend's military leadership and advanced degrees in organizational leadership could translate to the role.
  • https://www.yahoo.com/news/lewis-county-gop-chair-eyes-045900814.html

Finding 5.3: No PDC campaign finance records filed as of May 2026 MODERATE

What happened
As of May 23, 2026, no campaign contribution records appear in the WA PDC SODA API for Townsend's Lewis County auditor campaign. Both opponents (Corbin and Merrill) have active PDC filings showing ~$6,000+ in contributions each.
