Statement
I’m local and was raised in Lewis County. I am running for Lewis County Auditor because I want to serve the people here. The Auditor’s Office plays an important role in elections, licensing, recording, public records, and financial accountability. Lewis County citizens deserve an office that remains accurate, transparent, responsive, and professionally managed by a Certified Public Accountant.

My background in auditing, accounting, and management has prepared me to lead an office where details matter and public trust is essential. As a CPA, I have held positions of public trust and hope to continue that service in the Auditor’s Office. I understand the importance of leading with honesty and integrity, upholding the law, and maintaining confidence in our elections. Elections must continue to be free from material error and fairly administered so the voice of the people is honored.

If elected, my priorities will be to follow the law in an ethical and accurate manner, continue to leverage modern technology, and give power to the people through access to information. I will build on the foundation laid by my predecessor’s service and keep government resources working for Lewis County citizens.

I respectfully ask for your vote for Lewis County Auditor.

Finding 7.1: Endorsed by two former auditors, the treasurer, and two of the three commissioners MODERATE

What happened
Merrill has secured endorsements from two former auditors (Larry Grove and Gary Zandell, who between them held the office for over 40 years), the current county treasurer (Arny Davis), and two of the three county commissioners (Scott Brummer and Sean Swope; the third is Lindsey Pollock). These endorsements position Merrill as the establishment-backed candidate. In a strongly Republican county, this may be used by opponents to paint him as an insider or part of the "old guard."
Source tier
T2
Political impact
Moderate
Defense
Endorsements from experienced officeholders relate to Merrill's qualifications. Grove and Zandell collectively held the auditor office for 40+ years.

Finding 7.2: Youngest candidate, with no prior elected office MODERATE

What happened
Merrill is described as "the youngest by far" of the three candidates and as "less politically experienced" compared to his opponents. He has no prior elected office, government board service, or political party leadership experience. His entire professional career has been in private-sector accounting since starting as an intern in 2016. This lack of public-sector experience contrasts with Corbin's extensive board service and Townsend's school board and party leadership roles.

T2

Defense
Merrill frames his CPA credentials as directly relevant to the auditor role, calling it a "lateral move." His professional competence in auditing, tax, and accounting directly maps to the county auditor's core functions. Youth could be viewed as an asset for a long tenure in office.

While none of these contributions are improper, the pattern shows Merrill's fundraising base is heavily anchored in his professional and personal networks.
