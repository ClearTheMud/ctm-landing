Statement
As your Representative for the 38th District, I’ve spent four years delivering real results for working families and our communities, expanding affordable housing opportunities, standardizing juvenile diversion programs, strengthening wage theft protections for workers and much more. My career has always centered on service, from preventing youth homelessness to improving Everett’s economic vitality, and I bring that lived experience to every bill I champion. I’ll keep fighting for community safety, reproductive rights, working families and economic opportunity for all. Proudly endorsed by Governor Ferguson, Congressman Rick Larsen, WA State Labor Council, Nurses, Teachers, 38th LD Democrats, Planned Parenthood, and more!
