Statement

As an Army Combat Medic, Registered Nurse, Everett Delta Neighborhood Association Chair, and Family Readiness Group leader, I’ve served where results matter. I’m running to bring accountability and leadership to Olympia.

I’ll prioritize supporting local industries, lowering taxes, and funding police and behavioral health services to improve public safety.  My goal is to help people build self-sufficient lives with accountability and support. Services should heal, not enable, through clear expectations, real treatment, and incentives. I'll embrace collaboration and deliver results for families. I will always serve our communities, fighting for solutions that boost affordability, opportunity, and safety in District 38.
