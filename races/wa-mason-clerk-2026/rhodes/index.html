Statement

It has been my honor serving the people of Mason County as your County Clerk. The Clerk’s Office plays a vital role in supporting our justice system and ensuring the public has reliable access to important information. It has the responsibility to preserve official court records, collect and disburse fees and payments ordered by the court and maintain accurate and timely financial reporting.

During my term in office, I have focused on strengthening the efficiency and integrity of the office while providing residents, attorneys, and court staff with timely, professional, and respectful service. I am proud of the work our office has accomplished. We’ve improved services by upgrading outdated technology, modernizing procedures, and investing in staff training, all resulting in better service to you and cost savings for Mason County. Collections are now current, and we actively pursue delinquent accounts to protect public resources.

I believe public service is about responsibility, fairness, and earning the trust of the community. It takes showing up every day and getting the details right for the people who depend on us. I promise to keep working hard to deliver the dependable leadership and service Mason County deserves and I am asking for your vote.
