1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

Wahkiakum County Sheriff.



Other Professional Experience

Appointed to Wahkiakum County Sheriff by unanimous vote of County Commissioners in 2024. Promoted to Patrol Sergeant in 2019. Hired as a Wahkiakum County Sheriff’s Deputy in 2013.



Education

Public Safety Leadership the West Point Way in 2026. FBI Supervisor Leadership Institute in 2020. Basic Law Enforcement Academy in 2013. Washington State University Pullman with a Bachelor of Fine Arts in 2007. Skyview High School Vancouver in 2002. Wahkiakum School District grades K through 8th. 



Community Service

Wahkiakum County Community and Government leader as elected Sheriff responsible for Public Safety including Law Enforcement, Emergency Services, and 911 dispatch.



Statement

Sheriff John Mason has dedicated his Law Enforcement career to Wahkiakum County since 2013. He has put his leadership skills and experience to good use as a community leader to the public and a team leader to the Office. He has fostered partnerships with the community to ensure public safety preparedness and resiliency.

John is committed to providing countywide Law Enforcement, Public Safety, 911 Dispatch, and Emergency Management. He has overseen great advancements in the capabilities and skillsets of the Office to include community preparedness education while maintaining a conservative budget within current means.

Having firsthand experience with the difficulties and sacrifice it takes to serve while understaffed, John knows the importance of dedication and resiliency. John has proven himself as a working Sheriff by balancing his administrative duties with the immediate needs of the community.

John believes in the Rule of Law and representing the interests of Wahkiakum citizens fairly and impartially. Community partnerships and transparency are key to running an Office that is for the people and by the people. John has been inspired by Wahkiakum’s history of good will, volunteerism and public service. A community that takes care of each other is a healthier and safer community.
