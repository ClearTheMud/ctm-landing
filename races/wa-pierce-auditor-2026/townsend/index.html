Finding 5.1: Runs on competence over politics while his campaign is largely party-funded MODERATE

What happened
Townsend describes himself as having no allegiance to any political party and campaigns on the slogan "Competence over politics." At the same time, he serves on the board of directors of the Cascade Party of Washington and his single largest contribution is $3,000 from the Cascade Party itself. Party co-founders Krist Novoselic and Darbury Novoselic of Naselle each gave $1,200. Together the party and its founders account for roughly $5,400 of his $15,271.77 in reported 2026 contributions. Voters weighing his independence claim can see that the Cascade Party is his largest financial backer.

Defense
Townsend does not hide the affiliation; his party role and its centrist, cross-partisan positioning are public. Minor-party candidates commonly receive support from the party organizing their candidacy, and all contributions are fully disclosed to the PDC and within legal limits. The Pierce County Auditor is a nonpartisan office, and party backing does not by itself bear on administration of the office.
