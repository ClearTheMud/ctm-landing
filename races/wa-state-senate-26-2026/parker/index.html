Statement

Maybe you’d agree that helping others, when we can, is something we should all try to do? That belief in service and putting people above politics is what brings our community together.

But it takes humility and discipline to truly serve people. It means genuinely listening, saying no to outside special interests, and remembering who you work for. I’m that person. That’s why it’s so frustrating to see our incumbent spend her time working for Seattle while communities like ours feel forgotten.

I want to serve our seniors by protecting access to affordable care and making sure they are not taxed out of their homes. I want to serve our working families so they can afford to put gas in the car, food on the table, and provide for their children.

I’ve spent years as a corporate technology executive leading multi-national teams, and today I run a local business employing people and serving our community. Through all of it, one Truth has stayed consistent: if you want to serve people well, you have to genuinely care about them. I care about you, our community, and am willing to serve, if you’ll have me. If so, Vote for Gary Parker!
