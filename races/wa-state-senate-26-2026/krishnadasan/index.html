1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience
Current State Senator, LD 26, former Peninsula School District Board Member and President

Other Professional Experience
A mom, community leader, and advocate for public education, Deb has held professional roles at the Cystic Fibrosis Foundation and in mergers and acquisitions at Microsoft.

Education
BA, Western Washington University

Community Service
Deb founded one of the largest public school advocacy organizations, Stand Up for Peninsula Schools, earning Gig Harbor Chamber of Commerce’s “Citizen of the Year (2023)”. She also earned the “Golden Acorn” award for service as a PTA member and treasurer at Purdy Elementary. Past President of Peninsula Hawks Scholarship Foundation.

Statement
I stepped into public service to focus on the real issues people face. Whether it’s fixing our roads, creating jobs, or lowering costs, I’m committed to delivering for you. Every bill I passed was bipartisan and focused on making lives better. As State Senator, I expanded the Property Tax Relief Program for fixed-income seniors and disabled veterans, established an energy assistance program, and increased support for local healthcare and childcare programs.

My first experience in local politics was leading the effort to rebuild schools, delivering six new schools after decades of failed attempts. That experience showed me that when we listen and work toward commonsense solutions, together we can accomplish big things. I bring that same commitment to Olympia every day as your State Senator.

I secured funding for the 26th District’s transportation infrastructure, food banks, jobs, and other priorities. I voted against a gas tax increase and taxes on small businesses. I proposed the first sales tax cut in Washington in 40 years. We need leaders who listen, have integrity, and deliver results. That’s what I’ve done, and with your support, I’ll keep doing it.

Endorsements: Congresswoman Emily Randall, Planned Parenthood Alliance Advocates, teachers, union workers, and more.

Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, State Senator. Accessed 2026-07-16. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
