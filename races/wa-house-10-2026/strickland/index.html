Statement

The American people deserve a nation that is safe, just, and secure for all. This means housing and health care they can afford, stable jobs, safe neighborhoods with strong schools, and retiring with dignity. I am proud of my record to protect our civil rights and voting rights, honor tribal treaties, and delivering for the people of the South Sound.

I secured $50 million for our local communities to create jobs by investing in housing, main streets, pedestrian safety, bridges, public transit, and clean water. This includes funding for police and firefighters. I’ve helped thousands of people recover more than $10 million of federal benefits owed to them, and I secured pay raises and benefits for our service members, their families, and Veterans. 

But our work is not done. Too many hard-working families, seniors, farmers, and small businesses feel squeezed by the rising cost of gas, groceries, utilities, housing, child-care, and elder-care. It’s why I oppose Trump’s tariffs, his cuts to health care and food assistance, and the cruel chaos of this administration. 

Our economic security is on the ballot. Voting rights are on the ballot. Civil rights are on the ballot. The future of our nation is on the ballot. 

I’m grateful to be endorsed by Planned Parenthood, the Washington State Labor Council, the League of Conservation Voters, Sierra Club, and leaders from our tribal nations, business, human services, and faith communities. It is my honor to serve as your Congresswoman, and I humbly ask for your vote. 

Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, U.S. Representative. Accessed 2026-10-10. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
