Statement

I was raised in a family rooted in public service. My father served law enforcement, and from him I learned the importance of responsibility, discipline, and serving others with integrity. As the child of immigrant parents,I also came to understand the value of opportunity in this country, and how important it is that such opportunity remains open and fair to everyone.

This district means a great deal to me. It is home to neighbors from many different backgrounds and cultures, all living and working together. It includes military families who serve our nation , veterans who have given so much in service, and many families who work hard every day to build stable and meaningful lives. It is also home to teachers, healthcare workers, small business owners, and young people working toward their future.

At the same time, I have heard from many residents who are facing real challenges. The cost of living continues to rise. Housing has become more difficult to afford. Healthcare and education bring growing concerns for many families. And there are those who have served our country and communities who feel they are not receiving the full support they deserve.When I hear these concerns,I believe it is important to reflect and consider how things can be improved.

I don’t claim to have all the answers. what I do promise is to listen carefully, to learn from this community, and to work with honesty and responsibility.

My focus is simple: to help ease the burdens families are facing, to improve access to education and healthcare, to ensure that those who have served this community and country are treated with respect and dignity.

I respectfully ask for your support and your vote.

Finding 5.1: Ran as a Democrat in 2024, Now Running as a Republican in 2026 MODERATE

What happened
Chung sought the office of Washington Insurance Commissioner in the 2024 primary as a Democrat, appearing on the ballot and in voter guides under the Democratic Party label. For the 2026 U.S. House race in Washington's 10th Congressional District he is listed on the ballot and in candidate directories as a Republican. The party affiliation changed between his two most recent partisan candidacies, a span of roughly two years.
Source tier
T2
Political impact
Moderate
Defense
Washington's top-two primary lets candidates state any party preference without party approval, and a change in stated preference is legal. Chung has not publicly explained the change.
