Statement
WA-10 deserves a representative who answers to the people, not corporate PACs, AIPAC, or donors who will never feel the consequences of the votes they influence.

Families in our district feel the pressure every day: rising rent, grocery prices, unaffordable childcare, and utility costs that keep climbing. Schools are asked to do more with less, and too many jobs don’t pay enough to build a future. I live in the district, my children attend school here, and I see how these choices affect our classrooms, bills, roads, healthcare, and our kids’ futures.

Too often, federal decisions are made far from the people who feel the impact. That’s why I built Watchtower, a public-records platform that tracks those decisions and shows how they affect local costs, services, and families here at home. Voters deserve transparency.

I’m seeking the legislative authority and public position to fix systems I’m already auditing and stop federal waste driving up local costs. You deserve to know who stands to benefit, who bears the cost, and what your representative actually did.

My background is service and accountability. I’m a county program manager and Army veteran. I bring military discipline and project management experience to a Congress that too often fails to deliver. Real answers about lowering costs, expanding healthcare, creating good jobs, fixing infrastructure, and making government work.

Too many leaders ignore warning signs and leave working families paying for preventable crises. It is time to put families, not insiders or donors, at the center of every decision. WA-10 needs a representative who can plan ahead and deliver results for the people. I fought for this country, and I’m ready to fight for you.
