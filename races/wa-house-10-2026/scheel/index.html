Statement

The American working class is being squeezed between stagnant wages and rising prices. Instead of addressing these crises, our government is spending our money on killing schoolchildren on the other side of the world. On top of the immorality, this war is making the affordability crisis worse, with higher prices of oil, gas, fertilizer, and food. If elected, I pledge to oppose this and any other unjust war, not just by voting for a War Powers Resolution, but by voting against war funding and using my platform to organize public opposition to the war.

Instead of spending our money on mass murder, I will work for real universal healthcare and building the public housing needed to address the homelessness crisis. I have seen the effects cuts to the VA have had on my fellow veterans, but this is not an issue exclusive to veterans. Many of our friends and family are suffering from lack of healthcare, housing, or both, and the federal government can solve these problems for a fraction of what we spend on war.

I will continue supporting and amplifying the work organizers are doing to defend marginalized members of our community, including defending immigrants from ICE, defending healthcare and equal participation in society for transgender kids and adults, and helping renters organize to defend themselves from slumlords. When the tech oligarchs try to use our limited water and electricity to run their AI datacenters, I will stand with our communities to tell them no. The working class is what makes Washington great, and I am proud to to be a part of it. I will be honored to earn your vote. A better world is possible!
