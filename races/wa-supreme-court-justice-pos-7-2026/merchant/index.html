1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (Primary Election 2026)

Legal/Judicial Experience
As a Public Defender in King County, I spent the first 15 years of my legal career in the trenches of our Justice System, serving as a constitutional check on the Government’s power to prosecute and incarcerate. Since 2023, I have continued that mission by operating my own practice.

Other Professional Experience
Member—Washington Defender Association; Washington Association of Criminal Defense Lawyers

Education
Howard University School of Law, J.D. 2007; University of Washington, B.A. 2003; A.A., Bellevue Community College, 2001; Bellevue Public School, 2nd to 12th grade, 1997.

Community Service
Volunteer coach for youth sports; Educating Washingtonians on civics and constitutional law through social media.

Statement
I was born in Cleveland, Ohio to working class immigrants and raised here in Washington since 1987. As a judicial candidate, I bring deep constitutional advocacy, and I understand the struggles facing everyday Washingtonians.

For 18 years, I’ve represented people from every walk of life: working families, small business owners, parents, veterans, and individuals accused of serious crimes. I have never represented corporations, never represented the government. I've fought for real people when constitutional rights mattered most. My career has focused on protecting the right of the People to be free from unlawful government intrusion. Those protections must apply equally to all, regardless of status, wealth, or background.

I also believe the judiciary must remain independent, impartial, and free from politics. The promise of our Constitution is only meaningful if Judges faithfully interpret and apply the law, ensuring the People receive the government our Constitution guarantees.

You will not find judicial endorsements or a massive war chest here. What you will find is a legal mind grounded in the promise of our Constitution with a commitment to real-world solutions and equal justice for all—I would be honored to earn your vote.

Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, Justice Position #07. Accessed 2026-07-16. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
