1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

Gig Harbor Planning Commission. Endorsed by State Representative Michelle Caldier-Valdez, former State Representative Spencer Hutchins, and Key Peninsula Fire Fighters.

Other Professional Experience

Nonprofit leader, small business owner, and pastor with 18+ years leading teams, managing budgets, and developing successful programs that serve communities, families, refugees and empowering people out of poverty and addiction.

Education

Raised locally. South Kitsap High School graduate; Associate degree, Olympic College; Bachelor’s in Psychology, NU; Masters in Nonprofit Leadership, Seattle U.

Community Service

Co-President, Walk In The Light International; VP, Heart for the City; Organized Christmas presents, food, school supplies, baby products drives alongside my husband and three children.

Statement

You deserve a Washington where hard work still leads to opportunity, families can afford a home, small businesses can thrive creating good-paying jobs, and communities are safe. I’m running because too many feel these opportunities are gone. 

I’ve spent my life serving others, building solutions, and bringing people together to solve difficult problems with compassion and accountability.  We can fix the root causes of our affordability crisis, strengthen public safety, support roads, bridges, and ferries, and ensure the government serves people effectively through smarter spending, not higher taxes. Let’s fix what’s broken in Olympia and restore opportunity for everyone.
