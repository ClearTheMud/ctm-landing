1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (General Election 2026)

Elected Experience

First-time candidate for State Representative. Elected Faculty Representative, Tacoma Community College AFT-WA local.

Other Professional Experience

Special Educator, Holly Ridge Center. Adjunct Instructor, Tacoma Community College. Former CPS Investigator, Suquamish Tribe. Managed ECEAP, Early Head Start and childcare programs. Former small business owner.

Education

MS, Psychology, University of Phoenix. BA, History, Occidental College. Early Childhood Program Administration certificates, Olympic College.

Community Service

Representative, Salish Behavioral Health Administrative Services Organization Advisory Board. President, South Kitsap School District Native Education Parent Council. Home Visiting Advisory Member, Suquamish Early Learning Center. Former Tri-chair, DCYF Childcare Provider Supports Advisory Subcommittee.

Statement

Peninsula families are exhausted by housing costs, gridlock, unreliable ferries, and schools forced to do more with less. As an educator, working mom, and union member, I’ve spent every day bettering the lives of our children and their families. I'll fight for home prices that match local wages, excellent public schools, and roads and ferries we’ve waited decades for.

Endorsed and Approved: 26th District Democrats, Kitsap Democrats, State Labor Council, Washington Education Association, American Federation of Teachers, National Women’s Political Caucus, Washington Conservation Action, Planned Parenthood Alliance Advocates, Congressmember Emily Randall, County Councilmembers Hitchen and Denson, Commissioner Rolfes, and more.
