1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

I was elected President of the Associated Students of Tacoma Community College in 2024. In 2025, I was elected Vice President of Federal Affairs for the Washington Student Association, representing around 126,000 students across our state.

Other Professional Experience

+15 years of Washington workforce experience.

Education

Bachelor's of Applied Sciences degree in Applied Management, Project Management, in June 2026 from Tacoma Community College.

Community Service

For the '25-'26 school year, I volunteered and was elected to serve students across the country as the President for the United States Student Association, elevating the needs of over 2.2 million students to federal lawmakers. 

Statement

I believe in leaders who want to serve, not for personal gain but to make a real difference for the people. I will be a representative who listens and understands. My journey brought me back to school in my thirties, where I learned the importance of education, advocacy, and public service. I've seen what can happen when communities are heard. I took those lessons to our nation's capital. Now, I want to bring that energy back to our community. And I will work to be someone you can trust and believe in. Are you ready for Randy?
