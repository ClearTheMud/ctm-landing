1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (Primary Election 2026)

Elected Experience

Not a career politician. I’m a longtime community leader running to bring practical leadership and our community’s voice to Olympia.

Other Professional Experience

For almost 14 years, I’ve employed community members as a small business owner. Prior, I worked with the United Nations Commissioner for Refugees and later helped lead infrastructure projects serving underserved communities in Africa.

Education

Florida State University

Community Service

I support local education through the Peninsula Class of ‘88 Foundation, advocate for veterans with the Permission to Start Dreaming Foundation, support environmental protection efforts with Communities for a Healthy Bay, and contribute to the Carol Milgard Breast Center.

Statement

As a small business owner, employer, and longtime community member, I know how important opportunity is. I’m running because too many families are being priced out of housing, struggling with rising costs, and feeling ignored by politics that focus on division instead of solutions. I’ll fight to lower costs, expand affordable housing, improve healthcare access, strengthen public schools, protect reproductive freedom, and ensure equal treatment for LGBTQIA2S+ individuals and immigrant communities. I’ll work to make sure our community has a voice in Olympia! Proudly endorsed by Pierce County Executive Ryan Mello and approved by the 26th Legislative District Democrats. 



Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, State Representative Pos. 2. Accessed 2026-07-16. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
