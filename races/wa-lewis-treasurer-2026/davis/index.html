1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience
Arny Davis is the current Lewis County Treasurer, serving since January 2015 as the 28th Treasurer since 1850. A Lewis County native since age 11, he previously served as Chief Deputy Treasurer and on the Lewis County Planning Commission (2007-2014). With over 30 years in business management, finance, and banking, Arny brings strong expertise to county financial operations. He and his wife Ellen live in Adna. They have two daughters, six grandchildren, and are active in the community through Rotary and church. Arny is committed to delivering high-quality, trustworthy service to Lewis County citizens.

Finding 6.1: Processed the $1.2M Darigold assessment refund as treasurer LOW

What happened
In 2025, Lewis County was required to refund approximately $1.2 million to Darigold Inc. after the company successfully challenged a 2023 property assessment that had overvalued its Chehalis production facility. The reassessment reduced the property value by over $70 million. Davis reported that the refund took several smaller taxing districts "in the red." Davis attended the Port of Chehalis meeting to explain the situation and noted the assessor's communication to affected districts was "a little ambiguous, at best." While Davis did not cause the assessment error (that falls under the Assessor's office), his role as Treasurer required him to manage the financial fallout and process the refunds.
Source tier
T2
Political impact
Low
Defense
The property assessment is the responsibility of the Lewis County Assessor's office, not the Treasurer. Davis's role was to process the legally required refund and communicate the financial impact to affected districts. His proactive attendance at the Port of Chehalis meeting demonstrates transparency and responsible financial management. The situation was caused by Darigold's legal challenge, not by any action of the Treasurer's office.

Finding 6.2: Has run unopposed since 2014 LOW

What happened
Davis has served as Lewis County Treasurer since January 2015 and has never faced a contested election. He ran unopposed in 2014, 2018, and 2022 (only write-in opposition in 2022). If reelected in 2026, he will have served 16 years.
