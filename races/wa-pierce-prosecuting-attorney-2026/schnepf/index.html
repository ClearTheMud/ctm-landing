Statement
For nearly thirty years, Coreen Schnepf has worked to protect people and uphold justice. Her career started as a CNA in a nursing home dementia unit while she worked her way through college. This experience taught her to stand with people on the worst days of their lives, empathy she now brings to victims in court.

Her passion for public safety started while interning at a prosecutor’s office. Since then, she’s secured convictions of murderers, rapists, human traffickers, and domestic abusers — making our community safer. Coreen’s management expertise and courtroom skills are why she leads our county’s Criminal Division of 180 attorneys and staff, the largest division of the Prosecutor’s Office, and is trusted statewide to train police and prosecutors.

Coreen has no patience for crime of any kind. She knows stolen cars mean parents can’t get to work or kids to school. Retail theft isn’t just creating an inconvenience through locked shelves, it’s driving up the prices of everyday essentials. As Prosecutor, Coreen will stand up for you by holding criminals accountable who make life for you challenging, expensive, and unsafe.

Retiring Prosecutor Mary Robnett said, “Coreen is next-generation leadership for this office. Her expertise in complex digital evidence and evolving technology is critical for getting convictions. Coreen is committed to justice, not a political party.” That’s why Coreen is endorsed by public safety professionals and serious leaders from both parties. She and her husband Mike, a law enforcement officer, are raising their children in Tacoma.
