1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (General Election 2026)

Elected Experience

Your State Representative since 2012.

Other Professional Experience

UW School of Public Health faculty; Public interest attorney; Director of Heart of America Northwest, the citizens’ group leading efforts for the environmental cleanup of Hanford Nuclear site, supervises the region’s only hands-on Tribal Environmental law student program.




Education

UW School of Law

Community Service

Honored repeatedly as “Legislator of the Year” by groups advocating for affordable college, rights of persons with disabilities, special education, and open government. “Sunshine in Government” honoree of League of Women Voters.  Serves on the Washington Coalition for Open Government Board. Received UW School of Public Health, Community Service Award. Longtime PTSA activist. 



Statement

I’ve led on major progressive wins. There’s more to do. I championed the Millionaires and capital gains taxes to make the wealthiest pay their fair share to fund schools, healthcare, and childcare. I’m working to ensure our kids can afford to live in Seattle and seniors can age in place by building affordable housing, cutting sales tax, and making college free. I’ve fought Trump by passing ICE mask bans, funding abortion access, and protecting immigrants. I’ve always stood up to special interests. 

Endorsed: Planned Parenthood; Washington Conservation Action; Nurses; Teachers; Labor Council; Alliance for Gun Responsibility; Congresswoman Pramila Jayapal; more.
