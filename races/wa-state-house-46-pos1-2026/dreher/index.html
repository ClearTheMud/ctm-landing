1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

Longtime public servant who prosecuted January 6th rioters, defended civil rights, and clerked for U.S. Supreme Court Justice Elena Kagan; first time candidate.

Other Professional Experience

Attorney suing oil, tobacco, and AI companies to protect consumers and communities; former Assistant U.S. Attorney (federal prosecutor) who prosecuted January 6th rioters, hate and violent crimes, and civil rights violations by officers; energy and pro-environment lawyer; 4th grade public school teacher; researcher at union-affiliated political organizations.

Education

BSFS, Georgetown University; JD, Harvard Law School.

Community Service

Youth Law Day, Criminal Law Committee, Federal Bar Association; St. Bridget Catholic Church; American Association for Justice; NESLL girls’ softball coach.

Statement

Want an experienced candidate with fresh ideas instead of stale talking points? As a former teacher, I have a plan to fully fund schools and improve school performance while enacting social-media safeguards for kids. I’ll cut red tape, fund affordable housing, childcare, and transit, and fix regressive taxes. I’ll implement evidence-based public safety measures and hold our government accountable for our tax dollars. And I’m the only candidate promising to aggressively regulate tech to slow relentless AI disinformation, job loss from AI/automation, and data center growth.

Endorsed by City Attorney Erika Evans, City Councilmember Dionne Foster, College Dems, and more.
