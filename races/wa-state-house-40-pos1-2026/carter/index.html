1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience
Proven leadership in state and local politics

Other Professional Experience
Worked 5 years in the Chemistry Department of Seattle’s Virginia Mason Hospital Laboratory and held secretarial and office management roles on Orcas Island.

Education
Earned a B.S. Degree in Medical Technology, certified by American Society of Clinical Pathologists(ASCP).   Homeschooled her 4 children and supported many other children’s education in the community.

Community Service
Cindy has volunteered with many non-profits including the Buck Park Tennis Association, and Buck Park Association. She co-coached girls’ softball and held leadership roles at Orcas Island Community Church, demonstrating her commitment to local families, community connections, and Orcas Island’s future.

Statement
I am running for the 40th Legislative District to represent the people.   My opponent voted for the statewide income tax against the will of the people. I will fight to repeal this unconstitutional tax before it forces every Washingtonian to register their personal financial information with the state.   I am committed to bring fiscal responsibility back to the state. My priorities include improving Transportation, especially fixing the ferry and bridge issues, and restoring Washington’s education to national excellence.   Together, let’s put government back in the hands of the people and deliver results to strengthen our families and communities.

Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, State Representative Pos. 1. Accessed 2026-07-16. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
