1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience
Serving as your Representative in Olympia for the last eight years, Debra currently serves on the Healthcare and Wellness, Housing, Rules and Appropriations Committees, Joint Legislative Committee on Water Supply during Drought, Task Force for Missing and Murdered Indigenous Women and People and Legislative Committee on Economic Development and International Relations.

Other Professional Experience
Debra has over 30 years in public service ranging from healthcare, salmon recovery and protection, climate change, infrastructure, water, treaty rights, education, housing, workforce, and unions.

Education
BA, Business Administration/Finance, Central Washington University.

Community Service
Member of National Caucus of Environmental Legislators, MMIWP Task Force, past board member EarthJustice

Statement
Debra is committed to every kitchen table issue facing Washington families — affordability, healthcare, balanced budgets, housing, public safety, and schools. She works to unite communities and support governments to find solutions while protecting salmon, forests, parks, and the Salish Sea, supporting unions and jobs, defending reproductive freedom, and addressing the fentanyl and homelessness crises together. She’s also helped fund three new ferries and accelerated hiring for our ferry system. 

Endorsed by Governor Bob Ferguson, Washington State Labor Council, Lummi Nation, Washington Conservation Action, Washington State Nurses Association, Whatcom County Firefighters. 

Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, State Representative Pos. 1. Accessed 2026-07-16. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
