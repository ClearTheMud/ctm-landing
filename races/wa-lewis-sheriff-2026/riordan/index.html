Finding 3.1: East Lewis County Focus — Grassroots Rural Strategy MODERATE

What happened
Riordan has concentrated his campaign efforts in East Lewis County — the larger, more rural, and less populated portion of the county. This includes communities like Morton, Randle, Packwood, and Pe Ell. His strategy of meet-and-greets and small in-person meetings targets voters who may feel underserved by a sheriff's office headquartered in Chehalis. This is both a strength (building rural loyalty) and a vulnerability (may not build sufficient support in the more populated western corridor around Centralia-Chehalis where most voters reside).

Defense
East Lewis County voters are a critical constituency who feel underrepresented. Building rural support first and expanding westward is a viable strategy in a multi-candidate primary where no single candidate dominates.
