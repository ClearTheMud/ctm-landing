1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

SKEA Executive Board Member-at-Large; Uniserv Representative for WEA Olympic 





Other Professional Experience

Classroom teacher, Sidney Glen Elementary; Former Nuclear Test Pipefitter, Puget Sound Naval Shipyard; Air Force Veteran, Logistics Readiness Officer


Education

BA Political Science, Washington State University (2005); PSNS Apprenticeship, ATA Industrial Trades, Olympic College (2017); Graduate Certificate, Education for Environment and Community, IslandWood (2018); Master in Teaching, University of Washington (2019)


Community Service

In addition to my work as a teacher and union advocate, I serve as a Local Delegate to the WEA Representative Assembly and a State Delegate to the national Assembly. Actively involved in Indivisible Advocacy and a local Precinct Committee Officer.



Statement

I’m running to get better results for the people of the 26th LD. We’ve made progress in Olympia, but on affordability and outcomes from taxpayer-funded programs, we’re still missing the mark. Raised in Kitsap, I’m a mom, veteran, teacher, former shipyard worker, and tireless advocate for working families.

I’ll fight for progressive revenue and to minimize sales and property taxes. We need fresh ideas to lower housing and healthcare costs, strengthen public schools, and create resilient jobs. I’ll stand up to anyone that puts profits over results we can see and trust. Endorsed: Washington Educators’ Association, Washington State Labor Council.
