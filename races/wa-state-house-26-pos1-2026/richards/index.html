1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

State Representative, 2025-current.

Other Professional Experience

Northwest Justice Project, served domestic violence and human trafficking victims; Kitsap Legal Services, served people in housing crises; PCVA, Associate Attorney.


Education

Peninsula High; UW; Villanova Law, JD.

Community Service

Peninsula High Cross Country Coach, Rotary, Foundation for Washington Courts, and volunteer for many organizations. I grew up here and have lived in every part of the district, working through school for my dad’s small business. My mom’s a public-school teacher. As State Representative, I’ve heard from thousands of you at the doors and share your concerns. I love our community and want to help our state work better.

Statement

You deserve common sense leaders in Olympia. As your Representative, I’ve worked to address the rising cost of groceries by securing a tax cut for Washington farmers, sponsored legislation to fund more local police officers, and voted against raising taxes on working families and seniors. But we must do more to bring down the cost of housing, healthcare, gas, and utilities, while creating well-paying jobs.

I helped complete a trade school in our community, and I’ll prioritize funding for our schools to expand opportunity for all. I’ll continue protecting reproductive rights and our democracy. I ask for your vote.

Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, State Representative Pos. 1. Accessed 2026-07-16. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
