Statement

Olympia has lost focus. As a Navy veteran, public finance banker, and 13-year school board member, I help lead one of Washington’s top 10% school districts. We focus on literacy, math, school safety, responsible spending, and career, technical, and skilled-trades pathways.

Olympia should strengthen schools, not cut K-12 programs, including Running Start and Transitional Kindergarten, while passing an income tax and diverting money from the police and firefighter pension fund. Washingtonians deserve better. I will bring balance, accountability, and common sense to Olympia, standing with parents, students, seniors, veterans, law enforcement, and working families. I respectfully ask for your vote.
