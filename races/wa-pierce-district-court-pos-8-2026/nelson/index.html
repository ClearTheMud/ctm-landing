Statement
Sven Nelson has dedicated his career to serving Pierce County. As a Deputy Prosecuting Attorney, he's tried serious felonies involving sexual assault, elder abuse, and homicide. He served as a Judge Pro Tempore, earning a reputation for integrity, fairness, and knowledge of the law. His experience on both sides of the bench provides a practical understanding of how a judge's decisions affect victims, defendants, and families.

For a decade, he led Pierce County's Elder Abuse Multidisciplinary Team, coordinating law enforcement, prosecutors, and social services to better protect vulnerable adults. Beyond the courtroom, he's an elected Trustee of the Tacoma-Pierce County Bar Association and former Board President of Rebuilding Hope Sexual Assault Center.

Endorsements: Retiring Pos. 8 Judge Lineberry, a majority of Pierce County's District Court and Superior Court judges, Former County Executive Ladenburg, Former County Executive Dammeier, County Executive Mello, and Tacoma Mayor Ibsen. Please vote Sven Nelson for Judge.
