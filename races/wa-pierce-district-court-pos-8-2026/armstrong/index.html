1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (General Election 2026)

Education

Statement
As a proud United States Marine Corps veteran, I learned that leadership requires discipline, integrity, and duty. I have carried these values throughout my 26-year legal career as a veteran trial attorney, Judge Pro Tempore, and Presiding Judge. I will bring that leadership to the Pierce County District Court to command a fair, orderly, and efficient courtroom.

As the Presiding Judge at Bremerton Municipal Court, I exercised full responsibility for the court’s budget, personnel, and day-to-day operations. I made the tough decisions necessary to restructure staff, restore public trust, and streamline processes. I know exactly how to manage high-volume criminal dockets, preside over complex jury trials, and issue fair, well-reasoned decisions grounded in law and evidence.

I ask for your vote to bring this proven experience to the Pierce County District Court bench. I would be honored to have your support to deliver real, community-changing results. Thank you.
