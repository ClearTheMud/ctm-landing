1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

Appointed County Clerk, January 2025 & Elected County Clerk, January 2026



Other Professional Experience

Professional experience includes Payroll and Accounting with the Salvation Army from 2012–2018. I joined Wahkiakum Courts in 2018 as Chief Clerk for District Court, later serving as Deputy Clerk and Chief Deputy Clerk for Superior Court before being appointed County Clerk in 2025.



Education

B.A. in Social Sciences, Washington State University, 2010 & A.A. Degree, Green River Community College, 2007 



Community Service

My commitment to this community has primarily been through public service and professional dedication.  I have focused on serving residents with fairness and accessibility while remaining responsive to community needs. 



Statement

Serving as County Clerk has been an honor, and I remain committed to providing professional, efficient, and compassionate service to our community. Building on the office’s strong foundation, I have worked to improve the efficiency, transparency, and accessibility of the Clerk’s Office by updating internal processing systems and have successfully implemented an electronic filing system. These improvements have streamlined operations, increased convenience for the public and legal community, and strengthened our ability to serve the county effectively.

For many people, the Clerk’s Office is their first experience with the court system, often during difficult or stressful circumstances. I believe every person deserves to be treated with kindness, respect, and professionalism. Clear communication, reliable assistance, and a willingness to help remain at the center of how I lead the office.

Having served in the Superior Court Clerk’s Office for many years, I bring firsthand experience, a strong work ethic, and a genuine passion for public service. Serving this community has been deeply meaningful to me, and I would be honored to continue that work as your County Clerk.
