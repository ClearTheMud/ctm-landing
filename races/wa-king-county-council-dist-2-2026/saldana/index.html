1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (General Election 2026)

Education: 37th LD Senator (2017–Present); Executive Director, Puget Sound Sage (2014–2016)

Occupation: B.A. Humanities and Theology, Seattle University

Statement: I’m running for King County Council to build more housing that is affordable at any income, raise wages, improve transit, and make universal childcare a reality. Making King County a place every family can afford will require transformational change, and that’s exactly what I have been doing in the state senate for a decade. 

Too many families are choosing between paying for rent or health insurance. As the daughter of a formerly undocumented Mexican farmworker, I will never stop fighting to protect our communities from masked ICE agents terrorizing our neighbors and corporations stealing our wages. 

In the state senate, I championed and passed taxes on the ultra-wealthy, including the Capital Gains Tax and Millionaires Tax, to fund the essential services we need. As Labor Committee Chair, I passed the most progressive Paid Family and Medical Leave programs in the country and passed a Domestic Workers Bill of Rights to ensure fairness and dignity for care workers. I also delivered on landmark investments in affordable housing, transit, and community investments.

I’m proud to have earned the sole endorsement of the King County Democrats. I’ll bring new energy and proven leadership to the council to expand behavioral health and crisis response, improve transit access, and lower costs for working families. Addressing our toughest challenges will mean standing up to powerful special interests and never compromising on our values. That’s what I’ve spent my life doing.

Endorsements: King County Councilmembers Mosqueda, Perry, Balducci, and Gossett (fmr); Seattle City Councilmembers Foster, Hollingsworth, and Lin; progressive legislators Scott, Macri, Farivar, Street, and Javier Valdez; unions representing essential workers (SEIU 775, SEIU 925, and UFCW 3000); county employees (PROTEC17; Teamsters 28, 117, 174, and 763; and AFSCME Council 2); machinists (IAM 751); graduate students (UAW 6); more at rebeccasaldana.com!

Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, Metropolitan King County Council District No. 2. Accessed 2026-10-10. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
