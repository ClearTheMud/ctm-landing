1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Education: Masters, Criminal Justice; Bachelors, Criminal Justice & Spanish; Seattle University; Garfield High School; Kimball Elementary

Occupation: Port of Seattle Commissioner; Small Business Owner, Agency Executive Director

Statement: People across our district are tired of hearing promises made but not seeing them kept. The cost of living keeps climbing; housing, childcare, and groceries are too expensive. Our communities pay the price for the lack of state support, as local governments are left underfunded and overstretched. Our district deserves investments and outcomes. People deserve to live in the communities they love. The moment for courage and change is now. 

This fight is personal. I’m raising my children on Beacon Hill in the same home my grandparents bought after being released from a Japanese American concentration camp. I’ve watched neighbors have to leave because of politicians who don’t understand the consequences of their votes. We deserve better.

Under Governor Inslee, I led a state agency where we fought for economic development, directed vital resources to local small businesses, and delivered aid to communities facing healthcare crises. At King County's Office of Law Enforcement Oversight, I led critical reforms to advance police accountability and establish the grassroots, community-driven solutions needed to prevent gun violence. As Port Commissioner, I’m delivering on affordability by creating a childcare center and securing good union jobs with real benefits. 

As your County Councilmember, I will build affordable housing, promote safe and reliable public transit, protect renters, and expand childcare access. I will fund mental health systems and invest in community-led strategies that stop gun violence before it tears another family apart. I will bring real accountability and oversight to our county, and champion progressive funding options so the ultra-wealthy pay their share.

City Attorney Erika Evans, Councilmembers Alexis Mercedes Rinck and Eddie Lin, former Governor Inslee, legislators, labor unions, Democratic organizations, and over 100 progressive District 2 community leaders endorse me because they’ve seen me deliver on what I promised. Let’s choose a better future.

Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, Metropolitan King County Council District No. 2. Accessed 2026-07-16. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
