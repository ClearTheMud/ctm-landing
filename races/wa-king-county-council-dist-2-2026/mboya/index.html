1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Education: BA Political Science W/ specialization in Legal Studies; History Minor; MA Communications

Occupation: King County Program and Operations Professional

Statement: I am running for King County Council because I believe government should work for everyday people and deliver real results for the communities it serves. District 2 deserves leadership that understands both the lived experiences of residents and how County government operates internally.

As someone who has experienced homelessness, food insecurity, mental health struggles, and as the daughter of a man who was incarcerated, I understand personally how deeply government systems impact people’s lives. I know what it feels like when families are struggling to find stability, support, and opportunity, and those experiences shaped my commitment to public service and community advocacy. As an immigrant, I also understand many of the barriers families face while trying to build security and opportunity for future generations.

For the past seven years, I have worked inside King County government, where I have gained firsthand experience with County budgeting, operations, contracting, and community partnerships. I understand how decisions made at the County level directly impact housing, transportation, behavioral health services, public safety, and economic opportunity across our communities.

King County manages a $20 billion biennial budget while serving more than 2.2 million residents across 39 cities and unincorporated communities. That responsibility requires thoughtful stewardship, transparency, collaboration, and a commitment to ensuring resources are reaching the people and programs that need them most.

My priorities include housing affordability and homelessness prevention, behavioral health and addiction treatment, transit access, youth opportunity, economic stability for working families, and community-centered public safety. I believe we must focus not only on crisis response, but also on prevention, long-term stability, and building systems that help people thrive before they reach crisis.

District 2 deserves leadership that listens, leads with empathy, and understands these systems from both personal and professional experience. I would be honored to earn your vote.
