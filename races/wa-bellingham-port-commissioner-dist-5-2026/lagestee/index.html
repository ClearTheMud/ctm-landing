4 Notable Public Statements

“I retired after 23+ years serving as a Bellingham firefighter, and I’m ready to continue serving as your next Port Commissioner. Before that, I protected our nation as a Navy Nuclear Operator aboard submarines.My experience as a credit union director and Public Utility District Commissioner has prepared me to navigate complex financial and infrastructure challenges. I support local investment strategies, such as a Public Development Authority (PDA), that keep public tax dollars invested locally, instead of Texas or California. A PDA captures development profits to build affordable housing, while supporting living-wage jobs, and can help keep our waterfront publicly owned!As our Port expands its economic development role into agriculture, we need a strategic plan with measurable goals, and greater accountability. Increasing transparency by making contracts and financial reporting easier to access and understand is my goal. Public investment should create lasting community benefits for everyone. That means supporting sustainable industry, protecting environmental resources, and ensuring local workers and businesses share in the opportunities created by our Port’s projects. I will listen and work collaboratively with unions, local governments and Tribal Nations to build an economy that is both resilient and responsible.A rising tide should lift -all- boats!”
