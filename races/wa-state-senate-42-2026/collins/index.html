Statement

Every day in my classroom, I see the human cost of a broken economy pushing families to the brink. Too many people are doing everything right yet struggling to pay bills and buy groceries. As a husband and parent raising my family here, these struggles are deeply personal.

Our community cannot afford to be shaped by insiders protecting a broken system. We need new leaders who understand the urgency people feel every day and will fight for real solutions. I’m running for State Senate to be the fierce advocate your family needs.

As a teacher and former U.S. Senate policy adviser, I bring the experience to write laws that work for regular people. Through my leadership at the Kulshan Community Land Trust, I know how to build permanently affordable homes in our community. In Olympia, I’ll focus on lowering everyday costs, reducing the tax burden on working families and small businesses, safeguarding local water and energy, and protecting healthcare and reproductive freedom.

I’m proudly endorsed by local leaders like State Representative Joe Timmons, a majority of the Whatcom County Council, the State Labor Council, and many others. Together, we can build a community where everyone has the opportunity to thrive.
