1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

While I have no experience I do have 14 years involved in politics. Job shadowing in Olympia, attending County Council and City Council meetings along with administrative meetings to prepare for this honor. 

Other Professional Experience

President of Rye's CBD - Ryescbd.com, 13 years as a Professional Massage Therapist



Education

A Massage Therapy degree attained from Ashmead College in Fife, WA 2007.



Community Service

I've given time to the Bellingham Food Bank, Growing Veterans, Wildbird Charity, Bellingham Misfits, RESources, along with just the acts of community service without ties to any entity.

Statement

Aloha, my name is Rye Bow, and I’m running to bring practical leadership, accountability, and long-term thinking back to government. Our communities are facing rising costs, strained infrastructure, public safety concerns, housing pressure, environmental decline, and growing distrust in institutions. Too often, politics has become performative division instead of productive problem-solving. I believe we can do better.

My campaign is centered on economic renewal, workforce development, environmental stewardship, and restoring civic trust. I support policies that strengthen local industry, invest in sustainable infrastructure, expand opportunities for working families, and create pathways for people to contribute meaningfully to their communities. I believe government should operate with transparency, fiscal responsibility, and measurable outcomes. Public dollars should deliver real value to the people funding them.

As a resident of Whatcom County, I understand the importance of balancing growth with preservation of our natural resources and quality of life. I also believe collaboration with tribal nations, local businesses, labor, agriculture, and community organizations is essential to building durable solutions. This campaign is about moving beyond partisan noise and focusing on results that improve everyday life for Washington families. We rise up better together.
