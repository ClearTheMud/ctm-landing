1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience
Port Commissioner in his third term; former Democratic precinct committee officer.

Other Professional Experience
Western Washington University Anthropology Department faculty member; instructor for incarcerated student prison education program. Previously, an eLearning Director at Whatcom Community College and Cultural Documentation Coordinator at Northwest Indian College.

Education
Ph.D in Anthropology from the University of British Columbia. M.A. in Anthropology and B.A. in Environmental Studies from Western Washington University. 

Community Service
Whatcom County Economic Investment Board Member, Former Lummi Tribal Schools Marine Trades Advisory Committee, Whatcom County Portage Bay Shellfish Protection District Advisory Committee, and Kulshan Community Land Trust Board of Trustees member.

Statement
My wife and I are raising our kids here, and like many of you, we feel the pressure of rising costs every month – groceries, gas, housing, and childcare. It’s getting harder to keep up, and that has to change. In the State Senate, I’ll work to bring down costs.

As Port Commissioner, I focused on creating good jobs while protecting the air we breathe and the water we drink. We modernized our shipping terminal, expanded broadband internet into rural areas, opened the waterfront district, and cleaned up Blaine Harbor. And that’s why I’m running for Senate - we need that same kind of focus on jobs, and building a strong, sustainable economy at the state level.

As an educator, I believe every student deserves a strong start and a clear path forward. I’ll invest in high-quality Pre-K, K-12, and postsecondary education, including college, trade schools, apprenticeship programs, and adult retraining. I also strongly support public safety, environmental sustainability, the protection of public lands, and I am 100% pro-choice.

Endorsed by Sen. Shewmake, Sheriff Tanksly, Mayor Steward, Executive Sidhu, Whatcom Commercial Fishermen’s Association, Washington Conservation Action, Washington State Labor Council, NW Carpenters, Washington Education Association, and Bellingham/Whatcom County Firefighters.

Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, State Senator. Accessed 2026-07-16. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
