Statement

As a longtime attorney, District Court Judge Pro Tem, and Superior Court Commissioner, I have dedicated my career to serving our community with fairness, professionalism, and respect.

District Court is often where people first encounter the justice system. The court handles criminal, civil, infraction, and protection order cases that directly impact individuals, families, and public safety throughout Clallam County. Every person who enters the courtroom deserves to be treated with dignity and heard fairly.

I have served as a District Court Judge Pro Tem since 2016 and as a Superior Court Commissioner since 2019. Alongside my judicial service, I have practiced as both a prosecutor and defense attorney, providing broad experience and perspective within our legal system.

I am committed to maintaining a courtroom that is fair, respectful, efficient, and accessible to all members of our community. Clallam County deserves a judge who is experienced, even-tempered, and ready to serve on day one. I would be honored to earn your vote.
