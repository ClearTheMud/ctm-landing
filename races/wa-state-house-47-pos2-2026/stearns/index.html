1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (General Election 2026)

Elected Experience

State Representative; Speaker Pro Tempore; Vice Chair, State Government & Tribal Relations; Committee member: Capital Budget, Environment & Energy, Rules; former Auburn City Councilmember

Other Professional Experience

Career working with tribes, rural, and marginalized communities to create jobs, invest in clean energy, and protect rights; appointed by President Clinton as U.S. Energy Department’s first-ever Indian Affairs Director; former Chair, WA State Gambling Commission


Education

Cornell Law School, JD; Williams College, BA

Community Service

King County Affordable Housing Commission; King County Mental Illness and Drug Dependency Committee; WA State Missing and Murdered Indigenous Women Task Force


Statement

As your Representative, I’ve worked to deliver real results for working families—expanding affordable housing, strengthening worker protections, improving behavioral health services, and defending civil rights. As a Navajo attorney, I’ve spent my career standing up for justice and speaking up for communities left out of decision-making. I’ll continue fighting for strong schools, safer communities, and an economy where every family has the opportunity to succeed.

Endorsed: Auburn Mayor Backus, Kent Mayor Ralph, Covington Mayor Smith; Congressmember Adam Smith; Governor Bob Ferguson; 47th LD Democrats; WA State Nurses Association; Aerospace Machinists 751; WA Conservation Action; WA State Labor Council and more! 



Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, State Representative Pos. 2. Accessed 2026-10-10. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
