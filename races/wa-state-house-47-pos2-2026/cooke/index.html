Community Service

Community Service:  Formerly on the Board of Directors for Portable Academic Lapdesk (education non-profit);  Past volunteer work:  Cops With Cancer, Boots & Badges, Echo Glen Children’s Center, Green Hill School, Vine Maple Place Women's Shelter, Union Gospel Mission, Maple Valley Food Bank, American Indian Fellowship Northwest Network (Colville Reservation), Miguel de Cervantes Elementary School (Nicaragua), and Rainbow Evangelical College (Fiji).

Statement

Democrats made Washington unaffordable. I have a plan to fix it. I'll freeze all new taxes and eliminate existing taxes on income, property, and fuel. I'll order audits of every state office and program and cut waste. I'll end unaccountable public-private partnerships and legislative commissions. I'll prioritize starter homes with yards over high-density apartments forced on us by Olympia. I'll replace DEI with merit in government hiring, restore full parental rights in education, and uphold the biological definition of gender. Public safety and a classical American education come first. Kent, Auburn, Covington, and Maple Valley deserve better. I'll deliver it.

Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, State Representative Pos. 2. Accessed 2026-10-10. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
