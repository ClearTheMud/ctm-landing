Statement

Washington stands at a crossroads. Our families deserve safe neighborhoods where law enforcement is supported, schools that truly prepare our children for success, affordable housing, and an economy that rewards hard work. Yet today, a rapidly expanding state government has left us overtaxed and overburdened, with higher bills and programs that too often deliver no measurable results.

As your next state senator, I will get to work on Day One restoring fiscal integrity.  I will fight to reduce the heavy burden on families and businesses, treating every taxpayer dollar with the respect it deserves, because it's your money. Progress is possible through hard work across the aisle and by listening closely to the needs of our communities.  My record as an elected leader proves I deliver results by building relationships and getting things done.

Is Washington worth it? As a wife, mom of five, and proud Grammie, I say yes! Thirty-five years ago, I arrived here as a military spouse.  Our family chose to stay, put down roots, and build our life in this beautiful state, with no regrets.

I'm running because I believe in Washington's purpose for future generations.  With your vote, we can restore common sense leadership together.
