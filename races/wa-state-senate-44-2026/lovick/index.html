1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (General Election 2026)

Elected Experience

Current State Senator, Former Snohomish County Sheriff, State Representative, Acting Speaker of the House, Snohomish County Executive, Mill Creek City Council.

Other Professional Experience

Decorated Coast Guard Veteran; 31-year Washington State Patrol Trooper and Sergeant.

Education

AA, Criminal Justice; Graduate, Coast Guard Basic Training, Washington State Patrol Academy, National Sheriffs Institute.

Community Service

Youth sports coach, classroom volunteer, and mentor. Committed community advocate who’s raised thousands of dollars for local charities by donating his famous deep-fried turkeys. Award-winning public servant, including: Domestic Violence Service Award, NAACP President’s Award, Trooper of the Year, and the Washington State Crime Prevention Association Sheriff of the Year.

Statement

Senator John Lovick is running to continue providing the leadership we expect from our elected officials and the results our community deserves.

John served 41 years in uniform, including the United States Coast Guard, Washington State Patrol, and as Snohomish County Sheriff. He believes that we should not only be safe, we should feel safe. Safe in our homes, safe on our streets, and that our children should always be safe in their schools. John worked with the Criminal Justice Training Commission to open three new regional training academies for peace officers. These academies have graduated more than 700 law enforcement professionals who are now serving communities in Washington. John has worked to build a robust accountability system. From road safety legislation to making school sports safer, John is a leader who gets results.

A frequent transit user, John is committed to ensuring a complete expansion of light rail to Everett and beyond. Accessible transit options are essential as the cost of living continues to rise. John’s experience, knowledge, and respect make him the best leader to meet this unprecedented moment. 

Endorsed by Washington State Patrol Troopers Association, Washington State Labor Council, Planned Parenthood. John Lovick: Leadership with Results.
