Other Professional Experience

I grew up on a small farm in the shadow of Mt. St. Helens, and studied to become a freelance writer and photographer. As a photographer, I have seen through my lens and through personal experience how corporate influence has chipped away at the fabric of our communities here in Southwest Washington. I’ve observed in my interactions with the oversight boards of government agencies how this corporate influence has corrupted our institutions. We’ve got to evict big money interests from our government, so that it will once again represent the people and the place we call home.

Statement

Washington needs to invest in rural communities, and act proactively to create a robust, sustainable economy which provides us with good union jobs, but not at the expense of our health and the character of our vibrant landscapes.

I will work to create new parks for small towns, build affordable housing while preventing sprawl from paving over our fields and forests, invest in the production of sustainable alternative materials, improve access to healthcare, expand and improve public transportation, stop data centers, and ensure that our young people have everything they need to find success without leaving their home town.
