Statement

Water connects all of us—to our families, our communities, and the future we leave behind. I have spent twenty years protecting the water and environmental resources that sustain our quality of life, focusing on communities most vulnerable to environmental harm. As climate change and human activity put pressure on our water resources, we must modernize infrastructure with care, strengthen resilience, and protect these resources for future generations.

Affordability – Utility rates have risen over 25% in the past five years, outpacing inflation already in our lives. I will examine every opportunity to increase affordability and reduce the cost of service, including implementing solutions from the recent legislative report on utility affordability.

Accessible and accountable – The PUD has a responsibility to clearly explain its decisions, including how rates are structured and projects prioritized. I will work to enhance PUD accessibility and accountability and support broader efforts to protect our environment and water resources.

I am honored to have the support of our current PUD commissioners, local electeds, and community members who know the importance of this work. Together, we can protect our water, strengthen infrastructure, and keep the PUD accountable to the people it serves. I’d be grateful for your vote.
