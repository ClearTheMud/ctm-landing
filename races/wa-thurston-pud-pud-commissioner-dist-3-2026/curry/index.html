1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (General Election 2026)

Elected Experience

No information submitted

Other Professional Experience

My experience includes serving on the board for the Scott Lake community and its local water district.

Education

As a local small business owner with a two-year technology degree from SPSCC, I bring practical expertise to the PUD.

Community Service

My deep commitment to public service is reflected in decades of volunteer work, including supporting the Kiwanis Club, serving at the Union Gospel Mission in Seattle and Olympia, and delivering food for local food banks statewide. I am ready to bring this dedication and hands-on water management experience to Thurston County.

Statement

As your Thurston County PUD Commissioner, my top priority is ensuring our utility services remain fair, reliable, and affordable for every resident. I am committed to common-sense leadership that protects your hard-earned money while preparing our county for the future.

To achieve this, I will focus on three key initiatives:

Affordability First: Keeping rates reasonable and ensuring transparency in how your utility dollars are spent. Expanding Solar Energy: Adding practical solar resources to our local grid to increase energy independence, diversify our power supply, and improve long-term sustainability. Clean Water Technology: Developing a user-friendly water quality mobile app. This app will allow anyone in Thurston County to instantly check the water quality in their area right from their phone.

With a background in running a local small business and managing complex logistics, I know how to get things done efficiently. I ask for your vote to bring practical solutions, accountability, and modern innovation to our Public Utility District. Together, we can keep Thurston County strong, sustainable, and affordable.
