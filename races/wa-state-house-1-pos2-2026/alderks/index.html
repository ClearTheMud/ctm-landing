1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

Current Deputy Mayor and former Planning Commissioner for the City of Bothell. Elected in 2021, re-elected 2025. 



Other Professional Experience

Currently employed as a human services specialist leading trauma-informed systems change efforts in behavioral health programming. Formerly self-employed birth and postpartum doula for over a decade. 



Education

Masters of Public Affairs (2024), Public Leadership Credential from Harvard University's Kennedy School of Governance (2021), Masters of Early Childhood Education (2008), Bachelors of Science in Human Development (2005). 

Community Service

Current board member of North Urban Human Services Alliance (Vice-President) and Lake City Partners Ending Homelessness, former Emerge WA Cabinetmember, former LGBTQIA+ youth group leader.

Statement

Voters in the 1st deserve a representative who delivers on the issues that matter to our communities. I stand up for those who are struggling and deliver needed change. From strengthening public safety and transportation to supporting human rights, housing, parks, and local businesses, I have a proven record of progressive accomplishments that improve quality of life for everyone. In Olympia, I will continue fighting for affordability, stronger communities, individual freedoms, and real progress for Washingtonians. I’m honored to be endorsed by 1st LD, Snohomish and King County Democrats, neighbors, and local elected leaders across the district. More at votejenne.com.

Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, State Representative Pos. 2. Accessed 2026-07-16. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
