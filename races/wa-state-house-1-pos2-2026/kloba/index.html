Statement

It’s been an honor to represent you in Olympia since 2017.  I have fought to improve data privacy, reign in data brokers, and diminish the harms of AI on consumers' lives.  With your vote, I'll continue working to improve transportation infrastructure, fix our regressive tax code, create green jobs, cut costs for working families, fully fund education, increase access to behavioral health care, and protect civil rights. Together, we can create security, opportunity, and justice for all.

Endorsements: Snohomish County Democrats, Sierra Club, Washington State Labor Council, Washington Education Association PAC, Washington Alliance for Gun Responsibility, Planned Parenthood Alliance Advocates
