1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (General Election 2026)

Elected Experience

State Representative; Vice-Chair of Technology, Economic Development, and Veterans Committee; member of Consumer Protection and Business and Environment and Energy Committees; Ex-Officio member of WA State Gambling Commission; National Conference of State Legislatures Artificial Intelligence, Cybersecurity, and Data Privacy Task Force; founder of House Technology caucus. Previously: Problem Gambling Task Force, City of Kirkland Councilmember 2013-2016

Other Professional Experience

Licensed health care provider for over 20 years 

Education

B.S. Kinesiology, University of Illinois; Certificate of Energy Policy Planning, University of Idaho; Seattle Massage School

Community Service

Currently: Kirkland Kiwanis Club. Previously: Lake Washington Schools Foundation Trustee, Kirkland Park Board Member, WA State PTA Legislative Director

Statement

It’s been an honor to represent you in Olympia since 2017.  I have fought to improve data privacy, reign in data brokers, and diminish the harms of AI on consumers' lives.  With your vote, I'll continue working to improve transportation infrastructure, fix our regressive tax code, create green jobs, cut costs for working families, fully fund education, increase access to behavioral health care, and protect civil rights. Together, we can create security, opportunity, and justice for all.

Endorsements: Snohomish County Democrats, Sierra Club, Washington State Labor Council, Washington Education Association PAC, Washington Alliance for Gun Responsibility, Planned Parenthood Alliance Advocates
