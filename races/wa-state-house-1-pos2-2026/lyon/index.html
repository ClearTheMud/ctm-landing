1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience
Proudly unaffiliated with the political establishment. Ran for Woodinville City Council in 2025.

Other Professional Experience
Disabled U.S. Navy veteran, former Sheriff's Deputy, and cybersecurity engineering manager.

Education
Bachelor’s degree, The College of William and Mary (2005). Master’s degree, American Military University (2016).

Community Service
As a Woodinville City Council candidate and community organizer, I've fought corruption, unethical behavior, and overreach. I speak at city council meetings and march with small business owners. In 2025, I led a campaign to increase voter turnout to its highest in eight years. These experiences strengthened my commitment to working for Washingtonians and fighting a two-party system that fails residents.

Statement
I’m running to eliminate state taxes for most families earning under $125,000, permanently fund our public schools, and make higher education affordable for students entering high-paying fields in our state. Any new income tax must receive direct voter approval, completely eliminate regressive taxes, and guarantee full school funding. I will restore local control by eliminating regulations that drive up housing costs and that prevent cities from managing their own character and environmental protection. I will work across party lines for a government that serves all residents effectively without favoring partisan agendas or picking winners and losers.
