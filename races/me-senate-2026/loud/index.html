1 Background

Paige Ashley Loud is a 29-year-old social worker from Old Town, Maine, and a citizen of the Cherokee Nation. She holds a Bachelor of Social Work from the University of Maine (2024) and was completing a Master of Social Work at the University of Maine, expected May 2026. She describes herself as a first-generation college graduate. She grew up in a small Maine town in a family that relied on SNAP and Tribal healthcare, an experience she cites as the anchor of her platform. Her career has been front-line social work with working-class Maine families, and she identifies as a daughter, wife, and renter. (T2 media; T3 campaign biography.)

Her platform centers Medicare for All, food and shelter as fundamental rights, and healthcare, housing, and food affordability, framed through social-work ethics. (T3.)

2 Electoral Record

Finding 2.1: Single prior candidacy, fourth-place finish, small budget VIABILITY

What happened
Loud ran in the June 9, 2026 Democratic primary for Maine's open 2nd Congressional District seat, which opened when Jared Golden chose not to seek reelection. She finished fourth with about 10.4 percent, and the race went to a ranked-choice runoff among the leaders. For that House race she reported self-funding roughly $30,000 and raising about $17,000, a modest operation.
