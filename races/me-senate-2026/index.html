Race Dynamics: 2026 Maine US Senate

Democratic Nominating Convention Field and General Election Matchup

TLP:GREEN Date: 2026-07-14 Convention: July 25, 2026 (Bangor) General: November 3, 2026
Executive Summary
Graham Platner won the June 9, 2026 Democratic primary but withdrew on July 10, 2026 after a sexual-assault allegation, which he denies. His withdrawal before the July 13 statutory deadline lets the Maine Democratic Party name a replacement nominee, which it will do at a convention in Bangor on July 25, 2026. The Democratic nominee is not yet decided. Seven Democrats have declared and are running to face Republican incumbent Susan Collins, who chairs the Senate Appropriations Committee. The race is rated a toss-up and is central to control of the Senate.

1 The Democratic Nominating Convention

Because Platner withdrew before 5:00 p.m. on the second Monday in July, Maine law (Title 21-A, section 374-A) lets the party name a replacement general election nominee, who must be chosen by 5:00 p.m. on July 27. The Maine Democratic Party is making that choice at a convention.

  • Date and place: Saturday, July 25, 2026, 9:00 a.m., Cross Insurance Center, Bangor.
  • Delegates: 601 total (101 Democratic State Committee members plus 500 delegates apportioned across Maine's 16 counties).
  • Voting: successive rounds. The five candidates with the most first-round votes advance, and the lowest vote-getter is eliminated each round until one candidate holds a majority.
  • Qualifying: a declaration of intent by July 15, then at least 500 valid signatures from registered Democrats, including at least 50 from each of at least eight counties, by July 20.

3 The Incumbent

Susan Collins (R)

Record

  • Six-term senator, first elected 1996; chairs the Senate Appropriations Committee.
  • Announced re-election February 10, 2026; won the June 9 Republican primary.
  • Raised $12.17M with $9.67M cash on hand (FEC, through 5/20), the largest war chest in the race.

Documented Vulnerabilities

  • The 2018 Kavanaugh "settled law" vote against the 2022 Dobbs reversal.
  • STOCK Act late disclosure of a spouse stock purchase.
  • A record of high-profile dissents alongside support for most of the Trump agenda and most nominees.

4 Structural Factors

Ranked-Choice Voting

Maine uses ranked-choice voting in federal general elections. Any independent candidates who qualify for the ballot could affect the general election dynamics.

Race Rating

Sabato's Crystal Ball moved Maine from Leans Republican to Toss-up on October 14, 2025, and Cook Political Report also rates the race a toss-up. Collins is widely described as the most vulnerable Republican incumbent of the cycle. No head-to-head poll against a named replacement nominee had been published as of July 14, 2026, because the nominee is not yet chosen. Earlier polling of Collins against Platner had Collins trailing (Emerson March 2026: Platner 48, Collins 41; UMass Lowell June 2026: Platner 48, Collins 43).
