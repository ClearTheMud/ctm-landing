1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

None. This is my first time running for office, but not my first time fighting for working people. See my website for concrete policy solutions.

Other Professional Experience

Horticultural retail worker. Founder of the Immigration Defence Coalition (Tacoma). Founder and Director of Resist Overreach, a non-profit dedicated to protecting vulnerable working-class communities from violent federal action.


Education

University of Washington Tacoma, School of Urban Studies. (B.A. Urban Engagement, minor in Politics).

Community Service

Collaborating with the Olympia City Council to draft and implement viable, data-backed legislative solutions to neutralize ICE presence and protect marginalized communities from federal overreach.

Statement

My opponent is a commercial landlord funded by the massive corporations responsible for the cost-of-living crisis. She takes maximum PAC contributions from corporate landlords, private equity, pharmaceutical giants like Genentech, health monopolies like Regence and UnitedHealth, and PSE. She proudly endorses pro-ICE and pro-Israel representatives Marie Gluesenkamp Perez and Marilyn Strickland.

I'm a retail worker and renter running a volunteer-led campaign. I have concrete, data-backed plans to freeze rent, deliver universal healthcare and childcare, and lower utility costs without taxing working people. I am the candidate fighting the oligarchy, not doing their bidding. I will fight to make Washington livable.
