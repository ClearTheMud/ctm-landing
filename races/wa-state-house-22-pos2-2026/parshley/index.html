Statement

When I first ran for office in 2017, I felt called to step up to help my community in face of what was happening nationally. Ten years later, it’s an even deeper problem. Affordability, workers’ rights, houselessness, equality, voting rights, and the environment. These are the battles before us, and in this Washington, we won’t go down without a fight, and we leave no neighbor behind. I will keep taking on these issues to help make life better for all Washingtonians.

Proudly Endorsed by: Senator Jessica Bateman, Representative Beth Doglio, and the Washington State Labor Council, and more.
