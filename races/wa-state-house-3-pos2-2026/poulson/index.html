1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

District Leader. PCO. HeadStart advocate working with families and policymakers to strengthen early childhood education. Worked to bring people together, listen, and advocate for practical solutions serving families and communities.

Other Professional Experience

Launched private schools' curriculum development and systems coordination. Firsthand understanding of challenges facing families, and educators across Washington.

Education

Elementary and Special Education BA from Gonzaga, Mary Stuart Rogers Scholarship recipient. MA and Professional Certification from Whitworth. Foursquare Ministry certificate.


Community Service

Serve Spokane outreach ministry, meeting practical community needs. Worked and volunteered with Spokane Therapeutic Recreation. Mission work in Africa. Service projects with Habitat for Humanity, Maddie’s Place, World Relief, and SpoFi.

Statement

Growing up in a blue-collar home; hard work, fairness, and looking out for neighbors mattered. I’ve spent nearly two decades as a special education teacher in Spokane, standing up for students and families who needed a voice.

Across Washington, we’re working hard but Olympia leaves us unheard, rejected, and made to feel like we don’t matter. The people are all that matter! Our communities face addiction, homelessness, safety concerns, and exorbitantly high housing costs. We need action, treatment and recovery, safer streets, more housing, cost of living relief, and leaders willing to work together. I will be that leader!

Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, State Representative Pos. 2. Accessed 2026-07-16. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
