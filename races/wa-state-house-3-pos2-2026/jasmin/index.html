1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

Chair, Police Ombudsman Commission; Chair, Chief Garry Park Neighborhood Council; Elected 3rd Vice President, NAACP Spokane Chapter.

Other Professional Experience

Eastern Washington Regional Representative for Governors Jay Inslee and Bob Ferguson, serving ten counties. Co-founded The Jasmin Group, Parkview Early Learning Center, Northeast Youth & Family Services, and Washington Childcare Centers Association. Tri-Chair, Washington State Chamber of Commerce Child Care Task Force.

Education

B.A. Elementary Education, Eastern Nazarene College. Currently pursuing MBA in Organizational Leadership, Eastern Washington University.

Community Service

Habitat for Humanity Spokane Board Member; Family Promise of Spokane Board Member; Spokane Regional Land Bank Board; EWU Career and Technical Education Advisory Council.

Statement

Costs are rising. Wages are stagnant. Cuts to healthcare and education are happening alongside attacks on our civil rights. I plan to bring my lived experience as a first-generation Haitian American, father of two daughters, and childcare provider, along with my professional experience helping community navigate state government, especially those often left behind, to push for policy in Olympia that prioritizes all of us here in Spokane.

Endorsed: State Labor Council, Washington Education Association, Washington Conservation Action, Governor Ferguson, Former Governor Inslee,  Former Senate Majority Leader Andy Billig, Representative Natasha Hill, Spokane Council President Betsy Wilkerson, Council member Kitty Klitzke.
