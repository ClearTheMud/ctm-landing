1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

3rd LD Democratic Delegation Chair, not a career politician



Other Professional Experience

Licensed physician and attorney, Spokane Medical Reserve Corps volunteer physician, and former Washington Medical Commission Policy Manager and Staff Attorney and EWU Public Health Lecturer

Education

M.D. (University of Nebraska Medical Center and medical class and student body President), Emergency Medicine Residency (University of Illinois-Chicago), J.D. (Gonzaga University), B.S. Biology, minors Chemistry and Political Science (University of Nebraska)

Community Service

Teach free suicide prevention courses across Spokane. Feed homeless at Shalom Ministries. Board member Partners with Families and Children, Frontier Behavioral Health, and NAMI-Spokane. Former SRHD Ethics committee, Board President of St. George's School.

Statement

Our legislature needs a doctor in the House. Our healthcare system deserves someone who understands it from the inside as an experienced physician, to the outside as a frustrated patient. I am committed to making it better for our state. As an ER doctor, lawyer, health policy educator, and LGBTQIA+ advocate mom, I’m uniquely qualified to serve. I will fight to expand healthcare and mental health access, improve housing affordability, protect Medicare and Medicaid, strengthen our economy so everyone can earn a living wage, stabilize the homeless crisis, address substance use disorder, protect individual liberties, and serve all people.
