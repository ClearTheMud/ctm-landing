Statement
My sons and six grandchildren are here in Vancouver. This community is my community. When I say I want Clark County’s courts to work, I mean it the way any neighbor does.

The County Clerk manages every court record in this county - criminal cases, divorces, adoptions, guardianships, and mental health proceedings. Families depend on the Clerk’s office for clean, accurate records and efficient administration. It takes someone who actually knows courts.

I have spent 25 years as an attorney - as a prosecutor and as a defense attorney, in cases argued at the highest stakes, including a capital murder case. I know what the courts need to function well, and what it costs families when they don’t.

This year, the right of Clark County voters to elect their Clerk was questioned. That right matters. I am running to make sure the office is run so well, by someone so clearly qualified, that no one feels the need to question it again. Courts work when the Clerk works. I am asking for your vote.
