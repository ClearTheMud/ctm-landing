1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Other Professional Experience

Statement
I am running for the nonpartisan position of Clark County Clerk because the public deserves an office that works with accuracy, efficiency, transparency, and respect.

As a PCO, I have listened to residents, answered questions, and helped people better understand local government. That experience has strengthened my belief that public offices should be accessible, responsive, and easy to navigate. As Clark County Clerk, I will focus on accurate record keeping, timely service, improved access to information, and a more user-friendly office.

Public trust requires accountability. I support greater transparency in government budgeting and spending, including easier online access to financial information so taxpayers can see how their money is used. I will serve with fairness, professionalism, accountability, and respect for every resident who interacts with the Clerk’s Office. I will also hold monthly open office hours on the first Wednesday at noon, where residents can bring a bag lunch, ask questions, share concerns, and have real conversations about how local government can better serve our community.

I respectfully ask for your vote to serve as Clark County Clerk so we can build a more responsive, accessible, and trustworthy office for our community.
