Other Professional Experience

Small Business Owner for 20 years; SEIU Board Delegate; Non-Profit Executive Director; Art Director Seattle Symphony; Urgent Care Staff

Community Service

LD48 Precinct Committee Officer; American Legion Auxiliary; Recording Academy Federal Advocacy Committee; NAMI Youth Mentor; League of Women Voters King County Treasurer; Seattle Symphony Pride Co-Founder; Special Olympics

Statement

Housing and healthcare costs are climbing faster than wages; education is underfunded; the climate crisis is here; working families are struggling with increasing prices. I’ve balanced billion-dollar municipal budgets, won tenant protections, secured $7.5M for housing and human services, improved financial transparency, championed small business pandemic support, and fought for LGBTQIA+ and immigrant communities. I'll take my inclusive and pragmatic approach that led to these wins to Olympia to fight for all families.

Endorsements: 48th District Democrats; King County Democrats; Washington State Labor Council; Insurance Commissioner Kuderer; Senator Slatter; Planned Parenthood Alliance Advocates; Redmond Firefighters; 60+ elected officials. More: voteforsythe.com.
