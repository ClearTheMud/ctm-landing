Community Service

I have always worked to make life better for people in my community, serving in volunteer and civic roles with organizations like Food Lifeline, Youth Eastside Services, PorchLight and Studio East, and securing scholarships for students to attend Lake Washington Institute of Technology. As a cancer survivor, I make it a priority to support services for families impacted by cancer.



Statement

I’m a pragmatic governing Democrat who works across the aisle. I champion public safety, affordability, balanced budgets and fiscal responsibility.

I stood up to the federal government by banning ICE from wearing masks and backfilling money cut from Planned Parenthood. As one of few small business owners in Olympia, I have focused on creating and protecting family wage jobs, particularly for small business and tech workers, which is why I voted no on the income tax.  Endorsements: Bellevue Police Guild, Alliance for Gun Responsibility, Governor Gregoire, Congressman Smith, Councilmember Claudia Balducci
