1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

I served 12 years on the North Beach School District Board of Directors. I was president for 9 of those years. Ocean City Drainage District Commissioner (chair).



Other Professional Experience

I am a local business owner. I am part of the North Beach School District administrative team and the current Maintenance and Facilities Supervisor.

Education

Graduated from North Beach High (Moclips). I have completed Building Operator Certification. Completed many leadership training courses.

Community Service

Volunteered as a youth coach, worked on WDFW projects. 27 years as a committee member of Rocky Mountain Elk Foundation Twin Harbors Chapter, serving as chair for the last 16 years.

Statement

Putting Grays Harbor First. I have filed as a Nonpartisan candidate so I can put the needs of the citizens of Grays Harbor before anything else. I have the experience necessary to be an effective County Commissioner. My years as Board Chair  have set me up to start working for the residents of Grays Harbor County my first day in office. Grays Harbor County is a special place and has provided me and my family many life memories. I want to see the county grow and continue to provide life memories for all who live and work here. If elected I will put the needs of the county first. I will lead by example using open communication, being flexible and  use integrity as a basis for all of my decisions. The three areas I feel are important for success are fiscal responsibility, providing family wage jobs, and affordable housing. Health care is another area that will require partnerships between the county, the local community and business's to keep us safe and able to provide for ourselves. I would be honored and  humbled to be your next Grays Harbor County Commissioner representing district #3. Thank you for reading this and voting.
