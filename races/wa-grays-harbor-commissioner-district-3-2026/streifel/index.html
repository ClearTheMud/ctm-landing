Finding 5.1: Two Prior Election Losses — Serial Candidate Pattern HIGH

What happened
Streifel has lost two consecutive races — Commissioner D3 in 2022 (22.17%, third place) and State House LD24 in 2024 (23.12%, third place). Both times he finished third and did not advance past the primary. His appointment to the very position he lost in 2022 raises questions about persistence vs. electoral appeal. Voters explicitly rejected him in favor of Raines and Zaborac in 2022.
Source tier
T1
Political impact
High
Defense
Streifel's appointment demonstrates that the current commissioners (who interviewed all 5 applicants) found him the most qualified for the job. His prior losses were to a strong incumbent (Raines, who served 11 years) and in a crowded primary. The appointment gives him an opportunity to demonstrate competence before voters decide.

Finding 5.2: PDC Enforcement Warning — Late Disclosure Filings MODERATE

What happened
In 2022, PDC enforcement Case #111040 was filed against Streifel for failing to timely disclose contributions and expenditures on C-3 and C-4 reports, violating RCW 42.17A.235 and .240. The case was closed with a written warning. For a candidate running on public accountability, a campaign finance disclosure violation is an optics issue.

Finding 5.3: Appointed by the Board He'll Serve On — Insider Selection MODERATE

What happened
Streifel was appointed to the commissioner seat by the same two commissioners he will serve alongside (Georgia Miller, Rick Hole). This creates a perception of insider selection rather than voter mandate. The other two commissioners chose their own colleague from a pool of 5 applicants.
