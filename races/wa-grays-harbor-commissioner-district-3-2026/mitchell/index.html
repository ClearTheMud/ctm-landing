Statement

I believe County forest lands should offer free and easy public access for all to enjoy wholesome outdoor recreation. Visitors can hunt, hike, bike, pick wild berries, and many other activities while enjoying the stunning natural landscapes, fostering healthier lifestyles and stronger community connections. These accessible forests provide low-cost opportunities for families, sportsmen, and nature enthusiasts alike. 

Responsible land management ensures sustainable use while prioritizing conservation. Through careful stewardship, counties protect wildlife habitats, maintain water quality, preserve biodiversity, and prevent overuse. Public access paired with thoughtful management balances recreation with long-term ecological health, securing these vital green spaces for current and future generations to cherish.

Commissioners, department heads, and staff must be held fully responsible and accountable for the responsible use of taxpayers and grant funds on all projects. Clear priorities should be established with firm timelines to ensure projects are completed efficiently, on budget, and deliver maximum value to citizens.

We must prioritize affordable housing, job creation, and public safety to build a stronger, healthier community. By tackling drug abuse and homelessness through coordinated enforcement, treatment, and prevention programs, we can reduce crime, restore hope, and create opportunities for all residents to thrive.
