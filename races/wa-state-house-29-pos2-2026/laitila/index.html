1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

None.

Other Professional Experience

Natasha worked as a Contract Manager at the WA Department of Commerce and a Legislative Assistant to Senator Bob Hasegawa and Representative Jamila Taylor. She served on the Executive Board of WFSE Local 443 and at the WA Office of Public Defense.

Education

Natasha attended Sheridan Elementary School, Stewart Middle School, Henry Foss High School, and PLU. She earned an MPA from the UW Evans School.

Community Service

Natasha founded Voices for Choice at PLU, served as a Peer Health Educator with Planned Parenthood Teen Council, was an AmeriCorps Member at Pierce County United Way, and was in Pierce County Young Democrats.

Statement

I'm a working-class daughter of Tacoma, raised by a union father and a caregiver mother. I watched my family fight to get by, and I carried that fight into state government, delivering for working families in Tacoma, Parkland, Spanaway, Midland, and Frederickson. I know this community because I come from it.

To the neighbors who raised me: thank you. I'm running to stand with immigrant families, protect workers' rights, fully fund our schools, and build housing people can actually afford. I'm not running to be another voice in Olympia. I'm running to carry yours. Your voice. Your district. Your fight.
