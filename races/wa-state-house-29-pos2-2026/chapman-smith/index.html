1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

This is my first time running for office however I’ve spent years on the ground level of our housing and homelessness crisis. I believe my expertise on these issues will move the ball forward on making real progress for those who desperately need our help

Other Professional Experience

Executive Director, ROOTS Young Adult Shelter; Senior Director of Housing Services, YouthCare; Housing Specialist, Associated Ministries; Program Coordinator, ROOTS Young Adult Shelter; Lead Case Manager, Friends of Youth.

Education

Bachelors of Science from Central Washington University

Community Service

ROOTS Young Adult Shelter Board of Directors, Policy Advisory Committee National Network for Youth, Washington Coalition for Homeless Youth Advocacy.

Statement

I’m running for office because after 20 years working in housing and homelessness services, I know we need leaders who understand these crises and have solutions. I’ll fight to build more housing, invest in social housing and services, and work toward a universal healthcare system that’ll lower costs for all. As a parent, I’ll always fight to fully fund public education. 

As a proud member of Washington’s LGBTQ community, I’ll defend our right to live openly, safely, and with dignity. I’m honored to be endorsed by Councilwoman Walker, State Senator Orwall, State Representative Macri, College Democrats of Washington and more.
