1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

Current Deputy Mayor of Tacoma; Tacoma City Council. 

Other Professional Experience

South Tacoma Neighborhood Council Chair; Tacoma Public Utilities Board Member; Staff Sergeant, United States Marine Corps combat veteran. 

Education

Stadium High School, Tacoma Community College, and The Evergreen State College (B.A.) 

Community Service

As the child of a refugee, Joe is the first Cambodian-American Councilmember in our State’s history. He’s served our local community as a substitute teacher in Tacoma Public Schools, as well as Chair of the South Tacoma Neighborhood Council. Joe advocated for small businesses and workers during COVID, and volunteers regularly with Toys for Tots, planting trees and neighborhood trash cleanups. 

Statement

Washington families are under attack by the Trump Administration. Prices are rising for gas, groceries, utilities, and especially healthcare where many are losing coverage. Immigrants across the country are living in fear. We need to fight back.  As Deputy Mayor, I’m leading the work to raise Tacoma’s minimum wage. As your State Representative, I’ll introduce anti-corruption, anti-price-gouging legislation, and I’ll champion Universal Healthcare for all. 

 

Endorsements: Democratic former State Legislators Rosa Franklin, Connie Ladenburg, and Steve Kirby, Representative Jake Fey (D), County Councilmember Rosie Ayala (D), Tacoma Mayor Anders Ibsen, Labor Unions, and more. I humbly ask for your vote.
