Community Service

Chair, Washington State Lawyer Chapter, American Constitution Society (national legal reform organization). Legal volunteer on voting rights and consumer product safety issues. Civil legal aid intern and intern for the Thurston County Prosecuting Attorney’s Office, Civil Division. Member, Higher Education Affordability Workgroup, Washington Student Achievement Council. Endorsed by the Washington State Labor Council, WFSE, and AFSCME Local 120. 

Statement

Washingtonians can’t afford to buy groceries or stay in their homes, and are being forced to reconsider retirement. I see it in my family and hear it from our neighbors. I know how our government works, and how it must do better. I’ll use my experience to tackle the issues facing our families, prioritizing solutions for rising costs, housing, workers, small businesses, and federal overreach. Together, our community can be a place where we all thrive.

Endorsed by members of the Pierce County & Tacoma City Councils, our local Democratic parties, and labor. I’d be honored to earn your vote!
