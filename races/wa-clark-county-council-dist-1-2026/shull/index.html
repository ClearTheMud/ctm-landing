Statement
I love Clark County, and I’m deeply concerned about the rising costs facing working families, seniors, and people living paycheck to paycheck. Too many good people are being pushed out of their homes and neighborhoods because the government keeps making life more expensive.  Clark County deserves leadership that prioritizes real affordability—not just political slogans.

I was born and raised in Clark County and graduated from Hudson’s Bay High School. I’ve built successful local businesses, creating jobs, supporting nonprofits, and I remain heavily invested in our community. My experience solving complex problems and producing real-world results is exactly the kind of practical leadership our County needs right now.

I support a “tax-last” approach focusing on responsible budgeting before asking taxpayers to pay more. We need strong public safety, practical transportation solutions, balanced growth policies, and compassionate but accountable approaches to homelessness and addiction. I also support policies that encourage small business growth, protect local jobs, and promote affordable, reliable renewable energy solutions.

Clark County can remain a thriving, beautiful place to live, work, and raise a family, but we must lead differently. I'd be honored to earn your trust, your support, and your vote. Learn more at www.electbyranshull.com
