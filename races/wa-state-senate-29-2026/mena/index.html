1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

State Representative, 29th Legislative District (2023–Present); Committee Chair, State Government & Tribal Relations; Vice Chair, Latino Democratic Caucus; Precinct Committee Officer.

Other Professional Experience

Special Assistant at the Washington Department of Ecology (clean air/water, intergovernmental relations). Former roles in the U.S. Congress, Washington State Senate, and Governor's Office. Broad policy experience supporting working families.

Education

B.A., Communications & Philosophy, Washington State University.

Community Service

Volunteer Coach with Girls on the Run. Legislative Designee for Governor's Family Separation Task Force. Board Member for Win With Women. Former Board Member for the Elections Administration & Certification Board.

Statement

I am the daughter of immigrants who worked tirelessly to give my brothers and me a strong start in life. But today, high prices make it impossible for families to afford the strong foundation they deserve. This isn’t right. This isn’t a world I’m willing to accept. You shouldn’t have to either. 

I’m running for State Senate because our community should be a place where families can build a promising future — just as mine did. This means great schools; affordable, quality healthcare and childcare; good paying jobs that provide enough to feed our families; a clean environment; and a democracy that includes all of us. 

When you first elected me, I promised to deliver for you. So far, we’ve won on the Millionaire’s Tax, a statewide low-income utility bill assistance program, rent stabilization, our strong Washington Voting Rights Act, combating federal overreach, and much more. 

As your State Senator, I’ll continue fighting to deliver the fair shot you deserve. I’m proud to be endorsed by Attorney General Nick Brown, Executive Ryan Mello, Mayor Anders Ibsen, County Councilmember Jani Hitchen, the 29th District Democrats, WFSE, and many more. I would be proud to earn your vote.
