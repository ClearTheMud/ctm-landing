Statement

My years of working as a small business owner gives me a unique understanding of what it's like living in the 8th most expensive state and what needs to change; budget management is my primary focus. 

Fundamentally, Olympia has a spending issue. Every year our revenue is increasing 7.5%, while our spending is set to increase 10%. Although the millionaire's tax will increase revenue, if we don't focus on returning to a fiscally responsible budget to gain a surplus and return to our rainy-day fund, the cost of living is going to continue to increase causing a ripple effect of issues. Already, this has led to people searching for a more livable state ultimately decreasing revenue. 

We also need to re-focus on where this spending is going. Focusing on rehabilitating those with addiction and mental illness will ultimately decrease spending in other areas and bring about safer and cleaner streets. For mental illness calls, social workers not police should handle them, allowing police to focus on criminal activity. We need to bring back common-sense policies that allow police to focus on theft; from retail, to break ins. We need to bring responsibility back to Washington.
