Other Professional Experience

Jaelynn is a nationally recognized leader in non-profit leadership and progressive advocacy. She currently serves as the Executive Director of Lavender Rights Project. She has a deep range of past working experience, as both a union member and an ordained family minister, and is a consultant with expertise in mindfulness-based organizational management.

Education

Master of Divinity (MDiv)

Community Service

Jaelynn is a community organizer in housing, criminal, racial, and gender justice movements. She has served as Board Treasurer and Member-at-large, and has volunteered with organizations across the country.

Statement

Our district is one of the most vibrant and multicultural communities in Washington, and it deserves a representative who will stand up for everyone who calls it home. I have delivered tangible results, redirecting $16 million from jails into supportive housing and helping to establish a nationally recognized, community-based public safety model. 

In Olympia, I will fight for compassionate public-safety, a fairer tax code, universal healthcare, lower costs for working families, and expand affordable housing. I am proud to have endorsements from Pramila Jayapal, Bernie Sanders, labor, and elected officials who fight hard for working people.
