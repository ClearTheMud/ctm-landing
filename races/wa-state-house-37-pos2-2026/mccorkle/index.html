Statement

I’m running for State Representative because I believe District 37 deserves leadership that listens, understands struggle, and fights for real solutions. As a student and lifelong member of this community, I’ve seen families pushed aside by rising costs, housing instability, and barriers in education. I’m not backed by powerful insiders or political machines  I’m running to give everyday people a stronger voice in government. My campaign focuses on education, affordable housing, community safety, and restoring trust between people and leadership. Together, we can build a future where every person in our district feels heard, respected, and represented.
