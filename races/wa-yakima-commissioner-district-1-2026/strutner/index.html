Finding 6.1: Ecology determined he is a potentially liable person for the Caton Landfill site MODERATE

What happened
On August 22, 2025 the Washington Department of Ecology issued Strutner a Final Determination of Liability for the Caton Limited Purpose Landfill near Naches (Cleanup Site ID 11536), a site Ecology listed as awaiting cleanup as of October 5, 2026. Ecology wrote that credible evidence exists that he is liable as an owner or operator for the release or threatened release of hazardous substances at the site, and that it therefore considers him a potentially liable person under the state Model Toxics Control Act. The letter states as its basis Ecology's records that he willingly participated in operating the landfill between July 1, 2023 and April 11, 2025, a period when the landfill did not hold a solid waste permit, and a signed declaration to the Yakima Health District hearing officer dated August 7, 2023 which, as Ecology quotes it, says he is responsible for all aspects of facility operations. Ecology issued separate determinations to other parties on the same date. A potentially liable person determination is an agency finding on credible evidence that begins the cleanup process. It is not a court judgment, a fine, or a finding of a crime. The letter states that liability under the Act is strict, joint and several. Ecology's site page states that in April 2026 Ecology negotiated a remedial investigation agreed order with Caton Landfill and Recycling, LLC; the agreed order text (No. DE 24633) is with the company and does not name Strutner as a signing party.

Defense
Strutner responded to Ecology in a letter dated July 10, 2025. Ecology describes it as implying that the landfill produces no hazardous emissions, and wrote that the laboratory report attached to his letter supports its prior determination. His letter was not reviewed; this is as described by Ecology. He told the Yakima Herald-Republic in 2026 that he came aboard officially as the landfill operator after fires at the site in 2022, as paraphrased by the paper.
