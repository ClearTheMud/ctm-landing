1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (General Election 2026)

Elected Experience
Elected Yakima County Sheriff for two terms, first term 2019.

Other Professional Experience
36 years law enforcement. Chair of Yakima Valley Local Crime Lab executive committee, chair of operations committee for YACORPS, Yakima County technology collaborative board.  Past fire chief Naches Heights Fire Department, former owner of Udell Apples, a family fruit ranch.  EMT for local ambulance company. 

Education
Bachelor of Science from Washington State University.  Law enforcement sponsored leadership training over past dozen years.

Community Service
Volunteer firefighter, coach of youth sports teams.  Former member Kiwanis, current member Rotary.  On the Salvation Army board, chair of the Safe Yakima Valley, formerly on Comprehensive Healthcare board.

Statement
Bob Udell is a local leader who has spent 36 years in law enforcement, eight of them as sheriff.  Now he wants to be County Commissioner.  Bob knows how government works.  He's led one of its largest department for nearly a decade.  He is the candidate with the most relevant experience.

Bob has spent decades working with others to ensure taxpayer dollars are spent wisely.  He has earned the trust of local law enforcement officials, business leaders, and community members alike. His commitment to fiscal responsibility and transparent leadership makes him the right choice for County Commissioner.

Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, County Commissioner; District 1. Accessed 2026-10-10. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.

Initiative 1639 (2019 statement as sheriff)In January 2019, as newly elected sheriff, he said he believed Initiative 1639 was unconstitutional and that his office would provide the enhanced background checks if the measure was not struck down but would not spend manpower enforcing its other provisions, noting that sheriffs have discretion in what laws to enforce.
