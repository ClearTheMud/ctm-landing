Statement
Rich Byrd is running for Pacific County Sheriff. He is committed to providing our citizens with experienced, professional leadership necessary to ensure public safety and accountability. A lifelong resident of Pacific County, Rich was born and raised in South Bend and dedicated over 20 years to the Pacific County Sheriff’s Office. He served as a Corrections Officer, Deputy Sheriff, Sergeant, narcotics investigator, and Lieutenant. Rich’s law enforcement career provided extensive experience in patrol operations, narcotics enforcement, and search and rescue. Following his retirement, he successfully transitioned to the private sector, managing large-scale operations with similar multi-million-dollar budgets and teams of over 25 employees. This combination brings valuable fiscal management and administrative oversight to the Office of Sheriff. Rich believes every community—from the Long Beach Peninsula and Naselle to the Willapa Valley, North Cove, and Tokeland—deserves dependable law enforcement and responsive leadership. As Sheriff, he will prioritize deputy safety, professionalism,  training, fiscal responsibility, and restoring strong community relationships. Rich has assembled a veteran leadership team, with over 70 years of experience, committed to professionalism, integrity, and effective public service from day one. He asks for your vote to lead the Pacific County Sheriff’s Office into a safe accountable future.

Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, County Sheriff. Accessed 2026-07-16. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
