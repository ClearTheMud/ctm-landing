Statement

I have a vision for Pacific County Sheriff’s Office that’s practical and achievable. Building strong relationships with local police departments, County Commissioners, the media, and all stakeholders is critical to the success and growth of PCSO. For more than twenty years I have built these relationships and will continue collaborating, putting an end to the division and contention that has affected PCSO in recent years. Life-saving public safety and community trust will be reestablished through implementing a simplified patrol and investigations work schedule. By restructuring into two squads of nine, we will now provide 24-hour policing coverage, double supervision from two sergeants to four, and improve county-wide response capabilities. Putting more boots on the ground, especially in higher crime areas, will create response times faster than Pacific County has ever seen! Restoring the Drug Task Force through grants and legislative funding is another top priority. Drugs are impacting our communities more than ever, and PCSO must lead the fight against this crisis. Additionally, I plan to provide a PCSO presence on the five school campuses we serve to strengthen community outreach and relationships at no cost to schools. I greatly appreciate your support and your vote. Thank you.
