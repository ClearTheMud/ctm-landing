1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience
No information submitted
Other Professional Experience
I served four years in the Marine Corps as an Infantry Officer (Desert Storm) and two more years in the Reserves where I reached the rank of Captain.  Afterwards, I joined the Los Angeles Police Department where I served for 30 years. 

Education
BA Sociology

Community Service
My expertise was patrol and community relations. As a Sergeant I had three City Council districts in my area, and I was responsible for interacting with and resolving issues brought to me by the City Council members, the citizens and my chain of command.  My entire job focused on quality-of-life issues within my community.

Statement
I see a need for a strong and experienced leader in the Pacific County Sheriff’s Department.  My background and experience make me the best candidate for the job as I have dedicated my adult life to public service in both the Marine Corps and the LAPD. I look at law enforcement as a partnership with the community. I want to take the Pacific County Sheriff's Department to the next level both in terms of response time and quality of service. I have many connections in law enforcement, and I will lean on them to assist us with training and best practices to improve our quality of service. As your Sheriff I will bring all of my experience, ideas, and resources to the Pacific County Sheriff's Department and community to make it the best Department in the State.

Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, County Sheriff. Accessed 2026-07-16. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
