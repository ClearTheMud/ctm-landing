Other Professional Experience

Perasso, 49, is a bakery worker in Seattle and is a member of BCTGM Union Local 9. He began organizing protests against police brutality and for immigrant rights in San Jose, California thirty years ago. He traveled to Cuba, helping convince him of the capacity of working people to reorganize society based on solidarity, not profit. 


Education

No information submitted

Community Service

He has been politically active defending the interests of working people over the past three decades, joining the Socialist Workers Party in 2000. He has worked as a meat cutter, a freight railroad conductor, and other industrial jobs, always active in labor solidarity.



Statement

The US rulers’ war against Iran, and other war moves, threaten workers and the oppressed the world over. The crisis of the capitalist system is intensifying conflicts between ruling classes around the globe, bringing nuclear world war closer. Only the working class taking power can stop this! US Out of the Middle East! Washington targets Cuba, which is no threat to working people, because they made a socialist revolution. US Hands off Cuba!

A tiny class of billionaire families rule through their Democratic and Republican parties. Working people need to break from both to defend against rising prices, layoffs, job speedup, and other assaults. A revolutionary political party is necessary to lead millions to fight for workers’ power, to build a society based on human solidarity.

Fight for a federally-funded public works program to put millions to work at union wages building and repairing infrastructure, schools, parks, housing and hospitals; for a shorter workweek with no loss in pay to prevent layoffs; for workers control of production to guarantee safety on the job and protect the environment!

Fight rising Jew-hating attacks worldwide and defend Israel’s right to exist as a refuge for Jews! Unite working people in the Middle East of all nationalities to defend common class interests!

Oppose all attacks on constitutional freedom of speech and worship; freedom of the press, association, and assembly; due process; and other liberties needed and used by the toilers! Oppose all violence against people for their political views!

Support methods of struggle that build the solidarity, confidence, and independent fighting capacity of the working class!  Build support for labor struggles! Fight for higher wages and work schedules that allow family time and recreation! Amnesty for immigrants—unify the working class! Defend Ukraine’s sovereignty! US troops and nuclear weapons out of Europe!
