Statement

People are suffering. Inflation and rent rising faster than income. Choosing between groceries, gas, medicine, childcare, and bills. Watching Congress find endless money for never ending wars, ICE terror, corporate subsidies, and billionaire tax breaks while families, veterans, elders, immigrants, students, and workers are told to… wait. I am running for Congress because waiting is a trap. We need courage. Competence. And a Representative who answers to We the People.

I am the only candidate in this race who has already taken on the weaponized federal government and won. When ICE illegally abducted my husband Zahid, an Honorable disabled American Military veteran, I fought back in federal courts without big law firm or even a single attorney. We won. That’s how I learned: when you stand up to bullies, they fold.

That case is proof of what I bring: courage under pressure, discipline under fire, and refusal to let powerful institutions violate people’s rights.

My three pillars: Care: housing, education, healthcare, childcare, eldercare, disability support, veterans’ care, consumer rights, and wages people can thrive on. Courage: corruption has consequences, powerful predators are prosecuted, and our money funds our communities — not endless wars for private profit. The Constitution: due process, voting rights, equal protection, freedom of speech, and Liberty and Justice for All — with no asterisks attached.

I've spent twenty years around one question: what does it actually take to ensure all people can live lives of dignity, safety, and opportunity? I am prepared for the future in ways the incumbent cannot be. Climate disruption, AI, unaffordable housing, broken institutions, and rebuilding trust from the ground up — I was made for this moment.

Unlike the incumbent, I take no money from corporate PACs, weapons industry, killer robots, or foreign interests. I’m ready to serve. Vote Melissa for Congress.
