Statement

Most of the time, working people have no political representation under this system. The billionaires call the shots while working people face endless wars and a severe cost-of-living crisis.


In stark contrast, for a decade in Seattle, independent socialist Kshama Sawant fought back against the political establishment and won historic victories. 


Kshama led the movement that won the highest minimum wage in any major U.S. city (now $21.30/hour). Kshama defeated billionaires like Jeff Bezos to win the Amazon Tax. She won unprecedented renters’ rights laws like the cap on late fees and publicly funded eviction defense. She won Indigenous Peoples’ Day and the nation’s strongest resolution against the genocide in Gaza. 


Now Kshama is running against 29-year incumbent Democrat Adam Smith. Smith has voted for military funding for Israel during the genocide in Gaza and to block UN food aid to Gaza, to create and fund ICE, to block the railroad workers’ strike, and for massive cuts to Medicare and Medicaid. Smith’s donors include AIPAC, the weapons industry, and billionaires who profit from AI and from ICE. 


Kshama is the only candidate in this race fighting for free healthcare for all funded by taxing the rich, an end to all military aid to Israel, to shut down ICE and the detention centers, to stop the data centers and AI layoffs, and for national rent control. She’s the only candidate with a proven track record of fighting and winning for working people.


If elected, Kshama will only accept the average worker’s wage as she did for a decade, and donate her remaining salary to workers’ movements. She’s running as an independent socialist because we need a new party for working people. Kshama is the principled fighter we need to take on Trump and both political parties of the billionaires.

Finding 7.1: Settled Seattle Ethics Complaint Over Use of Public Resources for Tax Amazon Campaign MODERATE

What happened
In 2020 Sawant settled a complaint with the Seattle Ethics and Elections Commission over using City Council staff and resources to support the "Tax Amazon" ballot initiative, a political campaign. Under the settlement she paid the city $3,516. Two earlier ethics complaints, in 2015 and 2018, involving city resources used for a town hall and a rally were dismissed.
Source tier
T2
Political impact
Moderate
Defense
Sawant characterized her council office as an organizing base for movement politics and resolved the 2020 matter through a settlement and repayment rather than a contested finding. The two prior complaints were dismissed.

Finding 7.2: Faced a Certified Voter Recall, Retained Seat by 310 Votes MODERATE

What happened
Sawant was the subject of a 2021 recall election after the Washington Supreme Court allowed charges to proceed to the ballot. The certified charges were using city resources to promote the Tax Amazon ballot initiative, admitting a crowd into a closed City Hall during COVID-19 restrictions on June 9, 2020, and leading a June 2020 protest march to Mayor Jenny Durkan's residence, whose address was protected under state law. Voters rejected the recall on December 7, 2021, and Sawant kept her seat by 310 votes.
Source tier
T2
Political impact
Moderate
Defense
The recall failed and Sawant retained her seat. She framed the effort as a politically motivated response to her socialist organizing and to the 2020 protest movement.
