1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (General Election 2026)

Elected Experience

This is my first run for elected office.

Other Professional Experience

WA Environmental Justice Council – Co-chair and Community Representative. People's Economy Lab - Collaborative Governance Strategist. Campus Sustainability Fund, UW Seattle - Program Lead. Sage Leaders Community Leadership Institute. Distinguished Fellow, UW Evans School.



Education

MPA and MPH from UW-Seattle; BS in Anthropology from Virginia Commonwealth University. Trained in economics, environment, public health, and policy.

Community Service

I have served my community to expand food access, support immigrant families, close the racial wealth gap, and mentor the next generation of leaders with organizations like Sage Leaders and Standing for Democracy. 



Statement

I’m a community organizer who has spent my career fighting alongside working families for justice, opportunity, and turning grassroots advocacy into durable change.

In the 37th Legislative District, I’ve partnered with my neighbors, workers, youth, and community leaders to help shape groundbreaking policies like the Climate Commitment and HEAL Acts. I’ve helped lead some of the nation’s first government-funded community assemblies because the people most impacted by public policy should help create it.

I’m running for State Senate to bring community voices directly into Olympia and deliver on the priorities I hear every day: affordable housing, stronger public schools, safer neighborhoods, and reliable transit. I will champion accessible healthcare, climate justice, and an economy where working people can thrive. I will direct investments into Seattle, Skyway and Renton, protect worker and immigrant communities, small businesses, and ensure our tax system is structured so the wealthiest pay their fair share.

The 37th is my home and I will work each day to fight for a government that serves us all. You deserve leadership centered in community and action, and with your vote – we will Build Different together.



Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, State Senator. Accessed 2026-10-10. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
