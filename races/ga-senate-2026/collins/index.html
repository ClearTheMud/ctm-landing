1 Primary Position & Trump Endorsement

Finding 1.1: Leads Primary at 28–33% with Trump Endorsement CRITICAL

What happened
Collins leads all primary polls consistently: 21.6% (AJC/UGA), 32.6% (Quantus), ~30% (Emerson). He is the only candidate with Trump’s endorsement, positioning him as the MAGA standard-bearer. The Laken Riley Act — first bill signed by Trump in 2025 — provides his signature legislative credential.
Source tier
T2 (multi-source: AJC, Quantus Insights, Emerson College polling; Trump endorsement confirmed by campaign announcement)
Political impact
In a Republican primary electorate, the Trump brand is the most powerful signal. Combined with 40+ state legislator endorsements and Club for Growth backing, Collins has the broadest institutional support among Republicans.

  • Quantus Insights: Collins at 32.6% (April 2026)
  • Club for Growth endorsement announcement

4 General Election Weakness

Finding 4.1: Weakest Matchup Against Ossoff HIGH

What happened
Among the three Republican frontrunners, Collins polls worst against Ossoff. His MAGA positioning and social media baggage limit appeal with Metro Atlanta suburban voters essential for a statewide win. The Kemp 2022 coalition (54.1%) won by carrying suburbs that Collins’ profile repels.
Source tier

Political impact
