BLUF — Bottom Line Up Front

Georgia’s Senate race is shaping into one of the most expensive contests in the nation, with total spending projected to exceed $150M. Incumbent Jon Ossoff holds a historically extreme financial advantage — $60.4M raised, $31M cash on hand, 4:1 over the Republican field combined. The May 19 Republican primary will almost certainly fail to produce a majority winner (90% probability), triggering a June 16 runoff. The Trump-Kemp factional divide, playing out through Collins (Trump-endorsed) vs. Dooley (Kemp-backed), is the single most consequential variable. Collins leads at 28–33% but carries significant social media liabilities; Dooley at 20–23% with the strongest upward trajectory. Carter’s $5.5M in ads has produced no polling movement. The Senate Leadership Fund has committed $44M to the eventual Republican nominee.
