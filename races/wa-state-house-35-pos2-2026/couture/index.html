Statement

I’m a veteran, father, husband, small business owner, and your State Representative fighting for safer communities and lower costs.

While families struggle, Olympia Democrats passed the largest tax increases in state history - including an income tax - and raided funds from a pension for retired first responders to fuel reckless spending. Meanwhile, soft-on-crime policies have made our communities less safe. I fought to hold criminals accountable, defend parental rights, and stop dangerous policies that put children at risk. I’ll keep fighting for affordability, less taxes, public safety, and our rural way of life. I respectfully ask for your vote.
