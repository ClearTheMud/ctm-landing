Statement

I’m a lifelong Shelton resident, mother of a special needs child, and caregiver to my veteran grandfather.  As a social worker, crisis responder, and healthcare worker, I’ve seen the challenges our community faces. I'm running to ensure our district is Represented by someone who knows the struggles working families are facing and will fight to build a community where everyone can thrive. I’ll  focus on making life more affordable, expanding housing, strengthening our public schools, and increasing access to healthcare. 

Endorsed: Mary Johnson, Olympic Middle School Principal; Washington State Labor Council; 35th LD Democrats; Mason County Democrats; Skokomish Tribal Council
