1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (General Election 2026)

Elected Experience

State Senator, Chair of the State Government, Tribal Affairs, and Elections Committee. Former State Representative.

Other Professional Experience

Javier has worked for the City of Seattle for over two decades, where he’s led programs to expand opportunities for women and minority-owned businesses.

Education

The son and grandson of farmworkers and laborers, Javier earned his Bachelor’s degree in Business Administration from the University of Washington and Master’s degree in Public Administration from Baruch College, the City University of New York.

Community Service

Javier has served on the board of the Washington State Hispanic Chamber of Commerce, President of Hispanic Seafair, and president of AFSCME Local 21C.

Statement

Javier is the leader in Olympia standing up to Trump, leading the charge to ban ICE agents from wearing masks, and terrorizing our communities. He also helped backfill money that Trump cut from Planned Parenthood, said no to Trump’s buddies from stealing our voter data, and banned federal agents from using cameras to spy on immigrants.

Like you, rising costs have impacted him, too. That’s why he helped reform antiquated zoning laws so that we can build more affordable housing, put a cap on rent increases, and voted to tax millionaires and the very wealthy to pay for free school lunches, tax relief for small businesses, and healthcare investments. 

Javier is a champion in the legislature on preventing gun violence, writing laws that banned ghost guns and high-capacity magazines. Next session, he wants to pass legislation to prohibit carrying guns in our parks and fairgrounds. 

Javier adds a unique voice in Olympia, being one of the first Latinos in the Senate, he provides a needed equity lens in policy decisions. Javier is proud to be endorsed by: 46th District Democrats, Washington State Labor Council, Planned Parenthood Alliance Advocates, Washington Conservation Action, One America Votes, Washington State Nurses Association, and more.
