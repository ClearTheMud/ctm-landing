1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (General Election 2026)

Elected Experience

Elected to the National Association of Pro-Life nurses for two terms: 1988-1992

Other Professional Experience

In my vast nursing career, it was never just a job for me.  As a Med-Surg Nurse, working in various hospitals, mental health home care centers, stroke centers, clinics, and community health centers, in my last sixteen years I was charge nurse at the UW Northwest Hospital, 5th floor.

Education

Graduated in 1978 as a Registered Nurse with a Bachelor of Science from Oregon Health Sciences University.

Community Service

I've served on hospital boards, worked feeding the homeless, volunteering at youth prisons, Salvation Army Emergency Response Teams, and much much more. 


Statement

From an early age I've wanted to help the most vulnerable among us.  I'm a grandma that has raised seven children who loves our nation, and our Constitution. I believe in the sacredness of life, and the individual's freedom of conscience that is the bedrock of our society.  The biggest threat to this, and our way life in Washington State, is the ongoing destruction of the economic contributions of generations that has given us a good place to raise our families.  This is being done by the current Democratic Party political machine, which is no longer a party, but a criminal syndicate. The financial base for this is the theft of  taxpayers funds through unaudited nonprofits which are then laundered to politicians.  This theft is what is driving the collapse of services and the increasing of taxes.

I am proud of the endorsement I have received from the King County Republican Party.  Their platform is an excellent one and a good representation of my policies.  I urge all those who feel their voice has no importance to elect me.  We are living in the times where more and more people are waking up and together we can make a difference.
