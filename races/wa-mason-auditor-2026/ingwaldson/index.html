Finding 6.1: Management background, but no direct elections-administration credential MODERATE

What happened
The Mason County Auditor administers elections, recording, licensing, and county financial operations. Ingwaldson brings nonprofit Executive Director experience (budget and operations management), a 2025 business-management degree, and an active local party-organization role as a Precinct Committee Officer, but public records show no prior elected office and no professional credential in elections administration, accounting/CPA, or election-systems IT. This is informational context for voters, not an adverse finding, a targeted vetting of lawsuits, criminal records, and ethics complaints returned nothing material.
Source tier
T1
Political impact
Moderate
Defense
County Auditor is an elected administrative-management role, not a technical certification; executive-level budget and operations leadership and a business degree are squarely relevant. Washington county elections run on standardized, state-certified systems operated by trained career staff, which limits the need for the elected official to be a systems specialist. Her clean public record supports her "integrity" framing.
