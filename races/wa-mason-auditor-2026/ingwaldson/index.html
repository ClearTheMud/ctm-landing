Community Service

I’m passionate about community service. I’m the incoming Hood Canal Lions Club President, and have held volunteer leadership positions with Kiwanis Club of North Mason, Lake Limerick Country Club, P.E.O. Sisterhood Ch-B, Saint David of Wales, and various North Mason School District committees.

 Statement

With over 40 years of experience in corporate and nonprofit organizations, I am ready to serve as Mason County Auditor—protecting fair elections, improving access to services, and restoring trust in public institutions. I will bring steady leadership, practical solutions, and a deep commitment to the people of this community.

As Auditor, I will model ethical leadership, integrity, accountability, and transparency. Our community deserves elections that are secure, efficient, and accessible to all. I will ensure staff have the tools and technology needed to carry out their work effectively, while making the office more open and responsive. Every voter should be able to see democracy in action and trust that it works for them.

Throughout my career, I have led teams, managed complex projects, and worked closely with volunteers and community members. I have built strong relationships through outreach and public service, and I am honored to have earned the support of respected retired auditors, elected leaders, the Mason County Democrats, and local residents who believe in my vision.

I care deeply about this community and the people who call it home. I would be honored to earn your vote and serve as your next Mason County Auditor.
