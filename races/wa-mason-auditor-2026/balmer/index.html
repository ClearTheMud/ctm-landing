Statement

Mason County deserves a County Auditor who is accessible, dependable, and committed to serving the public fairly and efficiently.

My professional experience and education enable me to understand the importance of accountability, openness, responsiveness, and treating every member of the community with respect and fairness.

As your County Auditor, I will work to ensure elections are conducted fairly, impartially, and without conflicts of interest; county records and financial operations are managed accurately; vehicle registrations and taxpayer resources are handled responsibly and efficiently; while continuing to offer passport services plus free filing services and certified copies for veterans’ DD214s.

I believe strong fiscal oversight, transparency, and operational accountability are essential to maintaining public trust and ensuring county government remains focused on serving the people of Mason County.

I also believe leadership matters. Supporting employees, improving communication, maintaining strong financial accountability, and upholding high operational standards are essential to building confidence and trust in the County Auditor’s Office.

Mason County is a community built on hard work, independence, and neighbors helping neighbors. I am running for County Auditor to bring steady leadership, accountability you can trust, and a strong commitment to serving this community with fairness, reliability, and dedication to our community.

Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, Auditor. Accessed 2026-07-16. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
