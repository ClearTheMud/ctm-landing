1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Skamania County is our chosen home for its natural beauty, rural lifestyle, and independent spirit. As Commissioner, I fight daily for practical solutions that address our challenges without raising taxes. We’re taxed enough already—I have never voted for or supported the annual 1% property tax increase. Instead, I champion responsible forest management, reliable local revenue, smarter tourism accountability, and fiscal discipline to fund public safety and services. I’ll continue serving you with results that protect the reasons why we all choose to live here. My straightforward, populist approach puts Skamania families, taxpayers, and our quality of life first.
