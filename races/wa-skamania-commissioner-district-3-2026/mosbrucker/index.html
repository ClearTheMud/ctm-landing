1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Living in Skamania County for 22 years, it pains me to see what's happening. Housing is scarce and unaffordable. Home insurance premiums keep increasing. Our schools have suffered years of cuts. The county budget is at the mercy of politicians who don't live here. Job creation is slow. My management experience provides me with the tools to address these challenges. My school board service gives me insight into the hopes and dreams of our youth and their families. We have the people with the skills to find solutions, but we need new leadership and innovative ideas to make it happen.
