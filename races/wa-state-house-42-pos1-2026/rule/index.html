1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (General Election 2026)

Elected Experience
Third-term State Representative, Blaine City Councilmember

Other Professional Experience
My family is fifth-generation Whatcom County, and I’m a small-business owner. As a social worker and mental health professional, I’ve helped families through adoptions, supported children who are struggling or experienced abuse, and cared for hospice patients.

Education
Meridian High School; bachelor’s degree, University of Washington; master’s degree, San Diego State

Community Service
Founded and served as president of Blaine Downtown Development Association, working with small-business owners to help attract family-wage jobs; organized school supply and backpack drives for children; led a communitywide health fair to help uninsured children receive healthcare.

Statement
As a working mom of three boys, I know it's hard to make ends meet. I worked to cut taxes for small businesses and voted no on tax increases, because these costs just get passed on to the consumer. I passed laws to cap rent increases and changed zoning laws to build more housing that our community needs.

I’m 100% pro-choice and introduced the bill to protect access to IVF. I passed laws to stop ICE agents from hiding behind masks, prevent the state from sending your private data to DC, and replace funding for Planned Parenthood cut by Trump.

Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, State Representative Pos. 1. Accessed 2026-10-10. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
