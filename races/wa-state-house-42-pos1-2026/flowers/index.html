I know the financial burden you bear with Washington's tax-raising Rule. It is time to restrain Olympia's spending, so you don't have to choose between buying food or fuel. I am deeply committed to leadership that protects your pocketbook, your property and your progeny. I will prioritize public safety, restoring roadways, increasing job opportunities, stabilizing housing, and lowering the tax burden for struggling Washingtonians. We can restore Whatcom County's resiliency with common-sense policies and your Vote!
