Finding 6.1: Office sanctioned $10,000 for systemic juvenile discovery violations HIGH

What happened
In a May 5, 2025 order, King County Superior Court Assistant Presiding Judge Tanya Thorp sanctioned the Prosecuting Attorney's Office $10,000 for discovery violations across four felony cases filed in juvenile court. The order arose from a case involving a 15-year-old accused of a Burien shooting in which the state withheld potentially exculpatory evidence. Judge Thorp wrote that the office's mismanagement of juvenile cases and failure to disclose evidence was not an isolated incident and had reached systemic levels, citing delayed and withheld discovery, finger-pointing at law enforcement, delays in witness identification, and settlement negotiations when exculpatory evidence was not disclosed. Manion and Chief Juvenile Deputy Jimmy Hung appeared before the court on remedies.

Defense
The office did not contest the need for change and responded with additional training across the juvenile division, improved coordination between the adult and juvenile divisions, and updated instructions for police on submitting case materials.
