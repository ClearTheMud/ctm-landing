Statement

As an attorney with 40 years of experience doing appeals in Washington, I'm running to serve on the Supreme Court to help it do justice in each case: fairly, impartially, according to law.  

Appeals safeguard the fundamental fairness of our courts. They are usually the only way to correct a court’s error. That’s why experience matters on the Supreme Court: it needs to “get it right.” 

I have worked on 135 appeals in courts across Washington — including the Supreme Court. I’ve helped courts get decisions right for clients of all backgrounds: students, small business owners, house cleaners, physicians, employees, deputies, families. I’ve won unpaid wages, defended protection orders, and ensured fairness in family disputes, health care, business deals, and trial procedures. The other candidates have little or no experience on appeals, or cases before the Supreme Court. You need not settle for that. 

My experience goes beyond the courtroom. I worked to improve health care in jails across Washington. I served as non-partisan counsel in the State Legislature. I’ve promoted excellence and ethics in the legal profession.  I’m fair, independent, trusted. I ask you to send my experience and belief in justice under the law to the Supreme Court.



Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, Justice Position #05. Accessed 2026-07-16. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
