Community Service

For nearly a decade, Jean has served on the Citizens Advisory Committee to the Sheriff, the Northwest Watershed Institute board, and in leadership roles with the Jefferson County Democrats. Jean led the successful 2025 Transportation Benefit District initiative, which voters approved with a supermajority, and she has worked to support public schools, wildfire response operations, and long-term environmental protection throughout Jefferson County. She currently serves as Vice President of the North Hood Canal Chamber of Commerce and lives in Quilcene.



Statement

Jefferson County deserves a commissioner who shows up, listens carefully, and speaks plainly and honestly about our challenges and opportunities.

I am running because county government works best when it’s responsive, understandable, and accountable to the people it serves, including rural communities too often left out of important conversations.

Three bedrock principles will guide my work: First, every dollar must work hard in a county with a small tax base. I will prioritize roads, public safety, essential services, and responsible fiscal stewardship, while being honest about difficult trade-offs; Second, every part of the county deserves consistent attention and clear communication from county government. From the West End to Brinnon, Quilcene, Chimacum, Port Ludlow, and Port Townsend, I will stay visible, remain accessible, and communicate clearly about how and why decisions are made; Third, we need economic development that broadens the tax base, supports local businesses, creates lasting opportunity and local prosperity while protecting the rural character, working lands, forests, and shorelines that define Jefferson County and make it a special place to live.

I will work to make county government more transparent, effective, and connected to the people who live and work here.  I’d be honored to earn your vote.
