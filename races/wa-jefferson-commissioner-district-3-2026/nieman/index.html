Statement

The biggest challenge facing Jefferson County is balancing the budget without further tax increases. This includes budgeting for long term infrastructure costs which are often ignored and must be considered. With my 30 year experience as a construction manager, I have the tools to ensure county revenues are prioritized toward the most effective outcomes in public safety, roads, housing affordability, and homelessness. County government must be accountable to taxpayers, and when programs or departments continue producing the same ineffective results, there must be measurable change, stronger oversight, and a renewed focus on what works.

Federal COVID era subsidies and related Washington State assistance helped mask serious deficiencies in the Jefferson County budgeting process.  We now must face reality and make hard budget decisions which may mean cuts in some services or making them more efficient. The recent change in how the county handles waste recycling is a case in point. We must objectively review the new program based on results and adjust accordingly.  A thorough review of permitting requirements is essential to lower the cost of new housing in Jefferson County. I have managerial experience to lead those efforts.
