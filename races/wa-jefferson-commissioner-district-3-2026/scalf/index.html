Other Professional Experience

I have deep roots on the Olympic Peninsula- with Washington State University Extension and formerly as Executive Director for the Sequim-Dungeness Valley Chamber of Commerce.  I’ve consulted for Non-profits and Small Businesses, supporting Finnriver’s expansion to the orchard in Downtown Chimacum.  I operated Mountain View Pool for the City of Port Townsend and coached after beginning my career as a Swimming Instructor, and Lifeguard.

Statement

We need a welcoming, affordable, and connected County for the next generation.  I believe spending wisely, planning for rainy days, and fighting for our fair share will be critical in the coming years.  We need to improve housing and infrastructure, responsibly manage natural resources, improve access to healthcare and transportation, and build a stronger workforce.  I grew up on Eaglemount, the daughter of two public servants and experienced the impact local government has on our economy when it invests in working families.  I will return that investment by ensuring people know and feel their power, build on my trusted relationships, and shore up our civic infrastructure.  We are all responsible for the outcomes of our Government, and I will mobilize the power of our people to face the coming storms.  My experience working with non-profits, recreation, tourism, hospitality, and education, coupled with a background in habitat restoration and agriculture give me unique skills to tackle the challenges of our future.

I am endorsed by more than 100 Jefferson County residents, including past District 3 Commissioners Greg Brotherton, Kathleen Kler, and John Austin.
