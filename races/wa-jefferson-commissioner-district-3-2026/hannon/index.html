Statement

For more than a decade, I have served Jefferson County through youth programs, community advocacy, and housing initiatives. As Housing Resources Manager at OlyCAP, I work with budgets, grants, and community partnerships to expand affordable housing and support working families, seniors, veterans, and young people.

My work has included advocating for housing resources and community support at the local, state, and federal levels, including in Olympia and Washington, D.C. I have worked alongside nonprofit leaders, service providers, and local governments to bring investment and practical solutions to our communities.

I understand the challenges many residents face, including rising housing costs, access to services, and the growing difficulty of building a stable future here. I am running for County Commissioner to support responsible growth, strengthen local infrastructure and services, and ensure Jefferson County remains a place where local families, workers, and future generations can afford to live, work, and thrive.

Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, District 3. Accessed 2026-07-16. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
