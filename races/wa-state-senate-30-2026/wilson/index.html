1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

Current State Senator; Federal Way School Board 2011 - 2019

Other Professional Experience

35-year educator and administrator leading work on early learning, family engagement, and governance; WIC Project Director; small business owner. It’s been my life’s work supporting children and working families to help foster successful and healthy households across Washington.

Education

Washington State University, BS, Food and Nutrition, Child and Family Studies; University of Northern Colorado, MA, Adult Education, Vocational Home Economics.

Community Service

Extensive volunteer experience, including South King County Tool Library, WA State Leadership Board, WA State School Directors Association, Citizens for Federal Way Schools, PTA, and Mentor and Me Program. 



Statement

Caring for our kids, families, and communities is what I do every day– and with your support, I will continue fighting for the people and places that matter. As a longtime educator, former Federal Way School Board Director, and now your State Senator, I place the needs of families and communities first, focusing on solutions and results. 

I lead Senate efforts to expand services and support for young people, keeping kids in school and away from harm. I sponsored and passed landmark investments in affordable childcare, quality early learning, and help for vulnerable families. I delivered resources for affordable housing options that serve seniors, low-income families, working people, and help people out of homelessness.

After devastating floods, I secured over $7.5 million in funding for the city of Pacific. Committed to safety, I voted for pay raises and additional training for law enforcement officers, and funding for drug treatment and recovery. I worked to reduce the tax burden on workers and small businesses, while securing needed funding for K-12.

I’m proudly endorsed by Firefighters, Planned Parenthood, Teachers, Nurses, Alliance for Gun Responsibility, Children’s Campaign Fund, State Labor Council, Machinists. With your vote, I’ll keep fighting– for all of us!
