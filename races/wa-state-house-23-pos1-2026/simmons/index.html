1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

State Representative (2021-present); Deputy Speaker Pro-Tempore; Vice Chair, Community Safety; Joint Select Committee on Civic Health. Member: Technology, Economic Development and Veterans, Healthcare, and Rules Committees.

Other Professional Experience

Attorney, small business owner, nonprofit consultant. RN (former), Harrison Hospital and Martha and Mary; Case Aide, Child Protection Services.

Education

Central Kitsap High School; AAS, Olympic College; BSN, Pacific Lutheran University; JD, Seattle University School of Law (magna cum laude).

Community Service

I am a U.S. Marine mom supporting military families and volunteer regularly with anti-poverty and civil rights organizations. I have served on several Boards and Commissions for seniors, women, and people with disabilities.




Statement

Raised in Kitsap, it’s my greatest honor to serve you. Since 2021, I’ve passed landmark healthcare legislation, expanded voting and civil rights, reduced barriers to behavioral health, increased affordable housing and fought to protect our environment. I know working families need urgent solutions to lower housing, health and childcare costs, and you deserve proven leadership. I am the progressive leader with the record of results to ensure opportunity isn’t reserved for the ultra-wealthy.

Broad Support: Governor Bob Ferguson, Washington State Labor Council, Washington Conservation Voters; Planned Parenthood Alliance Advocates; Kitsap Firefighters, teachers, nurses, law enforcement, construction workers, elected officials, more.
