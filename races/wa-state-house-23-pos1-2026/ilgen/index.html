1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

Democratic Precinct Committee Officer, State Committeeman 23rd Legislative District Democrats

Other Professional Experience

Business and Community leader with 20 years experience as a strategist for Amazon, Zillow, and Expedia, reforming systems and uniting teams to build success. Transitioned to support my wife to help grow her Kitsap small business and build a family farm with our children. 



Education

BA, Political Science, University of Colorado; MBA, Hult International Business School

Community Service

Board Member: Kitsap Community Foundation, Olympic College Foundation, Kitsap Economic Development Alliance, YMCA, County and District Democrats Executive Boards; Kitsap Intra-County Ferry Board Advisor; North Kitsap School District Levy Campaign Lead

Statement

Divisive politics and the status quo are failing us. Kitsap should be a place where seniors age in place, kids build a future, and families aren’t forced to choose between groceries, gas, childcare, medication, and rent. Too many are paying more, getting less, and losing trust.

In business, nonprofit, and community leadership, I’ve united people to fix broken, wasteful systems, and lead innovation. With your vote, I’ll fight for fair taxation, honest budgets, reliable ferries, affordable homes and childcare, strong schools, and safer communities. I’ll use my expertise to end insurance and retail price gouging. It’s time for new leadership.
