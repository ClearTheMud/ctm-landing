Statement

Divisive politics and the status quo are failing us. Kitsap should be a place where seniors age in place, kids build a future, and families aren’t forced to choose between groceries, gas, childcare, medication, and rent. Too many are paying more, getting less, and losing trust.

In business, nonprofit, and community leadership, I’ve united people to fix broken, wasteful systems, and lead innovation. With your vote, I’ll fight for fair taxation, honest budgets, reliable ferries, affordable homes and childcare, strong schools, and safer communities. I’ll use my expertise to end insurance and retail price gouging. It’s time for new leadership.
