1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

I have served as the appointed Pacific County Auditor since December 1, 2025.

Other Professional Experience

Prior to the Auditor’s Office, I was a Logistics Manager for one of the largest steel tube and bar manufacturers and distributors in the country. I oversaw the export paperwork, truck manifests, and route schedules for a fleet of seven, in addition to overseas shipments.

Education

I attended South Bend schools (K-12) and graduated South Bend High School in 2007.

Community Service

I’ve found the best way to give back is to build up the next generation. I have enjoyed coaching my daughters’ soccer and basketball teams.

Statement

My journey has been defined by a dedication to excellence, a passion for service, and a commitment to continuous growth. Throughout my previous professional career as a Logistics Manager, I have demonstrated adaptability, integrity, and a collaborative spirit, all of which I believe are essential qualities for this role. If elected, I will bring my best effort, fresh perspective, and unwavering dedication to the people of Pacific County. I am eager to contribute, learn, and grow alongside colleagues and citizens, and am committed to making a meaningful impact for the good of our communities. My time in this role has been filled with lots of challenges and opportunities to grow. From the implementation of a new payroll system to the building of a new county website, I rise to the occasion to ensure the Auditor’s Office is on the right path to better serve you. My plans to strengthen the Office go beyond the election, and with your help, I hope to be the one to bring these visions to fruition. I believe that the people of Pacific County deserve an Office that is run with humble service and bold integrity. It is my mission to do just that!

Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, County Auditor. Accessed 2026-07-16. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
