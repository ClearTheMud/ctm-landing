Community Service
I have always been committed to serving and giving back to my community.  Before becoming Fair Manager or a paraeducator, I volunteered in both roles.  I’ve also coached t-ball for many years, supporting local youth and families.  Community involvement is very important to me, and I remain dedicated to making a positive impact wherever I can.

Statement
My name is Dotsi Graves, and I am proud to be running for Pacific County Auditor.  Serving as Pacific County Auditor, and continuing my commitment to this community, would be the greatest honor of my career. With 30 years of county service, leadership experience, and extensive knowledge of the Auditor’s Office, I am prepared to serve the people of Pacific County with dedication, integrity, and accountability. From 2003 to 2007, I worked in the Auditor’s Office, where I advanced to the role of Chief Deputy. During that time, I gained hands-on experience in elections, as well as the many other responsibilities the Auditor’s Office oversees. In 2023, I returned to the Auditor’s Office to become the Elections Administrator, where my passion for elections was reignited. I completed the rigorous certification process and in June, I received my certificate. For the past seven months, as the only certified Elections Administrator in Pacific County, I have been responsible for conducting elections, building strong professional relationships, relying on trusted resources, and working diligently to ensure every election was conducted accurately and professionally.  With your support, I will continue to work hard every day.
