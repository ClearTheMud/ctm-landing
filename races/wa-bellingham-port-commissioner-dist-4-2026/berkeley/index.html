Finding 5.1: Censured by fellow fire commissioners in 2017 for threatening and disruptive conduct MODERATE

What happened
While serving as an elected commissioner of North Whatcom Fire and Rescue (elected 2013), Berkeley was censured in January 2017 by the other fire commissioners and the district chief for what they described as an outburst of threatening and disruptive behavior at a prior meeting. The censure was a formal action taken by the board on which he served. The conflict also included a small claims lawsuit Berkeley filed against the same district in 2016 seeking roughly $1,000 for personal firefighting gear he said went missing from a station locker after he lost volunteer status in 2012.

Defense
Berkeley has characterized the missing-gear dispute as a legitimate grievance, stating the equipment he purchased himself disappeared and was never returned. The censure reflects one board's account of a meeting and Berkeley remained in office; voters can weigh it against his later service.
