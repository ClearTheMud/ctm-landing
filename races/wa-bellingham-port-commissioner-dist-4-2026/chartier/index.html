Finding 6.1: Largest donor is the commercial fishing association in an industry he works in and would regulate MODERATE

What happened
Chartier is a commercial fisherman, and his single largest campaign donor is the Whatcom Commercial Fishermen's Association, which contributed $1,400, about a third of his $4,175 in receipts. As a port commissioner he would vote on marina fees, moorage, and marine trades investments that affect the commercial fishing industry he works in. His stated top priority is expanding marine trades and the working waterfront. The financial tie between a candidate, his own industry, and the trade group funding his campaign is a conflict a voter would want disclosed.

Defense
Industry and trade-group support is common and legal in port commission races, the contribution is fully disclosed in PDC filings, and Chartier has been public about running as a commercial fisherman focused on the working waterfront rather than concealing the connection.
