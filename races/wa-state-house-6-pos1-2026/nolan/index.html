1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

Mead School Board Director, Position 2

Other Professional Experience

Retired USAF Lieutenant Colonel with 22+ year career spent in fighters and helicopters with command experience leading large organizations.  Small business owner of award winning construction business.  Recognized industry expertise led to selection for state-level code writing group and nomination to board overseeing state building codes.  Vice President of school board responsible for educating over 10,000 students.

Education

US Air Force Academy – Bachelor in Economics with French minor.  Troy State University – Masters of Science, International Relations

Community Service

Spokane County Planning Commission Board Member.  Technical Advisory Group member for 2018 Washington State Energy Code, Residential.

Statement

Washington needs fiscal responsibility and accountability. Families feel Olympia’s failures in every paycheck, every grocery bill, and every tank of gas. Reckless spending, rising taxes, and foolish, ideologically driven policies are pushing families and businesses out, driving many to leave communities they love.

It doesn’t have to be this way. We can stop the financial squeeze, strengthen education, restore public safety, and reestablish a government for the people.  As a veteran, business owner, and school board director, I know leadership means service, accountability, and delivering results. I humbly ask for your vote to restore opportunity and build a stronger Washington.
