Statement

Jennifer believes public service begins with listening to the people she represents. She is committed to addressing the district’s most pressing challenges with practical, common-sense solutions that strengthen public safety, support small businesses, expand economic opportunity, and protect the quality of life families deserve. Jennifer believes government should be accountable, transparent, and focused on delivering results for working families and local communities. With collaborative leadership, real-world experience, and a fresh perspective, Jennifer will work every day in Olympia to deliver meaningful results for the citizens of the 6th District.

Endorsed by Medical Lake Mayor Terri Cooper and Representative Rob Chase.
