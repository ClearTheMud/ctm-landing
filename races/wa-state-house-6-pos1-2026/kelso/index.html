1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (General Election 2026)

Elected Experience

Democratic PCO and State Committee Member 2020 to present.  

Other Professional Experience

I immigrated to the US from Germany in 1993. I joined the US Army in 1995 and retired after 23.5 years in 2018. I spent the last 15 years in the Military Intelligence Corps working as a linguist and interrogator. My combat deployments include Iraq, Afghanistan, and Bosnia.  I know how to work effectively with others regardless of personal differences.

Education

AA (X3) in Arboriculture, Landscape Management, Small Farms Production from SCC.  I support all forms of higher education.

Community Service

I have been active in support of Freedom, Democracy, and Truth since 1995.  

Statement

A representative of, and for the people has to support all of their constituents, not just the ones in their party.  I know how to work with others to achieve our common goals. I will fairly represent all constituents regardless of their politics, and introduce and support legislation that will benefit our district.  My legislative priorities will be housing inventory and costs, education funding, healthcare costs, and rural infrastructure. I will work to ensure our West Plains neighbors have safe water to drink, and all of our children can attend safe and high quality schools, regardless of where they live.

Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, State Representative Pos. 1. Accessed 2026-10-10. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
