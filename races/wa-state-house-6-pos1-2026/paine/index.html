1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

Republican Precinct Committee Officer; Vice Chair, Spokane County Republican Party

Other Professional Experience

Housing policy work with the Spokane Home Builders Association, former small business owner, and roles in nonprofit and church ministry. Previously served as a Legislative Aide for former Washington 6th Legislative District Rep. Kevin Parker.

Education

Political Science/Communications, Wheaton College; Nonprofit Management, Whitworth University; Jennifer Dunn Leadership Institute

Community Service

Youth soccer coach; Vice Chair, Spokane Solid Waste Advisory Committee; Spokane Safe and Healthy Task Force; GSI Regional Advocacy Committee; DSP Public Policy Committee; Adult/Teen Challenge Advisory Committee; Serve in my local church

Statement

As a husband and father of four, I want a Washington where families can afford to live, kids have opportunity, and communities are safe. Instead, Olympia passed the largest tax increase in state history while housing costs soar and jobs leave our state. We need to strengthen families, support law enforcement, and make government work for taxpayers again. Eastern Washington deserves leaders who will fight for common sense, lower costs, and our way of life. I’m proud to be endorsed by Senator Jeff Holy and Representatives Mike Volz and Jenny Graham. I would be honored to earn your vote.
