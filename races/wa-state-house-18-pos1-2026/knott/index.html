First Time Candidate

Other Professional Experience

Before she owned and operated a B&B and a horse ranch in Brush Prairie, Randi had over 25 years of experience in public, private and non-profit sectors, evaluating policy's financial impact on working families. She was honored to serve board appointment by both Democratic and Republican Governors.



Education

University of Southern California, MPA, CSU Northridge, BA

Community Service

Board Member for Developmental Disabilities Advisory Board and Joint4ces, a nonprofit supporting veterans transitions to careers in agriculture; Board Member, Transitional Living and Community Support, helping the unhoused get services and move off the streets;  N. Clark County Food Bank volunteer.

Statement

Affordability must be part of every decision, because wages aren't keeping up. Randi will support policies to attract higher paying jobs, make housing more affordable, and oppose policies that shift costs onto families while protecting the wealthy and big corporations. Affordability is about keeping people in their homes and planning for the future.

Randi is an Autism Mom and advocate for families navigating education systems, healthcare, and public services that too often fail. That perspective has shaped how she approaches policy: thoughtfully, inclusively, ad with deep respect for how decisions affect families day to day.
