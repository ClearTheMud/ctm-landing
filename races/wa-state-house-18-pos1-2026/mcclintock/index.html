Statement
I will continue fighting policies that raise taxes, protect your hard-earned income and oppose reckless spending. I will work to stop unfunded mandates on K–12 education and support reforms to improve outcomes.  As the daughter of a police detective, I support law enforcement and efforts to restore safety in our communities and close dangerous loopholes in our sex offender system.
