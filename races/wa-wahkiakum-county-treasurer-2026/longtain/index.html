Statement
As a candidate for Wahkiakum County Treasurer, I offer proven experience and a strong commitment to responsible financial stewardship. Over the past three years in the Treasurer’s Office, including two as Chief Deputy Treasurer and Investment Officer, I have developed a comprehensive understanding of the office’s operations and statutory responsibilities.

In my current role, some of the many duties I assist in are managing and investing county funds, processing and reconciling property taxes, assessments, and fees for the town, school and junior districts ensuring accurate distribution. I also oversee daily financial transactions and reporting to maintain accuracy, accountability, and compliance. This experience has prepared me to maintain stable operations while upholding the highest standards of public service.

I have worked closely with Treasurer Tammy Peterson and have received extensive training through hands on experience in the office. This preparation has given me the knowledge and confidence to step into the role and continue providing consistent, reliable service to our community.

If elected, I will prioritize transparency, fiscal responsibility, and efficient service to the public. My focus will remain on safeguarding county resources, maintaining public trust, and ensuring the Treasurer’s Office continues to operate with reliability and professionalism.
