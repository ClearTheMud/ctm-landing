Statement
I'm running for County Commissioner because I care more about the work in front of us than the politics that divide us.

My focus will be on the basics: budgets that balance, roads that hold up, infrastructure that lets our small towns grow, and an economy that gives young people a reason to stay after graduation.

District 3 stretches from west of the freeway all the way to Ashford and Packwood with over a hundred miles of farms, forests, small towns, and families in between. Out east, the question I hear most often is, "who's my commissioner again?" That's a problem, because every part of this district deserves a seat at the table, not just the parts closest to the courthouse.

I'll hold regular town halls across the district. And I'll be straight with you, and respectful, even when we disagree or the answer is hard to hear. As your commissioner, I'll build up District 3, and Lewis County as a whole, the same way you'd frame a shed or light a fire in the rain: by starting with a solid foundation.
