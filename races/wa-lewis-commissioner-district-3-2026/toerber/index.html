Statement
Tim Toerber is a veteran, aviation executive, public servant, husband, father, and outdoor enthusiast.. Raised with rural Midwest values and shaped by his family’s Centennial Farm in eastern Iowa, Tim understands hard work, personal responsibility, stewardship, and rural communities. 

 Lewis County families deserve county government that is practical, transparent, right-sized, and focused on results. Tim is running to protect our rural way of life while preparing for the growth, tourism, infrastructure demands, and public safety pressures facing Lewis County.

His priority is growing the county’s general fund without relying only on grants, higher taxes, or short-term fixes. We have natural advantages in working forests, renewable energy, transportation access, outdoor recreation, and rural industry. We should use those strengths to attract responsible private investment, support family-wage jobs, and build new revenue streams that help fund roads, law enforcement, fire, and emergency management.

Tim will focus on practical economic development, better coordination across county departments and local agencies, and stronger partnerships with cities, fire districts, state agencies, and federal land managers. Tim actively supports Packwood’s small businesses and community life, including as one of the first local Mug Club members at Longmire Springs Brewing. Protect what we inherited. Build what they deserve.
