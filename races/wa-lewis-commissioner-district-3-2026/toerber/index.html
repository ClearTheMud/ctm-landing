Finding 2.2: Aviation Executive Living in Rural Packwood — Professional/Residential Disconnect MODERATE

What happened
Toerber is President of the Americas division at Assaia, an aviation technology company. He previously held senior roles at Amazon Air and the Port of Seattle. His professional career is in urban/global aviation operations, which has essentially no overlap with the issues facing Lewis County Commissioner District 3 (rural budgets, roads, flooding, timber, agriculture, public safety). The county commissioner role is a full-time salaried position that would need to be balanced with his aviation executive responsibilities.

Defense
Aviation executives can have flexible schedules, particularly with remote work. Living in Packwood demonstrates commitment to the east county community. Business management experience at a large scale could transfer to county government oversight. The disconnect between career and local politics is not unusual for rural candidates with professional careers outside the county.
