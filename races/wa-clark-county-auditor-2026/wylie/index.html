Statement
Trust in our government is essential.  Being a trusted leader in my community is a huge honor and responsibility. The Auditor is responsible for providing safe elections, secure public records, and responsible financial stewardship. These duties demand leadership rooted in clarity, integrity, and a deep respect for open government. I have passed laws to protect your right to vote, to help your county provide better customer service, to provide the resources to prosecute criminals, train police and sheriff deputies, and protect the civil rights of vulnerable people.

Advocating for democracy and the rule of law, and using my experience in County operations to protect your county election and financial systems is my goal. I will enforce rigorous fiscal stewardship, safeguard public funds, and ensure accountability. Using modern tools to manage public records, deeds and licensing, while maintaining data privacy is essential.

 

Experience counts. When you record a deed, renew a license, or register to vote, you deserve absolute confidence that the systems supporting you are accurate and dependable. The County has state mandated responsibilities. My state experience will make that link more effective.

I respectfully ask for your vote to keep Clark County’s systems strong, secure, and accountable to you.
