ResidenceLives in Bellevue. Represents King County Council District 6, which includes all or parts of Bellevue, Kirkland, Mercer Island, the Points Communities (Clyde Hill, Medina, Hunts Point, Yarrow Point), and Redmond in east King County.T1
PartyKing County Council is a nonpartisan office and carries no party label on the ballot. Balducci is a Democrat and filed with the PDC under the office of County Council Member, King County.T1
EducationBachelor of Arts from Providence College. She studied abroad at Oxford University and played on the Oxford women's basketball team.; Juris Doctor from Columbia Law School.T2
EmploymentBegan her legal career at the Northwest Women's Law Center, now Legal Voice, working on discrimination, reproductive rights, and domestic violence issues. Later worked as a labor negotiator in Bellevue.; Served as Director of the King County Department of Adult and Juvenile Detention, overseeing the county jail system and leading reforms to inmate physical and mental health services.; Appointed to the Sound Transit Board of Directors in 2010 and has served as a longtime regional transit leader.; Elected president of the Puget Sound Regional Council in 2021 after serving as vice president.T2

TopicPositionSource
Transit and Light RailDescribes herself as a transit die-hard. She fought to route East Link light rail through downtown Bellevue and later led the push to open the 2 Line early. She proposed a starter line light rail concept for the Eastside and supports bus rapid transit, pedestrian safety, and the Eastrail regional trail.T1
Affordable HousingFounder and chair of the King County Regional Affordable Housing Committee and co-led the Regional Affordable Housing Task Force. Campaigns on expanding affordable housing supply.T1
Public Safety and Detention ReformPoints to her record as detention department director on jail mental health services and supports youth programs such as Best Starts for Kids.T3
