Finding 4.1: Operates Antisemitic Conspiracy Websites, Including Holocaust Denial CRITICAL

What happened
Blomstrom is the author and operator of a network of personal websites, including one titled 'Jewarchy,' that publish antisemitic conspiracy material. Haaretz reported in 2016 that his sites claim a 'Jewish mafia' controls politics, banking, and media, call Israel 'an illegitimate terror state,' refer to the Holocaust as a 'Holohoax,' and blamed Jewish people for the 2016 Orlando nightclub shooting. Everyday Antisemitism documented the same sites and their crossed-out Star of David logo. Blomstrom has run this content across multiple Washington candidacies, including his 2016 governor run and his 2026 U.S. House campaign.
