Statement

My name is Gwen Kirkland, and I’ve been a political organizer as long as I’ve lived in Washington state. I moved here from Missouri in 2023 to escape anti-transgender discrimination in the Bible Belt and finally acquire real healthcare for the first time in my life. 

As a community organizer, I’ve knocked thousands of doors for universal healthcare and canvassed for successful progressive campaigns. As a labor organizer, I advocated against corporate grocery mergers, organized my coworkers to strike and fought back against systemic corruption. Together, we endorsed progressive politicians against the wishes of billionaires, politicians and establishment leaders alike.

Facing people on their porches and at their workplaces, I promised that the policies I’ve always championed would pass with the people’s vital support for these progressive candidates who share our working class values. Policies like universal healthcare, free education for all, and publicly-owned utilities and grocery stores are a necessity for working families to have any hope of an affordable future.

Sadly, the Democratic Party has zero interest in fighting the billionaires and their fascist lackeys. This means we have to take over the Democratic Party first in order to guarantee we ever have democracy in this country. Complacency breeds corruption, and when our leaders fail us so completely, they cannot stay in office. Their failed leadership is what brought us to this point. 

So I promise that is precisely what I will do. Lead. More hands make less work and we need all hands on deck. As a union strike captain, I know that when working people fight back, we always win.

Let’s put our money to work for us instead of these career politicians and the poisonous, genocidal corporations they serve. Together, we can free ourselves from the establishment, the Republican Party and the Epstein Class. Solidarity.
