Finding 6.1: Late STOCK Act Disclosure of Microsoft Stock Sales MODERATE

What happened
DelBene disclosed two Microsoft stock sales by her husband well past the deadline set by the Stop Trading on Congressional Knowledge (STOCK) Act, which requires reporting securities transactions over $1,000 within 45 days. A sale executed August 30, 2022, and a second executed March 1, 2023, were both reported June 14, 2023, making the first disclosure 288 days late and the second 105 days late. The transactions were part of a forward contract her husband, Kurt DelBene, created after his confirmation as a senior Department of Veterans Affairs official, a self-executing sale schedule her office says was drafted by ethics experts and approved by the VA. The forward contract itself was disclosed over a year in advance, but the individual sales were reported late.
Source tier
T2
Political impact
Moderate
Defense
DelBene's office states the underlying forward contract was disclosed more than a year before the sales occurred, was structured with ethics counsel, and was approved by the Department of Veterans Affairs, and that the arrangement was designed to remove her husband's discretion over the timing of sales. Some analysts view the reporting as technically covered by a 45-day window; others dispute that reading.
