1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience

First-time candidate. Home care aide and proud union member.

Other Professional Experience

For more than five years, I have worked as a Home Care Aide, caring for my brother with Down syndrome and supporting another client with long-term care needs. That work, and the daily fight against a healthcare system designed to take your money and wear you down, is why I am running for Congress.

Education

Bachelor of Arts in Political Science from Western Washington University.

Community Service

While at WWU, I joined LEAD (Learning, Environment, Action, Discovery), a graduate student-led environmental conservation program focused on habitat restoration across Northern Washington.

Statement

Our country is at a breaking point. Working families are doing more for less. Our healthcare system is punishing the people it is supposed to help. My brother depends on Medicaid, and so do millions of others here in Washington. The Trump administration is moving to cut it further, and I won’t sit idly by while that happens.

I grew up here. My family bought our home before the tech boom; and my generation has now been priced out of the communities we grew up in. I watched Microsoft and other major employers lay off thousands of Washingtonians while the people responsible kept getting richer. Working families across this district are being hollowed out by the same economic forces. Our representatives keep telling us to wait.

We cannot wait.

In Congress, I will fight for Medicare for All and the protection of Medicaid and Medicare from any further cuts. I will fight for a billionaires tax to make the richest Americans pay their fair share, with rigorous tax enforcement against the top 1% and an end to the corporate takeover of our politics. I will fight for a much higher minimum wage tied to productivity, national rent stabilization, major public housing investment, and limits on Wall Street ownership of single-family homes. I will fight to hold big tech accountable, regulate AI, and halt data center construction while protecting tech workers and creating good-paying union jobs. I will fight to codify Roe, defend gender-affirming care, and protect every Washingtonian's right to make decisions about their own body.

My campaign accepts no corporate PAC money, no AIPAC contributions, and no donations from the big tech companies. Politicians are acting like it's business as usual while the house is on fire. Help me put it out.



Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, U.S. Representative. Accessed 2026-07-16. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
