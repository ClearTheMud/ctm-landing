1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement

Elected Experience
This election is my first government campaign.

Other Professional Experience
I am a small business owner of a travel agency. I am also a certified personal trainer and have been doing that work for the past 9 years.

Education
BA - Gonzaga University, Magna Cum Laude -2014. MM - Seattle Film Institute -2016

Community Service
I am currently serving as President of the Seattle Civic Band. I have served for several years on the parish council for St. Matthew Catholic Church in Seattle, and have volunteered with them and other parishes in the Seattle Archdiocese for many years in a variety of roles and services.

Statement
As I write this statement in May, we are in an unauthorized war that has only brought death and disaster. Every day, I pray that this war ends, and I hope by the time you read this, it will have. But the effects of this administration and ineffective congress will take years to recover unless we make a dramatic change.

We all have the right to clean air, safe water, food, a place to live, and medical care. People deserve to live in the assurance that if they get laid off, they can still afford their medicine. I want to be able to go to the doctor knowing it won’t bankrupt me. But right now, corporate interests are given priority over people, and life is too expensive for us working class folks.

The billionaire class is destroying our planet, hoarding our money, and buying government officials. Mega-corporations are given more rights than our children, who just want to go to school without being killed.

Career politicians and their pockets full of lobbying money need to retire. It’s time to set term limits in all branches of government. It’s time to enshrine in the Constitution, rights of the people over companies. It’s time to end lobbying and multi-million dollar political campaigns. It’s time to hold all of the criminals in the files accountable, no matter what position of power they’re in.

All over the country, young progressives like me are running and winning. We have the power to make real change to protect Americans. All Americans. Regardless of how you voted in 2016, 2020, 2024, or ever, I want to protect you. Your voice matters, your vote matters. I hope you will use it to give me a chance to work for you. I ask for your vote. Thank you.
