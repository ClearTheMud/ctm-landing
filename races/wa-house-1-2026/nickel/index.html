Statement
America is in a crisis for working people. Housing costs have skyrocketed and wages have not kept up. Millions of families work harder than ever just to get by while billionaires accumulate massive wealth. This is no accident; it is the result of a corrupt system that puts money ahead of families, disabled people, seniors, and young people.

I am a developmentally disabled self-advocate who has experienced homelessness and poverty firsthand. I know what it means to be left out of systems. We must fundamentally change priorities in this country and ensure that no one is left without dignity, income security, or a roof over their head.

I support Medicare for All because healthcare is a human right. No one should face bankruptcy because of a medical emergency. We must transition to a system that prioritizes patients over insurance profits, ensuring every person can access the care they need when they need it.

To address the housing crisis, I support fully funding Section 8 and building large-scale affordable housing. We must end the treatment of housing as a speculative commodity. Housing is a human right, and every family deserves the stability of a safe, permanent home.

I support a universal basic income and a federal jobs guarantee so no one falls into poverty because of disability or economic shifts. To ensure a true economic floor, I believe artificial intelligence and automation should be nationalized. These technologies must benefit the public good rather than enriching corporate executives and shareholders.

We must rebuild the middle class by taking on extreme wealth, strengthening unions, and investing in public services. This campaign is about a simple idea: every person deserves healthcare, housing, and the opportunity to live a life of dignity and the freedom to pursue happiness. Join me in building a better future.
