Statement

Washington’s 1st District deserves a representative who puts working families first not corporate donors, not party ideology and not the status quo. Representative Suzan DelBene has served in Congress for over twelve years. During which time her personal net worth more than doubled  from $60 million to over $141 million while residents face worsening housing costs, homelessness and public safety challenges. As Chair of the Democratic Congressional Campaign Committee she bore direct responsibility for the 2024 election losses that gave Republicans control of Congress. This campaign is different. I will fight for federal investment in next generation housing manufacturing that builds homes faster, cheaper and with 70% less energy consumption than conventional construction . Treating the housing crisis, the energy crisis and the coming electricity demand surge from AI and data centers as one connected problem. I will fight to eliminate federal income tax and filing requirements for Americans earning $61,000 or less, putting real money back in the hands of working families. I will fight for a federal Secure Storage Law targeting the 200,000 firearms stolen every year that fuel street crime. Homelessness reform framework that addresses root causes with real accountability rather than endless spending with no results. I believe men should not be in women’s sports or women’s prisons. I believe in the protection of women only spaces. Zero tolerance for antisemitism. Socialism doesn’t work. Taiwan is not part of China. Positions that are not controversial but simply common sense. I support a real world pilot program to test universal healthcare before any national rollout. I don’t support the government forcing the end of private health insurance. I believe that every nation that loves freedom should support Ukraine. And support the people of Iran against the Regime. People Over Politics. That is the promise.
