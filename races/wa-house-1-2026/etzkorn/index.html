1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (Primary Election 2026)

Elected Experience

Monroe School District Board Director since March, 2023.

Other Professional Experience

Over a decade of experience at large and small tech companies as a hardware engineer and senior manager in the fields of semiconductors, smart contact lenses, batteries and quantum computers.

Education

Bachelor of Science in Electrical Engineering, University of Washington, 2007; Master of Science in Nanoscale Engineering, National Institute for Applied Sciences, 2011.



Community Service

Nearly 20 years volunteering in classrooms tutoring math, reading and science. Founded and coached the Monroe High School first Robotics Team (2018-2020). Echo Lost Lake Community Group Board Member (2022-2024).



Statement

The two-party system has failed us. Dysfunction and corruption create volatility, driving up the cost of housing, gas, food and healthcare. Neither party offers solutions. They’re too busy fighting for power and trying to convince us the other side is worse. While I am completely against the chaos of the current administration, relying on the same career politicians who helped create this mess is not the answer. We cannot keep doing the same thing over and over and expect different results. 

I am running for Congress because our government needs a significant course correction that can only be addressed at the federal level. Congress has failed to hold each other and the Executive Branch accountable, leaving us governed more by executive orders and litigation than legislation. 

We must replace the 14-year incumbent who prioritizes party power over issues plaguing our district. I am an independent-minded engineer running to break partisan gridlock, force compromise and deliver solutions that put workers ahead of special interests. 

My platform addresses affordability by stripping government friction to increase the supply of housing, healthcare and energy. We must incentivize companies to build here in America by investing in infrastructure and workforce training. When companies compete for our labor, wages go up like we saw in 2021. Wage growth is key to boosting the American economy while addressing the rising cost of living.  

I make data-driven decisions for the long-term prosperity of working families, not for political convenience. I will protect your rights, end dark money, establish term limits, ban insider trading, close tax loopholes abused by billionaires and work with allies to restore global stability. We must replace the career politician with a pragmatic engineer. I will represent you, not party bosses. Read the plan at JamesForWashington.com.



Source: Washington Secretary of State Voters’ Guide, U.S. Representative. Accessed 2026-07-16. Reproduced verbatim and unedited; the wording, punctuation and emphasis are the candidate’s own.
