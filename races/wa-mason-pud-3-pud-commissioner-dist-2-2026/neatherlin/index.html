Statement
My priorities:

Slow or stop these huge rate increases by budgeting and living within our means.

Prioritizing PUD Broadband options. Creating competition, better service and access in rural areas. 

Local service hubs providing quicker access for customers and outage response.

Investing into infrastructure expansion and upgrades to profit from inevitable future growth.

Aim for zero rate increases for 4 years

I have spent 20 years successfully representing you in local government. 6 years as a Port commissioner learning the basics of government budgeting, grants, economic development and utility infrastructure. 14 years as your Mason County Commissioner learning every aspect of a responsible government with 400+ employees. I successfully focused on restructuring our county’s fiscal position. Rightsized our government personnel, refocusing on Law Enforcement and services that benefited you. Took us from a low of $165K cash on hand to a whopping $29 million cash in the General fund alone. We did all this without raising county taxes for 10 years. We simply lived within our means! No other county in Washington can claim this today. I have decades of experience in managing a controlled budget and yes, experience does matter!

I fix broken things. I can help here too!
