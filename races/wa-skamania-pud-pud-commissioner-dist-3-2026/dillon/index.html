1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (General Election 2026)

Reliable utilities are essential, but rising costs are putting real pressure on working families, seniors, and small businesses. Aging infrastructure, purchased power, and major capital projects all affect your monthly bill, and ratepayers deserve clear answers. You shouldn't have to go on a scavenger hunt to understand your rates. We should prioritize affordability, evaluating major projects carefully and reducing unnecessary exposure to outside energy costs that can be unpredictable. The PUD must protect reliability while being efficient, transparent, and responsible with every dollar. I hope to earn your trust and serve as a thoughtful advocate for ratepayers as your Commissioner.
