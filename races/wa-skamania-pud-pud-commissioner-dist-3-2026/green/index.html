1 Voters’ Pamphlet Statement (General Election 2026)

As your commissioner for 12 years, I have worked to ensure the customers have a voice locally, statewide and regionally. Continually working to improve and rebuild the 90-year-old system has been challenging but I’m confident we are laying a solid foundation for the next 90 years. The utility industry is facing a challenging regulatory environment that is increasing costs and chances of power shortages. I have advocated for Skamania PUD, and will continue to encourage and support sound business practices with collaborative relationships between the PUD, communities, county and state. Thank you for trusting me with your vote.
