Elected Experience
Your State Representative since 2019—my greatest responsibility and deepest honor



Other Professional Experience
Behavioral Health: Washington Recovery Alliance founder, UW School of Social Work lecturer, adolescent mental health program director. International Development: Gates Foundation analyst, Fulbright Scholar (West Africa). Education: taught preschool, adult ESL

Education
Brown University (BA, Ethnic Studies)



Community Service
Advisory Boards: Shoreline Sister City Association, Latino Center for Health (Domestic Violence Board), UW Suicide Care Research Center, Lynnwood Community Justice Center. Awards: Center for Human Services Outstanding Community Support, NAMI Behavioral Health Champion, Lynnwood Chamber Community Service finalist, Public Health Champion, PSBJ 40 Under 40, City Champion, Crosscut Courage in Public Service

Statement
To fight addiction, I passed Ricky’s Law, created the Recovery Navigator Program, launched treatment programs inside jails and ERs, and spearheaded the Lynnwood Crisis Care Center. To combat domestic violence, I funded training for judges, legal assistance for survivors, and victim advocates who respond with police. I’ve championed healthcare for immigrants and protections against ICE. I’m completely pro-choice. I’m committed to tackling affordability, education funding, homelessness, and gun violence.

Endorsements: Governors: Ferguson, Gregoire; Congress: Representatives DelBene, Larsen, Smith; Councilmembers: Seattle, Shoreline, Lynnwood, Mountlake Terrace, Edmonds; Unions: Firefighters, Nurses, Teachers, Police Officers, Grocery Workers, State Labor Council; 32nd District Democrats
